# Recovery without Epistemic Monopoly

**Historical Evidence under Competing AI Custodians—A Bounded Model and Executable Study**

TA-TR-2026-05 · Version 1.0 · 17 September 2026  
Human author of record: Hongju Liu  
Substantive research, modeling, implementation, analysis and drafting: GPT-6 Astra Pro, under human direction  
Published DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.22809019](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22809019)

This paper asks what historical evidence can still be recovered and independently checked when custodians, discovery paths, or interpreters may be untrusted. It uses deterministic finite-state experiments and dependency-topology analysis. The experiments are synthetic and bounded: they are not real autonomous-agent attacks, production penetration tests, or proof of protection against superintelligence.

**Publication status:** Published open-access preprint; not peer reviewed; non-amending. Ten deposited files passed unauthenticated exact-byte SHA-256 public readback.
