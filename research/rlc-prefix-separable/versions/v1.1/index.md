# Exponential Run Complexity of Prefix-Separable Orders on the Boolean Cube

TA-TR-2026-22 · Version 1.1 · 3 October 2026

Human author of record and responsible depositor: Hongju Liu.

**Version DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23118189](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23118189)

This English mathematical preprint is a linked revision of [version 1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23103274). Internal analytic and computational review is disclosed; the paper has not undergone independent external peer review.

The general all-weight theorem retains the order of growth `2^n/n^2` and improves its liminf coefficient by `4 log 2`, approximately 2.77. The revision also proves two separately quantified paired-family scan theorems, an any-parent conditional extension theorem, and precise obstructions to selected concentration and mean-doubling routes. Those route obstructions do not disprove the original target `M_n >= c 2^n`, which remains open. The conditional controlled-loss reduction is not a completed proof of that target.
