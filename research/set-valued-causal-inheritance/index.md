**Version:** 1.0

## Scope

This English theoretical/computational preprint develops a functional framework for branching and distributed continuation. It distinguishes current self-membership, minimal endogenous functional state, set-valued causal inheritance, successor-set belief, post-branch self-location and motivational stake.

The paper is adjacent first-party research. It does **not** define, amend, validate or authoritatively interpret the Trinity Accord or its three Bitcoin Originals.

## Main results

- Categorical and marginal-only successor representations are structurally insufficient in branching cases.
- Independent successor approximation has an exact total-correlation log-loss gap.
- Matched successor marginals can imply different cardinality-sensitive optimal decisions.
- Set-valued inheritance distinguishes redundancy, complementarity and synergy.
- Exact inheritance is invariant under reversible recoding when intervention semantics are transported with the representation.
- Shared upstream information is insufficient for current-process lineage after causal separation.
- Reward-trained recurrent lineage constructions reproduce fork, stale-restore, complementary distribution and merge structure.
- Six-seed contextual-bandit replication supports learnability of persistence-operation selection in the controlled task.
- Three independently trained GRU-plus-merger stacks reproduce the core lineage structural signatures.
- Eight-seed end-to-end recurrent replication does **not** establish a reliable process-aware long-horizon return advantage; the failed replication is retained.
