## New finding QC-20261008-13 — target binding is stipulated rather than constructed

Severity: **MEDIUM for the R181 positive interpretation**. Status: **OPEN**.

Precise sources:

- R181 manuscript §§1, 3, 4 and 6;
- `model.py`, where `target`, `directive` and `closure` are independent input coordinates and `target` directly selects either `command` or `rehearsal`;
- R181-C3 and R181-H1 in `CLAIM_LEDGER.json`;
- R181-G1–G3 in `GAP_LEDGER.json`.

Argument and counterexample: R181 correctly says that an analyst label is insufficient, but its finite construction does not itself supply the missing physical binding. The value `target="overt"` or `target="imagery"` is passed in from outside; `directive` and `closure` are stipulated role bits. The 128 rows therefore establish conditional combinatorics once target-specific use and same-policy consumption have already been admitted. They do not construct the actual carrier, consumer path or counterfactual that makes one token about overt action rather than imagery.

Consider two cross-wired systems with the same directive code, represented movement, downstream waveform, movement/report condition and local `D=C=1`. In system O the overt policy consumes the directive and bodily feedback; in system I the imagery policy consumes the directive and rehearsal feedback. If the label `tau` is assigned from the report or from the desired phenomenal interpretation after the run, the same physical evidence can be relabelled either way. A valid distinction must follow the actual consumer path fixed before the target judgement, not the analyst's name. This is compatible with R181-C3 as a model-level conditional result but blocks its promotion to an actual organization without a binding instance.

Impact: local model results R181-C1/C2 and their nonidentification witnesses remain valid on the declared domain. What remains unproved is the physical premise needed by C3/H1 and by any later C1 transport. Check counts cannot discharge it. No conclusion about basal experience, voluntariness, subject count or current systems follows.

Required action / closure evidence: declare `TARGET_BINDING_INSTANCE(P,I,K,tau)` before any report/feeling comparison, including the actual directive carrier, selected consumer policy, feedback or mismatch path, admitted intervention family, and a counterfactual showing which policy's operation changes when directive content changes. Give a cross-wired negative control in which represented content and output are fixed but consumer paths are exchanged. Classification must track the actual path rather than an analyst label or later attribution. Closure fails if `tau` can be reassigned after seeing the report/feeling, or if `D`/`C` remain free bits without a jointly realizable path model.

Verification basis: direct source inspection, rerun of the 128-row/12-check program, and the explicit cross-wired countermodel above. This is an inferential-scope finding, not a claim that R181's code is arithmetically wrong.

## Directional obligation under QC-10 — do not rename generic regulation as felt trying

R181 itself notices the strongest current alternative: a thermostat, autopilot, spinal controller or automatized skill can instantiate a directive/closure/mismatch-consumption motif. UCT need not deny such systems basal experience, but the local motif alone does not select the named quality *trying*. The next round should stop after one discriminating comparison rather than extend another role taxonomy.

Concrete question: with one target-binding instance fixed, can two jointly realizable systems share the local `(D,C,E)` profile while differing only in an independently grounded body/action-reference relation, and does any independently identified trying-related consequence follow? Acceptance requires a predeclared target, actual consumer paths, one generic-controller rival, one body/action-relative candidate, a held-out consequence and a failure criterion. Failure is informative: if both candidates remain compatible, retain `PC_tau` as an organizational coordinate and leave the phenomenal name OPEN.

## Two focused thought experiments

1. **Cross-wired target.** Fix bearer, interval, directive code, represented movement, downstream waveform, output and report/no-report condition. Vary only whether the overt-action policy or imagery policy actually consumes directive and feedback. The target must follow the physical consumer path. The experiment rejects post-hoc target labels; it cannot establish what either case feels like.
2. **Generic-controller twin.** Fix the local update equations and `(D,C,E)` values in a blocked-action candidate and an isomorphic adaptive regulator. Vary the wider body/action-reference embedding while holding local control performance fixed. If the proposed trying interpretation depends only on the local motif, it predicts no named-quality distinction; if it depends on the wider relation, that relation must be independently defined and admitted. The imagined outcome is not empirical evidence and cannot rank total experience.
