## Why the net contribution is now substantial enough for this scoped paper

The fixed problem asks whether continuing correct control requires complete recalibration. The one-probe lower bound and two-round obstruction rule out an apparently repeatable one-round solution, even with complete retained history. The exact7/8 three-port value makes the failure quantitative under a stated prior. The five-port construction then shows that the solution need not be full reidentification: carefully retained partial uncertainty suffices, and two is the optimal uniform probe cap.

These are not four unrelated examples.
