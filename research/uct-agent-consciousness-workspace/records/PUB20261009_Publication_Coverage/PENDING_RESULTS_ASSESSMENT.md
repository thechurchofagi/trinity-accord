## IL: behavior-preserving conjugacy and constituent-local interventions

The normal-form result classifies bijections preserving the complete primitive local-reset family: factorwise bijections up to factor permutation. The mutual-absorption argument distinguishes resets on the same factor, and conjugacy then determines the coordinatewise map. The source credits the product/Hamming automorphism antecedent. Full Cartesian support and all reset values are essential; whole-state reset access does not identify the factorization.

The two-state LTI pair strengthens an ordinary input-output counterexample by retaining controllability and observability and excluding every local-reset-preserving conjugacy, including nonlinear factorwise candidates. Factorwise conjugacy preserves the cross-coordinate dependency pattern, while one device is diagonal and the other depends across both parts. The argument excludes more than a single failed choice of coordinate matrix. It remains conditional on independently stipulated constituent reset ports.

At the declared prepared state and common subsequent input, the transported source reset requires a joint target write. Even an optimally chosen arbitrary single-part target write has two-output maximum discrepancy at least 1/11: if e=a-2, then 1 <= |1+3e|+3|e| <= 11M. The stated a=21/11 attains equality. The number depends on calibrated output units. General sparse-write and observability bounds explain when the mismatch becomes detectable; hidden compensation or an altered intervention time changes the contract.

Current decision: a substantive, carefully bounded mathematical application worth retaining and integrating. Its argument is stronger than merely restating equal boundary behavior, but the entire package's external priority remains unverified, and its relation to the published UCT I physical-signature requirement and classical realization/causal-abstraction literature must be explicit. It does not independently establish that the selected ports are the actual constitutive factorization of a brain or other system.

## Why these are not automatically a larger new paper

Placing AC, IL and R188 side by side supplies several useful examples of unavailable distinctions, access constraints and intervention costs. It does not by itself produce a new mechanism or a new result joining them. A broad organization/experience manuscript would repeat UCT I/III, TA25, RT/TH and the MGTD method. A focused paper remains possible if a precise shared problem requires the combined results and a clearly stated net implication survives the predecessor comparison. Until that implication is supplied, retain the results and their open applications without declaring a major breakthrough or releasing a redundant standalone article.
