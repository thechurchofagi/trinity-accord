## 0. Five questions, not one

For a fixed bearer P, time interval I, physical scale g and comparison signature K, keep separate:

1. **Physical actual support:** Do the supposed constituents and cross-links exist in the same episode, rather than merely in descriptions?
2. **Consistency/existence of a global completion:** Are all local constraints jointly realizable by a *single actual* organization with each shared carrier counted once?
3. **Uniqueness of the complete physical type:** If a global completion exists, do the local views identify its physical organization up to a defensible isomorphism, including mechanism, timing, interventions, provenance, boundaries and joint/actual participation?
4. **C1 transport:** If a valid actual full organization is fixed, UCT I v1.2 C1 *postulates* its structural–experiential identity. C1 does not license retroactive identification of full organization from insufficient local measurements.
5. **Selected feeling and subjecthood:** The named interpretation *felt trying*, *my body*, or an exclusive subject partition is not inferred by merely completing (1)–(4). Such targets require additional noncircular bridges / individuation evidence.

This is a provenance-and-inference ladder, not a new hierarchy of degrees of consciousness.

## 1. Mathematical interface and a safe inference rule

Let \(\mathcal{O}_{P,I,K}^{\mathrm{ontic}}\) denote a complete token-relative organization only at its justified scope. Let \(r_i\) be a *declared, physically legitimate* local-view operation (not necessarily a true closed subsystem). A local observation family is \(\mathbf L=(L_i)_i\).

Define the admissible completion set, relative to the stated scope and physical realization constraints, as

\[
\mathrm{Ext}(\mathbf L)=
\{[\mathcal O]_{\cong}\mid r_i(\mathcal O)\cong L_i,\;\forall i;\ \mathcal O\text{ satisfies the stated physical-admissibility contract}\}.
\]

**Conditional completion trichotomy:**
- \(\mathrm{Ext}=\varnothing\): no single physical organization satisfies the proposed local constraints. This indicts their joint physical admission, not the presence/absence of experience.
- \(|\mathrm{Ext}|=1\): within this declared model class, the views determine one full *model type*. Whether the class is ontically complete for a real brain remains an independent question.
- \(|\mathrm{Ext}|>1\): local views underdetermine the complete organization. Under C1-OI, if the completions are genuinely distinct actual full structural types, they correspond to distinct **possible complete experiential structural types**, but selected named feelings may still coincide.

Here \(\mathrm{Ext}\) is not automatically an empirical posterior and its cardinality is not an amount of consciousness. This completion logic is standard inverse/extension mathematics, with precedents in marginal problems, sheaf contextuality and compositional causal modeling; UCT's work is specifying physically admissible completions and preventing inference-type substitutions.

## 2. T1: shared physical memory and incompatible closed-local specifications

Three constituents A,M,B with **one actually shared** memory M. Treat the left (A,M) and right (M,B) as two separate *closed* deterministic descriptions:

\[
m^+=u(a,m)=a\oplus m,\qquad
m^+=v(m,b)=m\oplus b.
\]

Both specifications must determine **the same actual event's** next value. They agree exactly when a=b. Of the 8 binary triples, only 4 are compatible.

Therefore one cannot assume the full independent product of states and simultaneously give each overlap a full, closed update equation without verifying agreement. On a genuinely independent product of allowed a,b, the identity u(a,m)=v(m,b) for all states forces both sides to depend only on m; that is a restriction of the **closed-local modeling assumption**, not a prohibition of actual left/right causal feedback.

**Repair:** use open local mechanisms and a common joint rule \(m^+=h(a,m,b)\), with boundary ports and joint intervention semantics declared before gluing. The current full token then has a coherent common physical history. A duplicate simulator state called M is not the same as a single shared physical carrier.

**Limit:** if the physical admissible state domain enforces a=b, the original local rules can agree. Domain constraints cannot be silently replaced by a full product assumption.

This revisits the earlier dialogue's exact shared-update proof; it is not newly attributable to this note.

## 3. T2: pairwise overlap consistency still need not admit any global assignment

Take binary variables a,m,b and proposed pairwise distributions:

\[
P(a,m)=\tfrac12\delta_{00}+\tfrac12\delta_{11},
\quad
P(m,b)=\tfrac12\delta_{00}+\tfrac12\delta_{11},
\quad
P(a,b)=\tfrac12\delta_{01}+\tfrac12\delta_{10}.
\]

Each one-variable marginal is uniform \(\{0,1\}\); every overlapping pair therefore agrees on its shared one-variable marginal. Yet positive joint distributions on a,m,b satisfying all three are impossible: the first two require a=m=b while the third requires a\neq b. Enumeration confirms zero globally admissible states.

**Important qualification:** this is a mathematical obstruction for a *stipulated joint classical description*. It is **not evidence that a real brain exhibits quantum/Bell-style contextuality**. If the three pairwise constraints came from incompatible preparations, time windows, intervened mechanisms or different bearers, the assumption of one common actual P/I/K would be false. A physical theory must not impose simultaneous compatibility on mutually exclusive contexts without a further common-regime witness.

Reference: Abramsky & Brandenburger, *New Journal of Physics* 13:113036 (2011), DOI 10.1088/1367-2630/13/11/113036 (sheaf-contextuality/global-section framework), and subsequent work on causal contextuality. This mathematics predates UCT.

## 4. T3: even all pairwise probability tables can fail to determine one whole

Two stochastic whole-model candidates on the same three binary carriers:

- \(\mu_U\): uniform over all 8 global states, probability 1/8 each.
- \(\mu_E\): uniform over the 4 **even-parity** states, probability 1/4 each.

For every one of the three *pair* selections (a,m), (m,b), (a,b), and every pair value, the marginal is 1/4 under **both** models. Hence every pairwise joint table, not merely the one-variable overlap, matches exactly.

Their triple relations differ: \(\mu_U[a\oplus m\oplus b=0]=1/2\), but \(\mu_E[a\oplus m\oplus b=0]=1\). Global supports have cardinalities 8 and 4, so a bijective state relabeling does not identify them. In a physically installed model whose joint stochastic constraint is actual and part of the constitutive signature, this is a bona fide **full organizational difference with matched pairwise views**. It does not identify valence, selfhood, or any named phenomenal content. Pairwise brain recordings or pairwise model comparisons could fail to expose such a physical joint relation.

This higher-order-correlation possibility is established prior mathematics and overlaps existing TA16/TA20 warnings; it is a concrete falsifier of a naive "pairwise reconstruction always gives the whole" shortcut, **not** independent proof of C1.

## 5. A separate diagram consistency debt

UCT I v1.2 C1 is formulated **tokenwise** and isomorphically. It is necessary to distinguish objectwise isomorphism from **compatible assignments across multiple token-inclusion/restriction maps**, if one additionally claims that local experiential diagrams can be glued.

An exact four-object set-model illustrates the issue:
- A,B,C,D each have a 2-element carrier; all physical diagram arrows are identities and their diamond commutes.
- All four individual phenomenal objects have 2-element carriers, so each is separately isomorphic to its physical counterpart.
- Suppose proposed experiential inter-object arrows along A→B, B→D and A→C are identity but C→D swaps the two elements. The experiential diamond does not commute.
- No selection of four objectwise bijections \(h_A,h_B,h_C,h_D\) satisfies all edge equations: the first three demand \(h_D=h_A\), the final requires \(h_D=\mathrm{swap}\circ h_A\). Exhaustive binary check finds 0 assignments.

**Status of the example:** the inconsistent experiential arrows were deliberately *imposed* by the analyst and are **not justified as actual physical/phenomenal restrictions**. This is an audit of a too-strong inference: separate tokenwise isomorphisms do not, by themselves, authorize an arbitrary cross-token map or a noncommuting phenomenal atlas. A genuine extension should define the relevant restrictions and prove their naturality/compatibility from the common physically anchored full organization, not insert a new "consistency law" as an empirically established principle. There is no new C1 theorem here.

## 6. T4: an explicit crossed physical consumer, repairing QC-20261008-13 *at toy scope*

R181's named target parameter was an independent input; its positive meaning remained stipulated. Build a concrete finite implementation where **physical endpoint and counterfactual response** determine the target. Fix a bounded assembly P_T, interval I=[0,2], and a recorded q bit. Two installed ports connect to different physically declared destinations: \`U_o\` is a proximal effector-command port; \`H_i\` is an internal rehearsal register. The actual route is chosen **before the episode** by a physical two-position switch \`s\`; no semantic feeling label or inferred subject is wired in.

Let \`e_o=e_i=1\` be a fixed alternative value for each off-route destination. Define:

\[
U_o = \begin{cases}q &s=o\\ 1&s=i\end{cases},
\qquad
H_i = \begin{cases}1 &s=o\\ q&s=i.\end{cases}
\]

An **externally forced** plant displacement \`B=1\` and a report \`R=1\` are held fixed by separate stipulated sources, so they do not trivially reveal which port actually received q. At q=1 the internal register values, displacement and report are all matched for both switch settings; but under the physically admissible fixed-mechanism content change do(q=0):
- If s=o: (U_o,H_i)=(0,1);
- If s=i: (U_o,H_i)=(1,0).

A **physical route/path signature** together with this perturbation distinguishes an overt-command *target from an imagery-register target* without reading off a post-hoc title. Note: in this limited model the external plant force screens movement from U_o, so overt movement is *not* evidence that the q command caused that movement; the actual path and contribution have to be recorded independently, consistent with TA18 actual participation.

**Do not overclaim:** This toy establishes the actual routing rule by specification; the counterfactual is a check, not a way of creating actual membership. A real human bearer P_H with independently grounded motor reference, sensory feedback, history and admissible interventions has **not** been supplied. The route can be functional even if a reflex controller uses it; no specific felt trying has been identified. Thus QC13 gets a candidate *toy-contract repair*, not closure of its actual-instance requirement.

## 7. T5: a generic-controller twin defeats a fast bridge to "felt trying"

Freeze a local control motif (directive used, consequence fed back, mismatch consumed) \((D,C,E)=(1,1,1)\) in two hypothetical physically realizable devices. In the first, the local policy routes commands to an effector embedded in a broader human body reference and proprioceptive regulatory history; in the second, the **same local equations** regulate a simulated visual cursor or an artificial actuator.

Any proposed classifier of named \`F_try\` from **only** the triple \((D,C,E)\) must give the same classification in both cases. That fact does not prove the actual feelings differ. It proves that if one wants the two feelings distinguished, the local triple cannot be sufficient; an independently characterized physical embedding, additional bodily reference relations, or a different interpretation is required.

A naive fix "biological tissue rather than artifact" is not safe: human prosthesis/robotic-limb embodiment findings and conceptual separations of agency, body representation and ownership are established independently (e.g. Graczyk et al., 2023, PMID 36590084; research synthesis in Frontiers in Neurorobotics 2022 DOI 10.3389/fnbot.2022.902162). A prosthesis may become action-relevant to the person's body model. Biological composition cannot be smuggled in as a universal consciousness threshold. Nor does generic goal-directed control prove familiar mineness.

Therefore **R181's PC_tau remains an organizational coordinate** rather than a discovered marker of a named feeling. The positive research direction is a physically grounded *body/action reference embedding*, not another set of report labels.

6. Complete ontology ≠ finite model; state which part of the complete P is only assumed;
7. C1 transport is explicitly conditional, and any specific felt-quality label requires an independent bridge;
8. Lower-scale and larger-scale processes may overlap; no exclusive owner, hierarchy of consciousness scores, or subject count is inferred.

Completion tests: a realizable toy witness, its crosswired negative control, one nontrivial failure of gluing or uniqueness, and a prospective explanation that survives human prosthesis / generic-controller and no-report thought experiments. Steps 1–6 can be model-tested now; item 7 requires a noncircular bridge still missing.
