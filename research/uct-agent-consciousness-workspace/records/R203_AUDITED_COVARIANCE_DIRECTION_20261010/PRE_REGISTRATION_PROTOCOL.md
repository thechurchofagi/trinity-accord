# Adult reporting audit — version 0.2.0; not yet a human study

## Frozen target and scope

H is the selected retentive bodily/action familiarity coordinate, distinguished from ownership O, agency G, actual coupling C, task success and pleasantness. The proposed comparison asks “Which episode felt more continuous with your established way of acting?” This wording fixes the intended target but does not establish reliability. J is a fallible binary comparison assigned to one prospectively specified episode of a pair; M must refer to that same episode. Comparisons with an unknown or shifting reference are inadmissible.

Freeze the instrument and threshold for one marker before confirmatory data. No physiological marker is validated by this protocol. Candidate names, physical route, bearer/interval/signature, missingness model and the justification for b>0 and kappa are mandatory unresolved fields; until filled, the output is observed association only.

## Sequence and minimal factorial design

Use familiar and newly learned cursor-control mappings as manipulable task contexts, without identifying mapping history with H. A preregistered software gate routes action through either a retained adaptive mapping or a yoked bypass controller. This is physical/software route evidence E_U, not complete human organization or direct H measurement.

1. Use a separate development cohort to freeze question comprehension, semantic recoding, marker and all cutoffs. Comprehension/repeatability do not estimate a,c relative to H.
2. Confirmatory independent adults receive randomized mapping-history and route-gate cells, crossed with externally randomized feedback value. Familiarization duration, mappings and yoked outputs must be fixed before data. Do not select successful trials to match outcomes.
3. Choose one evaluation episode per adult in advance. Conceal randomized audit selection until after recording M. Randomize response-key coding only after M is recorded. Include prospective no-prompt expectation controls if anticipation is a candidate cause.
4. Ask J about the already recorded episode. Record refusal/missingness. Randomized invitation does not justify deleting nonresponders. Do not silently substitute complete-case data for V.
5. Record O,G, success and fluency separately for dissociation, without defining H from them or conditioning the primary estimate on post-treatment variables.
6. Analyze each preregistered cell with its own fixed-n bound and alpha allocation, rather than pooling cells whose means/relations differ. Stop collection at fixed n; sample size is chosen only after an externally justified minimum effect and kappa exist. No sample size is currently recommended.

## Observable tests versus external warrant

| Requirement | Audit can test | What remains external or conditional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Key recoding | Semantic J should be invariant within declared tolerance after reversal | Comprehension does not prove b>0 |
| Audit assignment | Balance, concealed assignment and completion rates | Nonignorable missing J/H may remain |
| Route manipulation | Gate logs and perturbation show the declared machine route differs | Complete bearer U and H necessity remain separate |
| Feedback/control | Randomized feedback and action outcomes show dissociation from simple reward coding | A passed control does not bound all residual R |
| Marker relation | K and fixed-n confidence bounds | H-class dependence and kappa cannot be recovered by conditioning on J |
| Route-necessity hypothesis | Verified bypass preserves effect beyond preregistered tolerance | Rejects only the frozen necessity hypothesis, not all H bridges |
| H direction | No self-certifying direct test here | Independently meaningful b>0 and residual bound required |
| Nonreport target | No validation from this adult audit alone | Explicit V-to-T bridge required |

Reject or narrow the concrete marker if semantic key invariance fails, it follows only feedback/success, the route manipulation fails, or a frozen necessary-route prediction is falsified. Passing these checks permits the association analysis; it does not discharge latent warrants. Sensitivity output reports the largest residual budget supported by L_K and the external budget actually supplied. If no independently justified budget exists, abstain from H attribution even at infinite n.
