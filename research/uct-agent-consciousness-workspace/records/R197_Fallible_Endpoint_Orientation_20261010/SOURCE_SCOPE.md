- Patané et al. (2026), *Cortex*, DOI `10.1016/j.cortex.2025.12.008`: implicit/explicit self-movement recognition dissociation; modality distinction, not UCT target validation.

## Exclusions
No source is treated as proving C1, actual route use, complete organization, a unique owner, assistant consciousness, or a universal endpoint. Search was targeted, not an exhaustive worldwide priority review.
