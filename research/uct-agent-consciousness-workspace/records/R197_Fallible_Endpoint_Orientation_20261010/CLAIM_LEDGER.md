# R197 Claim Ledger

## R197-C1 — conditional fallible-endpoint orientation
- **Type/status:** conditional exact result; disabled pending review.
- **Statement:** for `F=A xor s`, a same-target endpoint with independently fixed meanings and `P(E=1|F=1)>P(E=1|F=0)` makes the sign of the endpoint contrast identify `s`.
- **Domain/quantifier:** every declared binary complement pair and directed binary channel.
- **All premises:** fixed target semantics; same application; signed reliability independent of axis names and observed code; nonzero contrast.
- **Proof/source:** direct algebra and exhaustive checker.
- **Counterexample:** without the sign premise, swap latent labels and channel rates.
- **Thought-experiment role:** accurate/inverted report separates evidence from target constitution.

## R197-C2 — modality-neutrality correction
- **Type/status:** correction/new UCT application.
- **Statement:** verbal/nonverbal modality alone entails neither endpoint reliability nor its direction; nonverbal evidence is neither necessary nor sufficient.
- **Domain:** endpoint evidence proposed for R196's target.
- **Proof/source:** the same probability channel can receive either modality label; empirical examples constrain but do not identify `F`.
- **Counterexample:** accurate verbal judgment and inverted nonverbal reflex.
- **Thought-experiment role:** blocks medium labels from doing semantic work.

## R197-C3 — unsigned endpoint nonidentification
- **Type/status:** negative result, inherited label-switching structure.
- **Statement:** absent independent direction, `(F,p1,p0)` and `(1-F,p0,p1)` generate identical observables.
- **Domain:** all binary endpoint channels.
- **Proof/source:** bijective relabeling; 81 finite grid cases.
- **Counterexample/limit:** an independent signed reliability premise breaks the symmetry conditionally.

## R197-C4 — conditional nonreport transport
- **Type/status:** new UCT-specific application of TA15, not new general mathematics.
- **Statement:** orientation transports from calibration to a nonreporting domain only under preserved target semantics, same measurement relation, and class-conditional invariance; equal endpoint marginals are insufficient.
- **Domain:** two domains with one declared binary target/channel pair.
- **All premises:** R197-C1, class-conditional invariance, same target interpretation, actual application bridge.
- **Proof/source:** exact transport check and `4/5,1/5` versus `1/5,4/5` countermodel.
- **Thought-experiment role:** infant/adult and copy/switch cases attack transport.
