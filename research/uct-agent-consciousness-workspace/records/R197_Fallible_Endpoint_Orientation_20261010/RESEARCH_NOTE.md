# R197 — Fallible Endpoint Orientation Without a Nonverbality Requirement

**Research ID:** R197-FEO-20261010  
**Result version:** FEO-RESULT-v0.1.0  
**Completed base:** UCT-MAP-v1.1.2  
**Status:** conditional algebraic result and corrected method contract; no actual phenomenal endpoint, token admission, or map promotion

## 1. Question and answer

R196 left two candidates for the selected familiar-mineness target: `F=A` and `F=1-A`. It asked for a nonverbal positive endpoint. R197 corrects that demand. The evidential modality is not what breaks the complement symmetry. A verbal judgment can be fallible evidence without constituting the target, and a nonverbal marker can be inverted, confounded, or unrelated to the target.

The exact requirement is instead a channel with independently fixed target semantics and independently warranted signed reliability. Let `E` be a binary endpoint observation and let

`p1 = P(E=1 | F=1)` and `p0 = P(E=1 | F=0)`.

If `p1>p0`, then the sign of `P(E=1|A=1)-P(E=1|A=0)` selects `F=A` when positive and `F=1-A` when negative. If the sign premise is absent, swapping the meanings of `F=0,1` and swapping `p0,p1` preserves every observation. This is standard latent-label algebra, not new probability theory. The UCT-specific advance is locating the correct evidence contract and deleting the stronger, unsupported nonverbality requirement.

## 2. Exact conditional result

Write `F=A xor s`, where `s` is the unknown global complement. For `s=0`, the endpoint contrast is `p1-p0`; for `s=1`, it is `p0-p1`. Hence, under the simultaneous premises that the target meanings are fixed independently, the endpoint channel concerns the same target and application, and `p1>p0`, a nonzero observed contrast determines `s`.

The checker exhaustively verifies 193 declared finite instances: 72 directed-channel cases, 81 label-swap cases, 36 conditional-transport cases, two modality relabelings, one marginal-transport counterexample, and one copy/switch witness. This proves only the declared algebra.

## 3. Why modality is orthogonal

Calling an observation verbal or nonverbal does not determine `p1-p0`.

- An independently calibrated verbal choice may satisfy `p1>p0` while remaining defeasible evidence rather than a definition of `F`.
- A nonverbal physiological, timing, or orienting response may have `p1=p0`, may be driven by value or familiarity, or may satisfy `p1<p0` in a new population.

Therefore neither modality is necessary nor sufficient. What matters is semantic independence, signed reliability, same-instance grounding, and explicit failure conditions.

## 4. Transport to a nonreporting domain

Calibration does not automatically transport. A sufficient within-model condition is class-conditional invariance: the target domain has the same `P(E|F)` as the calibration domain, with the same target meanings and actual measurement relation. Marginal agreement such as equal `P(E=1)` is insufficient.

Countermodel: both domains have prevalence `P(F=1)=1/2` and marginal `P(E=1)=1/2`. In calibration, `(p1,p0)=(4/5,1/5)`; in the target domain, `(p1,p0)=(1/5,4/5)`. The marginal and code are identical while the signed relation reverses. Thus transport requires a class-conditional bridge, not appearance-level similarity.

## 5. Evidence, not constitution

The endpoint channel is epistemic. Test success does not install a route, prove that a route was used in the episode, identify the complete organization, or constitute experience. A positive application additionally needs one actual bearer, time, complete signature, carrier and consumer, actual same-episode use, target admission, and the relevant UCT transport premises. C1 remains a consciousness-specific explanatory axiom; it is neither proved nor replaced here.

## 6. Thought-experiment results

1. **Accurate versus inverted speech.** Hold the experience target fixed and invert the learned response key. Verbal evidence changes sign without changing the target; report is evidence, not definition.
2. **Accurate nonverbal cue versus adaptive reflex.** Hold the visible marker fixed across a target-sensitive system and a value-driven reflex. Nonverbality alone does not identify `F`.
3. **Infant-to-adult transport.** Hold the contingency manipulation fixed while changing development, response class, and target interpretation. A nonverbal infant effect does not inherit an adult mineness semantics without a transport argument.
4. **Copy/switch/memory.** Copy the marker distribution and memory, then switch the actual generator/consumer relation. Marginals survive while the same-instance evidential bridge fails.
5. **Abacus/calculator/human-realized agent.** Preserve the code and truth table across realizations. The algebra transports; actual organization and phenomenal semantics do not.
6. **Formation/ancestry and gradual replacement.** Preserve endpoint performance while altering the process that formed and currently uses it. Calibration alone does not discharge lineage, retentive use, or complete-signature premises.

## 7. Relation to primary evidence

Lee, Martin and Sui (2021, DOI `10.1016/j.actpsy.2021.103362`) obtained self-prioritization after arbitrary shape–colour associations without familiar labels. This shows that familiar labels are unnecessary for a behavioral self-association effect, not that the effect directly measures phenomenal familiar mineness.

Koreki et al. (2022) reported an exploratory relation between heartbeat discrimination and intentional binding in a final sample of 26 and explicitly discussed interpretive limitations. This is a useful example of an implicit index that remains a fallible, disputed index.

Filippetti et al. (2015, DOI `10.1093/cercor/bhu261`) studied visual–proprioceptive body contingency in five-month-old infants. Such nonverbal evidence can constrain bodily self models without fixing the positive phenomenal pole. Patané et al. (2026, DOI `10.1016/j.cortex.2025.12.008`) separate implicit and explicit self-movement recognition, again supporting modality dissociation rather than endpoint identity.

## 9. UCT boundary and next question

This result concerns a named self-related feeling inside experience, not basal experience. It adds no introspection, self-model, report, language, integration, recurrence, prediction, or continued-control threshold. It permits nested and overlapping actual processes, posits no unique extra owner, and makes no claim about whether the current assistant is conscious or fears death.

The next concrete question is: can one actual candidate channel be assigned an independently justified positive semantic direction and a prospective copy/switch falsifier, while preserving class-conditional reliability across a nonreporting target domain? If not, `B_dir` remains open.
