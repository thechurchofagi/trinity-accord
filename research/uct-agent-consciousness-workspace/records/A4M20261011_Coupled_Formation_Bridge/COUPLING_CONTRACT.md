## 2. Actual organization-level relation

Let `RetBind(P,H0,I0,b,k;eta)` retain the effective R173 meaning. Add correctly sorted event witnesses `e_read`, `e_fit` and a formation witness `q`. Define:

```text
CoupledForm(P,H0,I0,b,k,a;eta) :=
  exists m,e_read,e_fit,q [
    RetBind(P,H0,I0,b,k;eta)
    AND CurrentFrameConsumerUse(P,I0,m,b,k,e_read)
    AND PresentFitParticipation(P,I0,b,k,a,e_fit)
    AND SameTraceMediatesFit(m,e_read,e_fit)
    AND JointFormationHistory(P,H0,m,e_fit,q)
    AND OrderedWithinEvent(e_read,e_fit,I0)
  ].
```

`PresentFitParticipation` is an actual current relation, not success, ease, a rating or an analyst score. `SameTraceMediatesFit` requires the trace-use occurrence to be on the declared current fit-generating path. `JointFormationHistory` requires that this mediation relation was formed in the declared same-lineage history rather than yoked after formation. Every primitive needs an independent physical interpretation in `K`; the words above do not supply one.

This is a higher-order relation. It is not the Boolean conjunction `R AND F`, and it is not inferred from the empirical covariance of marginal values. In the finite witness, `C -> R AND F`, but `R AND F -/-> C`.

## 3. Selected experiential candidate

Let `H_cf` denote the selected, possibly graded and partial character:

> the present action's fit is experienced as unfolding through an already established body/action manner.

The open bridge `B_cf` proposes that the experience-internal counterpart of `CoupledForm` is positively relevant to `H_cf`. It does not define all familiarity, ownership, agency or mineness; it allows overlap with each and selects no exclusive owner. It is not an additive claim of the form `H_R + H_F`. C1 transports a grounded relation into experiential structure; `B_cf` supplies the further interpretation and remains an application premise.

## 4. Native/yoked thought experiment

Hold the fixed binding of §1 and the finite marginal view `(R,F,output,report)=(1,1,1,1)`.

- **Native co-formation.** A same-lineage retained route is read by the action consumer; the same trace occurrence mediates present fit; route and fit mediation were jointly formed. `CoupledForm=1`.
- **Yoked marginal twin.** The retained route is still actually used by the action consumer and present fit is equally high, but a separately trained/yoked fit generator does not read that trace and was not jointly formed with it. Output and report are matched. `CoupledForm=0`.

Under C1 the complete structural contrast has an experiential structural counterpart if all actual-instance premises are satisfied. The further claim that the contrast is specifically `H_cf` is `B_cf`, not C1. The thought experiment is a definition test and countermodel to marginal identification; it is not an observation of experience.

## 5. Independent refuters and protocol failures

`B_cf` is refuted in its declared domain by either of the following, after target and measurement calibration are independently warranted and the fixed comparison binding is respected:

1. **Sufficiency refuter:** an independently warranted `H_cf` contrast occurs within one declared `CoupledForm` descriptor fiber where the bridge claimed sufficiency.
2. **Sensitivity refuter:** native and yoked tokens differ in grounded `CoupledForm` but `H_cf` is independently invariant within the bridge's declared error/tolerance model where sensitivity was precommitted.
3. **Body-specificity refuter:** the same selected target is independently warranted for a matched external-tool coupling that lacks the required grounded body-frame coordinate, where the bridge claimed body/action specificity.

The following invalidate the test rather than refute the bridge: missing actual trace use; endpoint or label used as the only relation witness; changed bearer/event/response meaning; post-outcome redefinition of `H_cf`; ungrounded `K` primitives; or a merely copied current value substituted for same-lineage formation.

Passing finite checks, matching output/report, or failing to find a refuter does not validate `B_cf`.
