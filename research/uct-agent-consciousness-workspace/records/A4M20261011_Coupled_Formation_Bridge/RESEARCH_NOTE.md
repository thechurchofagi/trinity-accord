```text

  RetBind
  AND current trace use
  AND present fit participation
  AND the same trace-use event mediates fit
```

Every conjunct is simultaneous and same-instance. An old training file plus a current high-fit output is not enough. A current trace reader plus an independent fit controller is not enough. ```text
