# R198 — The Self-Profile Non-Scalarization Result

**Research ID:** R198-SPNS-20261010  
**Result version:** SPNS-RESULT-v0.1.0  
**Completed base:** UCT-MAP-v1.1.2  
**Status:** exact finite non-identification result and target correction; no actual phenomenal coordinate, token admission, or map promotion

## 1. Question and answer

R193–R197 represented a selected familiar-mineness target by one binary variable `F`. That representation is legitimate only after one target has been selected and its semantics fixed. It does not show that bodily/action-related self-experience is globally one-dimensional, nor that ownership, agency, familiarity, and practical coupling aggregate into a unique `F_fam`.

R198 tests that hidden scalarization step. The answer is negative. Empirical dissociations make at least body ownership and action agency distinct candidate coordinates. Adding UCT's retentive familiarity and current bearer-relative practical coupling gives a typed candidate profile

`S = (O, G, H, C)`

where `O` is body ownership, `G` is action agency/authorship, `H` is retentive familiarity/continuity, and `C` is current practical coupling. These are not claimed to be exhaustive or intrinsically binary. They are a finite audit vocabulary.

Endpoint values and coordinatewise monotonicity do not identify one Boolean scalarization of this profile. In four binary dimensions, 166 nonconstant monotone scalarizations satisfy `f(0,0,0,0)=0` and `f(1,1,1,1)=1`. Even the substantively inappropriate assumption that the typed coordinates are exchangeable leaves four threshold choices. Therefore the search for a positive endpoint must first name one coordinate or explicitly defend an aggregation convention. R197 may orient a preselected target; it cannot select that target.

## 2. Exact finite result

Let `X={0,1}^k` with the componentwise partial order. An admissible scalarization is a monotone Boolean map `f:X->{0,1}` with fixed endpoints `f(0)=0` and `f(1)=1`. Exhaustive truth-table enumeration gives:

| dimensions | all monotone Boolean functions | endpoint-fixed nonconstant functions | permutation-invariant functions |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| 3 | 20 | 18 | 3 |
| 4 | 168 | 166 | 4 |

The counts are standard Dedekind-number combinatorics. UCT does not claim them as new mathematics.

For the two-coordinate ownership/agency profiles `x=(1,0)` and `y=(0,1)`, the four admissible monotone scalarizations realize all four label pairs `(f(x),f(y))`: `00`, `01`, `10`, and `11`. Thus the empirically meaningful contrast between ownership without agency and agency without ownership does not merely leave their ordering unknown; it leaves every binary aggregate classification compatible with monotonicity and the extreme anchors.

For crossed four-coordinate profiles `x=(1,0,1,0)` and `y=(0,1,0,1)`, the 166 admissible functions split as follows: 47 produce `00`, 36 produce `10`, 36 produce `01`, and 47 produce `11`. No ranking or positive-pole assignment between these incomparable profiles follows from coordinatewise improvement alone.

## 3. Interpretation: three claims that must not be conflated

1. **Typed-profile claim.** Several self-related relations can occur within experience and can overlap or dissociate.
2. **Coordinate-orientation claim.** A named coordinate, such as retentive familiarity `H`, may still have a complement ambiguity; R197 supplies a conditional method for orienting that already named target.
3. **Aggregation claim.** A rule that compresses `(O,G,H,C)` into one global familiar-mineness bit is an additional modeling choice. It is not delivered by dissociation evidence, monotonicity, endpoint anchors, or R197's sign theorem.

R198 establishes only the finite underdetermination of the third claim and the consequent typing discipline. It does not establish that human self-experience literally has four binary coordinates.

## 4. Positive UCT replacement

The correction is not to abandon a positive explanation. It is to type the explanatory target.

- `O` asks whether a body or part is experienced as belonging to this experiential field.
- `G` asks whether a particular action is experienced as authored or controlled from this organization.
- `H` asks whether the present bearer-relative pattern is experienced as familiar or continuous with the retained pattern.
- `C` asks whether this process is currently installed and used in bearer-relative perception/action correction.

`O`, `G`, and `H` are experience-related targets. `C` is an actual-organization relation and is not itself a feeling. Tests of `C` can supply evidence relevant to the other coordinates only under a stated bridge. The four coordinates therefore must not be treated as exchangeable votes.

For the immediate mainline, familiar mineness is narrowed to `H`, not to an aggregate of all four coordinates. Ownership and agency become typed comparison variables; current practical coupling becomes an organizational candidate bridge. Any later aggregate must state its purpose, domain, weights or threshold, and counterexamples. It must never be reified as an extra owner.

## 5. Empirical motivation and limits

Kalckert and Ehrsson's moving-rubber-hand experiment systematically varied movement timing, active/passive movement, and anatomical congruence. Passive movement removed agency while leaving ownership; incongruent placement diminished ownership without eliminating agency. This supplies a direct double-dissociation witness for `O` and `G`, not a proof of UCT and not a measurement of `H`.

Longo et al.'s psychometric analysis of rubber-hand embodiment found separable ownership, location, and agency subcomponents. Serino et al. later analyzed ownership, self-location, and first-person perspective as components that can dissociate. These results motivate a typed profile and warn against one global score. They do not establish a unique inventory or binary ontology.

Recent work continues to treat ownership and agency as separable but interacting. The 2023 full-factorial fMRI study by Abdulkarim et al. reports behavioral and neural dissociations under movement conditions; the 2024 psychometric investigation of virtual embodiment again models multiple factors. These are convergence checks, not semantic endpoints for familiar mineness.

Every cited paradigm uses operational measures, often including reports. R197 therefore still applies: a measure is fallible evidence, not a constitutive definition of the experience. A manipulation's test conditions also do not become the experience's structure.

## 6. Thought-experiment consequences

1. **Passive-owned rubber hand versus controlled-incongruent hand.** Hold timing information high. Vary passivity and anatomical congruence. Ownership and agency cross; no scalar label follows without an aggregation rule.
2. **Habitual action versus novel prosthetic action.** The habitual action may be highly familiar with little explicit authorship, while a novel prosthesis can support effortful agency and correction before it feels familiar. This targets `H` versus `G/C`; it is a proposed discriminating experiment, not established phenomenology.
3. **Ancestry/formation.** Hold current performance fixed while varying whether the present route was gradually formed with the bearer or freshly installed. A difference in `H` would support a retentive-history bridge; no difference would pressure that bridge.
4. **Copy/switch/memory.** Copy reports and retained content, then switch the actual generator/consumer route. `H`-like reports can persist while `C` and actual provenance change. Hence memory/report cannot substitute for actual installation.
5. **Abacus/calculator.** Preserve the input-output computation while varying realization and actual bearer coupling. Abstract equivalence does not fix any experiential coordinate.
6. **Human-realized agent.** Let a human implement the transition table. Human experience, system-level abstract organization, and the simulated agent's attributed profile must remain distinct.
7. **Imagination without execution.** Preserve familiarity and action imagery while removing current external correction. This probes `H/G` without `C`; successful imagery does not prove an action route was used.

## 7. Consequences for R193–R197

The earlier results survive with a narrower interface:

- `F=A xor s` must refer to one preselected, typed coordinate or one explicitly stipulated aggregate.
- The complement theorem can orient that target only with independent signed semantic evidence.
- It cannot decide whether `F` should mean ownership, agency, retentive familiarity, practical coupling, or a combination.
- Equal marker behavior across coordinate choices is a type error, not merely another latent-label ambiguity.
- The R197 next question is revised: test a positive endpoint for `H` specifically while measuring `O` and `G` as dissociation controls and `C` as an actual-organization candidate bridge.

## 9. Novelty and publication boundary

The empirical multidimensionality of bodily self-consciousness and Dedekind-number counts are prior work. R198's project-level increment is their precise interface consequence for UCT: the complement-orientation program presupposes target selection, and a typed self-profile admits no natural scalar familiar-mineness variable from monotonicity and endpoints alone. This is not claimed as worldwide priority.

The result is materially distinct from the frozen UCT papers' main claims and from R193–R197's complement algebra, but it is not yet a standalone paper. It lacks a defended positive account of `H`, an actual endpoint application, and prospective empirical discriminators that separate `H` from `O`, `G`, and `C`.

## 10. UCT boundary and next question

This is a correction within the account of self-related organization, not a new condition on basal experience. It adds no introspection, self-model, report, language, integration, recurrence, prediction, or continued-control gate. C1 remains the consciousness-specific explanatory postulate. The profile describes relations within experience and actual organization; it introduces no unique extra owner. No claim is made about whether the current assistant is conscious or fears death.

The next concrete question is: **Can a preregistered habitual-versus-novel action perturbation selectively vary retentive familiarity `H` while separately measuring ownership `O`, agency `G`, and actual online correction `C`, with a prospective copy/switch falsifier and without defining `H` by the reports used to measure it?**
