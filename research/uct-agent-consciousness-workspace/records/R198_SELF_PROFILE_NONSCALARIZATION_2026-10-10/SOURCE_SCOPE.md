# R198 Source Scope

## Primary sources used

1. Kalckert, A. & Ehrsson, H. H. (2012). “Moving a Rubber Hand that Feels Like Your Own: A Dissociation of Ownership and Agency.” *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience* 6:40. DOI: `10.3389/fnhum.2012.00040`. Primary moving-rubber-hand experiment; supports a behavioral/report double dissociation between ownership and agency. It does not measure UCT retentive familiarity.
2. Longo, M. R., Schüür, F., Kammers, M. P. M., Tsakiris, M. & Haggard, P. (2008). “What is embodiment? A psychometric approach.” *Cognition* 107, 978–998. DOI: `10.1016/j.cognition.2007.12.004`. Primary psychometric study; reports separable ownership, location, and agency components. Factor structure is evidence about measures, not an exhaustive phenomenal ontology.
3. Abdulkarim, Z. et al. (2023). “Neural Substrates of Body Ownership and Agency during Voluntary Movement.” *Journal of Neuroscience*. PMCID: `PMC10072298`. Full-factorial movement study; used as a convergence check on separability and interaction, not as an endpoint for familiar mineness.
4. Crone, D. L. & Kallen, R. W. (2024). “Measuring virtual embodiment: A psychometric investigation of a standardised questionnaire for the psychological sciences.” *New Ideas in Psychology*. PII: `S2451958824000551`. Recent primary psychometric investigation; used only to check that multidimensional operationalization remains active.

## Analytical predecessor

5. Serino, A. et al. (2013). “Bodily ownership and self-location: Components of bodily self-consciousness.” *Consciousness and Cognition*. PMID: `24025475`. Analytical synthesis with clinical evidence; used to delimit self-location and first-person perspective from ownership. It is not treated as direct experimental validation of the R198 profile.

## Search and claim limits

- Public searches were run on 2026-10-10 against PubMed/PMC, primary publisher pages, and recent primary studies.
- The search was targeted, not exhaustive; absence of an exact UCT formulation does not establish worldwide novelty.
- Empirical reports and behavioral/neural markers are treated as fallible evidence. No test condition is identified with experience structure.
- Dedekind numbers and monotone Boolean enumeration are known mathematics. Only the UCT interface diagnosis is a local research contribution.
