# R198 Gap Ledger

## GAP-R198-01 — Unjustified global scalar target

- **Location:** R193–R197 shorthand `F_fam` when read as a global aggregate rather than one selected target.
- **Impact:** endpoint orientation could be mistaken for a proof that ownership, agency, familiarity, and practical coupling form one positive axis.
- **Counterexample:** ownership-without-agency and agency-without-ownership profiles admit all four aggregate label pairs under endpoint-fixed monotone scalarizations.
- **Corrected:** R198 restricts the complement theorem to a preselected typed coordinate or an explicitly stipulated aggregate.
- **Open premise:** a positive account and measurement bridge for retentive familiarity `H` remain absent.

## GAP-R198-02 — Type crossing between experience and organization

- **Location:** candidate profile coordinate `C` versus experiential coordinates `O/G/H`.
- **Impact:** current practical coupling could be silently treated as a feeling, or a test for coupling could be promoted to experience structure.
- **Counterexample:** imagination can preserve familiar action content without current external correction; copied reports can persist after generator switch.
- **Corrected:** `C` is explicitly typed as an actual-organization relation and only a candidate bridge/evidence variable.
- **Open premise:** no actual same-episode route use, carrier, consumer, or complete signature is validated.

## GAP-R198-03 — Binary/exhaustive profile idealization

- **Location:** exact checker domain `{0,1}^4`.
- **Impact:** the finite result could be overread as an ontology of human self-experience.
- **Counterexample:** graded, context-sensitive, temporally extended, or additional dimensions can exist.
- **Corrected:** the profile is labeled an audit vocabulary; the exact result is a finite non-identification witness.
- **Open premise:** coordinate inventory, granularity, and temporal dynamics require empirical and phenomenological work.

## GAP-R198-04 — Scalarization purpose missing

- **Location:** any future aggregate `f(O,G,H,C)`.
- **Impact:** a threshold or weight vector may encode pragmatic or normative choices while being presented as discovered phenomenology.
- **Counterexample:** 166 admissible monotone four-axis Boolean functions; even full permutation symmetry leaves four thresholds.
- **Corrected:** future aggregation must state domain, purpose, convention, and falsifiers.
- **Open premise:** no aggregation is currently adopted.

## GAP-R198-05 — Empirical dissociation does not fix semantics

- **Location:** use of rubber-hand and psychometric results.
- **Impact:** questionnaire factors or task manipulations could be identified with phenomenal coordinates.
- **Counterexample:** factor structure can vary with items and context; a manipulation affects multiple latent processes.
- **Corrected:** external studies are used only to motivate typing and dissociation; R197's fallible-evidence contract remains active.
- **Open premise:** independent semantic anchors and signed reliability for `H` remain required.
