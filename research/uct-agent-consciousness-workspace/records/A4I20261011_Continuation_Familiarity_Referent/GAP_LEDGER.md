## A4I-G01 — report lag cannot be removed

- **Location:** `QUESTION_CONTRACT.md`, target/response distinction.
- **Impact:** blocks the old “memory-free report” objective and any identity claim `J_cont = H_cont` by timing alone.
- **Counterexample:** a participant answers from a decaying action trace with no explicit memory of practice; the answer still depends on retention.

## A4I-G02 — the wording may teach the policy

## A4I-G03 — effort perturbation is not a clean target-preserving intervention

- **Location:** `RESEARCH_NOTE.md` §§4 and 6.
- **Impact:** an effect of load or assistance cannot by itself choose `B_relation` over `B_fit`.
- **Counterexample:** load raises effort while also changing current sensorimotor fit and the selected experience.
- **Repair:** effort is a separately measured substitution rival, not a post-treatment covariate used to purify the primary effect.
- **Open premise:** a two-intervention prediction table with explicit cross-effects is still required.
