# Referent-preserving question contract

## Target and response are different events

Fix one just-enacted bodily continuation

`x = (bearer P, interval I=[t0,t1], body/effector b, context c, continuation a, actual organization K)`.

`H_cont(x)` names the selected experiential character of that continuation at the enacted event. It is not observed by definition. `J_cont(x,tr)` is a later judgment at response time `tr>t1`. The response necessarily uses a short-lived report route; this does not make remembered practice the target. It creates a measurement obligation from `J_cont` back to the earlier event.

Keep separate:

- `E(x,tr)`: judged effort or ease;
- `M_pr(x,tr)`: explicit recollection/recognition of having practised the continuation;
- `J_part`: familiarity of individual movement components;
- `J_src`, `J_ag`, `J_own`: source, agency and ownership judgments;
- `R(x)`: actual retained-relation use;
- `F(x)`: independently specified present coordination/fit.

## Candidate wording

English:

> Think only about the bodily character of how the last movement just followed the preceding two. Did that continuation itself feel like an already established way of carrying on? Do not answer merely from how easy it was or from whether you remember practising it. If you cannot separate these, choose “cannot distinguish.”

Chinese operational gloss:

> 只考虑刚才最后一个动作接在前两个动作后面时，那一小段接续本身的身体感觉：它是否像一种身体已经熟成、已经接得上的方式？不要只按轻松不轻松回答，也不要只按是否记得练过回答；如果分不开，请选“无法区分”。

Response options are `established continuation / unfamiliar continuation / neither / cannot distinguish`. The wording is a candidate, not a validated scale.

## Six referent conditions

1. **Event anchor:** the prompt names the just-completed context and final continuation, not the whole task, skill, person or training session.
2. **Time anchor:** `t0,t1,tr` and the maximum report lag are fixed; later narrative memory is not silently treated as the original event.
3. **Body/grain anchor:** effector, movement alphabet, boundary convention and three-action grain are fixed before training.
4. **Non-substitution:** effort, practice memory, component familiarity, source, agency and ownership are separately queried, never folded into the target definition.
5. **Abstention:** inability to separate the target is retained as data rather than forced into a scale endpoint.
6. **No semantic promotion:** passing comprehension and response-consistency checks establishes at most content admissibility. It does not establish `J_cont=H_cont`, its sign, or an actual `R(x)` route.

## Four countercase vignettes for pre-session comprehension

The vignettes stipulate their intended content; they are not empirical claims that all four are physically realizable without further effects.

| Case | Intended continuation character | Effort | Remembers practice | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| loaded old route | established | high | no | effort is not the answer |
| assisted novel conjunction | unfamiliar | low | yes, for the parts | ease and remembered components are not the answer |
| ordinary old route | established | low | yes | canonical agreement does not identify the target |
| ordinary novel route | unfamiliar | high | no | canonical agreement does not identify the target |

Use these only before the confirmatory session, with exclusions fixed in advance. Repeated explanation after observing outcomes would train the response policy and invalidate a clean confirmatory interpretation.

## Three one-sided vetoes

- **Effort-substitution veto:** if the participant cannot distinguish the loaded-old and assisted-novel cases, or if the event-indexed response is fully replaced by the effort response under the predeclared analysis, the instrument does not support the selected target.
- **Practice-memory veto:** if only the disclosed/remembered practice label controls the response while the event and declared organization are fixed, treat the item as a practice-recognition report, not `H_cont` evidence.
- **Event-drift veto:** if responses change from the last continuation to the whole sequence, skill, source or autobiographical training episode under paraphrase checks, the referent has drifted.

Passing all vetoes does not certify phenomenal identity or truth. It only removes three specified substitution policies from this bounded instrument.

## Prospective order

Use separate counterbalanced replica blocks rather than asking all constructs after every trial. In the primary block, ask `J_cont` immediately. In replicas, ask effort, explicit practice memory, component familiarity, agency and ownership. Estimate question-order effects rather than assuming that later questions leave the target unchanged. The primary causal contrast remains the randomized formation-schedule total effect; do not condition it on post-formation effort or memory and call the remainder pure.
