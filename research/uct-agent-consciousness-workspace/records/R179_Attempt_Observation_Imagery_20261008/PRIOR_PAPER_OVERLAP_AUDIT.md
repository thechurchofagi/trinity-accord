2. Fixed evidence cannot discriminate a hidden difference: elementary/general prior principle. R178 supplies a bearer-fixed live/replay application; do not advertise a new nonidentification theorem.
3. Action-mode comparison at a fixed represented movement feature: CURRENT PROJECT APPLICATION. R179 supplies explicit routes, blocked/imposed-motion witnesses and mixed-use cases. These are not new empirical discoveries, and historical priority for the precise package is unverified.
4. Feeling of doing c versus feeling of actively imagining c: CURRENT EXPLANATORY REFINEMENT. It prevents target substitution in the R178 candidate. Familiar distinction, not a claimed invention; needs prior-art comparison and independent phenomenal admission.
5. Grounded source/commitment sufficient for felt trying: UNRESOLVED. R179 expressly fails to distinguish voluntary and externally imposed proximal dispatch. It cannot headline a completed explanation.
