# A4T Research Note — Transductive Lineage Routes

## Result

A sampler, synapse, human read–write step or driver normally produces a numerically distinct successor occurrence. The correct composition condition is a typed boundary stitch: either one actual occurrence is physically relayed, or an actual conversion event consumes the upstream occurrence and produces the successor.

An exact finite boundary model has three mutually exclusive modes per interface—none, relay identity, transduction—and two independent value-equality observations. It contains 36 valid worlds. With both local values equal, all nine boundary-mode pairs remain possible: four contain an end-to-end route and five do not. Among the four coherent route modes, strict identity at both interfaces accepts only one and falsely rejects the three modes containing at least one transduction.
