## Primary empirical studies used

1. Krubitzer L, Huffman KJ, Disbrow E, Recanzone G. “Organization of area 3a in macaque monkeys: contributions to the cortical phenotype.” *Journal of Comparative Neurology* 471 (2004): 97–111. DOI: [10.1002/cne.20025](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.20025). Multiunit mapping; area location varied across animals and the field contained a contralateral deep-receptor/musculature representation. This supports a named candidate field, not exclusive source or consumer identity.
2. Yamada H et al. “Representation of Afferent Signals from Forearm Muscle and Cutaneous Nerves in the Primary Somatosensory Cortex of the Macaque Monkey.” *PLOS ONE* 11 (2016): e0163948. DOI: [10.1371/journal.pone.0163948](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0163948). Local-field responses in area 3a included muscle and cutaneous nerve stimulation; the authors explicitly discuss possible mixed recruitment. This is evidence against assuming source selectivity from the area label.
3. London BM, Jordan LR, Jackson CR, Miller LE. “Electrical stimulation of the proprioceptive cortex (area 3a) used to instruct a behaving monkey.” *IEEE TNSRE* 16 (2008): 32–36. DOI: [10.1109/TNSRE.2007.907544](https://doi.org/10.1109/TNSRE.2007.907544). Area-3a microstimulation could function as a behavioural instruction. It demonstrates writable access and behavioural availability, not readout of a selected natural afferent.
4. Moore CI et al. “Segregation of somatosensory activation in the human rolandic cortex using fMRI.” *Journal of Neurophysiology* 84 (2000): 558–569. DOI: [10.1152/jn.2000.84.1.558](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.2000.84.1.558). High-resolution fMRI localized a central-sulcus fundus region compatible with area 3a during kinesthetic/motor stimulation. This is localization evidence, not a direct record of a consumer-specific read event.
5. Johnson TR, Moralle S, Luo Z, Taylor DM. “Implanting microelectrode arrays in the bottom of the central sulcus targeting somatosensory area 3a for restoration of proprioception.” *Neurosurgical Focus* 60(2):E8 (2026). DOI: [10.3171/2025.11.FOCUS25916](https://doi.org/10.3171/2025.11.FOCUS25916). Custom arrays were implanted in two rhesus macaques and recorded spiking for over one year. This materially advances physical access to a named consumer candidate, but the paper is an implantation-method study and does not by itself execute the double-selective natural-input/read contract.

## Exclusions and limits

- Reviews were used only to locate primary studies, not as evidence for the central empirical claims.
- Human fMRI, macaque multiunit/LFP, intracortical stimulation and chronic implantation are not pooled as if they were one same-instance experiment.
- No source establishes a human area-3a `motor-only` cell, complete organization, familiar mineness `H`, ownership, agency or conceptual self.
- Literature retrieval was targeted, not an exhaustive worldwide priority search. Mathematical novelty is not claimed for the row-space criterion.
