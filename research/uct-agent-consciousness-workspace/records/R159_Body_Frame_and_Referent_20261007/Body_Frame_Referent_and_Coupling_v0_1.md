# Body-frame binding, external reference, and coupled ownership/agency roles

R159, 7 October 2026. Continuation of R158. Research manuscript only; no published bytes changed.

## 1. The explanatory advance

An erroneous bodily attribution need not be a fictitious organization. An actual process can bind an internal representation token into a physically installed body-centered role while that token refers to an object outside the organism. The binding is actual; the represented anatomical-membership claim can be false. Under C1, the actual internal relation has its experiential structural counterpart. Naming that counterpart familiar bodily ownership still needs a fixed application bridge. Neither anatomical truth nor accurate causal inference becomes a gate on basal experience.

This round makes the internal-token/external-referent distinction explicit. It also repairs a possible overreading of R158: ownership and agency roles can be dissociated without their graded responses being independent. A small coupled family exhibits both features. This is a constructive explanatory schema, not a new neural mechanism, a proof of human mineness from computation, or a relocation of experience into a model hand.

## 2. Fixed actual protocol and what is changed

The actual intervention domain is the four-condition moving-hand protocol in Kalckert and Ehrsson (2012), DOI 10.3389/fnhum.2012.00040, experiment 3. A hidden biological finger and visible model finger remained mechanically linked and synchronous. Movement mode was active/passive; anatomical orientation was congruent/rotated 180 degrees. The model-finger viewing position remained fixed. The passive condition used external force, not removal of the linkage. Reported ownership and agency dissociated qualitatively, but orientation modulated agency ratings and movement mode modulated ownership ratings in the congruent condition. Thus qualitative separation does not establish quantitative independence. These are published group-level reports, not individual Boolean F observations. No raw data were obtained or reanalyzed.

For each trial fix one actual token P_i, interval I_i, a specified participant lineage, model candidate c, and protocol conventions. Distinct trials are distinct tokens, not the numerically same token at the same time. A within-participant comparison preserves lineage and declared apparatus identity, not the complete actual organization, history, attention or neural state. The apparatus's interventions are physically realized; the latent architecture below remains a hypothesis about it.

| Operation | Changed | Retained / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical model rotation | Environment-to-body-frame relation and ensuing sensory organization | Candidate model identity, specified viewing position, mechanical linkage and synchrony; not the complete neural state |
| Active to passive force | Performed movement-source role and relevant intention/input history | Linkage and sensory consequences; not elimination of every latent motor capacity |
| Coordinate renaming | Names in a mathematical description | All physical relations and their parameters transported together; no physical manipulation |
| Report instruction change | Operational question and potentially actual cognitive organization | Cannot assume the same measured target; not included as a performed intervention in this protocol |

Confounds are not erased by this table: physiological heterogeneity, trial history, demand/attention and measurement error remain. The four conditions do not independently intervene on a known single neural B or A coordinate. In particular, protocol condition bits are not measured internal binding/source states.

## 3. The missing sort: representation token versus referent

R158 distinguished bearer P from candidate c. That was insufficient for applying an internal K-isomorphism: an external model hand c need not be in the carrier of D_ontic,K(P). There is then no licensed expression h_P(c).

Fix complete actual internal organization D=D_ontic,K(P), with sorted carriers containing an internal frame token r and representation token k. Let:

- Anchor(r): a grounded body-centered reference role actually installed in P, with declared sensory/reference ports. This is not an extra conscious observer.
- Bind(r,k): k is assigned to a specified body-frame slot in the installed process. It is a relation of internal tokens, not the external object's physical incorporation.
- Use(r,k): specified installed consumers respond to that assignment under admissible local perturbations. This is a clause of this selected functional schema, not a universal condition for all bodily feeling.
- psi(k)=c: a separately grounded, possibly partial reference map to an external candidate domain E. Interpretation of its reference is not supplied merely by writing a body label on k.
- M(O(P),c): anatomical membership in the declared organism O(P), when both objects have this type. It is distinct from membership in the neural token's support. For a model hand c, M can be false even if mechanical coupling is present.

The internal selector is theta_frame(r,k)=Anchor(r) AND Bind(r,k) AND Use(r,k). A K-isomorphism h_P maps r,k and this selected relation. It does not automatically map E, psi or c. If external reference is included in an expanded signature, supply its sorts, realization and admitted environmental transport separately. A larger process containing the apparatus is a different declared bearer, not an implicit change in the human P.

One possible actual configuration is:

  theta_frame(r,k) is true; psi(k)=c; M(O(P),c) is false.

There is no logical contradiction: the first is an installed internal role, the third an external anatomical relation. This is an admissible schema conditional on actual grounding, not proof that every participant instantiates exactly this architecture. A finite abstract instance demonstrates joint logical satisfiability; it does not discharge the biological premise. We do not assert that changing external reference leaves every complete K-fact fixed, or that identical complete organization receives different experiences.

## 5. Two candidates and a jointly coupled countermodel

Compare only these two ownership-oriented hypotheses, with interpretation fixed before outputs:

**H_B:** selected ownership depends on the installed body-frame binding/use organization, potentially modulated by source-attribution organization.

**H_A:** selected ownership depends solely on source-attribution/agency organization, with no independently relevant body-frame role.

H_B is not closed by fitting condition names: B must be physically distinguished without using the ownership report itself. H_A cannot equate a questionnaire score with an exhaustive causal-source state. The known double dissociation pressures a simplistic shared ownership/agency readout; it does not logically exclude every sophisticated H_A with hidden state or a different independent measurement bridge. Such additions must be declared, not refitted post hoc.

Reject two stronger shortcuts: 'ownership has no action-source modulation' and 'agency has no body-frame modulation'. A fully specified abstract example clarifies why role separation does not imply either shortcut.

Let b,a range over {0,1}, representing declared abstract binding and performed-source coordinates, not physiological measurements. Fix 0<t_O,t_A<1 and lambda,mu>0. Define signed role readouts:

  o(b,a)=b-t_O+lambda*b*a,
  q(b,a)=a-t_A+mu*a*b.

Positive/negative signs classify this toy's readouts only. At (b,a)=(1,0), o>0 and q<0; at (0,1), o<0 and q>0. Nevertheless o(1,1)-o(1,0)=lambda>0 and q(1,1)-q(0,1)=mu>0. Both roles dissociate and both are cross-modulated. These calculations hold jointly for every declared parameter choice; a nonempty witness is t_O=t_A=1/2, lambda=mu=1/4.

The model is a counterexample to the abstract inference 'double dissociation therefore no interaction', not a new ownership algorithm, neural fit, reproduction of subjective experience or estimate of its magnitude. Its parameters are not fitted to the published means. At lambda=mu=0 the same sign pattern survives but cross-modulation disappears, demonstrating that coarse categories omit graded relation information. Values outside the declared threshold/coupling domain are not covered.

H_B's strongest exclusive-factor version would predict invariance to a under matched B and measurement conditions; H_A's simple shared-readout version would require an independently declared relation between targets. Neither invariance condition is established by condition labels alone. The appropriate next comparison is between physically grounded pathways with convergent target evidence, not four hand-picked output labels. Known multisensory, causal-source and predictive mechanisms are prior art; the UCT contribution here is their typed actual/experiential/reference placement and conditional limits.

## 6. Positive account and its remaining test

An organism's body-centered frame supplies a relational perspective through installed sensor positions, body configuration and consequence routing. A representation token referring to an external model can be bound into that perspective and used as a bodily location/attribution coordinate. A second route distinguishes whether the movement source is attributed to an intended action or external forcing. They can modulate each other without being identical. Propositional belief about anatomical membership can be represented by a different relation and need not agree with the bound body-role readout. No contradiction follows from keeping different targets separate.

Under C1 the actual installed configuration, including any error, is interpreted intrinsically; the referent does not have to become part of the organism for that configuration to be actual. This explains what a mistaken body representation amounts to organizationally. To interpret its intrinsic presentation specifically as familiar ownership, fix B_min on the selected human domain, ground B/Use independently, and identify report/behavioral evidence with declared fallibility. There is no rule that 'a role called body' automatically yields ownership.

Failure criteria: (i) a grounded B/Use interpretation cannot distinguish body-slot from an arbitrary visually tracked tool under admissible perturbation; (ii) a supposedly internal selector uses an external c as an untyped K-token; (iii) candidate target changes after seeing its output; (iv) an independently warranted target contrast remains inside a supposedly sufficient descriptor fiber; (v) the shared readout cannot account for target distinctions without additional declared organization. Each blocks that particular application, not basal U1. No existing observation is called an infallible F witness.

## 7. Four thought-experiment families

**Ancestor/formation.** Body-frame reference, source attribution, coupling and conceptual membership belief can acquire distinct roles during formation. The toy does not identify the first animal, developmental age or threshold of bodily feeling. The question is whether a physically witnessed relation changes, not whether a new owner appears.

**Abacus/calculator.** An observer interpreting a bead as a hand supplies no installed Bind/Use. Add actual routing and consumers and the functional model changes; this still does not supply a selected human F target. A verbal body label is not a physical relational witness.

**Human-implemented agent.** A human carrying out a body-binding computation has an executing organism and a represented computational process. Do not assign the executor's body anchor or feeling to every nested algorithm. Admit overlapping processes and specify which P,r,k and external psi are under discussion.

**Copy/reconnection/memory.** Copying the representation k does not copy its grounded sensory reference, consumers or external referent map. Reconnection can change those roles while a verbal output persists. Pure relabeling transports all internal relations; physical remapping changes them. Neither operation proves numerical self identity or death fear.
