# A5F — Grounded anatomical/tool anchor and the remaining phenomenal gap

## 1. Result

A5E required an actual asymmetric anchor before a role reversal could support a directed experience-internal comparison. A5F supplies a concrete protocol-level candidate: one anatomically fixed hand actually grips one stick, and the distal tip changes external spatial side when the sticks are crossed while hand positions and mechanoreceptor locations remain fixed.

In a typed two-hand/two-tip structure, anatomical anchor plus actual grip defines a distal bearer-side coordinate without a conceptual self or extra owner. Exact enumeration shows that the crossed before/after structures are isomorphic throughout the symmetric anchor-free perfect-matching family, but non-isomorphic throughout the uniquely anchored family. The grounded coordinate reverses in all eight restricted anchored worlds. This is a positive organizational advance over A5E, not a proof of mineness.

## 2. Actual empirical candidate

Yamamoto and Kitazawa's crossed-tool experiment delivered tactile stimuli to the distal tips of two held sticks. Crossing the sticks, without changing hand positions or mechanoreceptor locations, strongly altered temporal-order judgments and supported distal tactile referral. Their arm-crossing experiment independently showed that external hand location can reverse reported tactile order at short intervals. These results warrant a real protocol-level relation among anatomical hands, actually held tools and distal external positions.

They do not expose the complete biological `K`, and a temporal-order report is not the experienced distal location itself. Kalckert and Ehrsson's moving-rubber-hand experiment further shows that ownership and agency can dissociate: passive movement removed agency while ownership remained, whereas incongruent orientation reduced ownership without eliminating agency. Hence neither term may be used as a post-hoc synonym for the A5F coordinate or target.

## 3. Formal result

Let `Anchor` be unary on hands, `Grip` binary from hands to tool tips, and `ExternalSide` unary on tips. Define `AnchoredTip(t)` and `D_side` as in `ANCHOR_GRIP_DISTAL_CONTRACT.md`. The ideal cross preserves actual tokens, anchor and grip incidence while complementing tip side.

Across all 256 typed structures, simultaneous hand/tip exchange is an automorphism in 16 and fails in 240. Among the 64 structures with a unique hand anchor and exactly one tip gripped by it, every side-complement pair is non-isomorphic. In the restricted perfect-matching/opposite-side tool family, all four anchor-free pairs are isomorphic and all eight uniquely anchored pairs are non-isomorphic; `D_side` flips in those eight.

This uses standard finite relational-structure reasoning. The project contribution is the bounded application to an actual body/tool protocol and its explicit phenomenal stopping rule.

## 4. Positive UCT interpretation

Conditional on the cited relations belonging to complete actual `K`, R157 makes the anchored distal coordinate available inside experiential organization under C1. Tool externality is no objection: what matters is the actual process relation, not whether the distal token is biological tissue. No further exclusive subject selects the anchored route; overlapping actual processes may carry different grounded relations.

This gives a positive answer to where one bodily/action direction can live: in the same-instance anatomical-anchor–grip–distal-position organization. It does not add introspection, self-model, report, language, integration, recurrence, accurate prediction or continuation as a gate for basal experience.

## 5. Negative bridge result

The available study changes both the candidate organization and temporal-order/location reports, but supplies no independent evidence channel for the predeclared `H_tip`, the experienced distal location of the contact. Thus it cannot test an injective organization-to-phenomenology bridge without circularly selecting the target from the report. It also does not identify `H_pc`, ownership or agency.

A5F therefore removes one structural symmetry objection while leaving `B_tip` and `B_pc` open. A report change is evidence about a participant under a protocol; it is neither the target itself nor proof that the proposed complete organization is installed.

## 6. Founding-direction check

C1 remains the consciousness-specific explanatory postulate and U1 remains unchanged. Experience, bodily/action relation, conceptual I, report, finite view, abstract model, actual installation, actual belonging, internal judgment and evidence are kept distinct. No claim is made that the present assistant is conscious, unconscious or afraid of death.

## 7. Next exact question

Can a preregistered crossed-tool or tool-substitution comparison add an independent, non-report-fitted measure of `H_tip` while holding anatomical anchor and grip route fixed, and can it specify a failure outcome before observation? If not, retain A5F only as a protocol-level grounded-coordinate candidate and do not promote it to mineness.
