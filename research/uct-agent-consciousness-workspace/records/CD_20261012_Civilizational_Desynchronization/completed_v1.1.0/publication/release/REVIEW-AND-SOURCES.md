## Precise claims
For individual/decision/alternative i, practical access A_i and effective reconsideration window T_i differ from mere AI optimism, AI usage, competence as a voter, and substantive political power. For persistent access and prefix-window options J_i = 1(A_i<=D_i). This is a definition with limited scope, not evidence of a global civilization rupture.

The capacity-backed review calculation is conditional on an explicitly worsening relative restoration cost, enforceable review and sound alternative implementation capacity. Joint acceleration can move access earlier but close options even earlier. Its counterexample is NOT evidence that education is harmful when deadlines are fixed.

Classical fixed-marginal/Hall matching mathematics gives the sharp anonymous lower/upper opportunity bounds. New scoped inverse application: for ordered access times a_(i), deadlines d_(i), every pairing and a target k, the minimum common genuinely effective deadline extension s* = max(0,min_{i=k..n}[a_(i)-d_(i-k+1)]). The manuscript supplies proof, independent enumeration and counterexamples. No claim to invent the abstract matching theorem. Known identity links, unknown dates, capacities, representation and competing alternatives require their appropriate separate models.
