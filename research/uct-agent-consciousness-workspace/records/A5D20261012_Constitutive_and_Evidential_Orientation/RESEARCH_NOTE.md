# A5D — Constitutive orientation is not evidential orientation

## 1. Question and result

A5C showed that exact class-conditional transport across calibration and target domains still leaves one global complement for the latent `H_pc` label. The obvious but invalid conclusion would be that UCT cannot positively orient any action-related self-structure until a gold-standard phenomenal channel is found.

A5D separates three different orientation problems. A predeclared, physically grounded directed relation has a constitutive orientation fixed by its typed ports and direction. Under C1, an isomorphism in that signature preserves the relation as an experience-internal structural coordinate. This is not the same as fixing the named phenomenal interpretation `H_pc`, and neither fixes the sign of fallible evidence `E_pc`.

The net result is a conditional positive explanation plus a sharp remaining gap. UCT can say what is internally bearer-centred in an experience when the actual relation and complete signature are independently grounded: the transported relation itself, not an extra owner or report. It still cannot identify that coordinate with the familiar verbal/phenomenal category *felt practical continuity* without `B_pc`, nor infer `B_pc` from an uncalibrated endpoint. A5C's complement applies to semantic and evidential naming; it does not erase typed constitutive direction, and typed constitutive direction does not solve the complement.

## 2. Typed relation and constitutive orientation

Fix one actual candidate binding `b=(P,I,K,e,tau,c,t)`. `R_b(c,t)` says that a bearer-anchored route at port `c` is actually consumed by the target/consumer at port `t` in selecting, sustaining or correcting `tau`. Presence/absence, argument order, time and actual event incidence are part of the relation. The predicate is selected independently of phenomenal reports.

Under the simultaneous premises of actual grounding, complete relevant `K`, A:C1 on the same binding, preservation of typed direction and no admitted role-reversal symmetry, the C1 isomorphism preserves `R_b(c,t)` as `R_b^Phi(h(c),h(t))`. Define `X_R=1` when that experience-internal relation is present. External recoding of 0 and 1 does not alter it. Reversing the ports or removing the actual consumption relation changes the declared organization rather than relabelling it.

This claim is deliberately not that `X_R=1` feels positive, voluntary, familiar or mine. It is that the experience's internal structure contains the same directed bearer--target relation if the UCT premises hold. R157's semantic residual and UCT I's definition-vacuity warning remain decisive.

## 3. Exact two-bridge alias

Write `H_pc=X_R xor s_B`, where `s_B` is the unresolved orientation of the selected named phenomenal interpretation. Let an endpoint have high response probability when `H_pc xor s_E=1`, where `s_E` records whether the evidence channel is positively or negatively related to `H_pc`.

With endpoint probabilities `4/5` and `1/5`, a positive observed `E_pc`--`R` contrast is compatible with two worlds:

| semantic orientation `s_B` | evidence orientation `s_E` | interpretation |
|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | 0 | positive bridge and positive evidence |
| 1 | 1 | complemented bridge and inverted evidence |

A negative contrast is likewise compatible with `(0,1)` and `(1,0)`. The observable sign depends only on `s_B xor s_E`. If constitutive orientation is also left free there are eight layer conventions, four per observable sign; grounding `R` fixes that layer and reduces the family to four, two per sign. The exact checker verifies these counts with rational arithmetic.

This is not new latent-variable mathematics. The project-level advance is the placement of the symmetry: A5C's missing sign need not be attributed to the actual directed relation, but neither can an actual relation--endpoint correlation independently validate both the phenomenal interpretation and the endpoint direction.

## 4. Positive UCT interpretation

The candidate positive content is relational, target-indexed and nonexclusive:

- the relation originates in the actual bearer/route side of the declared binding;
- it is consumed in an actual target-directed episode;
- its direction and current occurrence are intrinsic parts of the organization, not an analyst's owner label;
- multiple overlapping routes and processes may instantiate their own relations without one additional master subject.

Under C1, this relation is inside the experience as structural content. It can distinguish an episode organized through the bearer's current route from an output-identical imposed, bypassed or copied episode. The explanation does not require introspection, self-model, language, later report, success or unique ownership.

The stronger sentence, “this structural relation is the selected familiar feeling that the action remains mine to carry through,” is `B_pc` and remains open. A5D therefore supplies an oriented **constitutive coordinate**, not a phenomenological lookup table.

## 5. Refuters

1. **Role reversal.** `R(c,t)` and `R(t,c)` differ in a typed directed signature. If a claimed constitutive coordinate ignores a real reversal, its signature was incomplete or its witness was extrinsic.
2. **Response-key inversion.** Learned button identity may reverse while the comparison target stays fixed. Raw code is not semantic direction.
3. **Copy/switch.** Copy endpoint and memory state, then switch the current route. The exact witness holds `E=1` while `R` changes from 1 to 0, defeating constitution-by-endpoint.
4. **Route replacement.** Preserve output success while an imposed or world-centred route replaces bearer-centred consumption. Success does not fix `R`.

Failures can reject a declared relation or bridge. Passing them cannot prove that `X_R` is `H_pc`; that asymmetry inherits A3Z.
