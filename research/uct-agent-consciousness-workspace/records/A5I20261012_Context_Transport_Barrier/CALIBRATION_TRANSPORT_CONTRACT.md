# Calibration-Transport Contract

## Intended claim

For a declared participant, tool, time window, target `H`, and illusion family, a preregistered channel `Z` is offered as evidence that the experienced distal location tracks the illusion position `X`.

## Simultaneous premises (`all_of`)

| ID | Premise | What must be fixed prospectively | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | Source-discordant calibration | A calibration contrast in which target anchor and nuisance cue are not perfectly co-aligned; declared target-sensitive criterion | `Z` follows the nuisance cue when `P` and cue disagree, or target sensitivity is only inferred from report accuracy |
| S | Selector transport | Same declared source variable across calibration and illusion, not merely same sensor, classifier weights, or label names | a context/cue-reading model fits both contexts without reading `H_I` |
| O | Orientation transport | Same pole/direction fixed before illusion data are inspected | label complementation or post-hoc relabelling restores the predicted association |

The target-identification conclusion is licensed only from `D AND S AND O` within the declared binary domain. Exact counts are 1 model with all three; 5 after dropping D; 5 after dropping S; and 2 after dropping O.

## Controls that do not substitute for the conjunction

- Same participant and same held stick constrain hardware and formation history, not the represented source.
- Frozen classifier weights constrain a fitted function, not semantic source identity.
- Cross-decoding accuracy constrains distributional generalisation, not target sensitivity.
- Reversed response mapping constrains fixed-key policies, not a direct sequence-to-channel route.
- Somatotopic activity constrains anatomical organisation, not by itself a particular experiential target.

## Evidential status

This contract is a measurement/bridge contract. It is not an axiom of UCT, not a definition of experience or mineness, and not a necessary condition for consciousness. D is especially demanding: the target-sensitivity part cannot be obtained merely by declaring veridical trials to be a ground truth for experience; it needs an independent bridge argument or intervention.
