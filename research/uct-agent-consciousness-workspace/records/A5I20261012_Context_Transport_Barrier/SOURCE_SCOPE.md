# Source Scope

## Fixed UCT sources read

- UCT I v1.2, especially §§3–5 and the bridge-method passages: complete token organisation versus finite views; C1/C1-W/U1; no hidden quale; probe invariance; finite bridge failure.
- R157: coordinate transport, semantic residual, and the still-open mineness bridge.
- R159: internal body-frame token versus external referent; actual relation transport; error attribution.
- TA25 §§3–4 and D4–D5: functional self-model, selected experiential target, and report/measurement separation.
- A5H: fixed coarse observation, valid within-K contrast, response-mapping result, and the proposed same-participant held-stick continuation.

## Primary empirical sources used

1. Miyazaki M, Hirashima M, Nozaki D. “The ‘cutaneous rabbit’ hopping out of the body.” *J Neurosci* 30(5), 2010. DOI: `10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3887-09.2010`. Provides the held-stick rabbit paradigm and pointer reports.
2. Blankenburg F, Ruff CC, Deichmann R, Rees G, Driver J. “The cutaneous rabbit illusion affects human primary sensory cortex somatotopically.” *PLoS Biology* 4(3), 2006. DOI: `10.1371/journal.pbio.0040069`. Provides a skin-rabbit/S1 association, not a held-tool localizer.
3. Miller LE et al. “Sensing with tools extends somatosensory processing beyond the body.” *Nature* 561, 2018. DOI: `10.1038/s41586-018-0460-0`. Shows that tool vibration motifs encode impact location and can predict localisation behaviour.
4. Miller LE et al. “Somatosensory Cortex Efficiently Processes Touch Located Beyond the Body.” *Current Biology* 29(24), 2019. DOI: `10.1016/j.cub.2019.10.043`. Provides EEG decoding and source evidence for held-tool touch location; its abstract also attributes location information to rod vibration patterns.
5. Wada M et al. 2020 stick/cutaneous-rabbit work retained from A5H. DOI: `10.1038/s41598-020-58536-z`.

## Exclusions and limits

- No secondary review is used to establish the core empirical premises.
- No paper is treated as proving phenomenal target identity, actual ownership, basal experience, or UCT C1.
- Cross-study conjunctions are not used as if they were one participant-level `all_of` intervention.
- The exact model is deliberately binary and finite; it establishes a countermodel and a minimal premise audit only in that declared domain.
