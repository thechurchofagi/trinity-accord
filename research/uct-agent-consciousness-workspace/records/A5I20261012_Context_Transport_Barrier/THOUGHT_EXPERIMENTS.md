# A5I thought experiments

## Formation and ancestors

- **Fixed:** admission of the actual earlier process, the declared basal-experience policy, and the earlier tool contact.
- **Changed:** a later learned decoder becomes accurate and stable.
- **Role:** blocks retroactively treating later calibration as what constituted the earlier experience; calibration is later evidence about a target, not an ancestor-changing operation.
- **Limit:** does not show that learning can never alter later experience when it is actually installed in the later process.

## Abacus and calculator

- **Fixed:** veridical calibration table `Z=P`, illusion transfer table `Z=X`, and every external response.
- **Changed:** the abacus carries an `H` register across contexts; the calculator switches from `H` during calibration to direct `X` during illusion trials.
- **Role:** realizes the context-switch countermodel and separates functional accuracy from target sensitivity.
- **Limit:** output equality neither proves nor denies experience in either implementation.

## Humans implementing an agent

- **Fixed:** electrodes, frozen weights, labels and trial schedule.
- **Changed:** in one group the same internal target variable supplies both contexts; in another, the illusion-stage worker receives the sequence label directly.
- **Role:** shows why physical sameness of apparatus and algorithm does not by itself establish one actual source selector across event stages.
- **Limit:** no individual or group is declared an exclusive owner; actual overlap and nesting remain allowed.

## Copy, switch and memory

- **Fixed:** the complete saved classifier, its positive calibration history, and the final sentence “touch was at the middle of the stick.”
- **Changed:** after copying, one installation preserves the source route while another switches the input multiplexer or flips the pole only in illusion context.
- **Role:** witnesses S and O as separate transport obligations; copied weights and memories do not settle actual current routing.
- **Limit:** does not make memory irrelevant when memory is an installed part of the current organization.

## Held-stick rabbit

- **Fixed:** participant, stick, grip, preregistered channel and response apparatus.
- **Changed:** actual distal contact `P`, sequence cue `X`, and their factorial alignment.
- **Role:** makes D failure-capable: a direct cue channel should be exposed when `P` and `X` disagree in the calibration family.
- **Limit:** even this control does not, without an independent bridge, turn `P` into a definition of `H`.
