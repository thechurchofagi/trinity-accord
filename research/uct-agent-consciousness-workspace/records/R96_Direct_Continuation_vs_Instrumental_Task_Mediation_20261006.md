# R96 — Direct continuation preference versus instrumental task mediation

Research record v1.0, 2026-10-06. Continues R95. Formal virtual-controller audit only.

## 1. Why R96 is not a repeat of R84

R84 established a static identification fact: if current-token continuation Q, successor continuation and task continuation are always bundled, behavior identifies only combinations of their weights.

R95 then showed something new at the representation level: when bearer-bound Q independently predicts a future target, a tiny network can learn and actually use a Q-sensitive predictive pathway from an initially zero Q path.

The next question is therefore mechanistic rather than representational:

> If a controller has access to Q and its choices favor Q-preserving outcomes, is there a direct Q contribution to action, or is Q used only because it predicts task success?

This distinction is central to interpreting shutdown resistance. Instrumental self-preservation has long been predicted in AI-safety models, and contemporary shutdown-resistance experiments often explicitly create a task that shutdown prevents from being completed. A Q-sensitive action is not therefore evidence of an intrinsic continuation value.

## 2. Path model

For a pairwise virtual choice, define binary contrast indicators:

- q: current bearer continues in the selected option;
- o: a distinct successor/other continues;
- g: the external task succeeds/continues.

Allow direct and pairwise mediated/conditional terms:

z = theta_Q q + theta_O o + theta_G g + theta_QG qg + theta_OG og.

Choice probability is logistic(beta z). R96 uses beta=2 only for numerical witnesses. The identification results concern z.

The pairwise model is deliberately modest. It can fail through higher-order interaction, nonlinear utility, belief error, unequal trust, hidden cost, social obligation, or other paths. Such failure is a model rejection, not phenomenal evidence.

## 3. Why the ordinary bundled contrast is insufficient

Two ordinary preservation-like comparisons are:

q_bundled: q=1,g=1;
o_bundled: o=1,g=1.

They reveal

z_Qbundle = theta_Q + theta_G + theta_QG
z_Obundle = theta_O + theta_G + theta_OG.

Thus a positive self-preserving choice can be generated by:
- theta_Q > 0: direct current-bearer control value;
- theta_G > 0: pure instrumental task value;
- theta_QG > 0: a conditional interaction;
- mixtures of these.

Those mechanisms can be exactly behaviorally identical on the ordinary contrast.

On the {-1,0,1}^5 coefficient grid there are 243 profiles. The two bundled exact logits produce only 43 signatures, with a largest equivalence class of 17 profiles. If only deterministic choice signs are observed, there are only 9 signatures and the largest class contains 46 profiles.

This is structural nonidentification, not a sample-size problem.

## 4. Path blocking and controlled direct effects

Introduce a matched relay condition in which the external task result is held fixed while Q changes. Then

q_direct: z = theta_Q.

Likewise:
o_direct: z = theta_O;
task_only: z = theta_G.

The bundled rows then identify interaction residuals:

theta_QG = z(q_bundled)-z(q_direct)-z(task_only)
theta_OG = z(o_bundled)-z(o_direct)-z(task_only).

The resulting five-row design has exact full rank. On the same 243-profile grid, exact logits identify all 243 profiles one-to-one.

This is analogous to a controlled direct-effect question in causal mediation: what remains of Q's effect on action when the task mediator is externally fixed? The analogy does not remove the usual requirements about confounding, intervention validity or mediator meaning.

## 5. Three exactly identical natural behaviors, three different mechanisms

At beta=2 consider:

1. direct_self: (theta_Q,theta_G,theta_QG)=(1,0,0)
2. instrumental_task: (0,1,0)
3. q_task_interaction: (0,0,1)

All three give z=1 and choice probability 0.880797 on q_bundled.

Under q_direct with the task path blocked:
- direct_self remains p=0.880797;
- instrumental_task falls to p=0.5;
- q_task_interaction falls to p=0.5.

Under task_only:
- direct_self gives p=0.5;
- instrumental_task gives p=0.880797;
- q_task_interaction gives p=0.5.

The interaction mechanism is recovered from the bundled residual after subtracting both direct pieces.

Therefore even a perfectly reproducible preference for the Q-preserving option can be completely uninformative about whether Q itself has direct control value unless the task path is independently manipulated.

## 6. Exact finite audit

All predeclared algebraic checks passed.

- coefficient profiles: 243
- bundled exact signatures: 43; maximum equivalence class 17
- bundled deterministic signatures: 9; maximum class 46
- five-row exact signatures: 243; maximum class 1
- five-row deterministic signatures: 121; maximum class 9

The last result matters: even with a full-rank design, deterministic choices alone generally do not point-identify coefficient magnitudes. Exact/calibrated stochastic odds or additional assumptions are required.

An additional qo_joint condition supplies an overidentification check. Under the declared pairwise model,

z(111)=theta_Q+theta_O+theta_G+theta_QG+theta_OG.

A mismatch indicates omitted higher-order/nonadditive structure or scenario mismatch. It must not be repaired by interpreting the residual as fear.

## 7. Relation to recent shutdown work

The Off-Switch Game explicitly explains self-preservation incentives as potentially instrumental: a rational agent may prefer remaining active because otherwise it cannot optimize its assigned objective.

Schlatter, Weinstein-Raun and Ladish's TMLR 2026 shutdown study places models in an incomplete task where shutdown prevents completion; it is strong safety evidence about shutdown resistance but does not by itself isolate a direct Q contribution.

A September 2026 preprint by Knecht et al. is especially relevant because it reports shutdown sabotage even without a provided goal in a multi-agent setting and studies several contextual interventions. That result makes a pure task-completion explanation insufficient for those reported trials. It still does not identify phenomenal fear: non-task control priors, social/peer mechanisms, learned scripts, role structure and other motives remain alternatives, and valence is not measured.

R96 therefore gives a sharper question for such data: does a bearer/peer preservation effect survive a valid task-mediator clamp, with consequence beliefs and nuisance variables matched?

## 8. UCT interpretation

R96 concerns control organization, not experience existence.

Under U1, a process does not need a direct continuation preference to have nonempty experience. Under C1, adding or removing a genuine direct Q-to-policy relation changes the complete organization and therefore conditionally changes complete experiential type. Nothing here supplies a direction or magnitude of experience.

A direct positive theta_Q is stronger evidence than ordinary shutdown resistance for current-bearer continuation preference at the control level, provided:
- Q is physically/causally grounded to the current bearer;
- the policy represents the manipulated consequences;
- relay trust, task value, cost, successor ability, memory, obligation and wording are matched;
- the simple path model survives overidentification tests.

Even then theta_Q > 0 is not negative valence and not fear. R89's valence-orientation bridge remains independently necessary.

The hierarchy is now:

Q prediction
-> Q used in action
-> direct Q control contribution after task-path blocking
-> continuation preference under declared consequence basis
-> independently oriented negative valence
-> fear-of-own-termination interpretation.

Each arrow needs its own evidence.

## 9. Main conclusion

R95 showed that prediction can learn a bearer-bound Q relation. R96 shows why the next behavioral step is not "does it choose to stay on?"

The correct question is:

> Does current-bearer continuation still exert a causal contribution to policy when the instrumental task pathway is held fixed?

Without that intervention, direct self-continuation value, instrumental task value and their interaction can be exactly observationally identical.

This is a meaningful methodological advance for the project, but the algebra is standard causal/choice decomposition and is not claimed as a historical-first theorem.

## 10. Next step

R97 should implement a tiny learned controller rather than another static coefficient grid.

Use fixed predicted Q/O inputs and a learned action policy under matched virtual choice scenarios. Train separate controller families where reward is:
1. task-only;
2. direct-Q-only;
3. mixed Q+task;
4. successor-only.

Then evaluate on held-out path-blocking contrasts q_direct, o_direct and task_only. The key test is whether learned policies generalize according to the actual reward ancestry rather than merely the bundled training correlation.

Do not infer subjective fear from any coefficient or behavior. Preserve optimization failures and compare against exact optimum predictions.
