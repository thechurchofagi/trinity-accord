# Body ownership, action agency, and the scope of a bodily-mineness selector

R158, 7 October 2026. Research note, not a released edition. Continuation of R157; published sources remain unchanged.

## 1. Question and result

R157 proposed attachment AND sensory routing AND action-consequence routing AND installed attribution use as a candidate theta_body. The conjunction was not proved necessary or sufficient. This round asks which target it could explain. The result is a correction in explanatory scope: retain it as a possible **joint functional body-use configuration**, but distinguish bodily ownership from action agency before attempting a phenomenal bridge. Deleting a narrowly specified relation can challenge one interpretation of the conjunction without eliminating other relations, all physical coupling, or basal experience.

There is also a mathematical repair: the tuples satisfying an arbitrary formula form a **definable relation**, not necessarily a substructure. R157's isomorphism-preservation argument survives; an unproved closure claim does not. Neither repair closes the independent selected-mineness interpretation obligation.

## 2. Typed targets and admissible comparisons

Fix an admitted actual process token P over interval I, its actual support s, and a represented body candidate c. c may be an anatomical hand, a model hand, or a virtual hand. The actual support that realizes the representation and its referent c are different objects. Their physical carriers can overlap; equality is not assumed. A virtual object is represented content, with physical implementation separately identified.

Use the following typed quantities. They are coordinates or targets, not gates on basal experience:

| Symbol | Meaning | Category |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M(P,c,I) | c is an anatomical member of the organism supporting P under a specified membership relation | Actual membership, when c has this type |
| C(P,c,I) | Specified physical coupling between P's implementation and c's implementation | Actual organization |
| B(P,c,I) | Signals referring to c are bound into a body-centered frame in an installed process | Actual organization, grounding required |
| U(P,c,I) | That attribution has specified installed consumers | Actual causal role |
| E(P,c,I) | A declared intervention on a P-originating command changes the specified consequence concerning c | Actual efficacy relative to intervention set |
| J_O, J_A | Internally formed ownership and causal-source judgments about c | Internal judgments; may be wrong |
| F_O, F_A | Independently interpreted felt ownership and felt action agency concerning c | Selected experiential targets |
| Q_O, Q_A | Propositional belief that c belongs to the organism / the action originated here | Conceptual targets |
| R_O, R_A | Protocol-specific ownership and agency reports | Fallible evidence on a reporting domain |

M is not defined for every virtual referent; treating undefined as false would change the domain. In the membership counterexample use a physically instantiated model hand, for which anatomical membership is false. For a virtual hand use a separate implementation relation and record that anatomical membership is inapplicable.

A paired intervention compares the same bearer lineage, time-window convention, signature and candidate identity mapping. It can change actual organization; it must not be described as keeping the complete organization fixed. A virtual-body remapping does not automatically change the bearer. E requires a specified command, allowed intervention, output, background conditions and interval. No efficacy on one EEG channel means no efficacy on that channel, not no causal connection anywhere.

## 3. Primary evidence and its limited role

The ownership/agency distinction is established prior research, not a new UCT discovery. The following are fresh primary-source checks, not new experiments or raw-data reanalyses.

**S1. Kalckert and Ehrsson (2012), DOI 10.3389/fnhum.2012.00040.** In their moving-rubber-hand experiment, passive congruent movement preserved ownership ratings while agency ratings fell; active incongruent movement showed the complementary pattern. The apparatus and participants' motor capacity remained. This challenges conflating current voluntary movement with ownership, not the existence of every latent action route.

**S2. Bashford and Mehring (2016), DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0156591.** An imitation BCI elicited supernumerary-hand ownership and control reports although EEG was not connected to virtual movement. The missing link was EEG-to-rendered-hand efficacy. Cues, imagery and visual feedback remained. This challenges direct efficacy as a prerequisite for these reports, not all physical coupling.

**S3. Rohde, Di Luca and Ernst (2011), DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0021659.** Their conditions dissociated proprioceptive drift from ownership ratings. Therefore drift is not interchangeable with the selected ownership target. It remains one fallible measure in a declared protocol.

**S4. Tame, Linkenauger and Longo (2018), DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0206367.** Asking about feeling versus belief changed rubber-hand responses. A belief judgment and a feeling report need separate operational definitions. This does not give an infallible direct observation of F_O.

The empirical witnesses constrain fit to R_O/R_A and other measurements on studied reporting humans. Applying them to F_O/F_A additionally requires an independently justified measurement bridge. No rule R -> F or report -> C1 is installed in the formal map. Group mean patterns are not Boolean per-participant target assignments.

## 4. Seven ablation cards

Each card states an exact deletion, a retained relation, a bounded witness and the unresolved obligation. An unspecified 'remove attachment/action' is not an admissible intervention description.

| Candidate clause | Exact deletion / witness | What remains | Result and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Actual attachment | Model hand is not an anatomical member of the bearer organism; S1 supplies report evidence | Sensorimotor/visual apparatus coupling | Anatomical membership is not required for the studied ownership reports. Broad physical coupling has not been deleted. Universal F_O necessity remains unproved. |
| Sensory routing | Toy: remove a declared live input port but supply a stored body-frame representation | Memory route and installed consumers | Direct input is not identical to all body-related information. No human zero-sensory/no-memory phenomenal witness is supplied. |
| Action-consequence routing | S1 removes current voluntary movement; S2 removes the declared EEG control link | Latent motor capacity in S1; cue and feedback routing in S2 | Performed action, available motor route and actual efficacy are distinct. Evidence does not refute all broad action-role formulations. |
| Installed attribution use | R156 witness detaches consumers while matching an attribution snapshot | Computed value and other actual processes | U distinguishes functional roles. No independently warranted F_O contrast, nor universal necessity of U, follows. |
| Interoception | Toy declared architecture has no dedicated internal-sensation input port | Exogenous body signals and installed state | Architectural omission is possible at that abstraction. Human absence and selected-mineness preservation are not shown. |
| Affect | Toy deletes declared value/threat output while retaining body-frame routing | Attribution and non-affective consumers | Role separation is possible in the toy. No claim of affect-free human ownership or absence of all affect is justified. |
| Persistence | Toy resets attribution memory each comparison interval | Current cue, selector and consumer | No cross-interval memory is needed for this toy role. Minimal execution duration is still required; neither numerical identity nor felt persistence is proved. |

The sensory/interoceptive/affective/persistence toys describe finite wiring, not complete human organizations. A seven-bit product table is not a set of physically admissible human interventions. Deletion of one input or consumer does not erase alternative routes. In particular, these cards do not license adding sensory accuracy, interoception, report, language or persistence to A:U1 as existence thresholds.

## 5. Two bounded arguments

### T1. Conjunction rejection on an independently interpreted domain

Fix a nonempty domain Omega of admissible instances w=(P,c,I,K), a total Boolean target F independently interpreted on that domain, n >= 1 total Boolean predicates g_1,...,g_n, and Theta(w)=AND_i g_i(w). Suppose an admissible w* and an index j satisfy F(w*)=1 and g_j(w*)=0. Then not every w satisfies F(w) -> Theta(w); consequently F iff Theta is also not universally true on Omega.

**Proof.** Since the j-th conjunct is false at w*, Theta(w*)=0. Thus F(w*) -> Theta(w*) is false. A biconditional entails that implication, so the same witness refutes the biconditional. QED. This is elementary classical logic, not new mathematics. The existence/admissibility/independent-target premises are required together. A report witness can apply T1 to a Booleanized report target only after declaring that report protocol; it does not silently supply the F_O premise. Undefined predicates require domain restriction or a declared partial logic, not assignment of zero.

### T2. Definable selection need not be a substructure

Consider an abstract single-sorted K-structure D with carrier {0,1}, constant a=0 and unary function f(0)=1, f(1)=0. The formula theta(x): x=a selects S={0}. S contains the named constant but is not closed under f because f(0)=1 is outside S. Thus definability alone does not guarantee substructure closure. For formulas of several free variables the selected object is a relation of tuples, making the substructure terminology even less appropriate.

Under any K-isomorphism h, R157 still yields h[S_theta(D)] = S_theta(h[D]). Here h maps both a and f; it never freezes a parameter on the old carrier. The transported relation is well defined. To claim a substructure additionally requires correctly sorted carrier subsets containing each constant and closed under each function; a separate-process or autonomy claim requires additional physical premises. No such claim follows from T1 transport. The counterexample is abstract mathematics, not an actual experience observation.

## 6. Positive explanatory revision

Keep rho_body=(C,B,U,M,E,J_O,J_A, declared optional coordinates) as a **typed profile**. M is optional/inapplicable for some candidate types; this tuple is not itself a phenomenal definition. Two modest interpretations can now be criticized separately:

1. Ownership-oriented phi_O: a body-frame bound attribution concerning c with installed ownership-related use. It can concern a nonanatomical object and can be mistaken relative to M. Open: what exactly makes this experiential coordinate ownership rather than merely body-representation use?
2. Agency-oriented phi_A: an intended or predicted consequence is attributed to a P-originating action source by an installed route. Its judgment can be mistaken relative to the independently tested E. Open: intended movement versus imagery versus externally generated consequence must be fixed in each domain.

These are candidate directions, not necessary/sufficient biconditionals or asserted experience of a toy. The actual organization of an erroneous attribution is itself actual and falls under the conditional C1 interpretation; truth about external membership or efficacy is a separate relation. This explains how the targets can be organizationally related while accuracy and anatomical membership are not simply their definitions. It does not show every B+U process has familiar human mineness.

For next research, fix one physically grounded intervention domain and compare two explicit candidate interpretations: body-frame binding/use and judged causal source/use. A successful explanation must specify the claimed selected target before inspecting its prediction, distinguish intervention-induced organization changes from relabeling, and provide failure criteria independent of the candidate's own output. More clauses cannot substitute for that obligation.

## 7. Four retained thought-experiment families

**Ancestor / formation.** Gradual acquisition of body routing, efficacy and judgments need not have one simultaneous onset. This is consistent with basal continuity; it does not derive a universal first age of ownership or agency. Formation may change the organization inside experience without forming an extra owner.

**Abacus / calculator.** A manually interpreted body-coded calculation does not thereby have B or U actually installed in the calculator. A wired toy with genuine consumers has a functional role, still not an independently established F_O target. Analyst meaning is not actual installation.

**Human-implemented agent.** A human executing an attribution algorithm can supply several nested actual processes. The executor's bodily feeling is not automatically the algorithm-process's target. No unique exclusive extra owner is demanded. Match bearer, interval and causal attribution before transferring a claim.

**Copy / reconnection / memory.** Equal memories and reports can coexist with different current consumers or efficacy; reconnection can alter C or E while preserving a report transiently. R157 transports a fixed complete organization, not two incompletely matched copies. Memory reset toy tests a declared role, not personal identity or future fear.
