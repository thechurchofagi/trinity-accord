## 3. Scope audit using the existing B rule

The following notation is an editorial unpacking of B:TARGET_DOMAIN/B:GATE, not a measured phenomenal variable or a newly proved neuroscience theorem. Fix one bearer P, its actual process interval, an input context and a content c. Keep the comparison domain fixed.

- E(P): that bearer has some experience.
- V_c(P): that bearer has the specified visual phenomenal content c in the interval.
- R_c(P): the declared content-relevant recurrent mechanism is engaged in the specified regime.

Do not define R_c as mere graph cyclicity or V_c as a successful report. A circuit segment, a whole person, a stimulus representation and an encompassing process are different candidates for P. The inferential comparison requires the same bearer and interval on both sides.

By the intended meaning of the first two predicates, V_c implies E. For a candidate source-scoped necessity claim V_c implies R_c, its contrapositive is not-R_c implies not-V_c. It does not imply not-E. The assignment E=true, V_c=false, R_c=false satisfies these implications. This is a logical compatibility illustration, not a demonstration that a particular physical process has those phenomenal properties.

Consequently U1 alone does not refute a necessity claim about one visual content. Nor does it entail that a simple process sees faces, colors or anything human-like. Even a full absence of visual experience is distinct from no experience of any kind; extending from one content to all visual contents still does not identify the two predicates without another premise.

By contrast, a same-domain necessity claim E implies R_c is incompatible with U1 at a supplied actual valid witness with not-R_c. That is precisely the existing B:GATE route. It is not established merely by giving a theory the label RPT, citing a masked stimulus, or selecting a microscopic token outside the rival's domain. RPT's strongest actual ontological claim must remain visible; it cannot be rewritten as only a performance hypothesis to manufacture agreement.

## 4. Three independent comparisons needed for the formation scenario

| Comparison question | What must be specified | Invalid shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Architecture formation | Constituent/connection changes across the comparison family | A long history supplies every relevant continuity premise |
| Episode engagement | Current state, stimulus, timing, effective operations in an already existing architecture | An anatomical return path guarantees content-relevant processing now |
| Experiential interpretation | Some experience, particular phenomenal organization/content, or access/report | A target-content absence proves that all actual subprocesses lack experience |

These are our argument-analysis categories. They do not claim that nature divides into three separate mechanisms or that a named source supplies a complete evolutionary trajectory.

On the first question, B:EDGE_CYCLE already gives the exact return-path condition for a new graph cycle. B:MODEL19/B:SPECTRAL provide the complementary case where all weights remain positive, the cycle already exists throughout, and a structural diagnostic crosses one. The state-dependent Jacobian is required for the separate stability question. These published cases illustrate the need to say which property is forming; neither is a biologically grounded RPT sufficiency proof.

On the second question, a comparison of different evolutionary architectures must not be substituted for a claim about different phases of processing within one architecture. It can still challenge a universal onset principle, but needs its own admissible family and a justified cross-scale interpretation. No such bridge is supplied by duration alone.

On the third question, R141's limitation remains: gradual organizational change does not independently establish A:CONT_BINARY_E. Conversely, mathematical specification of an onset boundary does not establish that the boundary correctly captures phenomenal existence.

## 5. Bidirectional comparison verdict

| Claim under evaluation | Verdict relative to the pinned UCT foundation |
|---|---|
| Report/access is a separate target from experience | No conflict follows from that separation; not uniquely UCT |
| A specified perceptual organization requires a specified mechanism | Compatible at the level of logic with U1, unless further premises create conflict; empirical/phenomenal bridge remains open |
| An actual valid process has no experience solely because it lacks a proposed universal gate | Conditional conflict under B:GATE, with exact target domain and witness |
| Any graph loop supplies the full target theory | Not established: B:RPT_BRIDGE and B:TFR prohibit this replacement |
| RPT as a whole has been derived from, or refuted by, UCT | Not established by this audit |

UCT's positive commitment is that actual organization has an intrinsic experiential presentation under C1, while its actual differences constrain experiential types. To interpret a change as the appearance of a *particular* percept rather than an unspecified type change requires identifying the relevant content organization and stating the physical/phenomenal interpretation. This requirement is not a new gate on experience existence, a demand for introspection, or a demand for one exclusive subject.

The rival must justify its proposed experiential boundary in addition to identifying a mechanism. UCT must justify C1 and explain its intended organization-dependent distinctions in addition to assigning E=1. A rival's missing explanation is not independent evidence establishing C1; the presence of empirical motivation for a rival is not a logical derivation of its identity claim either.

## 7. Consequence for the paper plan

Keep the opening ancestral scenario, then explicitly separate universal experience existence from the formation of particular experiential organizations and capacities. A useful provisional thesis is: **the formation of a specific experiential organization is not, merely by being a formation event, evidence of the first existence of all experience**. This is an interpretive distinction grounded in A/B/C, not an independent proof of universal experience or a novel mathematical theorem.

The selected RPT comparison is ready to be used as a bounded opposing-case analysis. Do not automatically expand into a nine-theory survey or another control-theory branch. The next planning decision is to assemble the accepted three-case argument (ancestral formation; an actual part/whole comparison; organization/capability/report) and assess whether its positive contribution exceeds the existing papers. If it does not, retain a synthesis/companion disposition. No manuscript is begun or publication authorized by this audit.
