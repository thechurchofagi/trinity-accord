# R202 source scope

## Fixed UCT sources

- `UCT_I_v1.2.md` §§2–4, 10–11: actual organization, C1, intelligence/experience distinctions and scope discipline.
- `TA25.md` §§3–4: actual occurrence/use and evidential limits.
- R157 report-domain limit; R173 use/evidence firewall; R197 fallible endpoint; R200 semantic calibration; R201 strict transport margin.

These sources fix the internal vocabulary and conditional obligations. They do not empirically validate `H`, `J`, `M`, `U`, `V` or `T`.

## External primary sources

- Rogan, W. J. & Gladen, B. (1978), *Estimating prevalence from the results of a screening test*, DOI `10.1093/oxfordjournals.aje.a112510`.
- Hui, S. L. & Walter, S. D. (1980), *Estimating the error rates of diagnostic tests*, DOI `10.2307/2530508`.
- Tsuchiya, N. et al. (2015), *No-Report Paradigms: Extracting the True Neural Correlates of Consciousness*, DOI `10.1016/j.tics.2015.10.002`.

The first two establish that correction/identification of fallible-classification models depends on explicit error and independence assumptions; the third motivates separating report processes from candidate correlates. None supplies UCT's phenomenal semantics, C1, actual-use facts, or `V→T` transport. R202 claims no priority for mixture nonidentifiability or matrix inversion.
