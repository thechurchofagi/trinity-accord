## 2. Enacted-frame binding

For one admitted actual bearer `P`, interval `I`, action event `a`, learned route `m`, bearer-state anchor `s`, target token `k`, and coordinate transform `gamma`, define:

```text
EnactedFrameBind(P,I,a,m,s,k;gamma) :=
  BearerStateAnchor(P,I,s)
  AND OnlineTargetRemap(P,I,s,k;gamma)
  AND BearerPerturbationDependence(P,I,a,s,k;gamma)
  AND SameRouteUsesFrame(P,I,a,m,gamma)
  AND OrderedWithinEvent(s,gamma,m,a,I).
```

- `BearerStateAnchor` is a physically grounded occurrence of the bearer's current pose/boundary/action state, not an anatomical word or analyst label.
- `OnlineTargetRemap` means that the event's target is expressed in coordinates updated from that anchor.
- `BearerPerturbationDependence` requires an independently admissible perturbation at fixed external target/tool state: changing the bearer-state anchor changes the route's mapping or command in the predeclared way; blocking that anchor disrupts the mapping and a same-interface anchor rescue restores it, within the declared model.
- `SameRouteUsesFrame` requires the A4M route-to-fit mediation itself to read/use `gamma`; a parallel body monitor does not qualify.
- The ordering clause binds the occurrences to the same action event.

Every primitive needs an independent physical interpretation in complete `K`. The contract is not established by output equality, ownership/agency ratings, an external tool label, or a body-part name.
