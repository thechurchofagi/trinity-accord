## Prior-art boundary

Body schema, tool incorporation, peripersonal-space remapping, sensorimotor coordinate transformation and perturbation/rescue logic have extensive prior literatures. A4N performs no novelty claim for these mechanisms and no fresh systematic literature review. Its bounded contribution is an internal consistency correction: A4M's tool-loop refuter must respect R159's already-fixed external-referent typing.

No external paper is used to establish a physical installation, phenomenal value or universal necessity. No raw data are analyzed.
