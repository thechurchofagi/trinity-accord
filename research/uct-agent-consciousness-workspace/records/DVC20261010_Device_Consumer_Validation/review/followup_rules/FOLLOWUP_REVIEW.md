Two details deserve special emphasis. First, a common-source or retained-state result does not show that the same *current* source token was read by the designated consumer. DVC's warm-buffer and header witnesses retain that distinction. A supplied zero-valued token is also not an absent token. Second, a read-only shadow has no causal effect on a participant endpoint when the declared structural model provides no relevant outgoing path and keeps all other functions and disturbances fixed. Such a shadow can validate its own event processing; it does not instantiate a human motor/proprioceptive branch intervention.

The extended review did not reinstate any suspended rule, assign an actual premise merely because its node exists, or change a reviewer-controlled status. The full binding of each rule is preserved. Existential countermodel comparisons remain comparisons between admitted alternatives; they are not conjunctions requiring one actual installation to have incompatible mechanisms.

## 3. Independent review of the three empirical nodes and three contexts

`EVIDENCE_CANDIDATE_ADDITION.json` was read in full. All six records are acceptable in their stated disabled or nondeductive roles. `EVIDENCE_ADDITION_REVIEW.json` records the complete per-ID review.

**PUBLIC_DATA_DOMAIN.** The fixed public archive and declared exclusions identify 18 included people, 9,704 in-range command records and 108 participant-condition cells. Two declared specifications yield 216 fits. These are different denominators and are not new participants or newly collected trials. The node does not enable any actual-consumer premise.

**EXPLORATORY_ENDPOINT_REANALYSIS.** The passive Start-minus-End sigma estimate of 0.68299755 agrees with the frozen reanalysis already independently reproduced in the preceding review. This follow-up did not rerun that numerical analysis. The node keeps a fitted response-location parameter separate from fitted precision and from the archive's source-labelled JND. The PSE is the fitted 0.5 response-probability crossing. The physical response-key orientation and command-to-amplitude calibration remain unverified. Complementing the response labels leaves the corresponding 0.5 crossing unchanged when the same fitted response function is complemented; it does not establish which physical comparison the stored label names. Nominal paired-t intervals are explicitly not an exact distribution-free finite-sample guarantee.

The optional editorial refinement `Pr(InputValue=1 | command=x)=0.5` would make the conditioning variable explicit. The current fitted-location wording is already scientifically acceptable and does not create a blocking issue.

**ACTUAL_ADMISSION_GAP.** The statement is bounded to the inspected articles, archive fields and interface documentation. It does not claim that a suitable experiment is impossible. Parsing 8,985 time-bearing rows conditionally does not measure physical tactile onset. Device candidate signals M_D and P_E remain distinct from identified human efference-copy and proprioceptive inputs. The clause requiring all installation, source, time, read and consequence evidence for the same episode is essential and retained.

The three context links are explicitly nondeductive. They connect the exploratory estimates to MPC's separately typed endpoint tensor, connect the evidence gap to DVC's actual-binding contract, and retain the nonidentification of familiar mineness, conceptual self and RetBind from a fitted comparison response. The referenced target IDs exist. No new deductive rule was introduced.

The assembled map has 24 nodes, six rules and 15 contexts. Against the independently reviewed frozen 39-item device component, all 21 original nodes and all 12 original contexts are unchanged. Each of the six original rules differs only in its relative `proof_location` prefix. These records are aliases of the original component's scientific items, not duplicate new contributions. The exact assembly comparison is saved with the empirical review.
