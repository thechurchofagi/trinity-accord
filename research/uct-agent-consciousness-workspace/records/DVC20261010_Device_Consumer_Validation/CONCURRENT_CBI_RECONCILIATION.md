`P := A AND T AND L AND R`.

For A=1 and P=0, at least one of T, L or R must be absent. CBI has already supplied this **consumer-relative minimal-cut necessity**, its three single-cut locations, the seven nonempty cut sets in its declared enumeration, and the matched-world-output/exoskeleton thought experiment.
