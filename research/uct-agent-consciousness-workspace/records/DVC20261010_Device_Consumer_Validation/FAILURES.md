# Failed inferences and unresolved reproductions

This record preserves failures as reusable restrictions, not as evidence of absent experience. The full finite witnesses remain in [device_model/EXACT_RESULTS.json](device_model/EXACT_RESULTS.json); the empirical discrepancies remain in [apparatus_evidence/DATA_REANALYSIS_REPORT.md](apparatus_evidence/DATA_REANALYSIS_REPORT.md).

1. **Configured gates did not certify consumer cells.** A closed capture gate retained old latch content. The reference consumer could still read two old values and return the nominal successful result. Current-branch absence and consumption of a historical token can coexist; this also reconciles concurrent CBI's narrowly indexed `P=0` with DVC.
2. **Equal values did not certify the intended occurrences.** Of 720 reference operation orders, 354 had committed matching values while at least one intended new token was not consumed; 360 consumed before commit and were rejected. These are exhaustive counts in that declared six-operation family, not hardware failure probabilities.
3. **Headers and final outputs did not certify source identity, reading or prediction.** Reheaded old tokens, swapped physical lanes, a gate-label reflex, a direct-port bypass and post-consequence echoes supply distinct counterexamples. Each counterexample defeats only its declared stronger inference.
4. **A common sampling instant was not necessary for a retained pair.** Separate valid windows can support captures from one grounded episode with retention and a validated commit. The pair is not thereby a simultaneous whole-world snapshot. Arrival, capture and consumer deadlines remain separate.
5. **A disconnected shadow did not supply a human intervention.** Under the explicit no-path contract, changing the shadow cannot change the human endpoint. Device-command copies and encoder samples cannot be renamed human efference copies and proprioceptive intake.
6. **The complete original psychometric preprocessing was not recovered.** The inspected fitting materials omit `anamax.m` and full experiment-control/preprocessing code. Several archived statistics reproduce, but another JND paragraph's degrees of freedom and values remain unreconstructed. No participants were selected to force agreement.
7. **Independent sigma was not equated to source-labelled JND.** Their observed ratio is not constant. PSE, sigma, a 75%-minus-50% threshold and archived JND remain distinct outputs. The paired-t intervals do not establish distribution-free finite-sample coverage.
8. **CSV time reconstruction did not certify physical events.** All 8,985 time-bearing rows admit the declared paired-token/NaN reconstruction. Units, event assignment and actual tactile onset remain unresolved. The analysis of the unambiguous first fields does not resolve those later fields.
9. **The response key provenance claim was too strong and was corrected.** The paper describes physical keys 1/2; the archive stores 0/1. The conversion is not directly exposed. The effective [correction](apparatus_evidence/RESPONSE_CODING_CORRECTION.json) preserves executed bytes while narrowing interpretation. The fitted 0.5 crossing remains a defined statistic; physical stronger/weaker naming remains open.
10. **Concurrent bibliography was not wholly exact.** CBI sources 1, 3 and 6 had misassigned bibliographic fields. The [non-destructive sidecar](CONCURRENT_CBI_RECONCILIATION.md) supplies primary-source corrections, retains the original records, and distinguishes the position endpoint in Gandevia 2006 from the movement endpoint in Walsh 2010.

No failure above negates C1/U1. None closes QC10, IA-QC11, QC12, QC13, the R191 normalization issue or historical audit depth. The standalone DVC-paper decision remains HOLD because actual installation and named-target evidence have not been obtained.
