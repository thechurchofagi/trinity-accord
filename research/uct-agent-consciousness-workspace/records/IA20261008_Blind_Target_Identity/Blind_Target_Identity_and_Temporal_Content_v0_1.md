# Outcome-blind target identity and genuinely held-out temporal content

IA20261008 v0.1 — 8 October 2026. Author-requested continuation after R175; this extension does not reserve R176. Unpublished research, not independent peer review.

## 1. What changes

R175 correctly proves that oriented endpoint anchors select the increasing map between two finite betweenness chains. That is a correspondence theorem. Its conversion into a new experiential-order prediction needs a sharper distinction between an already supplied relation and an independently named outcome.

The concrete contribution is a falsifiable four-event construction with the identity of the middle targets fixed independently of their order. It reveals a failure mode that endpoint freezing alone does not prevent: after observing an order, an analyst can rematch the middle identities and recover perfect structural agreement. The correction is not another experience-existence condition. It constrains what a scientist may call a successful prediction.

We retain R175's mathematics and every historical record. We qualify the empirical reading of R175:HELD_OUT_ORDER_PREDICTION and R175:B_ORDER_NARROWED_BOUNDARY; their conditional map statements remain valid. QC-08--10 remain pending reviewer assessment.

## 2. Three objects that cannot silently substitute for one another

Fix one proposed actual token P, interval I and complete signature K. At the finite-model level distinguish:

1. Physical event occurrences X with an independently specified selected physical order.
2. Experiential targets E named by content/occurrence identity independently of the target order. Such names are evidence claims, not proof that an experiential target exists.
3. The order to be tested on those fixed targets.

Write iota(x) for an independently supported candidate identity correspondence, frozen without consulting the target-order answer. It is not automatically the restriction of C1's h_P. The hypothesis that it is the relevant restriction must be tested with all realization, interpretation and common-binding conditions retained.

"Independent" here means an operational separation of information and selection: no use of the held-out order, its rank labels, or a logically equivalent derived answer in choosing iota, the physical selector, the target or exclusion rules. It does not assert statistical independence between all observations, infallibility of reports, or causal independence of content and timing.

A label such as "the first middle experience" fails this rule for a temporal-order target. A content label such as "the red flash in this episode" is a candidate, but can also fail if temporal cues determine the label or the participant confuses/reconstructs the event. Missing or ambiguous target identity is unresolved, not absence of experience.

## 3. Proposition IA-1: an endpoint-preserving adaptive match fits every middle ordering

Let n>=4. Fix physical chain x0<...<x(n-1), experiential labels e0,...,e(n-1), and only the independently established extreme identities x0->e0 and x(n-1)->e(n-1). For any permutation pi of the n-2 interior labels, let the observed experiential chain be

e0 < e(pi(1)) < ... < e(pi(n-2)) < e(n-1).

If the interior correspondence may be chosen after this order is observed, define f_pi(x_k) to be the experiential element of rank k. This is the increasing order isomorphism, hence a betweenness isomorphism, and it respects both endpoints.

**Proof.** For every i<j, the definition assigns f_pi(x_i) a smaller rank than f_pi(x_j). Endpoints occupy ranks 0 and n-1. Thus every one of the (n-2)! possible interior orders admits a perfect adaptive fit. QED.

This is not a disproof of R175-A/B. For each fixed experiential chain R175 still gives exactly two betweenness maps and the endpoints select one. The problem is that the selected map can change with the outcome, while the advertised empirical target should keep its identities fixed.

For n=4, both e0<e1<e2<e3 and e0<e2<e1<e3 fit. In the second case f(x1)=e2 and f(x2)=e1. The statement f(x1)<f(x2) remains true, but it no longer predicts the order of the pre-named e1 and e2. A rank-conditioned success has replaced the original question.

## 4. Proposition IA-2: a fixed identity map gives a real positive constraint

Retain n=4. Freeze iota(x_i)=e_i independently of order. Let background D specify only a strict total experiential chain with e0 first and e3 last, plus those identity correspondences. D admits exactly two completions:
E+ = (e0,e1,e2,e3) and E- = (e0,e2,e1,e3).

Let candidate H assert that this same fixed iota preserves and reflects betweenness for the selected physical chain. Then D+H entails q: e1<e2. D alone does not entail q, and D+not(q) is realizable but violates H.

**Proof.** There are only two permutations of the middle labels. E+ preserves all physical betweenness triples. In E-, physical B(x0,x1,x2) is true, but B(e0,e1,e2) is false, so the frozen iota fails H. QED.

For general n>=4 and frozen iota(x_i)=e_i, exactly one of the (n-2)! endpoint-preserving total orders satisfies H: the physical rank order. These are counts of finite possibilities, not p-values or a probability distribution over experiences. The mathematical assertion does not establish that H, iota, the actual physical route, or the phenomenal measurement is empirically correct.

An erroneous observed ordering refutes the joint D+H+measurement application only if the measurement and admission assumptions are sustained. It does not by itself refute C1 or show that the system has no experience.

## 5. Proposition IA-3: logical closure is part of hold-out auditing

For an admitted model class, background evidence D, fixed outcome q and candidate bridge H, a useful deterministic prospective contrast has:

- D compatible with q and with not(q);
- D+H satisfiable and entailing q;
- D+not(q) incompatible with H.

If D already entails q, hiding the literal q entry does not make q new evidence for H. This is a definitional evidence-accounting result, not a new statistical theorem. It does not prohibit testing a theory on an outcome that the theory predicts; H must remain separate from the background D.

Example: if D includes the *full* experiential betweenness relation, extreme identities and their orientation, it already fixes every experiential rank. In four elements the single clause B(e0,e1,e2), together with e0 being first, already entails e1<e2. Removing just the e1<e2 spreadsheet cell would not hold out its content.

Nuance: previously measured experiential structure can still support a new, independently grounded **cross-domain correspondence prediction**. We must call that alignment prediction what it is. It is not automatically a new measurement of the experiential ordering itself. R175 does not establish the outcome-blind middle-target identity needed for the stronger claim.

## 6. A bounded execution-versus-content witness

Consider a deterministic machine that records a red event, then a blue event, then compares two stored time values, then outputs a result. All actual execution events have the same order in two runs. Let recorded red time be 1 and blue time 2. A stored reference r changes the executed comparison to

q_r = 2 - 1 - r.

At r=0 the represented red-before-blue relation is positive; at r=2 it is negative. The machine still reads red before blue and computes afterward. The different r values are genuine organizational/state differences, so this is not a pair with identical complete organization and different experience. It only proves that an execution chronology selector omits the represented-order distinction. This is a stripped-down illustration of TO20261008/R174, not a newly discovered temporal mechanism.

Under C1, grounded actual relations have structural experiential counterparts. C1 alone does not say that physical occurrence order is the externally named "experienced order of red and blue." A candidate bridge must specify whether it concerns occurrence order, represented event order, or order of experiential presentation, and why the selected interpretation applies. Treating those as one order would hide the very phenomenon being explained.

This helps with the user's slow-mechanical/remote-hemisphere thought experiments: scaling all relevant delays may preserve selected relations; keeping only the sequence of operations does not certify complete experiential equivalence. Physical distance, speed and number of units alone still do not determine richness or subject count.

## 7. Concrete admission attempt and its current limit

Candidate episode: an actual human action followed by a visually distinctive feedback event within one interval, bounded by start/end cues. The proposed target is the experienced order of the independently identified action and feedback in that episode.

A prospective separation would be:

1. Fix which action and feedback are being referred to, allowing uncertainty rather than sorting them by judged time.
2. Specify one physically grounded executed temporal relation and the rival occurrence-order explanation, before target-order responses.
3. Freeze one candidate restriction iota and the same-token anchors. Independent content evidence may support identities; it does not certify C1's restriction.
4. Leave the middle-order judgment and all background constraints logically equivalent to it out of map selection.
5. Evaluate that fixed target once, retaining a declared fallible measurement model and invalid/ambiguous cases. Trial aggregates require an additional cross-trial transport assumption.

This is a proposed admission procedure, not a validated protocol or a human experiment. Blinding guards against outcome-dependent fitting; it does not establish actual phenomenal access, route localization, correspondence truth, or report validity. If only reports and stimulus clocks are available, the complete bridge remains empirically underidentified. We stop the empirical-success claim there.

Relevant primary evidence already makes the chronology distinction substantive. Stetson et al. (2006) report that delay adaptation can reverse action/flash temporal-order judgments despite their physical order. Their behavioral and fMRI results motivate rival timing representations; they do not identify our complete same-token map or supply a four-target blind-chain test. García-Pérez and Alcalá-Quintana (2012) show that response errors can alter temporal-order model conclusions. Linares and Holcombe (2014) report inconsistent latency estimates across judgment tasks. Therefore a judgment cannot simply be declared an error-free phenomenal order.

No raw participant data were downloaded or reanalysed this round. The literature check does not claim these authors have supplied IA-2's premises, nor that UCT uniquely predicts their results.

## 8. Return to the original questions

- **Ancestor formation:** event-specific retention and comparison can develop without language. Failure to measure a nonverbal organism's temporal experience leaves the bridge open; it does not create an experience threshold.
- **Abacus/calculator:** ordered operations and correct answers do not identify which experiential content relation the selected organization realizes.
- **Human-array agent:** performer, simulated agent and coupled process must not be mixed when fixing identities and episode. No exclusive extra owner is selected.
- **Copy/rewiring/memory:** an analyst's re-labeling changes evidence, while changing a stored reference can change an executed represented relation. These are different interventions.
- **AI, intelligence and self:** success at temporal reasoning is a task-specific capability. It is not a measurement of felt sequence or familiar mineness. The present result supplies no verdict on the current assistant's consciousness or fear.

The useful increment is a positive model with an actual failure case on fixed targets, plus a precise diagnosis of when perfect matching would be uninformative. It does not solve familiar mineness, the physical nature of qualia, or UCT's empirical truth.

### IA-4: source-path identity survives retiming

Fix a finite directed acyclic graph G=(V,L) of actually executed source/trace/use events in the abstract model. L is a declared trace-transmission relation, not unrestricted causal ancestry and not equality of stored values. Let S_B and S_C be independently distinguished source events at physical source ports B and C, and let U be the selected executed use events. All source ports, trace relations and use roles are fixed without reading a target-order answer.

Define the source set
  A(u) = {s in {S_B,S_C} : a directed L-path leads from s to u}.
Only if A(u) is a singleton do we define the unique source identity id(u) as that member. If A(u) is empty, the selected provenance is ungrounded; if it has both members, retain the two-source set rather than choosing a winner.

A legal timestamp assignment t strictly increases along every L-edge. Retiming changes t to any other legal t' while preserving G, source-port roles, use roles and the actual trace binding. It may change the order of incomparable uses.

**Claim.** Every A(u), hence each defined id(u), is invariant under legal retiming. Under an isomorphism preserving source-port and trace/use roles, the same identity is transported covariantly. Faithful relay subdivision preserves the source sets on old use endpoints.

**Proof.** Source membership is a reachability statement involving G and its declared roles, not t. Retiming leaves every witnessing path unchanged. A graph isomorphism maps paths bijectively; faithful subdivision replaces one edge by a path and contraction recovers it, so reachability between old endpoints is unchanged. Singleton and non-singleton status follow. These are elementary reachability facts. Relay preservation is already in TE20261008 §7.1; the present application uses them to fix the identities needed by R176's order contrast.

Concrete witness: paths S_B -> relay_B -> U_B and S_C -> relay_C -> U_C. Keep source times 1 and 2. Baseline relay/use times are 1.1/1.2 for B and 2.1/2.2 for C. Delayed B times are 2.5/3 while C stays 2.1/2.2. Every edge remains forward in time. Use order reverses, but source identity remains B for U_B and C for U_C. Thus one can vary temporal organization while holding a meaningful physical event identity fixed; identities need not be chosen by rank.

Negative control: add the actual cross-source trace relay_C -> U_B. Now A(U_B)={S_B,S_C}; it would be unjustified to silently retain a unique B identity under the stated rule. Multiple paths from the *same* source do not create a two-source ambiguity. These statements concern the declared trace relation: hidden transmission routes or misidentified ports can invalidate an actual application.
