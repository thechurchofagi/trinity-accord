# Actual authorship, experienced authorship, and the live–replay contrast

R178 v0.1 — 8 October 2026. Unpublished conceptual research; no new human experiment.

## 1. The advance and the question we actually answer

A feedback event can be **caused by my present action**, **represented as caused by my action**, and **experienced as my doing**. These are three claims, with different truth conditions. R158 already distinguishes efficacy, internal judgment and felt agency; R177 distinguishes source identity from executed feature. This note does not claim those distinctions as new discoveries. Its advance is to make the proposed live/replay test answerable: freeze its bearer and causal scope, expose an incompatibility in its evidential demands, and choose a bounded positive explanatory candidate rather than silently identifying causal success with feeling.

Our selected phenomenal target is **felt authorship of the currently presented movement**, F_A(P_H,k,I). The independent verbal description is a movement presenting as one's doing, contrasted with merely witnessing it happen. It is not defined by whether the live wire is connected. Body ownership F_O, familiar mineness F_F, an explicit causal belief Q_A and an utterance R_A remain distinct. This description fixes an intended target but does not establish a calibrated measurement or universal phenomenal taxonomy. No inference to every form of bodily mineness is made.

The result is a proposed explanatory location: what could constitute apparent authorship is a process's actually enacted, self-indexed relation among an action source, a prospective consequence and a presently used consequence representation. Its correctness about the external causal chain is a separate question. This is a candidate interpretation of organization inside experience, not a new owner or a proven sufficiency theorem. It can accommodate erroneous agency in principle; success in control cannot be its definition.

## 2. Freeze the bearer before drawing a causal arrow

For the formal comparison select **P_C**, the entire finite command–feedback assembly, over I=[0,3]. This is the primary scope for the live/replay construction. The same physical component roster occurs in both alternatives. It includes command register A, stored replay register V, selector/gate M, feedback relay Y, injection port X, receiver D, prospective consequence register E, internal attribution/consumer U, and optional report transducer R. Initialization is specified before I; the stored replay's earlier producer is outside I. An external auditor's log and assessment are outside P_C. The command is issued at t=1, Y/X at t=2, and use at t=3. The clock, operating rules, selected routes and mode are included in the finite model signature K_C^toy.

Membership is warranted **within this construction** by the declared physical component and executed-route specification, not by an ownership questionnaire or a wish to find a subject. A human instantiation would additionally need an independently justified actual process boundary; no such complete human realization is supplied. K_C^toy is complete for the stipulated toy model only. It is not a claim to know the complete ontic signature of an apparatus or organism.

Three scopes must not be interchanged:

| Scope | Inside | Outside / conclusion forbidden |
|---|---|---|
| P_C, the selected assembly | A,V,M,Y,X,D,E,U,R and their specified routes during I | Earlier archive production and auditor; not automatically the human's experiential bearer |
| P_down, a separately declared diagnostic restriction | X,D,U and their declared post-injection state and evolution | Source/gate; equality here does not establish complete organization equality |
| P_H, an intended human bearer in a future application | Its independently grounded action, sensory and attribution processes | External apparatus membership cannot be assumed; its boundary and complete K_H remain open |

P_down is used only to explain what the selected observation window omits. We do not replace P_C by it after seeing the answer. Nor do we transport a relation in the coupled assembly and relabel it a human feeling. C1 transport for P_C and transport for P_H are separately bound claims. Even if processes overlap, a restriction/target interpretation is required; no automatic inheritance of all selected qualities is assumed.

The human-facing term k denotes an internal movement representation; an independently grounded reference map may connect it to a hand or displayed referent c. Neither external c nor a written word 'self' is silently an internal K_H element. This retains R159's correction.

## 3. A jointly satisfiable replay pair, and its exact limit

In L, Y=A through the enabled current command route. In Yoked, that route is disabled and Y=V through the replay route. Use A=V=1 for the matched episode. X=Y, D(X)=X, E=A, and U stores the equality relation between E and D(X) with its fixed source-role binding. All downstream operations and their initial states are common. R, if present, reads only the common downstream state through the same fixed function. The mode/gate and active route differ; their physical differences are included, not hidden behind identical observed values. Unused V can remain present in L. This is a pair of alternative executions, not contradictory assignments to one execution.

Thus the sampled values, decoded feature, prospective/received match, consumer trajectory, use time and generated report coincide. The assembly has different actual enabled source-to-feedback organization. A perturbation of A with V held fixed would affect X in L and not in Yoked. That perturbation is a different execution, not an intervention performed while claiming the original matched waveform remains fixed. The structural equations and installed gate are stipulated model mechanisms; equal samples alone do not identify actual causation in a real apparatus. Redundant/overdetermined routes fall outside this particular sole-route contrast.

Let O be any deterministic observation computed solely from the matched downstream trajectory with a common readout. Then O(L)=O(Yoked), by substitution. For stochastic readout, equal conditional kernels give the same observation distribution, not necessarily equal independent random draws. This elementary consequence, R178-C1, is not a new general mathematical discovery and needs no additional graph theorem node.

A request to preserve **all** downstream trajectories/readouts and also obtain discriminating evidence **only from them** is therefore unsatisfiable in this declared model class. A source auditor can identify the gate, but that is evidence of provenance, not independently of F_A. An additional sensor, differently informed report, future feedback test or persistent trace can make a discriminating observation possible only by relaxing the relevant matching assumption. Matching output waveforms alone does not match all neural, bodily, memory or reporting organization.

This does not prove that lived experience is identical. P_down is a selected view, not complete K_H. It also does not prove that there is an unreportable difference. The strict pair underdetermines the named target through the declared evidence channels. A fully justified complete-organization comparison would be a stronger premise than anything obtained here.

## 4. Competing explanations, stated before outcomes

Keep the same target F_A, episode convention and movement identity in both candidates. Let W summarize the independently fixed background conditions under which the candidate is intended to apply. W may include an actual action attempt, body/reference calibration and a given range of intact processing; it must not include F_A itself or an outcome-selected diagnosis. Its actual human admission remains open.

**B_source, a strong provenance-sensitive candidate.** In domain W, changing only the admitted current-command causal route from live to replay, while preserving the matched receiver-side organization, changes the selected felt-authorship target: the live episode has greater F_A than replay. 'Greater' requires a predeclared experiential comparison, not an unannounced numerical scale. This is deliberately a strong candidate of intrinsic sensitivity to genuine causal authorship, not a claim that every successful action has agency or that every passive episode lacks experience.

**B_enacted, the rival.** Within a restricted domain W_H, the selected F_A comparison depends on the actually enacted self-indexed action/consequence relation Psi_A inside the grounded P_H, rather than on the correctness of its external-source attribution by itself. If Psi_A and all other declared constitutive conditions for this selected target are matched, hidden live/replay provenance does not change F_A. If the attribution route changes, F_A may change even with the same external feedback. This is source-blind only to the hidden external origin, not indifferent to source roles actually differentiated inside the bearer.

Neither candidate is implied by C1. B_source needs a source-to-named-quality interpretation on the correct bearer. B_enacted needs a well-motivated constitutive sufficiency/restriction claim; equality of a convenient Psi_A proxy is not enough. Neither premise may be supplied by copying the observed feeling into the definition of Psi_A.

| Comparison or evidence | B_source | B_enacted | What is established now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strict hidden replay with fully matched admitted receiver relations | Predicts difference | Predicts no difference on its restricted domain | Competing predictions; the stipulated downstream evidence cannot choose |
| Replay with independently warranted positive felt agency | Refutes a broader claim that actual efficacy is necessary | Compatible with erroneous apparent authorship | Published reports offer pressure, not a complete matched human token |
| Provenance unchanged, attribution/reference organization changed | No prediction from provenance alone | Allows changed F_A | A possible next contrast; not evidence already collected |
| Matched Psi_A proxy, independently grounded different F_A | Not decided | Defeats proxy sufficiency if other constitutive premises hold | No actual witness supplied |

A falsifier for the strong B_source contrast is an independently justified equal or reversed F_A comparison for an actually admitted live/replay pair meeting W and all matching conditions. A positive agency report under false control challenges the broader necessity claim only through an explicit fallible report-to-target interpretation; it does not logically refute the strict pair prediction. Equal reports alone are not equal feelings, and a nonsignificant comparison is not evidence of exact equality. If all admissible evidence is stipulated invariant, the strong hypothesis is not testable by that evidence, regardless of confidence in it.

## 5. Positive explanatory content: apparent authorship as a relation in experience

Psi_A is proposed as a **relational configuration**, not a scalar score: (i) an occurring action/attempt source differentiated from a witnessed external change; (ii) a prospective consequence representation bound to that source; (iii) a present consequence representation compared or bound to the prospective one; (iv) a source-relative attribution that is actually used in organizing the ongoing episode. Here 'self-indexed' must be grounded by actual body/action reference and consumer roles, not by inserting a SELF label. The represented action may concern a tool or model hand; this does not make that referent an anatomical member.

The explanatory proposal is that, on an admitted human action domain, the intrinsic presentation of this enacted source-relative configuration is a contributor to felt doing. C1 provides the general actual/intrinsic interpretation; the identification of this particular contribution with F_A remains the substantive bridge. Accurate external causation is an additional success condition for the attribution, not a condition for an attribution process to be real. Consequently a **false represented authorship can be an actual organizational relation**. This gives an intelligible location for illusory agency without positing a second observer that reads a model and produces experience.

What does it explain if eventually supported? Why doing and merely witnessing can differ despite similar visible motion; why apparent doing can persist when external efficacy fails; and why the same consequence feature can participate in different self-related experience. It does not explain all ownership, familiar mineness, unreflective experience or conceptual autobiography. The four roles are a bounded candidate package, not proven individually necessary, minimal or universally sufficient. Passive ownership is outside its target. Imagery is a boundary to study, not automatically an action command. Prediction, comparison and attribution are not basal-experience requirements.

This is a modest synthesis of existing agency mechanisms in the UCT vocabulary, not a claim to invent a comparator theory. R155's acquisition and R158's phi_A already motivate it; R178 adds the scope/evidence contract and distinguishes genuine provenance from the enacted relation in the exact replay comparison.

## 6. Primary constraints and what they do not show

Kalckert and Ehrsson (2012) separate ownership and agency using active/passive movement and hand orientation. Their questionnaire combines several agency-related statements; the authors acknowledge it was not designed to isolate possible differences between bodily and external-object agency. This supports keeping our target narrow and refusing to treat all 'my' experiences as one variable. It does not instantiate the strict replay pair: active/passive conditions change motor processing, not only a hidden wire. Reading: publisher methods, questionnaire and discussion, including that limitation. DOI: 10.3389/fnhum.2012.00040.

Bashford and Mehring (2016) used an imitation BCI with EEG disconnected from virtual-hand motion; the paper reports positive control judgments in the illusion condition. It therefore supplies a relevant challenge to identifying reported agency with that actual control link. Instructions and task context differed from baseline; this is not a proof of equal full organization or a source-only paired intervention. Questionnaire and physiological responses are different measures; GSR is not a direct agency meter. Reading: publisher methods and results, not raw data. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0156591.

Both were already cited in R158. This fresh focused reading refines their application; it is not new empirical evidence or a comprehensive prior-art search. Their results do not establish Psi_A as necessary/sufficient or adjudicate UCT C1.

## 7. Four thought-experiment families retained with actual work to do

- Formation/ancestors: fix the bodily process lineage; vary acquisition of source-relative consequence use. This asks how a self-related experiential organization might be formed, without making its acquisition the first existence of experience. Gradual organization is motivation, not a proof of continuous phenomenal labels.
- Abacus/calculator: keep the arithmetic output and external SELF inscription fixed; compare an analyst interpreting it with an actually installed source-relative action route. This tests installation versus observer-assigned meaning. Neither case is declared devoid of experience, and no toy agency is inferred.
- Human-implemented agent: keep the executed abstract instructions; distinguish the interpreter's own body/action experience, the implemented sub-process and the coupled ensemble. The live/replay causal path can belong to one scope and not another. An inferred ensemble relation is not automatically the interpreter's F_A.
- Copy/reconnection/memory: the matched L/Yoked pair holds current receiver states and selected memory fixed, varies the admitted live causal edge, and records which bearer includes it. Later informative probes or remembered detection of replay break the original matching and require a new interval. No numerical personal-identity or death-fear result follows.
