## 3. Exact result audit

### R204 propositions

Proposition 1 correctly separates a task-level success predicate, prediction/plant equality, and a stipulated feedback-writing gate. The construction realizes every Boolean triple for each finite alphabet size at least two. The phrase “independent variation” is not promoted into statistical independence. After row replacement, the predictor is correctly allowed to be a total function rather than a permutation.

Proposition 2 is a valid induction: conditional discrepancy-gated replacement and unconditional replacement have the same stored predictor values at every step, although a matched overwrite is a different write event. This is a projection-specific implementation limit. The paper does not infer an experience-free reflex or retrospectively certify an actual comparator.

### Pure-source contracts and probe cost

Theorem 3's observation fibers and same-root criterion are correct. A full pair of consumer traces yields the product of their equal-code classes. A mismatch-only trace yields the inverse image of column XOR. The zero fiber identifies equality precisely when every source column is distinct. The paper correctly avoids claiming ordered-pair identification from a symmetric discrepancy channel.

Theorem 4 correctly gives `ceil(log2 n)` for exact, uniform, worst-case unknown-pair decision with fixed pure routes. Its deterministic adaptive lower bound follows the all-zero response branch: fewer bits leave a column collision, and the associated unequal pair follows precisely that same branch. The finite optimizer does not establish the adaptive part; the proof does.

Theorem 5 correctly changes the information state. An independently known pure actuator root set to zero, with every alternative source set to one, gives one decisive probe. The nonempty OR-predictor extension is valid: output zero means the set is exactly the known singleton. The assumptions exclude an empty constant-zero predictor and do not allow reported authorship to supply source knowledge.

Nominal behavior and prediction are held equal before the diagnostic interventions. The interventions deliberately break synchrony and can change task success or prediction error. That distinction is explicit in the abstract's setup and §§4–5; the paper does not claim a successful probe is also a matched nominal trial.

### OR aggregation and resolution limits

Theorem 6 correctly lets **both** consumers choose arbitrary nonempty source sets. For every source i, the alternatives `(N,N)` and `(N,N minus {i})` differ only at the unit input e_i. All n unit inputs are necessary on the equal-case adaptive branch and sufficient by symmetric-difference readout. The n≥2 condition preserves nonemptiness. The four-source disjoint-set alias is valid and attacks an extension of the pure class, not the theorem within that class.

The cancellation, shared/separate-relay, node-versus-edge and late-echo constructions retain their distinct intervention domains. Source-level function equality does not identify every internally consumed branch; complete source assignments do not identify relay factorization; a targeted node write is not a branch substitution; and equal final values do not certify predictive chronology. The node-write illustration includes a nontrivial value change, correct timing and no overwrite/compensation.

The event implementation's eager OR reads and post-injection origin traversal have already been corrected. Configured root sets, actual modeled read events, present-context outcome sensitivity, and diagnostic evidence are distinguished. MPC's later eager/short-circuit example reinforces that limit.

## 5. Experience and UCT boundaries

The paper consistently separates selected task success from a full intelligence profile; actual source/self-related organization from conceptual self; and reports from the experience or organization reported. Agency, ownership, tactile intensity, precision and familiar mineness are not substituted for one another.

C1 is introduced as an inherited conditional framework commitment. The finite graphs do not prove it. If one grounded source relation belongs to the complete constitutive organization of an admitted actual token, its UCT counterpart is a structural relation within experiential organization. That does not name the counterpart as H or establish a complete phenomenal type from a finite source projection.

Complete organizational clones preserve their complete experiential type under C1; observationally matched but organizationally different installations are a weaker comparison. Local processes that continue in an admitted larger process are not erased. Prediction, memory, intelligence, autonomy, source correspondence and report do not become new prerequisites for basic experience. These boundaries survive the release recommendation.

## 6. How MPC should affect this release

MPC20261010 studies a different question: at one fixed motor/proprioceptive consumer, the usual observed cells 00, 01 and 11 leave the response at 10 undetermined. A deterministic two-input binary table therefore has two completions; k unconstrained endpoints have 2^k joint completions. Even all four outputs do not identify a read occurrence, because eager and short-circuit implementations can agree as functions.

That is a compatible protocol-design continuation, not a missing premise in SCU's already declared abstract source class. MPC's 0/1 indicators encode delivery of a frozen branch input, whereas SCU's 0/1 assignments encode the values of physical source signals. Reading a zero-valued signal is not the absence of intake. Their models should not be merged by silently identifying those two meanings. MPC's real-world difficulty lies in realizing the 10 branch state while preserving the selected movement/touch consequence, context and consumer meaning. It supplies no completed physical human installation and no named phenomenal calibration.

I checked the accessible primary 2026 article by D'Onofrio Pacheco and Zimmermann: the publisher abstract and introduction distinguish intensity bias from discrimination precision and describe an active/passive/still by attention design. This supports MPC's endpoint-specific caution. It does not validate an anatomical OR gate or identify the SCU pure/OR source class in a human system.

The clean release choice is to keep the SCU body intact apart from release metadata, with at most a brief pinned follow-up reference in §9.3. Suggested optional sentence:

> A subsequent design note, MPC20261010, makes this application obligation concrete: a motor-command/state-input factorial must specify the missing motor-only consumer condition, preserve endpoint and context distinctions, and distinguish branch availability from executed intake; it does not supply a realized human intervention.

Do not import new hardware or shadow-controller results into the present abstract unless independently reviewed as an explicit later version. A logger's probe-arrival record is not a neuronal read event, and an engineered consumer is not automatically the person's selected experiential mechanism.
