# Concurrent option influence is not a prerequisite for retentive bodily familiarity

Research ID: A3V20261010. Result version: A3V-SCOPE-v0.1.0. Status: bounded literature/application review and conditional conceptual analysis; no actual phenomenal bridge established; no new completed theorem. Parent: A3R20261010. This note uses the corrected R173 pathway, not its withdrawn eight-row independence table.

## 1. Problem and result

A3R supplied a sufficient selector-participation certificate, `SPC = LiveJoin AND LocalRead AND Mediate`. Can that certificate be applied to one concrete premotor action system, and does its failure obstruct a positive account of bodily familiarity?

The named feasibility case is macaque dorsal premotor cortex (PMd) during the delayed two-target reaching task studied by Dekleva, Kording and Miller (2018). Its population recordings are informative about candidate preparatory dynamics, but the cited evidence does not discharge the three clauses for an actual same-instance policy resolver. The first missing clause is `LiveJoin`: simultaneously live, individually distinguished upstream policy occurrences feeding that same resolver in a signed interval. The certificate application stops there; unvalidated local port identity and mediation also remain missing. This is an evidential stop, not a proof that the animals lacked concurrent alternatives, experience, agency or mineness.

The positive return is equally important. The active corrected rule `r173_retentive_coordinate_transport` contains no SPC premise. Given its actual, jointly bound action-use, retention and coordinate-transport premises, it yields a history-indexed experiential coordinate. A familiar single-route enactment may instantiate this conditional account even when there is no dual-policy conflict. A3R's stronger SPC conjunction describes a sufficient special application, not the domain of every R173 application. Neither rule identifies that coordinate with independently specified bodily familiar-mineness `H`.

## 2. Three interpretations of two-option effects

| Interpretation | What must vary or co-occur | What the observation establishes | What remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Across-trial mixture | Different single plans on different trials, or switches over time | A two-option mean can arise from different single states | Co-occurrence on one trial |
| Integrated within-trial state | One state depends on both target cues and affects an early response | Both options influence the same trial's process | Distinct live policy tokens and a separately identifiable resolver |
| Concurrent policy participation | Two specified policy occurrences remain live and enter the same local resolver | `LiveJoin`, if actual identities/timing/route are independently verified | `LocalRead AND Mediate`; H is a further question |

The alternatives are not an exhaustive ontology or exclusive owners. A process can include overlapping or nested occurrences. The table separates claims and evidence at a fixed grain; it does not decree how many conscious subjects there are.

Cisek and Kalaska (2005) is the prior primary report motivating parallel specification/selection in PMd. Here only its PubMed abstract was read; its full recording/selection protocol was not verified in this run. Dekleva et al. (2018) distinguish single-trial population activity from trial averages in two monkeys. Their analysis supports one-plan stay/switch preparation in their delayed task, rather than the simple simultaneous-plan reading of averaged activity. Decoder classification and post hoc thresholds are evidence about their decoding model, not independent validation of an A3R resolver port.

Selen et al. (2023) provides a counterweight to any universal single-plan claim. Human pectoralis EMG in immediate free-choice reaching showed early nonchosen-target influence in the 85–125 ms express response window; a weighted motor-average model fit better than the compared selected-only/spatial/unweighted alternatives. This is a within-trial behavioral/physiological result in another task, not a verified PMd two-token resolver. Sorting by eventual choice is not an intervention preserving a fixed winning token while selectively altering an independently verified live loser. Meirhaeghe et al. (2023) studies grasp preparation and proposes one intermediate preparatory state sensitive to two possible grips as an optimized tradeoff. The task description and summary support this alternative; this run did not independently verify all results or supplements. Neither paper can be conjoined with the Dekleva task to form one actual SPC witness.

## 3. Minimal exact countermodels

`witnesses.py` checks three stipulated architectures, using elementary finite functions rather than new mathematics. A trial-mixture architecture chooses a single preparatory plan on each trial; its balanced mean matches two coordinates being equally represented. A joint-token architecture has two distinguished live inputs to a resolver. An integrated architecture instead has one combined state `z=a+b` and output `z`: the same output function and the same available input-perturbation effects can occur without two distinct policy occurrences entering a separate resolver at the stipulated grain. The latter has two cue causes, not automatically two policy tokens. This model is an identifiability counterexample for limited readouts, not an ontological exclusion of finer process decompositions.

A fourth witness enacts a retained single route: earlier frame occurrence → continued trace → current frame consumer → route execution. No competitor or resolver is stipulated. It shows joint consistency of retention/current use and no dual conflict in an abstract event model. Physical realization and the C1 coordinate signature remain explicit assumptions; the witness does not prove a human familiar feeling.

## 5. Positive explanatory sketch and its remaining gap

RetBind describes how an earlier body's/action frame remains available through an actually continued trace and participates in this episode's frame consumer. Under C1-based same-signature transport, that historical relation has an experience-internal coordinate: the present episode is organized through retained bodily/action history. This is a positive structural account of an experiential aspect, not merely an external ownership classifier. It introduces no extra observing owner and no report requirement. A still-open bridge must explain why this aspect is the familiar character of this bodily action rather than a different experiential organization, and must give a discriminating consequence beyond renaming retention as H.

The next bounded question is therefore: in a familiar singleton body-route enactment versus an unfamiliar but presently congruent enactment, what independently signed, fallible bodily-familiarity endpoint would distinguish B_ret and B_fit while holding cue familiarity and response fluency apart? First establish the actual frame consumer and target meaning. Do not resume certificate growth or Bayesian attribution models merely because this bridge is difficult.
