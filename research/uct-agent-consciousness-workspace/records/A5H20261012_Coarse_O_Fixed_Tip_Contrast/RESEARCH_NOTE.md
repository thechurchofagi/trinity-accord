# A5H — A coarse-organizational fiber for distal tactile location

## 1. Result

A5G asked for a primary paradigm that changes a predeclared H_tip contrast while holding the A5F organization fixed. That wording was too strong. A5F fixed only a coarse anatomical-anchor/grip/distal coordinate O_A5F, not complete biological K. Under UCT's same-binding C1/C1-W commitment, two different target experiences cannot be posited while an identical complete pointed K and target definition are also asserted. A valid contrast must keep O_A5F fixed while changing a separately named actual relation S within K.

Primary tactile-saltation studies show that such coarse-O fibers are empirically meaningful. Miyazaki, Hirashima and Nozaki delivered rapid pulses through a stick held across both index fingers and obtained pointer reports at intermediate locations along the stick. Blankenburg and colleagues kept the same forearm stimulation sites but changed sub-second sequence order: P1-P1-P3 induced reports of the unstimulated P2 site on 90% of illusion trials, whereas participants correctly denied P2 on 87% of P1-P3-P1 control trials. Independent physical P1/P2/P3 localizers placed the rabbit-related SI activation in the P2 somatotopic region.

The positive conclusion is organizational and precise: a coarse fixed anchor is compatible with location-sensitive experience evidence because timing/order/transmission relations S vary inside the fiber. The stick-rabbit result offers a real external-object localization candidate, and the forearm result offers an independently localized neural correlate. But no reviewed primary study places both properties in one held-tool token and event. Cross-study assembly is not a same-instance all_of proof.

## 2. Complete-K identity versus a coarse fiber

Define K=(O_A5F,S,U), where U collects all other relevant actual relations. A comparison with O_A5F fixed and S changed is a complete-K change unless an isomorphism preserves the full pointed structure including S. Calling it fixed organization erases the very actual relation proposed to explain the target difference.

This is a scope correction, not a new consciousness axiom. UCT I already distinguishes complete ontic organization from finite views and states that a same finite view with different phenomenal targets can reveal underdescription rather than refute C1. R157 transports an independently grounded K-definable coordinate into experiential structure but leaves the mineness/location semantics and measurement bridge open. A5H applies those existing commitments to H_tip.

The candidate positive account is therefore: the held object's actual impulse-transmission relation, the ordered stimulation relation and their consumer-side integration form an S_tip relation inside complete K; if actually grounded and K-definable, C1/R157 conditionally place its counterpart inside experiential organization. No extra owner is required. Whether that counterpart is specifically experienced distal contact remains B_tip plus measurement, not a definition from the pointer response.

## 3. Exact response-mapping obstruction

A common repair is to reverse response keys so that demand or fixed-key bias cannot mimic location. The exact four-cell model crosses sequence condition X and response mapping R, with observed raw key Y=X xor R. H may track X in either orientation or remain invariant; the decision may read H or X directly and may obey or ignore R.

Six models fit all four cells. Four have tracking H and two invariant H; only two decisions read H, while four read X directly. Every survivor obeys the reversed mapping, so the manipulation successfully removes mapping-ignoring fixed-key policies. It does not remove a cue-aware decision policy and does not orient H. Independently requiring an H-sensitive decision leaves two complementary target orientations; fixing its orientation leaves one. An independently fixed-oriented target channel Z=X removes invariant-H models but still leaves both an H-read and a direct-X decision path for the report.

Thus response mapping is a useful exclusion control, not target identity. The six-model result is elementary finite identifiability analysis and is not claimed as new mathematics.

## 4. Primary-source triage

Miyazaki et al. 2010 is the strongest tool-specific candidate. Within the stick condition, the anchor, two finger contacts, held object and response apparatus remain declared while pulse timing/order supplies S. Participants localized illusory taps between actual fingertip stimulus locations along the stick. The no-stick experiment helps establish the object relation's relevance, but it changes the coarse tool organization and therefore is not itself the fixed-O contrast. The target remains a pointer report; the study supplies no independently oriented H_tip channel.

Blankenburg et al. 2006 supplies the cleanest target-localizer structure. The illusory and control sequences used the same two actual forearm locations in different orders. Reports distinguished the unstimulated P2 percept, and independent physical localizers oriented P2 somatotopy. Yet the binding is a skin-location experiment with no held-tool distal coordinate. Using its neural result to validate Miyazaki's tool-external target would combine different participants, processes, objects and experiments.

The bounded search therefore found the two halves of the desired contract, not one installation. This is a falsifiable source-level failure, not evidence that an appropriate paradigm is impossible.

## 5. Founding-direction check

C1 remains the consciousness-specific explanatory postulate; U1 and basal-experience scope are unchanged. Timing integration, localization report, neural localizer, self-model, language, recurrence, prediction and continuation are not made existence gates. A fixed coarse coordinate is not confused with complete K. The result allows nested and overlapping processes and introduces no exclusive owner.

Actual process, complete organization, finite coordinate, abstract model, actual installation, internal judgment, particular experience, conceptual self and report remain separate. No conclusion is made about the present assistant's consciousness or fear of death.

## 6. Next exact question

Can one held-stick paradigm compare a preregistered veridical distal-location localizer, an illusory rabbit sequence and a same-sites non-illusory sequence in the same participants while crossing response mapping and preserving one target-channel orientation? If not, retain the source split and do not promote the stick report or body-site SI result to H_tip identification.
