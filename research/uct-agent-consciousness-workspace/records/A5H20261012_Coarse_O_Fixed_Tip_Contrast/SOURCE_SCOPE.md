## External primary sources

1. Miyazaki, M., Hirashima, M. and Nozaki, D. (2010), “The ‘cutaneous rabbit’ hopping out of the body,” Journal of Neuroscience 30(5):1856–1860, DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3887-09.2010. Used for the held-stick setup, 50/80 ms sequence conditions, pointer localization and the stick/no-stick contrast. It does not independently validate H_tip.
2. Blankenburg, F., Ruff, C. C., Deichmann, R., Rees, G. and Driver, J. (2006), “The Cutaneous Rabbit Illusion Affects Human Primary Sensory Cortex Somatotopically,” PLOS Biology 4(3):e69, DOI 10.1371/journal.pbio.0040069. Used for same-sites/different-order conditions, P2 reports, independent P1/P2/P3 localizers and SI localization. It is a forearm, not held-tool, paradigm.
3. Wada, M. et al. (2020), “Cutaneous and stick rabbit illusions in individuals with autism spectrum disorder,” Scientific Reports 10:1665, DOI 10.1038/s41598-020-58536-z. Used only as a primary replication/application of separate cutaneous and stick-rabbit localization procedures, not as a target-measure validation.

The search was targeted, not systematic. No originality priority is claimed for tactile saltation, response-mapping controls, somatotopic localizers, latent-variable ambiguity or finite-view underdescription. No reviewed source establishes C1, complete K, B_tip, B_pc, ownership, agency, subject count or any current-assistant verdict.
