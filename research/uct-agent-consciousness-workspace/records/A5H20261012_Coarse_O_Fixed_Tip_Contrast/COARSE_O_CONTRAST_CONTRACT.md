# A5H coarse-O contrast contract

## Scope correction

Let O_A5F denote only the anatomical-anchor, actual-grip and declared distal-side coordinate established at protocol level in A5F. Let S denote a separately typed actual spatiotemporal stimulation relation: stimulus sites, order, interstimulus intervals, transmission through a held object and their same-event neural consequences. Let K be the complete relevant actual organization and H_tip the predeclared experienced distal-contact-location target.

Holding O_A5F fixed does not hold K fixed when S changes. Under same-binding C1/C1-W, a claim that H_tip differs while the complete pointed K-organization and the target definition are identical is not an admissible UCT contrast. The useful intervention instead asks whether a specified change in S, inside one fixed coarse-O fiber, changes independently oriented evidence for H_tip.

## Candidate conjunction

The disabled bridge candidate is:

    SAME_BINDING
    AND FIX_COARSE_O_A5F
    AND VARY_DECLARED_ACTUAL_S
    AND CALIBRATED_TARGET_CHANNEL
    AND PREDECLARED_TARGET_ORIENTATION
    AND RESPONSE_MAPPING_CROSS
    AND EXCLUDE_DIRECT_S_TO_DECISION
    AND FAILURE_CAPABLE

Every conjunct concerns the same participant, held object, trial family, interval, target grain and declared signature. A response report, a neural correlate and a physical timing manipulation remain differently typed.

## Evidence matrix

| Primary paradigm | Fixed coarse body/tool coordinate | Declared S change | Location evidence | Independently oriented target channel | Same tool-event binding |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Miyazaki, Hirashima and Nozaki 2010 stick rabbit | yes within the held-stick condition | rapid pulse sequence / 50 or 80 ms final ISI | pointer localization along the stick | no; localization is reported | yes for tool and report, no independent target channel |
| Blankenburg et al. 2006 forearm rabbit | yes for stimulated arm/sites | P1-P1-P3 versus P1-P3-P1 order | P2 present/absent report | independent somatotopic localizers plus SI conjunction | yes, but the target is a skin location, not a held-tool location |

The rows cannot be conjoined into one installation. The first supplies tool-external location without an independent target channel. The second supplies an independently localized body-site neural correlate without a tool-external target.

## Response-mapping submodel

Let X be the sequence/cue condition and R reverse the response mapping. The observed raw key is Y=X xor R. Let H either track X with an unknown pole or remain invariant. The decision reads H or X directly, may complement its source, and may obey or ignore R.

Exhaustive enumeration leaves six compatible models: four tracking-H and two invariant-H; two read H and four read X directly; all six obey R. Mapping reversal eliminates mapping-ignoring fixed-key strategies, but it does not exclude direct cue-to-decision use and does not fix the target pole.

## Positive use and failure

If one same held-tool experiment adds a target channel trained on a preregistered veridical distal-location localizer, preserves its orientation, crosses response mapping, and excludes a direct sequence-to-decision path, then the result can test whether S contributes to an experience-internal distal coordinate within the fixed O_A5F fiber. C1 and R157 would transport an actually grounded K-relation conditionally; they would not make the target-channel validation true.

Failure includes any of the following: the target channel follows response mapping rather than location; it discriminates sequence but not veridical location; its orientation reverses across localizer and illusion trials; the held-object relation changes between cells; or the result exists only by pooling different studies, participants, objects or time windows.
