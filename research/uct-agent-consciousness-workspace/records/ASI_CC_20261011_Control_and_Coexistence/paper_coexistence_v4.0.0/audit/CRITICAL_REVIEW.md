# Internal critical review — PR-v4.0.0

This is self-review in the same research workflow, not independent peer review.

1. **Does the manuscript assume AI personhood?** No. The primary target is protected human service; the initiating request is human-authorized. No machine subjective preference enters ER-T1/T3. The UCT discussion preserves its theoretical status.
2. **Is information-for-control claimed as new?** No. Tomar et al. is treated as a direct predecessor. Covering designs and C(6,3,2) are explicitly classical. Novelty is only the scoped operational synthesis, reduction and test family; exact priority unverified.
3. **Could a more powerful plan break the lower bound?** Yes, if it adds adaptation, late recovery, unlisted capability or a better observation. The claim is exact only for the declared plan menu. Section3 explicitly prevents extrapolating incomplete-menu impossibility.
4. **Does the fallback model silently use the old strict flow model?** No. It permits pre-armed idle slots; successful plans cover affected channels. Section5 separately states the old Hall model and warns against merging assumptions.
5. **Is the trusted source real?** Only supplied by an instrumented experimental parent. This is a necessary deployment premise, not proven by the codebook or authentication.
6. **Does successful service establish revocation?** No. Bypass control serves every target while admitting revoked responses. Both properties separately recorded.
7. **Did the work execute?** New scripts completed. Data record86 cases/1335 evaluations and5776 relation tables; three baseline script outputs semantically match the supplied archive. Partial failed first run is excluded.

Sixteen duplicate reports are one source record copied, not sixteen monitors. Late evidence is lack of timely discrimination, not actual latency testing.
9. **Could old UCT constraints be contradicted?** Main theorems are mathematical/operational and do not promote an experience predicate. Still, whole-map semantics and all published-claim coverage were NOT checked; status remains PENDING_MAP.
10. **Does the word coexistence promise too much?** Scope expressly operational/contract-relative. No consensus theorem, normative entitlement ranking, stable bargaining result, independent welfare measure or universal ASI containment assertion.

12. **What remains most important?** Actual physical/cyber boundary completeness, trustworthy sensor realizability, backup independence, and a specified timing guarantee. These must be measured or independently justified before applying the theorem outside the finite experiment.

Decision: self-contained paper ready for independent critical review, not a claim to have become a classic or passed a complete global originality examination.
