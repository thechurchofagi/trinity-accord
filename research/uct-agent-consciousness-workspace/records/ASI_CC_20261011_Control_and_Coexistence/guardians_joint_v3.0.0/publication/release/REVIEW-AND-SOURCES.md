# Scope, prior art, review and reproducibility

Version EAC-v3.0.0, Hongju Liu, research date 2026-10-12. This publication is the first planned Zenodo DOI of the formerly unpublished *When the Guardians Can Be Acquired* family, following EAC-v2.0.0. It is not a version of the separate published Liu papers with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23295776 or 10.5281/zenodo.23299153; those deposits must remain intact.

Core scope: minimal unsafe combinations of concurrently approved policy changes; protection-sensitive qualified witnesses, bounded moving-root exposure and single-proposal conditional completion; renewed authorization must carry the real committed effects and fence superseded approvals. Mathematical claims are conditional on complete conflict catalogues, correct root/qualification mapping, faithfully stored local journals and signed commitments to the correct parent state. It does **not** prove full consensus, unbounded concurrent liveness, real-world ASI controllability, human consensus or AI subjecthood.

Important predecessors acknowledged in manuscript: Bailis et al. 2014 invariant confluence, Kleppmann and Howard 2020 Byzantine eventual consistency, Malkhi and Reiter 1998 Byzantine quorum systems, Fast Flexible Paxos high-order quorums, and proactive secret-sharing/reconfiguration. The underlying set-intersection principles are not claimed as newly invented; the exact scoped combined application has not been confirmed globally first.

Executable reproducibility: unpack the ZIP under its EAC-v3.0.0 directory and run the scripts under `src/`, using declared dependencies in `requirements.txt`. The archive contains `data/*.json` and `RUN_RECEIPT.json` for crosschecking. Baseline reruns are inherited evidence, not independent samples or real AI attacks. Reviewer judgment and human-legitimacy standards are not provided by cryptographic signatures.

No global UCT graph promotion: UCT-MAP-v1.1.2 (913/424/261) remains unchanged; related results remain PENDING_MAP and require separate semantic audit. Authors' older public papers were not modified.
