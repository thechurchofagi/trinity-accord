9. **Herzberg et al., Proactive Secret Sharing**, CRYPTO1995: original research page bibliography. Mobile/adaptive compromise and restoration are not new ideas. No complete cryptographic proof reproduced.
10. **HRU1976, MulVAL2005**: inherited prior draft sources, not newly fully read. Mutable permissions and logical reachability are classical.
11. **UCT source**: current guide's actual-process/subject/type constraints reread; no new reread of every paper section and no whole-map proof audit.
