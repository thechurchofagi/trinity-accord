## Net manuscript increment
The exact finite-peak, all-time handoff margin for an arbitrary inherited unit-repair eligibility profile; its inverse safe renewed burst; robustness to partial maturity evidence; a clearly delimited composition condition; and a local asynchronous reservation test suite with actual commit-before-ack process exit. Core methods have established predecessors, and the global priority of this exact closed-form application is still open.

## Important nonclaims
No first discovery that revocation is not rollback; no first distributed reservation ledger; no universal recovery theorem; no experiment on a frontier model or subjective consent; no assertion that all humans share the declared normative contract. The paper is a completed, self-contained conditional manuscript, not a guarantee of novelty established merely by DOI or source storage.
