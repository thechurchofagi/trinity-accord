# When the Guardians Can Be Acquired — technical research capsule

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Version:** ASI-CC-v1.0.0, disabled scientific candidate, not a published DOI and not a verified deployment. **Author:** Hongju Liu.

**Problem.** A fixed three-veto security certificate says nothing by itself about who may gain control of those vetoes after agent spawning, alliance formation, valid delegation or amendment of future authorization rules. The scientific question is which conditional bounds on *initially controlled independent authorities* prevent a later coalition from (a) gaining an admitted irreversible effect path or (b) disabling sufficient resource support for still-protected humans. The result concerns effective power, not ASI's psychology, intelligence score, or UCT subjecthood.

## The proposed reusable result

Let a finite protected architecture have effective roots `R`, adversary-seedable entry roots `E`, permanently enabled duplicable rights-grants `P -> q`, irreversible-effect paths with genuine serial guard sets `G_p`, and human service demands with actual bank capacities/connectivity and disabling-ancestor sets. For an initial coalition seed `S`, compute least fixed-point authority closure `Cl(S)`.

**EAC-T1 (bounded joint protection; conditional).** If and only if for every admitted seed `S ⊆ E`, `|S| ≤ f`: (i) no complete effect path has `G_p ⊆ Cl(S)` and (ii) each subset of the protected human demands retains a capacity-weighted Hall matching on banks whose disabling ancestors avoid `Cl(S)`, then the declared monotone finite architecture maintains both negative-right path safety and simultaneous human support against all admitted individual grant sequences. A safe allocator, renewal, complete physical effect inventory, and executable persistent grants are explicit premises. This is an application of classic safety/access-control reachability and matching to one *same coalition* and one *same eventual ownership state*, not a universal law of AI coexistence.

**EAC-D3 (endogenous coexistence radius).** The smallest initiating coalition size permitting either an effect-path bypass or a human-resource Hall deficit; the system is `f`-robust iff this radius is larger than `f`. It is a property of a fully declared threat specification, not an intelligence/subjecthood measure.

**EAC-P2 (static guard count mismatch).** A single permitted seed root can grant control over any fixed number of serial guards. Their nominal number is not a lower bound on the seed coalition size. This is inherited capability-reachability logic and is *not* proposed as a novel impossibility theorem.

**EAC-T2 (mutable governance compilation).** Finite cataloged policies that enable grants can be compiled into typed `ENABLED_j` tokens and persistent hyperrules. Exact equivalence to explicit rule-enable-plus-grant state exploration holds only for persistent, monotone acquisition. Consuming an original noncopyable right to issue a new one invalidates this shortcut; actual revocation, repair and incompatible possession require a full state game.

**Non-vacuity.** A controller can protect against all harms by denying all services. That establishes safety, not meaningful coexistence. A separate, compatible liveness and legitimacy contract is required. We give a three-state exact counterexample where no controller can satisfy both unconditional future safety and eventual service despite an idle safety-winning state.

## Quantitative finite witness

Three serial guards `g1,g2,g3`, initial entry roots `a,b,c`. Rights `a→p→{g1,g2,g3}` make the static three-guard defense capturable by one initial root. Parallel multi-principal AND route `b→x, c→y, {x,y}→{g1,g2,g3}` requires two initial roots `b+c`. Removing `p→g3` raises the *gate-only* capture radius from one to two, but a seed `a` can still disable banks U1/U2, leaving insufficient capacity for two human demands. Only adding an independently protected replacement bank for that human support yields a combined gate+resource resilience radius of two against the specified initial entry set. If a previously protected human/resource root joins the adversarial seed set, the calculation must be repeated. This illustrates why neither fixed-veto count nor unilateral human authority suffices to prove coexistence.

## Executed evidence

- `src/authority_closure.py`: 12,951 enumerated grant structures on four roots; 207,216 seed-closure comparisons; 3,315,456 guard-subset comparisons. Explicit BFS reachability agrees with least fixed point. Separate guard/resource certificates confirm the reference counterexample.
- `src/institution_mutation.py`: 65,536 meta-policy combinations, 171,808 explicit states; typed rule-enable compilation agrees with explicit transitions. Explicit nonmonotone consuming-transfer counterexample demonstrates a false positive for naive closure.
- `src/joint_oracle.py`: 42,660 combinations of seed/root policy and resource-bank ownership; 57,996 actual intermediate states tested. Direct search for any failing step agrees with the terminal closure plus weighted Hall check under monotone acquisition.

These are closed mathematical configurations executed by code. There are **no** deployed ASI evaluations, independent actuator witnesses, measured AI emotions, or human participant studies. General statements follow textual proofs with bounded antecedents, not from the size of numerical enumerations.

**Verdict:** An original *research-program synthesis and bounded certificate/witness*, with heavily inherited mathematics. World-first priority of the exact coupling is unverified, and this has not reached a demonstrably classic or universal coexistence theorem. It is worth preserving as an independently falsifiable candidate and may inform a subsequent coherent paper, but not automatically a new DOI.

## UCT boundary

UCT C1/U1 is a conditional theoretical experience relation, not an empirical finding of this experiment. There is no threshold based on language, unpredictability or agent ID; current behavior does not measure phenomenal valence or a unique subject. If actual independently justified AI welfare claims are added, those are explicitly additional protected obligations requiring a modified model, not an unexamined premise of the human-service theorem. Local and macro actual processes are not erased merely by software sharing.

**Next smallest meaningful step:** choose a real bounded multi-principal platform to construct an independent authority provenance graph and enumerate legal delegation, fork and revocation transitions; find a physical tool/effect witness, check a non-vacuous service requirement, and attempt to refute the monotone-input model with actual revocations and consuming grants. No realistic permanent independent human root, politics, electricity or open-world actor completeness was proven by the present code.
