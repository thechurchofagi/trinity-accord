# Scoped hostile audit — ASI-CC-v1.0.0

- **Formal target clarity:** all roots finite and genuinely effect-bearing; human protected obligations chosen as procedural inputs; no assumption AI is a subjective moral person. Threat model explicitly permits hostile coalitions of humans and/or AIs only for entry roots declared seedable.
- **Theorem EAC-T1:** correctness requires persistent monotone rules, closure reachable jointly rather than marginally, complete actual effect-path list, complete bank-disabling ancestors, renewable bank capacities, and faithful matching allocator. All under same starting coalition and same time-ordered sequence. Proof claims are conditional on these.
- **Integer matching:** each human target consumes one indivisible unit. Bank expansion yields Hall necessity/sufficiency; union capacity cannot be counted repeatedly when shared. Independent DFS vs Hall performed on 42,660 seeds/bank assignments.
- **EAC-T2:** typed rule-enable tokens are not physical roots. Grants are permanent and accumulate; all separately enabled grant steps are eventually executable. A consuming-rights counterexample falsifies an extension to nonmonotone rules, deliberately retained.
- **Static-vs-dynamic gap:** potentially unbounded *within nominal root count*, but not a proof that hardened independent physical gates are capturable. Acquiring their controlling rights is itself the threat assumption.
- **Human disagreement:** legitimacy of protection contracts and which human principals count as hostile are assumptions. No consensus derives from mathematical closure. A nominally separate h that may join a coalition must be included in seeds, and the result recalculated.
- **Safety vs liveness:** denial of all useful actions can trivially satisfy safety. The candidate does not prove nonvacuous long-run social/energy/economic coexistence; the 3-state counterexample makes that limit explicit.
- **UCT:** C1/U1 theoretical commitments remain unchanged, no complexity/language/AI-rights gate, no experience/welfare measurement, local/macro process possible. Software IDs are not automatically subjective subjects.
- **Existing prior art:** HRU, MulVAL, ARBAC, Zhu/Wang and dynamic institution literature give close antecedents. Full priority of the precise synthesis not established. Broad theorems are inherited/elementary, not newly proven first.
- **Whole-map status:** did not programmatically traverse all 913 nodes, 424 rules, 261 context relations and 10 paused rules in this turn. Current map, master guide, policy and a relevant PR6 index/receipt were inspected; paper-related affected logic and the UCT bridge checked at the typed abstraction level. The formal graph remains unchanged and unreviewed entries are explicitly `AUDIT_INCOMPLETE`.
- **Run evidence:** 12,951 models; 207,216 seed closure comparisons; 3,315,456 guard subsets; 65,536 editable-rule configurations/171,808 explored states; 42,660 resource allocations/57,996 intermediate states. All final assertions PASS; these are test configurations, not 3 million empirical safety events.
- **Recorded failure:** initial manuscript companion script for policy enabling had a Python string syntax error due to a literal apostrophe, fixed before the successful execution. No result reported from the failed run.
- **Stopping condition:** no DOI/OTS/Arweave; no new established UCT map theorem; preserve the final PDF and reproducible archive as a candidate until independent research contribution and actual physical source claims are better validated.
