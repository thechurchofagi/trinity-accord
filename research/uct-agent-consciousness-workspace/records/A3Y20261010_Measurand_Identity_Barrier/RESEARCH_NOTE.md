# A3Y — The measurand-identity barrier

Version: `A3Y-MEASURAND-v0.1.0`  
Status: scoped conceptual and exact methodological advance; no participant data, installed bridge, or completed-map extension

## Question and net result

Can a no-gold-standard multi-indicator design supply the missing directional relation `C_H: J_H -> H_way` for the familiar felt character of a presently enacted bodily manner?

**Result:** not from latent-class recovery alone. Even granting that a statistical model recovers a common latent variable `Z` and stable indicator laws, the observable law is silent about the kernel `P(H_way | Z)`. The same indicator tables are compatible with `H_way=Z`, `H_way` independent of `Z`, and `H_way=1-Z`. Thus two debts must be separated:

1. **orientation debt** — which pole of a target-directed coordinate is positive (R194/R200);
2. **measurand-identity debt** — whether the recovered coordinate is the selected experiential target at all.

The independent case witnesses the second debt and is not removable by relabelling classes. A3Y therefore does not discharge `C_H`; it stops before comparing candidate retained-route/congruence explanations.

## Typed domain

Fix two reporting-capable adult populations executing the same declared present action under one endpoint contract. Let:

- `Z in {0,1}` be a statistical latent variable sufficient for the two indicator laws;
- `T1,T2 in {0,1}` be imperfect indicators;
- `H` abbreviate the selected `H_way` contrast for a particular enacted episode;
- `J_H` be a coded judgement intended to concern `H`;
- `S_H` be A3X's content-admissibility gate;
- `O_H` be actual same-instance organizational evidence.

`Z` is not defined as `H`. That equality is the proposition under audit. Statistical classes, selected experiential coordinates, actual organizational relations and language reports remain different objects.

## A3Y-C1 — exact measurand-identity nonidentification

Take `P(Z=1)=1/4` and `3/4` in the two populations. Conditional on `Z`, let the indicators be independent, with

`P(T1=1|Z=1)=3/4`, `P(T1=1|Z=0)=1/4`,

`P(T2=1|Z=1)=4/5`, `P(T2=1|Z=0)=1/5`.

The exact observed `(T1,T2)` tables, ordered `11,10,01,00`, are

- population 1: `(15,15,13,37)/80`;
- population 2: `(37,13,15,15)/80`.

Now add one of three kernels without changing `Z`, either indicator law, or either population table:

| Kernel | Relation of `H` to `Z` | `Delta_T1` | `Delta_T2` |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| aligned | `H=Z` | `+1/2` | `+3/5` |
| independent | `P(H=1|Z)=1/2` | `0` | `0` |
| opposed | `H=1-Z` | `-1/2` | `-3/5` |

Here `Delta_Ti=P(Ti=1|H=1)-P(Ti=1|H=0)`. `check_measurand_identity.py` verifies the tables and signs by exact rational arithmetic. More generally, the observable likelihood factors through `Z` and does not contain the freely chosen kernel `P(H|Z)`. Hence no indicator-only implication can establish `Z=H`, independence, or a direction between them.

This is a conditional impossibility for the declared evidence class, not a claim that target identity is unknowable. A genuinely target-directed intervention, independently grounded phenomenological comparison, or defensible causal measurement model could constrain `P(H|Z)`. Merely adding populations, indicators, sample size or fit quality does not do so.

## Relation to R194, R202 and R203

R194 proves that relative signed alignment leaves a global complement freedom. A3Y retains that result but adds a different failure: `H` can be independent of the recovered `Z`, so the problem is not only which class name is positive. R202's audit inversion assumes independently calibrated endpoint errors `a,c`; it cannot use a latent-class fit to manufacture the identity of that endpoint with `H`. R203 weakens exact error-rate knowledge to an independently warranted endpoint direction and residual-covariance budget; A3Y shows that “endpoint direction” must still be direction **about the selected phenomenal measurand**, not merely about `Z`.

A3X's `S_H` remains useful: it can reject visible substitution of practice, source, control or ease in a participant's interpretation. But a finite content pass does not force the other indicators to share `H` as their measurand. Therefore the prospective warrant is not simply “many indicators agree”; it is `S_H AND target-identity bridge AND signed reliability AND O_H`.

## Positive UCT consequence: structure is not semantic naming

Failure of `C_H` blocks empirical naming of a recovered class as a particular `H_way` value. It does not erase UCT's conditional structural claim. Under C1, when an actual physical relation is independently admitted in the complete organization, its experiential counterpart is internal to that experience. R157's semantic residual and R173's conditional retentive-coordinate route remain in force: actual RetBind/path structure may support an experience-internal coordinate without a latent statistical class thereby acquiring the label “familiar bodily mineness.”

This separates three questions:

1. **structural inclusion:** what actual organization has an experience-internal counterpart under C1;
2. **semantic measurement:** which selected phenomenal interpretation an indicator or report tracks;
3. **actual application:** whether the proposed route/use is installed and active in the same episode.

The separation advances the positive explanation by locating the felt self inside the experiential organization while refusing to let an external latent label become an extra owner or a surrogate for the experience.

## Prior work and source boundary

Hui and Walter (1980, DOI `10.2307/2530508`) establish a classic two-test/two-population no-gold-standard estimation design under conditional independence and population assumptions. Dendukuri et al. (2026, arXiv `2607.14473`) explicitly warn that tests can have different latent measurands from the target condition and analyze the resulting identifiability and bias. Da Costa et al. (2024/2026, arXiv `2409.20318`) treat the correspondence between phenomenology and formal belief descriptions as an operating technical assumption, not as a consequence of fit alone.

A3Y applies that measurand discipline to UCT's selected embodied-mineness target and connects it to the existing semantic/actuality ledgers. The latent-class mathematics and the general warning are prior work; no worldwide novelty claim is made. The search was targeted, not systematic, and none of the checked sources validates UCT C1 or this `H_way` endpoint.
