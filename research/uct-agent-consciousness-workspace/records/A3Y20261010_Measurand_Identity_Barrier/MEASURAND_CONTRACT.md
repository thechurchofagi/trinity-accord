# A3Y measurand contract

A proposed no-gold-standard calibration may be interpreted at the `H_way` level only if it declares, before inspecting the target association:

1. **statistical latent:** the variable `Z` and the assumptions under which its model is identified or partially identified;
2. **phenomenal target:** the independently specified selected experiential query `H_way`, including bearer, interval and present-enactment scope;
3. **identity/measurement bridge:** a defeasible reason why the indicators measure `H_way` rather than practice knowledge, source recognition, control, ease, task success, or another latent cause;
4. **orientation and reliability:** an independently warranted sign and error/residual bound, not merely a fitted class label;
5. **same-instance actuality:** evidence that the proposed physical route, bearer, interval, signature, history and carrier-to-consumer use are actual for the compared episodes;
6. **unfitted defeat condition:** an outcome that would reject the target bridge rather than relabel `Z` or reinterpret the indicators after the fact.

If item 3 is absent, the result must be reported as a relation to `Z`, not to `H_way`. If item 4 is absent, even a target-directed bridge remains unoriented or unbounded. If item 5 is absent, neither statistical layer establishes an organizational explanation. The conjunction is an application gate, not a new basal-experience condition and not a completed formal-map rule.
