# When can an experiential structure matter to intelligence?
## Target-relative constitutive relevance without causal double counting

**R183 v0.1, 8 October 2026.** Unpublished UCT research record. The set-family lemmas are established mathematics. The new contribution claimed here is a typed explanatory synthesis and application, not a new general theorem of causation, a proof of C1, or a named-feeling result. Its map layer remains `PENDING_MAP` because the whole current graph has not received a complete semantic audit.

## 1. Question and non-duplicate target

The author's question is whether experience matters for intelligence. Four statements are often run together:

1. an actual process has experience under UCT;
2. changing its complete organization changes its complete experiential type;
3. one selected constitutive relation is needed for one capability under one intervention protocol;
4. an independently identified human feeling causes general intelligence as an extra force.

UCT I's C1/U1 supplies (1) as an internal commitment. UCT III Proposition 1 already supplies the fixed-capability implication from capability difference to complete experiential-type difference. EI20261008 already shows architecture-dependent cue use and redundancy. CORE20261008 already defines minimal successful support families. This record does not reissue any of those results.

The missing bridge is a precise reading of (3) under C1, and a proof that it does not imply (4). We ask when the intrinsic experiential presentation of a selected actual relation can be called *target-relative relevant* to a capability, and when that sentence is false or underdetermined.

## 2. Four counterfactuals that must not be conflated

Fix an actual token `P`, interval `I`, complete constitutive signature `K`, capability contract `M`, target `T`, and intervention protocol `pi` before observing outcomes.

### 2.1 Fixed-organization experience-off

Hold `D_ontic_K(P)` fixed and set `Phi_K(P)` to empty or to a nonisomorphic structure. This is not an admissible UCT comparison: it contradicts C1/U1. It cannot establish that experience causally helps or fails to help intelligence inside UCT. Rejecting this pseudo-intervention is an internal consistency result, not evidence that C1 is true.

### 2.2 Constitutive-relation intervention

Change or remove a physically grounded relation `r` while keeping the declared background, boundary, time, allowed adaptation, external inputs and scoring contract fixed. This produces a different actual organizational comparison if the intervention is realizable. Capability may change, remain stable through redundancy, or be restored by an allowed substitute. Under C1 the experiential organization changes with the actual organization; experience is not a second force added after the physical change.

### 2.3 Evidence-channel intervention

Change a report, probe or investigator-access channel without changing the target-producing relation. Evidence may change while the capability does not. If the report channel is itself constitutive of the selected complete token, its experiential structure may also change; this still does not make report a basal-experience gate.

### 2.4 Boundary/resource intervention

Add an operator, oracle, external memory, compiled context-specific mechanism or retraining phase. The evaluated process and resources have changed. A restored score does not refute a dependence claim stated for the old boundary and protocol.

These four cases give the first required map: an impossible UCT-world, an organizational intervention, an epistemic intervention and a changed-contract comparison are different objects.

## 3. Finite target-relative support contract

Let `V` be a finite set of selected physically grounded relations or slots. For every admitted retained set `S subseteq V`, `pi(S)` denotes an independently specified actual transformation of the reference token. Hold fixed the actual-bearer lineage convention, interval, signature, boundary support, task/environment distribution, scoring rule and allowed compensation. Let

`F_T^pi = {S : pi(S) is admitted and succeeds on T}`.

`F_T^pi` is not inferred from a single observed score. It is a protocol-indexed family of actual or explicitly toy comparisons. Its inclusion-minimal members are `Min(F_T^pi)`.

Define the **individual unavoidable set**

`Core_T^pi = intersection Min(F_T^pi)`.

For a relation family `R subseteq V`, define **disjunctive support relevance** by

`forall M in Min(F_T^pi), M intersection R != empty`.

An inclusion-minimal `R` satisfying this condition is a minimal hitting set of the minimal supports. It says that at least one member of `R` is required in each minimal successful realization; it does not say that any particular member is universally required.

## 4. Exact propositions

### R183-T1 — individual unavoidable relevance

For every finite nonempty success family,

`intersection F_T^pi = intersection Min(F_T^pi)`.

Hence `r` is in `Core_T^pi` exactly when every successful retained set contains `r`.

**Proof.** Every minimal success is a success, so the intersection of all successes is contained in the intersection of the minima. Conversely, for any success `S`, finiteness gives a minimal successful `M subseteq S`. Any element in every minimal success lies in `M` and therefore in `S`. Apply this to every `S`. This is the CORE20261008 required-intersection result, reused rather than claimed anew.

### R183-T2 — when a single full-support deletion is enough

If `F_T^pi` is nonempty and upward closed, the full retained set `V` succeeds and

`r in Core_T^pi` iff `V minus {r}` fails.

Without upward closure this equivalence can fail: a smaller `r`-free configuration may succeed while adding another relation makes `V minus {r}` fail. Thus one lesion from the intact system identifies universal relevance only under the declared no-harm/restoration monotonicity contract.

### R183-T3 — constitutive-experiential relevance under C1

Define `TRER(r | P,I,K,M,pi,T)` only when all of the following hold simultaneously:

1. `P`, `I`, `K`, the role of `r`, the boundary and the comparison transformations are independently grounded actual-process facts;
2. `M`, `pi` and `T` are frozen and common across the comparison;
3. `F_T^pi` is nonempty and `r in Core_T^pi`;
4. C1 is applied to the same admitted actual tokens and complete signature.

Then every successful realization in the declared family contains `r`, and the tokenwise C1 isomorphism supplies an experiential structural counterpart `h_P(r)`. It is legitimate, in this precise indexed sense, to say that the experiential aspect of `r` is constitutively relevant to `T`: it is the intrinsic presentation of the same target-unavoidable organization.

This is **not** the claim that `h_P(r)` is an additional event that pushes a finished physical mechanism. Physical and experiential descriptions are two descriptions of the same constitutive organization under C1. It is also not a named feeling, a scalar amount of consciousness or a universal intelligence core.

### R183-T4 — the two nonconverses

First, an experiential structural change need not change the selected capability. A report-channel change or a bypassed-route change may alter the complete organization while both configurations remain in `F_T^pi`. C1 then permits different complete experiential types with equal selected capability, exactly as UCT III's noninjective capability fibers allow.

Second, intact capability equality does not identify `Core_T^pi`. Every nonempty upward-closed family on four relations contains the full support, so all 167 such families agree on intact success. Their individual unavoidable cores nevertheless have sizes 0,1,2,3,4 in counts 114,36,12,4,1. Intact behavior alone therefore underdetermines the dependence profile.

## 5. Three architecture twins

Reuse EI20261008's matched arithmetic organization only as an inherited witness. Hold background `B` fixed. The shared architecture needs cue route `H`; the bypass architecture needs `Q`; the redundant architecture can use either.

| Architecture | Minimal supports | Individually unavoidable | Additional disjunctive obligation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shared | `{B,H}` | `B,H` | none beyond individuals |
| Bypass | `{B,Q}` | `B,Q` | none beyond individuals |
| Redundant | `{B,H}`, `{B,Q}` | `B` | at least one of `{H,Q}` |

All can have identical intact answers. `H` is target-relevant in the shared architecture and not individually unavoidable in the redundant or bypass architecture. The redundant case does not make cue organization irrelevant; it changes the correct object from one compulsory route to a disjunctive route family.

Under an admitted actual C1 instance, each installed relation has an experiential counterpart. The table still does not identify `H` or `Q` with awareness, visual feeling, agency or human-like intelligence.

## 6. Thought-experiment map

### TE1 — the forbidden dimmer

Keep every constitutive physical relation fixed and turn experience to zero. This is outside UCT's model class, so it cannot answer the internal dependence question.

### TE2 — shared, bypass and redundant cue routes

Keep intact tasks and outputs fixed; vary which route the executor actually consumes. A matched clamp gives different capability effects. This distinguishes organization-relative relevance from output equivalence.

### TE3 — report-blind but task-preserving change

Keep `{B,H}` and the arithmetic target fixed; remove or alter a selected report relation `R`. Target success persists although the complete declared structure differs. This blocks `experience changed -> selected intelligence changed` and `report absent -> experience absent`.

### TE4 — external oracle restoration

Remove both cue routes, then let an operator provide the operation label. Performance returns, but the boundary/resource contract has changed. The operator-plus-calculator assembly is not the original context-blind calculator.

### TE5 — gradual route replacement

Replace an indispensable route one stage at a time while installing a functional alternative before removal. The individual core can migrate from old route to new route; during redundancy neither is individually unavoidable. Under C1, local processes that persist retain experience, while the larger selected organization can change. No moment of replacement establishes an exclusive owner or a scalar consciousness jump.

### TE6 — human formation implementing an agent

Let several humans and devices implement one capability. A route can be target-unavoidable for the composite contract while each persisting human and device process remains an overlapping experience-bearing token under U1/P3. Composite task relevance does not delete local experience or prove that one extra subject owns the formation.

## 7. What this answers

The unqualified sentence “experience matters for intelligence” is too coarse. UCT supports a precise conditional answer:

> For an independently admitted actual token, frozen capability/intervention contract and selected constitutive relation, the experiential counterpart of that relation is target-relative relevant exactly when the same relation is unavoidable across the admitted successful support family. Redundancy may shift relevance from an individual relation to a disjunctive family. This is constitutive identity, not causal double counting.

It follows neither that all experience is necessary for all intelligence nor that experience can be switched off while organization remains fixed. Experience may change without the selected score changing, and the same score may be realized by different relevance profiles.

## 9. Exact checks and limits

`check_relevance.py` traversed all 65,535 nonempty families on four relations and 262,140 relation-family instances. It verified the minimal-intersection equivalence. Among the 167 nonempty upward-closed families it verified the full-deletion criterion and the reported core-size distribution. It also checked the shared/bypass/redundant minimal supports, the redundant `{H,Q}` hitting set, and the report-change/equal-target witness.

These checks reproduce standard finite facts and test the application. They do not show that every retained set is physically realizable, validate C1, identify complete human organization or measure a feeling. The actual bridge and the named experiential target remain open.
