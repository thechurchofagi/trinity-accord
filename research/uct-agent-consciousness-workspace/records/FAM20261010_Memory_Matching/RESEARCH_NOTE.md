# Matching movement can remove the proposed memory contrast

FAM20261010; FAM-APPLICATION-v0.1.0. A bounded follow-up to A3V and NOV, not a new general mathematical method, validated familiarity endpoint or adopted map premise.

## Question and contribution boundary

Can retained motor memory be varied while present movement and agency are matched? In the inherited two-rate model, matching movement alone leaves a memory contrast. Matching movement plus an independent full-rank state readout removes it. This restricts one implementation of the proposed bodily-familiarity comparison; it neither makes that comparison universally impossible nor establishes a new feeling.

The mathematical ingredients are ordinary state-space reasoning. The project-specific use is an explicit feasibility stop for a route that otherwise risks interpreting an existing agency/memory effect as a new familiarity result. A3M already requires realized off-diagonal support; this record makes that obligation concrete for a nearby motor-memory model.

## Primary antecedents that narrow the question

Smith, Ghazizadeh and Shadmehr (2006), DOI 10.1371/journal.pbio.0040179, established a two-rate adaptation account and tested spontaneous recovery under error-clamp reaching. Hidden opposing memories can coexist with near-zero net motor output. This is an inherited phenomenon and model, not our discovery. The selected publisher passages also note that some expanded rival models can produce rebound.

Ishikawa, Ayabe-Kanamura and Izawa (2021), DOI 10.1016/j.isci.2021.103511, studied short-term ownership/agency recovery after virtual visuomotor distortion. Their modeled fast-memory contribution related to ownership; slow memory and condition-dependent fast contributions related to agency. These are fitted behavioral-memory associations, not direct identification of the R173 trace-consumer relation. Their few questionnaire items and short-term scope leave our selected H unresolved.

Kadambi et al. (2025 publication; 2024 accepted manuscript), DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0478-24.2024, studied recognition from point-light whole-body movements. The inspected motor-familiarity RDM is explicitly theoretical and partly identity/instruction based, with visual predictors included. Its neural fit cannot automatically become an independently measured current enactment-familiarity endpoint or a physical RetBind certificate.

Patané et al. (2026; inspected author-uploaded journal pre-proof), DOI 10.1016/j.cortex.2025.12.008, replayed recorded self/other reaches visually or through passive exoskeleton motion. The abstract reports implicit self-advantage in both modalities, and explicit advantage in vision but not proprioception. This supplies a useful recognition control and a boundary against equating task accuracy with a particular felt target. The null explicit advantage is not evidence of absent experience, and the study is not an active online action-use installation.

## Inherited model and exact formation witness

Let z=(f,s) be fast and slow adaptation states, m=f+s a scalar motor correction, and

    f_next=a_f*f+b_f*e; s_next=a_s*s+b_s*e.

The illustrative parameters are a_f=1/2, a_s=99/100, b_f=1/5, b_s=1/20. They are not fitted participant parameters. An error-clamp segment is represented by prescribed e=0 and unchanged rules. Consider x=(.4,.1) and y=(.1,.4). Both have present m=.5. Their next m values are .299 and .446, respectively. Thus one present output does not determine state composition. The script provides exact two-input formation histories from zero under the same law, but the error domain is unbounded prescribed rationals; it proves no feasible bounded human training schedule.

A cancellation illustration has f=-.4, s=.4: present m=0 yet next m=.196. It is the known opposing-memory idea, not evidence that a particular participant retains familiar bodily H.

Given known unequal rates and this exact zero-input model, two consecutive outputs determine f and s: f=(a_s*m0-m1)/(a_s-a_f), s=(m1-a_f*m0)/(a_s-a_f). If rates coincide, the distinct decompositions remain aliased for the entire illustrated zero-input trajectory. Uncertain rates, additional states, measurement noise and nonzero errors invalidate using this exact inversion as an actual biological identification result.

## Restricted general proof: matching two readouts

Define a modeled agency surrogate q=c_f*f+c_s*s. This is a mathematical readout, not agency itself or a validated participant report. For any two states, matching m and q gives

    Delta f+Delta s=0;
    c_f*Delta f+c_s*Delta s=0.

Substitute Delta s=-Delta f. If c_f!=c_s, Delta f=Delta s=0. Hence every deterministic state-only candidate j=g(f,s) is also equal. Equivalently the 2x2 matching matrix has determinant c_s-c_f. This proof holds for all real states under these premises; the 441-state rational grid is only an implementation illustration.

For q=f+2s the equal-m witness above has q=.6 versus .9. It therefore is NOT an agency-matched comparison. A difference in its hypothetical slow-state familiarity score could simply reproduce the inherited agency-like readout. If c_f=c_s, the controls are redundant and the conclusion does not hold; it also does not hold for a noninjective nonlinear matching map, a third state, different readout laws or noisy matched reports. None of these alternatives is ruled out in humans.

Do not silently demand equality of unobservable true agency from equality of reported agency. Conditioning on an observed report can introduce selection effects and does not fix the underlying state. This is a model-level matching implication, not a recommended data conditioning procedure.

## What an additional coordinate does and does not establish

In a model z=(f,s,r), let m=f+s, q=f+2s and j=r. Two states can match m/q while differing in r. The script checks this witness. But r is a deliberately assumed extra coordinate; assigning it the name RetBind or familiar mineness supplies no actual bearer, trace ancestry, frame consumer or independent endpoint. A physical additional relation must be located and tested rather than added to rescue a desired conclusion.

Our present conclusion is conditional: if familiarity is completely determined by the same two states and the two controls are injective, that design has no remaining familiarity contrast. A useful next design must either justify a remaining relation, use an independently justified noninjective control map, or acknowledge that the proposed pure separation is unavailable. Extra dimensions are not required for basal experience and are not proved necessary for every human familiarity effect.

## Thought experiments and UCT interpretation

1. Formation/adaptation: two histories leave different fast/slow mixtures and equal present correction; zero-input continuation exposes a functional difference. Current states and time-indexed relations differ. This is not a comparison of identical complete organization with arbitrarily different experiences. An external biography without a present trace changes none of the modeled states.
2. Copy/control: exact copying of f/s and their rules preserves all declared readouts, while copying only the present correction need not. A genuinely carried r can add a distinction in an extended model; an idle provenance tag or renamed questionnaire cannot. These are finite-view witnesses, not complete biological/AI signatures.
