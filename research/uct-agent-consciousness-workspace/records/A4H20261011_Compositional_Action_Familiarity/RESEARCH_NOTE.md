# Familiar elements do not settle the familiar character of an enacted sequence

A4H20261011 · v0.1.0 · Conditional conceptual refinement and finite organizational witness.
No established-map promotion, participant data, independently calibrated H_way, or novel mathematical theorem.

## 1. Mainline result

The positive retention proposal needs to say **which relation from the past is being enacted now**. A count of familiar movements, a count of practiced adjacent pairs, and a Boolean claim that some retained route is used can all omit how a present continuation depends on its longer context. Thus “this consists of movements I have often made” and “this manner of continuing is already established” are different candidate explanations of the selected action-familiarity target.

This is not a new discovery of chunking or a repeat of A3O's grain ambiguity. A3O already showed that changing route grain changes retainedness and supplied a sequence/selector construction. Here the grain is held fixed throughout: the current last action in a specified three-action context. We exhibit two training histories with identical elementary and adjacent-pair exposure but different currently read continuation relations. The comparison does not require a competing selector or a hardware provenance certificate.

The resulting positive candidate is modest: the familiar character of this continuation may concern the experience-internal organization of **enacting a retained context-to-continuation relation**, rather than a sum of familiar parts. That is a proposed interpretation of an organizational relation under C1, not a proof that the relation is H_way, a universal necessity claim, or a new consciousness threshold.

## 2. A fixed-grain construction

Let 0 and 1 denote two elementary motor commands in an abstract installed controller. Training consists of cyclic blocks:

- A: 00110011
- B: 00010111

Both contain four occurrences of each command. Both contain exactly two occurrences of each ordered adjacent pair 00,01,10,11. Cyclic wraparound is part of this definition; a finite human protocol would need explicit boundary accounting rather than silently importing this convention.

A's supported triples are 001,011,110,100, each twice. B supports every binary triple once. At the fixed target 010, elementary and pair exposure agree, but only B carries prior support for continuing 01 with 0. Likewise the contrast holds at 000,101,111. This demonstrates a difference in retained relational content; it does not declare any of those occurrences subjectively familiar.

The checker gives both controllers a currently executed consumer. If its two-action context plus required continuation is in the retained triple carrier, it uses the retained dispatch path; otherwise it uses a generic fallback. Both produce the same required last command successfully. A bypass arm keeps B's carrier stored but routes around its use. The output is again unchanged, while the internal path differs. We do not identify fewer internal stages with human speed, ease, intelligence, or H_way.

This last separation matters: an observer's triple histogram is not automatically a relation used by the system. The model stipulates an executed reader and exposes it in code. An empirical application would have to justify its actual carrier/consumer relation independently; our program does not supply a human installation or complete ontic signature.

## 5. Primary evidence and its limited role

Sakai, Kitaguchi and Hikosaka (2003; online 18 July; Experimental Brain Research 152:229–242; DOI10.1007/s00221-003-1548-8) report that trained movement sequences developed participant-specific clusters. Shuffling segments preserved performance better when those clusters were preserved. We checked the authors' abstract through PubMed, not the full methods or data. This supports treating learned relational grouping as a serious motor-organization candidate. It does not establish our two cyclic training schedules, their bodily implementation, or subjective H_way.

Chunking, higher-order sequence representations, finite-state consumers and the fact that coarse statistics omit higher-order structure are established ideas. A4H's contribution is a project-specific comparison at fixed grain that gives the bodily-familiarity bridge a more concrete positive explanatory obligation. The toy triple support is not equated with a biological chunk.

## 6. A prospective comparison that respects A4A

Randomize matched-duration formation schedules before training. Use the same predefined motor alphabet, body mapping and explicit boundary scheme; balance elementary and adjacent-pair exposure mechanically. At test present the same predeclared short continuation, with separate responses for the familiar character of the *continuation*, component familiarity, ease, ownership, agency and recognized practice. Retain “cannot judge” and content-admissibility checks from A3W/A3X.

The primary estimand is the randomized total schedule effect on J_H. Do not match or regress away post-training ease or success to advertise a pure relational effect: A4A shows that this can block the proposed route or create selection bias. A mediation claim needs a separate actual-use intervention and independently justified direct-effect restrictions. Equal endpoint success in our model is not a human fit match.

The learning probe and J_H test must be distinct. A probe of context-sensitive continuation can support a functional learning difference but cannot assign H truth, and may itself train the tested sequence. Predeclare short held-out trials and assessment order. If the schedules do not produce the anticipated retained organization, report a manipulation failure instead of rejecting B_relation. If component and continuation wording cannot be distinguished, the target grain remains unresolved.

No experiment has been run. No complete-K application or calibrated H measurement is claimed. This is a concrete theory-comparison design rather than a ready validated human protocol.
