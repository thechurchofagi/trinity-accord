# Organizational formation and experience onset: argument audit

7 October 2026, Asia/Shanghai. Research planning note, not a manuscript or new theorem. Parent: `f3189ea75215a632e3daaabe70876d707edf245c`. Four-paper baseline: A 1.2, B 1.1, C 1.0, D TA-TR-2026-24 1.0. R140 remains the common formal companion.

## 1. Question and decision

The user adds an important demand to A's All-Ancestors-Survive scenario: an organization proposed as an experience gate itself has a formation history. Unpack that history rather than treating a named mechanism as a finished binary object. Preserve this thought experiment in the main argument of any future paper.

The specific question is whether gradual formation alone rules out an experience-existence threshold more strongly than A §§8.1–8.3 already do. The answer is no under the stated premises. It makes the explanatory demand more concrete but does not supply the missing experiential regularity premise. No new deduction node is warranted. This audit records a writing decision and a failed strengthening, not a fifth-paper novelty claim.

## 2. Scenario contract

Use a declared comparison family of valid actual process tokens within the stipulated scenario. Representatives may coexist for comparison; no numerical identity of one subject across ancestors is assumed. Physical/chemical prehistory and biological ancestry are distinct parts of the construction. An actual finite ancestry, an arbitrarily refinable comparison family, and a connected mathematical parameter space are three different assumptions.

For each candidate organization specify the token boundary, environmental conditions, time interval, structural signature and the relevant changing relations. A long formation time or many individuals does not establish smallness in every structural metric. Even small steps in a declared metric do not establish continuity of every predicate defined on that metric space. Intermediate points drawn on a graph are not thereby actual or physically admissible tokens.

The original thought experiment motivates asking which differences matter and why. It does not demand identical experience throughout the family, a scalar hierarchy, erasure of local processes, or a unique exclusive subject.

### Route I — internal UCT, A:E1 (rule a19)

All premises: A:U1 and A:ALL_ANC_ACTUAL. U1 itself retains C1 and the nonempty actual carrier. For every admitted member P, U1 gives nonempty experiential organization. Applying it member by member gives E(P)=1 throughout. No continuity assumption is needed. This is an existing conditional consequence, not evidence independently establishing C1.

### Route II — structural graduality, A:E2 (rule a20)

All premises: A:C1, A:QSPACE, A:GEOMETRY and A:EPS_CHAIN. Complete physical and experiential descriptions represent the same quotient point under C1. Substitution preserves each supplied inter-point distance, hence the supplied fine chain. This does not establish that all actual evolutionary histories have arbitrarily fine steps or that experience has a scalar magnitude. A:U2_APP is the separate continuous-path route; do not silently conjoin or exchange its premises with E2.

### Route III — external conditional, A:E3 (rule a21)

All premises: A:CONNECTED_LAMBDA, A:CONT_BINARY_E and A:HUMAN_ENDPOINT. E maps the connected parameter space continuously into discrete {0,1}. Its image is connected and therefore a singleton; the endpoint selects {1}. The critical premise concerns continuity of binary experience existence itself. Physical continuity or a slowly forming mechanism is not a substitute for it. This is the published topological conditional, not a new no-threshold theorem.

On a finite list, separately assuming E(P_i)=E(P_(i+1)) at every step similarly propagates the endpoint value by equality transitivity. That elementary restatement does not justify any of those equalities. In particular, a finite ordered list is not automatically the connected domain of E3.

### Existing countermodel — A §8.3

Let a hypothetical nonnegative proxy be zero for lambda <= lambda_* and exp[-1/(lambda-lambda_*)^2] above lambda_*. It is smooth at the boundary and positive above it. Nevertheless the predicate that the proxy is positive changes there. This is A's existing logical countermodel, not a UCT experience measure or evidence that such a psychophysical law is actual.

It already grants more numerical smoothness than the user's graduality assumption. Consequently, adding a long smooth formation history without an additional relation to experiential existence does not defeat it. Conversely, this logical possibility does not explain why nature would follow that proposed onset law. Logical consistency and explanatory adequacy remain separate assessments.

## 7. Focused external comparison

Chalmers, *Absent Qualia, Fading Qualia, Dancing Qualia* (1995), author-hosted text, §§1–3 and §5: gradual replacement is used to support invariance when fine-grained functional organization is preserved. The author explicitly limits the argument's force and does not claim conclusive proof. Our formation scenario permits organizational change and tests an existence boundary; it must not borrow an invariance conclusion whose organization-preservation premise it lacks. Nor may C1's complete actual organization be equated without argument to a functional description sufficient for behavioral dispositions. Source: https://www.consc.net/papers/qualia.html . This is a narrow textual comparison, not an exhaustive priority review.

The Stanford *Sorites Paradox* entry, discussion of incremental neighbors and tolerance, was consulted for conceptual orientation: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sorites-paradox/ . It is a secondary overview, not evidence for a new result or the technical proof above. The finite chain argument is checked directly. We do not infer that phenomenal existence is linguistically vague, unknowable, or refuted merely because a tolerance-style issue arises.

## 8. What a future paper must add

An onset-free internal account is already in A; capability/type relations and positive memory examples are already in C. Simply joining them is a theoretical synthesis. Stronger independent research would require a separately justified restriction on admissible organization-to-experience laws, or a faithful comparison revealing a specific rival claim inconsistent with its own premises. Naming local stability is not an independent justification, and UCT C1 cannot be smuggled into premises offered as neutral to rivals.

The immediate next deliverable, if this direction is continued, is one scoped comparison: select a precise original recurrent-processing claim, specify whether it concerns perceptual content, access or all experience, and trace the proposed mechanism through a formation family. Use B's TFR and actual-domain requirements. Assess jointly (i) the rival's account of its proposed boundary and (ii) UCT's warrant for C1 and its account of organization-dependent experiential differences. Do not claim that every rival asserts one universal binary gate.

No new experiments, publication, model training or control-interface expansion is called for by this audit. No new manuscript is started. The current output is a reviewable argument plan with a negative result for the proposed unconditional strengthening.
