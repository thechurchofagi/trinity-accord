## Primary empirical sources checked

1. Matamala-Gomez et al. (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-97695-9. Virtual-body visual continuity modulated action-observation MEP patterns and questionnaire measures. Relevance: representation/motor coupling. Limit: report-mediated evidence and finite measured views, not complete organization or selected-feeling identity.
2. Critchley, Botan, and Ward (2021), DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0237282. N=37 rubber-hand protocol; illusion evidence in report/drift but no reliable HR, HRV, EDA, or SKNA differences, with Bayes factors supporting null differences. Relevance: counterexample to obligatory generic autonomic signature. Limit: does not delete all coupling or all body physiology.
3. Hong et al. (2026), DOI 10.1186/s12984-026-02160-x. N=23 prosthesis-like system; explicit ownership/agency, intentional binding, and proprioceptive drift changed with cues but had divergent correlations and mismatch behavior. Relevance: multi-coordinate measurement. Limit: accepted early version, small finite sample, no direct UCT installation or complete phenomenal readout.
