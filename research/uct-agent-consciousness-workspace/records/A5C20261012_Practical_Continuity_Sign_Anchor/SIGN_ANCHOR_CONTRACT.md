# Practical-continuity sign-anchor contract

Version A5C-PCSA-v0.1.0. Disabled application contract; not an admission criterion for basal experience and not an installed phenomenal bridge.

## Typed objects

- `H_pc(d,e) in {0,1}`: whether a selected target-directed action in domain `d` and episode `e` is felt as immediately carryable-through from within across the perturbation window.
- `E_j(d,e)`: fallible evidence channel `j`. A channel may be a phenomenally worded judgment, a rapid continuation choice or a physiological/kinematic marker; modality does not fix its sign.
- `Pi(d,e)`: actual route–target participation relation.
- `W(d,e)`: separately actual perturbation and compensation organization.
- `C(d,e)`: compensation endpoint under `W`.
- `S_d`: latent orientation convention in domain `d`; `S_d=1` complements the hidden `H_pc` labels and swaps every channel's class-conditionals.

`H_pc`, every `E_j`, `Pi`, `W` and `C` are different types. A report code is not the experience, and compensation is not a sign anchor.

## Exact complement result

For any binary channels whose joint law is a mixture over `H_pc`, simultaneous replacement

`H_pc' = 1-H_pc`, `P'(H_pc'=1)=1-P(H_pc=1)`, and `P'(E_j|H_pc'=h)=P(E_j|H_pc=1-h)`

preserves the complete observable distribution. Multiple channels, conditional independence and exact class-conditional transport between domains do not remove this global complement. In the A5C two-domain model, independent domain orientations give four observationally equivalent orientations; exact transport reduces them to two; one independently signed anchor in the connected transport component reduces them to one. Marginal agreement is not class-conditional transport.

This is standard latent-label symmetry. A5C claims only the scoped application and explicit countermodel, not mathematical novelty or a universal impossibility theorem.

## What would count as a sign anchor here

For this declared method, `ANCHOR_j(d)` requires all of:

1. target semantics fixed independently of the observed contrast;
2. an external warrant that `P(E_j=1|H_pc=1,d) > P(E_j=1|H_pc=0,d)`;
3. the same bearer, episode class, response code and actual measurement relation;
4. a prospective key-inversion/copy-switch failure test;
5. no use of `Pi`, `C`, success, ease, agency attribution or source number as the truth definition of `H_pc`.

A phenomenally worded judgment fixes what is asked but does not by itself warrant clause 2. An obvious physical interruption calibrates task interruption, not automatically felt practical continuity. Agreement among channels can recover a latent partition while leaving its phenomenal identity and sign open.

## Stop rule

The available fixed sources and feasible bimanual manipulation do not independently warrant clause 2. Therefore A5C does not orient `H_pc`, does not activate `B_pc^comp`, and does not treat a null/opposite `E_pc` contrast as evidence for `H_pc=0`. `Pi`, `W` and `C` remain organizational coordinates. Further controller-certificate expansion is stopped unless a genuinely independent sign anchor or new primary evidence is supplied.
