# Role-automorphism contract

## Fixed comparison

Fix one admitted actual-token binding

`b=(P,I,K,e,tau,c,t)`,

where `K` is the complete relevant typed signature at the declared token, interval and grain. Let `M_b` be its actual `K`-structure. Let `g` be a bijection of the relevant elements and ports. Calling `g` a **role reversal** does not decide whether it is an automorphism. That status must be checked against every declared relation, constant, sort, current-state point, temporal relation and actual-incidence fact in `K`.

Let `H(x)` be a proposed experience-internal role predicate. `H` is **K-internal without extra orientation data** only if it is parameter-free definable in `M_b`, or definable from declared parameters that are themselves physically grounded and transported.

## A5E-C1 — automorphism-invariance necessity

**Type:** standard model-theoretic lemma applied to one UCT bridge.

**Statement.** If `g` is an automorphism of `M_b` and `H` is parameter-free `K`-definable, then for every `x`,

`H(x) iff H(g(x))`.

If `H(x,p)` uses a declared parameter `p`, the correct covariance statement is

`H(x,p) iff H(g(x),g(p))`.

**Proof.** Automorphisms preserve the truth of every formula by induction on formula complexity. The parameterized statement follows by transporting the full assignment. This is established mathematics, not an A5E priority claim.

## A5E-C2 — bounded orientation trilemma

Assume a declared transformation `g` exchanges the candidate role-bearers and a proposed `H` is required to reverse. At least one of the following holds:

1. `g` is not an automorphism of complete `K` because a grounded asymmetric anchor, typed port, point, temporal relation or actual-incidence fact is changed;
2. `H` depends on a declared parameter that must itself be transported, so the proper law is covariance rather than parameter-fixed sign reversal;
3. `H` is not definable from the complete grounded `K` supplied for the application, leaving an additional phenomenal/semantic bridge.

The three clauses are not always mutually exclusive. The result is a diagnostic disjunction, not a positive phenomenal identification.

## Corrected prospective law

The unqualified demand “an independently verified role reversal must reverse the phenomenal comparison” is replaced by:

- **Orbit law:** a parameter-free structural target is invariant on automorphism orbits of the complete actual organization.
- **Anchored covariance law:** a target indexed by a grounded asymmetric anchor covaries when the anchor is transported. Holding the anchor fixed while exchanging other roles is a different, generally non-automorphic comparison.
- **Bridge stop rule:** if neither complete-`K` automorphism status nor the physical anchor/parameter is independently warranted, no phenomenal sign inference is licensed and `B_pc` remains open.

## Refuters

- A claimed true automorphism under which a purported parameter-free structural `H` reverses refutes its `K`-definability or the automorphism claim.
- A claimed grounded role reversal that preserves every complete-`K` fact is not a grounded asymmetric intervention; it is a symmetry operation.
- An analyst's names, response keys or subject labels cannot supply the missing anchor unless they are actual relations in `K`.
- A parameter fixed in the analysis but moved in the physical comparison creates a false non-invariance.

Nothing here makes self-model, introspection, report, language, success, unique ownership or continued control necessary for basal experience.
