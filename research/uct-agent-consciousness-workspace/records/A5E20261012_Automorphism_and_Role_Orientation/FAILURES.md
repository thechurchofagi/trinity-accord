# Failures and rejected moves

1. **Rejected: require sign reversal under every role swap.** A true automorphism preserves every parameter-free definable target; unconditional reversal is inconsistent with the structural premise.
2. **Rejected: infer definability from invariance.** Automorphism invariance is necessary, not sufficient in arbitrary structures. A5E proves no unrestricted converse.
3. **Rejected: let an analyst's role name break the symmetry.** A name, response key or conceptual-I sentence is not an actual `K`-relation unless physically installed and witnessed.
4. **Rejected: hold a physical parameter fixed in notation while moving its referent.** Parameterized formulas covary only when the parameter assignment is transported.
5. **Rejected: use the orbit law as `B_pc`.** It constrains any structural target but does not identify the selected familiar-mineness category or its positive sign.
6. **No actual asymmetric anchor found.** Internal and external sources provide candidate relations and methodological constraints, not a same-instance complete-`K` installation with an independently fixed phenomenal failure outcome.
7. **The finite checker is not a theory proof.** It verifies a declared two-object witness family and counts; the standard lemma supplies the general mathematical implication.
