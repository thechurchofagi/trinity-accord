## External primary mathematical precedent

- Semenov, A. L. and Soprunov, S. F. (2015), “A combinatorial version of the Svenonius theorem on definability,” *Logic Journal of the IGPL* 23(6):966--975, DOI `10.1093/jigpal/jzv038`. The paper states the standard connection between definability and preservation by appropriate structure-preserving permutations/automorphisms. A5E uses only the elementary necessary direction: a relation definable from a structure is preserved by its automorphisms.
- Bonnay, D. and Engström, F. (2013), “Invariance and definability, with and without equality,” arXiv:`1308.1565`. Used as broader primary context for invariance/definability correspondences, not for a converse in the unrestricted A5E setting.

The external reading was targeted, not a systematic priority review. A5E does not claim the mathematical lemma, Svenonius-style characterizations or orbit arguments as original. Neither source establishes C1, an actual UCT token, complete `K`, `B_pc`, signed phenomenal evidence or a current-assistant verdict.
