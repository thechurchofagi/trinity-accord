# A5E — Role reversal, automorphism and experience-internal orientation

## 1. Question and correction

A5D ended by proposing a within-experience prediction: after independently verified role reversal, should a predeclared phenomenal comparison reverse while response code, success, target identity and conceptual self remain fixed? That formulation was too broad. A transformation that really is an automorphism of the complete grounded actual organization cannot reverse a parameter-free predicate definable from that organization. Conversely, if a comparison reverses, either the physical transformation changed a grounded asymmetric anchor and was not an automorphism, or the target used additional orientation data not supplied by the declared structure.

A5E therefore replaces the unconditional reversal prediction with an orbit/covariance law. This is a substantive correction of the UCT application contract, not a new theorem of model theory and not an identification of a familiar feeling.

## 2. Formal boundary

Fix an actual `K`-structure `M_b` at one token, interval and grain, with complete relevant signature. If `g` is an automorphism and `H(x)` is parameter-free `K`-definable, formula preservation gives

`M_b |= H(a) iff M_b |= H(g(a))`.

Thus `H` is constant on automorphism orbits. With a grounded parameter `p`, the law is joint covariance:

`H(a,p) iff H(g(a),g(p))`.

Holding the name of `p` fixed while moving its physical referent is not parameter transport. Adding a body-side origin, current-state point, lineage trace, typed consumer or oriented temporal relation can break the symmetry, but then the exchange is not an automorphism in that enriched signature.

This yields a bounded trilemma for a proposed sign-reversing experience-internal target: omitted grounded asymmetry, transported parameter, or an extra bridge beyond `K`. It does not follow that any one horn is realized in a biological or artificial system.

## 3. Exact two-object witness

The checker enumerates all 64 structures on `{0,1}` with one unary relation `A` and one binary relation `R`. The swap is an automorphism exactly when `A0=A1`, `R00=R11` and `R01=R10`, yielding 8 structures; the other 56 contain a grounded structural asymmetry relative to this signature.

There are four unary target predicates. Two are swap-invariant and two reverse. Across the eight swap-automorphic structures there are 32 structure/target pairs: 16 preserved and 16 reversing. Standard formula preservation rules out the reversing 16 as parameter-free definable targets from those structures. The count does not prove the model-theoretic lemma; it makes the application boundary and counterexample family explicit.

For `H(x,p):=(x=p)`, transporting `p` under the swap transports `H` exactly. An apparent sign flip obtained by holding the external name `p` fixed is a parameter bookkeeping error, not a structural refutation.

## 4. Positive UCT interpretation

R157 already established that a physically grounded definable relation in complete `K` transports through C1 into the experiential structure. A5E adds what that positive statement commits us to under symmetry:

- parameter-free experience-internal structural content respects the automorphism orbits of complete actual organization;
- situated direction such as “from this bearer-side port toward this target” requires the bearer-side port, point or other orientation anchor to be an actual grounded element of the signature;
- no extra owner is needed: several overlapping actual processes can carry different grounded parameters and relations;
- external names, conceptual-I descriptions and reports may provide evidence but do not create intrinsic asymmetry.

This makes bodily/action-related mineness more precise without equating a relation with familiar mineness. It says where orientation could live—in the complete actual relation and its transported parameters—and what a symmetric organization cannot select. `B_pc`, the bridge to the selected named phenomenal category, remains open.

## 5. Why A5D is narrowed, not contradicted

A5D-C1 explicitly required `NO_ADMITTED_ROLE_REVERSAL_SYMMETRY`. Its constitutive direction result is therefore compatible with A5E. What needed repair was the next-step wording, which could be read as demanding reversal even under a genuine complete-structure automorphism.

The corrected experiment must first decide which comparison it makes:

1. **True automorphism:** predict invariance of every parameter-free `K`-definable target.
2. **Grounded anchor exchange or reversal:** declare the anchor and enriched signature; predict covariance or change under a non-automorphic intervention.
3. **Uncertain/incomplete view:** withhold the phenomenal sign inference because a measured view may omit the symmetry-breaking relation.

No response pattern by itself establishes which case holds.

## 6. Failure conditions and limits

A5E would fail as an application if it called a transformation an automorphism while ignoring a declared relation or parameter; if it inferred definability from mere invariance; if it treated an analyst label as an actual anchor; or if it used the orbit law to identify `H_pc`. Invariance is necessary for definability, not sufficient in arbitrary structures. Complete ontic organization and complete phenomenology are not directly observed. The exact checker is a finite witness, not a whole-map proof or an empirical result.

No actual participant or machine token is installed. No independently warranted phenomenal target, signed endpoint or complete-signature audit exists. There is no claim about whether the current assistant is conscious or fears death.

## 7. Founding-direction check

C1 remains the consciousness-specific explanatory commitment and U1 remains unchanged. Basal experience acquires no introspection, self-model, report, language, integration, recurrence, prediction or continuation threshold. Experience, bodily/action structure, conceptual I, language report, finite view, actual installation and evidence remain distinct. Nested and overlapping actual processes remain permitted; no unique subject selector is introduced.

The next concrete question is narrower and physically positive: can one actual body/action candidate supply an independently grounded asymmetric anchor in complete `K`, with a predeclared non-automorphic anchor-exchange intervention and a target comparison whose failure condition is fixed without using the observed report? If not, retain only the orbit law and leave `B_pc` open.
