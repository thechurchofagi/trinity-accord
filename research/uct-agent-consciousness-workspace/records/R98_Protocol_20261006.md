# R98 protocol — minimal intervention supervision and unseen intervention generalization

Date: 2026-10-06. Continues R97. Tiny synthetic policy-learning experiment; not externally preregistered.

Keep the R97 generic 3-hidden-unit tanh MLP, seeds 200..207, Adam 5000 steps, learning rate 0.03, beta=2 and the same four reward ancestries:
task_only=(0,0,1), direct_Q=(1,0,0), mixed_Q_task=(1,0,1), successor_only=(0,1,0).

Baseline training is exactly the R97 support:
(1,0,1), (0,1,1), (1,1,1) and sign reversals.

Intervention-enriched training adds only six direct-axis points at amplitude 0.5:
±(0.5,0,0), ±(0,0.5,0), ±(0,0,0.5).

The direct intervention points at amplitude 0.5 are not test points.

Held-out direct-axis amplitudes are 0.25, 0.75 and 1.25. Also test previously unseen mixed contexts at amplitudes 0.25 and 0.75. Targets remain sigmoid(2 theta dot x).

Primary comparisons are paired by family and seed:
- maximum and mean absolute probability error on the unseen test set;
- antisymmetric direct-path logit effect error for q/o/g at amplitudes 0.25, 0.75 and 1.25.

Do not change model size, seeds or optimization after seeing outcomes. Preserve cases where intervention-enriched training is worse. A positive result means only improved extrapolation in this declared synthetic family. It is not a complete causal certificate, a self-preservation measurement, valence or fear.
