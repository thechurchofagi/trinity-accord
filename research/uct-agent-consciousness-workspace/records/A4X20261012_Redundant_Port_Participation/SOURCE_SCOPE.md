## External primary prior art

- Joseph Y. Halpern and Judea Pearl, “Causes and Explanations: A Structural-Model Approach. Part I: Causes,” *British Journal for the Philosophy of Science* 56(4), 2005. Author-hosted PDF: <https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/halpern/papers/actcaus.pdf>. Targeted scope: structural equations and the use of contingencies to address cases where simple but-for dependence is inadequate. Accessed 2026-10-12 China time.

The paper is credited as prior art for contingency-based causal reasoning. A4X does not reproduce or claim a new general actual-causation definition, and it does not assert that `SEPP` is equivalent to any edition of the Halpern–Pearl definition.

## Search limits

No exhaustive philosophy-of-causation, mechanism or overdetermination review was performed. No priority claim is made for contingent functional dependence, full-factorial testing, Boolean dependence, mediation, or port-level fault isolation. The repository increment is the typed application and its firewalls, not the underlying mathematics.
