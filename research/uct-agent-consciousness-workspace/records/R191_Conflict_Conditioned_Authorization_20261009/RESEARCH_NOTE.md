# Online conflict-conditioned authorization at a fixed practical center

**Research ID:** R191-OCCA-20261009  
**Result:** OCCA-RESULT-v0.1.0  
**Status:** scoped working result; disabled map candidate; no actual or phenomenal application discharged

## 1. Question and inherited boundary

Fix one admitted actual episode, interval, complete signature, selected activity target `tau`, and one instance satisfying the inherited R190 practical-centering plus bearer-directed consequence-coupling profile (`PC_tau = BDCC_tau = 1`). Can the internal route by which rival action tendencies affect authorization explain something positive about bodily/action self-organization without simply renaming deliberateness or identifying felt endorsement?

The question is deliberately narrower than consciousness, intelligence, agency, ownership, or familiar mineness. UCT C1 remains the consciousness-specific explanatory axiom. The construction below adds no introspection, report, language, integration, recursion, predictive success, self-model, or continuation-control requirement to basal experience.

## 2. Two candidate relations

### 2.1 Failed weak relation

`WCU_tau` says only that mutually exclusive action-route carriers are present and some actual consumer receives them. This is too weak. A system can commit to an action first and later consume a conflict trace merely to rationalize the completed choice. The consumer and rivals are real, yet they did not organize authorization.

### 2.2 Repaired current-episode relation

`OCCA_tau` (online conflict-conditioned authorization) holds only if, within the same episode and target contract:

1. at least two actually instantiated, mutually exclusive route carriers concern `tau`;
2. an actually installed authorization consumer uses their conflict state;
3. that use occurs before the relevant commitment/completion boundary; and
4. under the predeclared admissible intervention family and fixed background, changing the rival carrier changes which route the consumer authorizes.

This is a physical/organizational relation. It is not defined by a report, a desired feeling, the name “deliberation,” or a successful diagnostic. Evidence for it still requires independent carrier, consumer, timing, intervention, boundary, and same-instance premises.

### 2.3 History-expanded relation

`LGRU_tau` (lineage-grounded revision use) adds a historical requirement: an actual earlier same-lineage conflict occasion must have revised an authorization disposition, the relevant carrier must continue through the declared lineage relation, and the current episode must actually use that continued disposition. A copied memory value or imported lookup table does not by itself establish the earlier revision occurrence.

LGRU is not a new general theorem about history. It is a restricted action-authorization application of R173's retained-history architecture and must retain R173's carrier, continuation, consumer, and actual-occurrence conditions.

All 15 declared checks pass. The crucial results are:

- WCU admits the post-hoc rationalizer, so it cannot characterize online authorization.

The finite result establishes existence and non-entailment only inside the displayed construction. It is not a population estimate or a proof about every biological or artificial system.

## 4. Scope fork and the main result

The apparent conflict between “the reflex twin is identical” and “formation history separates it” disappears when signatures are typed.

- Under the **present-episode signature**, deliberate online adjudication and the frozen reflex table are twins for all declared OCCA fields.
- Under the **history-expanded signature**, they differ because only one includes an actual earlier same-lineage revision occurrence.
- Matching a memory value does not recreate that occurrence.

This scope fork is useful but limited. It shows where an actual-history question enters; it does not show that the history difference is experienced as authorship or familiar mineness. The adaptive-reflex case preserves OCCA and LGRU while withholding the “deliberate” label, so neither relation identifies deliberateness, voluntary endorsement, felt trying, agency, ownership, familiar mineness, subject count, or an exclusive owner.

## 5. Relation to prior work and earlier UCT rounds

Selection conflict and fluency as prospective cues to sense-of-agency judgments are established in Chambon and Haggard (2012) and Sidarus et al. (2017). Automation/involvement effects on implicit and explicit agency measures are established in Berberian et al. (2012). R191 does not claim discovery of those effects. Those results motivate a candidate evidence route; they do not establish OCCA installation or make agency reports identical to episode structure.

R173 already established that present values do not recover actual retained history and that actual carrier continuation plus consumer use matter. R184 already used a reflex twin to limit a retained-target route. R190 established the fixed PC+BDCC background and its deliberate/reflex insufficiency. R191's net addition is narrower: it defines and stress-tests a precommit authorization-use relation, records the exact post-hoc timing failure and repair, and makes the present-signature/history-signature fork explicit at the conflict-to-authorization interface.

## 6. UCT interpretation

If an actual instance independently discharges every carrier, consumer, timing, intervention, boundary, same-lineage, and same-instance premise, OCCA and (where applicable) LGRU describe positive organization inside the experience under C1. They may help explain how a selected activity becomes practically centered and how prior conflict formation constrains a current authorization route.

They do not add a gate for basal experience under U1. They do not supply the missing bridge from organization to “this action is familiarly mine.” The correct current outcome is therefore a positive organizational account plus a precise non-identification result, not a phenomenal identity claim.

## 7. Thought experiments

1. **Frozen table / online adjudicator.** Hold current carriers, consumer, timing, intervention response, target, report, substrate, and outcome fixed. Vary only whether the authorization disposition arose from earlier conflict-driven revision or was installed as a frozen table. This tests current versus historical signatures.
2. **Adaptive reflex / deliberate controller.** Hold both OCCA and actual lineage revision fixed while varying only the “deliberate” source label. This blocks the inference from online revision organization to felt endorsement.
3. **Copied memory.** Give the frozen system a byte-identical record saying “I revised after conflict.” The memory value changes; the actual formation occurrence does not. This tests value/occurrence substitution.
4. **Post-hoc narrator.** Hold rival carriers and a consumer fixed, but move consumption across the commitment boundary. This isolates why timing is constitutive of authorization use rather than merely evidential.
5. **Yoked action.** Match the selected output while severing the conflict-to-authorization sensitivity. This separates outcome agreement from actual route use.
