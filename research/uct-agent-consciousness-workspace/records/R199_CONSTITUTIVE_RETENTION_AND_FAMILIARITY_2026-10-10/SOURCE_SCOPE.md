## Use and limits

The sources motivate current retained functional organization, dissociable memory components, actual long-term route use and selected reports. They do not prove C1, numerical lineage, complete-signature equality, a universal self coordinate, or phenomenal `H`. Cuberovic et al. is a single-participant mixed-method case study; no population-general inference is made. Source coverage is targeted rather than exhaustive.
