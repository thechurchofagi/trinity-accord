# Target persistence after obstruction
## A route-sensitive organization result and a limit on identifying felt trying

**R184 v0.1, 8 October 2026.** Unpublished UCT research record. The set-family reasoning is inherited from CORE/R183 and the controller algebra is elementary. The increment is a bounded body/action application with explicit stopping results, not a new general theorem of consciousness.

## 1. Question and inherited boundary

R181's `PC_tau` describes current target-bound directive use and closure. R182 adds a physically shared action-reference trace and the blocked coordinate `EAC_tau`. R183 then distinguishes an individually unavoidable relation from a disjunctive route family. The remaining R182 question is whether a target-specific relation spanning obstruction and release adds information beyond the state during obstruction.

Fix one bearer candidate `P`, interval `I=[t0,t2]`, complete-signature candidate `K`, overt target `tau`, target-binding instance `TBI`, declared obstruction at `t1`, release at `t2`, boundary/resource contract, and intervention protocol before examining any report. At `t2` there is **no newly issued directive**. The selected capability is:

`T_resume(tau)`: after release, the installed policy resumes a command for the original `tau`, for both values of `tau`, without a release-time directive.

This target is narrower than agency, voluntariness, ownership, persistence of a numerical person, or felt trying.

## 2. Typed relation: retained target route

For a route `r`, define `RTR_r(tau | P,I,K,TBI)` only if all of the following are actual relations of the same declared token and interval:

1. at `t0`, the independently fixed `TBI` binds `tau` to the overt consumer path;
2. `r` carries or makes available a target-discriminating state through the blocked interval;
3. after release, the same installed action policy actually consumes `r` in producing the resumed command;
4. no new directive event occurs at `t2`;
5. in a predeclared route-isolation intervention, changing the carried target changes the resumed target, while removing the only retained route prevents target-correct resumption;
6. route membership is a physical/path fact; a successful diagnostic test, analyst label or later report is evidence about membership, not membership itself.

Two route types are compared:

- `M`: an internal continuing target carrier in the declared bearer;
- `Q`: a continuing target-specific environmental cue and its admitted sensor/consumer path inside the fixed boundary contract.

`Q` is not a new directive at release. If the cue is re-created by an operator at `t2`, the episode violates the fixed target and becomes a changed-resource comparison. Biological material, introspection and language are not clauses of `RTR`.

## 3. Four architectures and the exact support result

The executable construction has four policies: M-only, Q-only, redundant with M priority under conflict, and redundant with Q priority under conflict. In deletion-only comparisons, every retained route carries the correct target. The minimal successful supports are:

| Architecture | Minimal support(s) | Individual core | Minimal disjunctive obligation |
|---|---|---|---|
| M-only | `{M}` | `{M}` | `{M}` |
| Q-only | `{Q}` | `{Q}` | `{Q}` |
| Redundant, M priority | `{M}`, `{Q}` | empty | `{M,Q}` |
| Redundant, Q priority | `{M}`, `{Q}` | empty | `{M,Q}` |

### R184-C1 — release-time resumption adds a temporal relation to the selected view

In the declared equations, the obstructed-time tuple can be identical while the release-time command differs according to whether an `RTR` route survives. Therefore the selected R181/R182 values at `t1` do not determine `T_resume` unless the spanning relation or an equivalent sufficient completion is included.

This is a finite nonidentification result. It does not say that every real resumption requires either named route; an unmodelled route is a direct counterexample to universality.

### R184-C2 — relevance is architecture-relative

Applying R183's already established support definitions, M is individually unavoidable only in M-only, Q only in Q-only, and neither is individually unavoidable in either redundant architecture. In both redundant architectures every minimal success intersects `{M,Q}`. Thus target persistence may be individually or disjunctively relevant without selecting a unique privileged carrier.

No new hitting-set theorem is claimed. This is the promised body/action instantiation of the distinction.

### R184-C3 — intact recovery does not identify actual use

When M and Q both carry `tau`, the two redundant policies have the same intact resumed command. Under a predeclared conflict intervention `(M=tau,Q=1-tau)`, M-priority follows M and Q-priority follows Q. Equal intact recovery therefore does not establish which route the policy actually uses under conflict, and a test does not create the use relation it detects.

The conflict is a definition test, not a proposed human manipulation. If the perturbation changes attention, policy, target binding or boundary resources, it is not an isolated route test.

### R184-C4 — the reflex twin survives

Cross deliberate/reflex, report/no-report and biological/prosthetic labels while fixing the complete selected route equations. All 256 declared baseline rows retain the same resumed command within each structural configuration. A reflex-labelled controller can preserve the target through obstruction and resume it without reissue exactly as a deliberate-labelled controller can.

Consequently, `RTR` plus `EAC` does not identify felt trying, effort, endorsement or familiar mineness. This is the required stopping result. It neither says the twins have the same complete organization nor says their experiences are equal or unequal.

## 4. Conditional UCT interpretation

Assume an independently admitted actual `P/I/K/TBI`, actual route membership, fixed comparison contract and C1 for each token. Then the target-spanning carrier, consumer and release relations have experiential structural counterparts under the tokenwise isomorphism. If one route is target-unavoidable in the fixed support family, its counterpart is target-relative constitutively relevant in R183's exact sense. If only `{M,Q}` is unavoidable, the correct statement is disjunctive: every successful admitted realization contains at least one corresponding route relation.

This provides a positive experience-internal description of *how the action target remains practically available across obstruction*. It does not add experience as a second causal force and does not make target persistence a condition for basal experience. A process without `RTR` remains within U1 whenever it is an admitted actual process.

The interpretation also does not require one exclusive owner. The internal carrier, sensor route, effector loop and wider organism may be nested or overlapping actual tokens. P3 says embedding does not erase a continuing token; P6 distinguishes token, type and lineage. Which token contains which relation is an actual membership question, not settled by the action label.

## 5. Thought experiments

### TE1 — blocked reach, internal retention

Fix target, body/action reference, obstruction and report. Remove the obstacle without another instruction. A continuing target carrier is consumed and the reach resumes. Swapping that carrier during route isolation swaps the resumed target. Role: positive definition witness for M; not evidence of felt effort.

### TE2 — the painted target remains

Keep the same obstruction and release but clear internal retention while a stable target cue remains in the admitted sensor-policy loop. The action resumes correctly. Role: counterexample to individual necessity of M and to equating persistence with an autobiographical memory.

### TE3 — two correct routes, conflicting probes

With both routes correct, M-priority and Q-priority policies are behaviorally identical. Set only Q to the opposite target under a predeclared intervention. Their resumed commands diverge. Role: distinguishes actual consumer rule from intact score and from evidence-channel success.

### TE4 — deliberate/reflex twin

Fix `TBI`, `ART`, `EAC`, retained route, release dynamics and output. Change only an external deliberate/reflex classification. Role: defeats the proposed inference from target persistence to felt trying. The label may correlate with wider real organization, but that organization must be named rather than imported.

### TE5 — copy/rewire/memory boundary

Copy the internal target bit into a disconnected register while the original route still drives resumption. The copied value does not become `RTR`. Rewire the policy to consume the copy and it can become `RTR`. Role: separates same content, causal use and numerical token identity; inherited from TA25's attachment discipline, not a new copying result.
