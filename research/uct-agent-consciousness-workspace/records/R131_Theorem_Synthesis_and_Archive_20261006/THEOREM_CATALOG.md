## Historical results to retain with correct priority

PLT §§753–755 already contain even/odd-parity blindness, its exact one-dimensional ambiguity family and a q-ary extension. §767 contains a high-order intervention counterexample. The X01–X38 drafts contain common-witness, recoding, redundancy and repair constructions. These are historical sources and regression cases, not newly discovered consequences of R131. Their original complete calculations have not all been re-audited this round. The selected probability proofs were read; calling them “phenomenal” does not supply an actual complete-type premise.

## Obligations not yet promoted to proved theorems

- A nontrivial resource/time/port-compatible joint-realization criterion. Separate existence does not imply a shared witness, but that quantifier observation alone is not the desired new composition theory.
- Minimal separating interfaces for a declared restricted model class, with admissible adaptive interventions and robust error analysis. The unrestricted n-1 scalar-expectation bound need not remain necessary.
- Grounded biological/AI transition and intervention transport, complete constitutive support, valence orientation and subject/continuation claims. No finite certificate closes these by itself.

## Suggested paper use

Use families 5–7, 11–14 as the main mathematical chain. State A's conditional interpretation and B's bridge discipline once, then use selected historical cases as explicit counterexamples. Put the full catalog and remaining case matrix in appendices. A standalone research paper needs a focused advance beyond the already published synthesis; a historical-novelty review is still incomplete.
