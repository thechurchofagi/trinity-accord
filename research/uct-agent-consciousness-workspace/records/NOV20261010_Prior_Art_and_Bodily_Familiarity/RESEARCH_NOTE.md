## 4. A discriminating next step, not another certificate taxonomy

The open comparison remains A3V's B_ret versus B_fit: does a currently used retained body/action relation, or current bodily congruence, better predict an independently specified familiar bodily quality? Neither simply follows from C1. Actual RetBind is stronger than number of training trials; current fit is stronger than task success. Both must be independently grounded at fixed route grain and actual frame consumer.

Borrow P6's PCE/NCE performance reversal as a challenge to a proposed proxy, rather than claiming a new fluency dissociation. Before any target comparison, test whether the proposed bodily-familiarity judgment merely behaves like the inherited control rating or a speed/effort score. A priming manipulation can itself change the actual selection route, so an unchanged training label does not establish unchanged RetBind. If joint retention/fit binding is unavailable, that contrast is invalid for the proposed bridge.

The candidate comparison question is: “Compared with the other episode, did this bodily action feel more like your usual, familiar way of acting?” This wording is a proposal for J_H, not a signed measurement of H. It needs construct validation and an independently justified positive interpretation; neither a verbal nor a nonverbal response format solves that problem. Separate control/agency judgment, cue recognition, action ease and ownership wording are competing explanations, not definitions of H.

Two claims must be declared before outcomes: (a) at fixed present fit, B_ret predicts an ordered effect of actual retained-route use; (b) at fixed retained-route use, B_fit predicts an ordered effect of present fit. The stronger pure versions predict invariance to the other factor; broader versions need explicit prediction sets before data. Simply allowing every nuisance to explain every result would remove discriminating content. Agreement with either proposal would support its particular auxiliary bridge, not uniquely confirm UCT over all other consciousness theories.
