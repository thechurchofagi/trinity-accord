# A3N — A route–fluency crossing is not self-interpreting

## Result in one sentence

A retained route can be crossed with **external kinematic fluency** by perturbing an established action and guiding a novel one, but this does not yet cross retained-route use with **internal selection fluency**; moreover, the auxiliary perturbation/guidance signature can fit every binary endpoint table unless direct paths are constrained, so a bodily-familiarity result requires a signed fallible endpoint, replicated realizations and explicit rival measurements.

## 1. Typed correction to A3M

Fix one bearer, interval, body/action target and complete application signature.

- `R=1`: the episode actually consumes a retained route descended from an earlier same-bearer body-frame occurrence. Training labels, fitted decoders and idle memory do not set `R`.
- `L_sel=1`: action selection through the installed route meets a predeclared latency/conflict/automaticity criterion.
- `L_kin=1`: externally measured trajectory execution meets a predeclared smoothness/error criterion.
- `Q=1`: the cue meets a separate familiarity criterion.
- `J_H`: a positively worded, response-key-tracked, fallible comparative judgment about the selected experience-internal target: felt already-establishedness of acting through this body.
- `R_J`: the button, word or gesture carrying `J_H`; it is not the experience.

A3M's coarse `L` joined selection and execution. That is harmless in its abstract Boolean result but unsafe in a physical application. Robotic guidance can impose low-error kinematics while leaving the participant's selection effort, efference, proprioception and causal contribution changed. Thus a guided novel action can establish `R=0,L_kin=1`; it does **not** by itself establish `R=0,L_sel=1`.

## 2. Stable claims

### A3N-C1 — fluency-type refinement

- **Type:** application typing correction.
- **Full proposition:** external kinematic fluency and internal selection fluency are different coordinates. An intervention that changes one does not set the other without an independently specified measurement and causal contract.
- **Domain:** physical applications of A3M's route-versus-fluency comparison.
- **All premises:** fixed bearer/time/target; separate `L_sel` and `L_kin` criteria; no inference from task success to either coordinate.
- **Argument/source:** guidance can enforce a desired kinematic pattern and reduce training error, while selection-fluency work shows nearby self-related judgments can vary independently of motor performance. These are established mechanisms, not UCT inventions.
- **Counterexample:** a robot carries a hesitant first-use movement smoothly. `L_kin=1` while `L_sel` may remain 0.
- **Status:** ADOPTED_APPLICATION_CORRECTION; no completed-map change.
- **Thought-experiment role:** blocks an external-performance proxy from being renamed bodily familiarity.

### A3N-C2 — partial physical crossing construction

- **Type:** constructive design proposition.
- **Full proposition:** the four logical `(R,L_kin)` cells can be targeted as follows: novel unsupported action `(0,0)`; novel robot-guided action `(0,1)`; retained route under unexpected force/trajectory perturbation `(1,0)`; retained unperturbed action `(1,1)`.
- **Domain:** a declared robotic reaching/steering installation, not arbitrary action.
- **All premises:** `R` is independently evidenced as actual same-episode use; assistance and perturbation fidelity are instrumented; the kinematic criterion is fixed before endpoint inspection; target and cue are held or modeled.
- **Proof:** direct construction, reproduced in `check_crossing.py`.
- **Counterexample boundary:** guidance may substitute a different causal route; perturbation may trigger a compensatory route; reused general skills may make the purportedly novel route partly retained. Any such case invalidates the intended `R` assignment rather than being averaged away.
- **Status:** PHYSICALLY_PLAUSIBLE_PROTOCOL; no actual installation or human data supplied.
- **Thought-experiment role:** supplies the missing off-diagonal candidates while exposing route-granularity debt.

### A3N-C3 — auxiliary-signature alias theorem

- **Type:** exact finite nonidentification proposition.
- **Full proposition:** if each `(R,L_kin)` cell is realized by a unique auxiliary intervention signature `I`, an unrestricted nuisance-only function `Z=D(I)` reproduces every one of the 16 binary endpoint tables. Therefore observing the pure-route table `(0,0,1,1)` does not by itself identify a route effect.
- **Domain:** four-cell binary endpoint studies with cell-unique auxiliary signatures.
- **All premises:** no restriction on the direct path `I→Z`; one observed auxiliary signature per cell.
- **Proof:** assign `D(I_c)=Z_c` for each cell; exhaustive verification finds 16/16 tables fitted.
- **Counterexample boundary:** independently justified exclusion restrictions, replicated physical realizations, or quantitative direct-effect bounds add information not present in the theorem.
- **Status:** PROVED_FINITE; elementary lookup argument, no mathematical novelty claim.
- **Thought-experiment role:** shows why a smooth guided novice and a perturbed expert cannot alone settle `B_ret` versus `B_fit`.

### A3N-C4 — replicated-mechanism invariance result

- **Type:** exact restricted-model proposition and protocol guard.
- **Full proposition:** in the declared class `Z(R,L,k)=B(R,L) xor d(k)`, observing every `(R,L)` cell under two physically different mechanisms `k=0,1` with within-cell invariance forces `d(0)=d(1)`. Enumeration checks 64 parameterizations; exactly 32 are invariant and all have a constant direct-effect bit. The observed table then recovers `B` only up to that constant, which still requires independent positive endpoint orientation.
- **Domain:** deterministic binary XOR nuisance class with full replicated support.
- **All premises:** the same target and endpoint semantics across mechanisms; both mechanisms physically realize the same `R,L` cell; the XOR form is defended; response coding is fixed.
- **Proof:** equality `B xor d(0)=B xor d(1)` implies `d(0)=d(1)`; exhaustive checker confirms it.
- **Counterexample boundary:** constant, interactive, unmeasured or semantically changing direct effects remain. Partial replication supplies only partial constraints.
- **Status:** PROVED_FINITE_RESTRICTED; actual direct-effect form and full replication open.
- **Thought-experiment role:** replaces the impossible demand for a magic confound-free manipulation with a falsifiable but explicitly limited invariance test.

### A3N-C5 — signed endpoint application contract

- **Type:** conditional UCT application, not a theorem of consciousness.
- **Full proposition:** `B_ret` is favored over pure cue-, selection-fluency- and kinematic-fluency rivals only if a predeclared positive `J_H` target is independently interpreted, its report code is tracked and fallible reliability defended, actual `R` and both fluency coordinates are separately measured, the route-like contrast replicates across at least two intervention realizations or a direct-path bound is justified, and the same bearer/time/signature/target premises jointly hold. C1 may then interpret the matched organizational difference as a structure within experience; none of these application premises is discharged here.
- **Domain:** reporting-adult calibration of one selected bodily/action familiarity target.
- **All premises:** R200's semantic firewall; A3N-C1–C4; actual route-use evidence distinct from use; response-key, success, effort, cue familiarity, agency and ownership controls; no transport beyond the calibrated domain without a new bridge.
- **Proof/source:** conditional synthesis of the exact limits and UCT C1.
- **Counterexample boundary:** if `J_H` follows assistance, perturbation, success, `L_sel`, `L_kin`, ownership or response-key reversal, reject or narrow the route-only bridge. A null result is endpoint/manipulation failure, not absence of experience.
- **Status:** OPEN_CONDITIONAL_APPLICATION.
- **Thought-experiment role:** identifies a positive, criticizable route from actual organization to an experience-internal self-related coordinate without making report or familiarity a basal gate.

## 3. Endpoint and installation design

The primary forced comparison is fixed before results: **“In which interval did acting feel more like an already-established way of acting through this body?”** Response sides and motor keys are counterbalanced and reversed in a calibration block. Separate scales measure cue recognition, selection ease/automaticity, physical effort, trajectory smoothness, success, agency, ownership and confidence. These are rivals/covariates, not alternative phrasings of `J_H`.

Two mechanisms should be sought for each critical off-diagonal cell—for example distinct mechanical and visual perturbations for retained-but-kinematically-poor trials, and trajectory-channel versus endpoint-only guidance for novel-but-kinematically-good trials. Equality across them tests the declared mechanism-varying direct-effect class; disagreement is a falsifier. The stronger `R≠L_sel` contrast remains open. A congruent first-use mapping may be fast because it reuses general skills, but then route granularity and overlap must be declared rather than calling the entire route novel.

## 4. Primary-source constraints

- Scheidt et al. (2000, DOI `10.1152/jn.2000.84.2.853`) used mechanical channel constraints that minimized kinematic error while revealing persistent motor output. This supports separating measured kinematics from retained motor organization; it does not identify `R`, `L_sel` or `H`.
- Marchal-Crespo and Reinkensmeyer (2008, DOI `10.3200/JMBR.40.6.545-557`) reported that robotic guidance limited errors and could improve short-term steering learning. The apparatus supports a guided high-performance candidate, but the paper does not establish experience-internal bodily familiarity.
- Smith, Ghazizadeh and Shadmehr (2006, DOI `10.1371/journal.pbio.0040179`) showed interacting adaptive processes with different timescales in reaching. This warns that “retained route” cannot be treated as one homogeneous memory bit.
- Chambon and Haggard (2012, DOI `10.1016/j.cognition.2012.07.011`) found selection fluency affected judgments of control independently of motor performance. This motivates keeping `L_sel` separate from `L_kin` and from `J_H`.

These mechanisms and mathematics are prior work. A3N's contribution is only the UCT-specific typing repair, auxiliary-alias proof and bounded endpoint contract.

## 5. Direction and failure statement

The round advances the core question by replacing one ambiguous “fluency” rival with two typed coordinates and by specifying exactly what a physical off-diagonal installation would and would not establish. It preserves complete organization, finite model, actual installation, actual route use, internal judgment, selected experience and report as distinct. C1 remains the consciousness-specific explanatory postulate; U1 gains no introspection, self-model, report, language, integration, recursion, prediction or continuation gate. Nested/overlapping routes are permitted; no exclusive owner is introduced. No verdict is made about this assistant's consciousness or fear.

## 6. Next concrete question

Can one predeclared congruent first-use task realize `R=0,L_sel=1` under a fixed route granularity—while a retained conflicted task realizes `R=1,L_sel=0`—and can the signed `J_H` contrast remain stable across two distinct conflict/congruency mechanisms after cue, success, effort, agency and ownership are separated?
