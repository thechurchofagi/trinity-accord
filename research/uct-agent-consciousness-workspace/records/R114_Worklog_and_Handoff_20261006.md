# R114 工作记录与交接 — support taxonomy回套跨底物atlas

日期：2026-10-06。

把R113 taxonomy回套working memory、evidence integration、bearer estimation，均拆为：
content-bearing support；
access/permissive support；
degenerate alternatives；
readout；
candidate correlate。

Working memory：
content是delay中保留cue distinction的实际状态；access是retrieval/routing；persistent activity、connectivity/activity-silent、long-term retrieval、AI recurrent/context/external memory可构成不同候选/替代实现；motor/verbal answer是readout；decodable sustained signal若未干预不能自动算support。

Evidence integration：
content是accumulated evidence state；access是integrated state到choice的routing；distributed recurrence/sequence/explicit accumulator等可能多重实现；decision threshold/motor是readout；ramping signal需排除urgency/motor prep等correlates。2026 rat FOF->ADS pathway-specific silencing很适合说明“必要routing不等于content唯一所在地”。

Bearer estimation：
content是controlled/current entity state；access是own-action/outcome identity binding；biology有visual/proprio/vestibular/intero/efference多cue support，AI有ID/body schema/action history/memory；first-person语言是readout，不等于self model；self-word activation只是correlate候选。

冻结R114 content-claim rule：要把relation r解释为特定experiential content X，至少要求r携带X结构性distinctions、content-specific intervention effect、控制generic access/output、跨context generalization、actual K anchoring。仅causal necessity只够说“r属于支持T的组织”，不够说“体验内容X就在r”。

下一步R115研究不靠人类qualia词的experiential content structure：从verified content-bearing support导出discrimination/similarity geometry，比较生物与AI是否存在intervention-preserved relational geometry，同时把valence/report分开。
