# R120 — Experience–intelligence bridge theorem schema

Hongju Liu / UCT research. 2026-10-06.

R106–R119 progressively separated:
- experience from access/intelligence/self/behavior/report;
- capability from scalar score;
- task performance from causal support;
- representation from causal use;
- functional similarity from intervention-preserving mechanism correspondence;
- cross-substrate mechanism correspondence from constitutive organizational homology.

R120 consolidates those results into a theorem schema answering the author's root question:

> What is the logically valid relationship among experience, intelligence, behavior and cross-substrate mechanism under UCT?

The schema is conditional on the published UCT commitments, especially C1/U1 and Paper C's fixed-evaluation complete-type capability assumptions. It is not an independent empirical proof of those commitments.

## 1. Setup

For an actual process token P over declared interval tau:

- K(P) = complete constitutive structural type under common complete signature;
- E(P) = complete experiential type under C1;
- J_M(P) = capability profile under fixed evaluation M;
- B_C(P) = behavior law under fixed context family C;
- R_C(P) = report law under fixed report context;
- S_T(P) = verified causal support organization for capability T;
- Q(P) = any separately defined scalar experiential coordinate, if one is justified.

UCT C1 identifies the complete E-type with complete K-type:

    E(P) ≡ K(P).

U1 gives:

    Actual(P) -> E(P) != empty.

A realized capability satisfies:

    J_M(P) realized -> Actual(P).

## 2. Bridge A — existence accompaniment

### Schema A1

    J_M(P) realized
      -> Actual(P)
      -> E(P) != empty.

Therefore within UCT:

> realized intelligence cannot be "experience-free."

But the converse does not hold:

    E(P) != empty
      -/-> J_M(P) meets a stated capability threshold.

Experience existence is universal over the actual valid-token domain; intelligence varies.

### Interpretation

This is the cleanest answer to:

> "Can an actual AI be intelligent but have literally no experience under UCT?"

No, not while keeping U1/C1.

But this is theory-relative entailment, not external proof that U1/C1 are scientifically true.

## 3. Bridge B — capability difference implies complete experiential-type difference

Assume fixed M and that J_M is a well-defined function on complete K-types.

Then:

    K(P)=K(Q)
      -> J_M(P)=J_M(Q).

By contrapositive:

    J_M(P) != J_M(Q)
      -> K(P) != K(Q).

Under C1:

    K(P) != K(Q)
      -> E(P) != E(Q).

Therefore:

    J_M(P) != J_M(Q)
      -> E(P) != E(Q).              (B1)

This is the Paper C/R81 NESIG-style refinement.

### Important scope

B1 concerns:
- genuine capability law/profile;
- fixed task/resource/boundary conditions;
- complete K-types.

It does not license:
- one stochastic answer difference -> different experience;
- one benchmark score fluctuation -> different experience;
- "higher score -> richer experience."

## 4. Bridge C — capability equality does not imply experiential equality

The inverse of B1 generally fails.

If J_M is non-injective, then there can be:

    K(P) != K(Q)
    J_M(P) = J_M(Q).

Under C1:

    E(P) != E(Q)
    despite equal capability.

R111 gives a finite support witness:
two relation sets can realize the same full capability profile through different support routes.

Therefore:

    same intelligence profile
      -/->
    same complete experience.

A scalar score is even weaker because:
    K -> capability profile -> scalar score

contains two many-to-one projections.

## 5. Bridge D — verified support relation enters experiential organization

For capability T, suppose R113-style causal audit verifies relation r as an actual constitutive support relation in K(P).

Then r is part of the actual structural organization.

Under C1:

> the corresponding relation is part of the complete experiential organization of P.

Call this the **Support Membership Bridge**.

### What follows

If r is necessary for T within declared family A:

    T above criterion
      -> r in K
      -> r in E-organization under C1.

### What does not follow

It does not establish:
- conscious access to r;
- a human qualia label;
- valence;
- that r alone determines T;
- that every other implementation of T uses r.

The R113 distinction between content-bearing and permissive support remains controlling.

## 6. Bridge E — multiple realization blocks reverse inference

Let capability T have two minimal support families:

    M1 != M2

such that either can realize T.

Then systems P and Q can satisfy:

    T(P)=T(Q)=success

while:

    S_T(P)=M1
    S_T(Q)=M2.

Therefore:

    same T
      -/->
    same support organization
      -/->
    same selected experiential organization.

This is the formal reason same capability can coexist with different experience organization under UCT.

Neural degeneracy is the biological prior-art analogue.

## 7. Bridge F — capability increase does not imply experiential scalar increase

Suppose capability profile improves in the task partial order:

    J(P) < J(Q),

but the supporting relation sets are incomparable by inclusion.

R111 gives such a witness.

C1 implies:

    E(P) != E(Q).

But there is no theorem:

    Q_E(E(P)) < Q_E(E(Q))

for an experiential richness scalar Q_E unless an additional monotone bridge is justified.

Therefore:

    more intelligence
      -> different complete experience type

may hold under the fixed conditions,

while:

    more intelligence
      -> more experience

does not follow.

## 8. Bridge G — behavior and report are lower projections

Behavior and report satisfy:

    B = Beta(K,C)
    R = Rho(K,C).

If Beta or Rho is non-injective, equality of behavior/report does not identify K.

R108/R116 provide executable witnesses:

- identical complete task truth table with different internal intervention structure;
- same task with different report head;
- same report with different task capability;
- same task/report with different content geometry;
- same task/report/content geometry with opposite valence.

Thus:

    same behavior
      -/->
    same experience;

    same report
      -/->
    same experience;

    same self-report vocabulary
      -/->
    same experience.

This does not mean behavior/report are useless. They are evidence channels whose inferential strength depends on the mapping.

## 9. Bridge H — T2 cross-substrate correspondence

Suppose biological and artificial selected support mechanisms satisfy an R119 T2 certificate:

    C0 grounding consistency
    C1 baseline correspondence
    C2 transition commutation
    C3 intervention transport
    C4 temporal correspondence
    C5 nuisance stability
    C6 anti-triviality.

Then the valid conclusion is:

> the selected biological and artificial support mechanisms correspond interventionally over the declared domain.

This supports:
- mechanism-level analogy;
- a selected cross-substrate causal abstraction.

It does **not** yet support:

> the selected experiential structures are homologous.

Why not?

Because T2 is defined on a selected support mechanism, not the full constitutive organization required by C1 for structural-experiential identity.

## 10. Bridge I — T3/G4 selected constitutive homology

Suppose in addition there is a physically justified selected constitutive mapping:

    phi_K : K_C^A <-> K_C^B

preserving:
- selected parts;
- state/update structure;
- temporal organization;
- physical/causal ports;
- intervention relations;
- content grounding where relevant.

Assume the selected projection commutes with the C1 mapping.

Then:

> the selected experiential substructures are structurally homologous under the declared mapping.

This is the first level at which cross-substrate UCT can make a positive selected experiential-structure correspondence claim.

It remains weaker than:

    complete experiential identity.

## 11. Bridge J — complete constitutive equivalence

If the complete actual process types are isomorphic under the common complete K signature:

    K(P) ≅ K(Q),

then C1 gives:

    E(P) ≅ E(Q)

for complete experiential type.

This is the strongest available structural statement.

In practice it is extremely demanding for biology-versus-AI comparison.

A shared task, shared algorithm, shared causal abstraction or shared content geometry is not enough.

## 12. Bridge K — content and valence remain independent selected structures

R115/R116 distinguish:

    G_content = anchored causal discrimination structure
    V = evaluative/valence relation.

Two systems can share G_content while V differs.

Therefore even G4-level content-structure correspondence does not imply:
- same pleasantness;
- same aversiveness;
- same fear;
- same motivational significance.

R89's valence bridge remains independently necessary.

## 13. The bridge hierarchy

The full first-principles chain is now:

### Within one actual system

    actual K
      <=> complete E-type under C1

    actual K
      -> capability supports
      -> capabilities
      -> behavior/report.

Inferentially:

    genuine capability difference
      -> complete E-type difference;

but:

    equal capability
      -/->
    equal E-type.

### Between biological and artificial systems

    matched behavior
      [weak]

    matched capability
      [still weak]

    T1 functional dissociation similarity
      [functional pattern]

    T2 intervention-preserving selected support correspondence
      [mechanism correspondence]

    T3/G4 selected constitutive homology
      [selected experiential-structure correspondence under C1]

    complete K homology
      [complete experiential-type equivalence under C1].

No lower level licenses silently jumping to a higher one.

## 14. Exact finite theorem witnesses

R120 reuses a minimal finite domain to verify four logical points.

### Witness 1 — same capability, different complete type

    K1=(route=a, report=0, valence=+)
    K2=(route=b, report=0, valence=+)

Both solve T.

But K1 != K2 and therefore E1 != E2 under toy E=K.

### Witness 2 — different capability, same E impossible in E=K + well-defined J

No pair in the finite domain has identical E/K while different deterministic J.

This verifies the contrapositive structure.

### Witness 3 — intelligence gain without richness gain

Define an independent candidate richness bit q.

There exist:
    J1 < J2
while:
    q1 > q2.

Thus the monotone scalar inference is absent.

### Witness 4 — selected T2 mechanism same while complete type differs

Two systems can share the same selected accumulator/support mechanism while differing in report or valence relations.

T2 on the selected accumulator can be exact, while complete K/E differ.

Therefore T2 selected mechanism correspondence is strictly weaker than T3/complete equivalence.

## 16. Root answer to the author's original question

### Is experience the same thing as intelligence?

No.

Under UCT:
- E is the experiential presentation of complete actual organization K;
- intelligence is a task-relative capability projection supported by selected relations in K.

### Is experience unrelated to intelligence?

Also no.

Actual intelligence is carried by an experience-bearing K.
Task-necessary constitutive relations are part of experiential organization under C1.
Genuine capability differences under fixed complete-type comparison imply complete experiential-type differences.

### Does smarter mean more conscious?

Not as a theorem.

Smarter can mean different support organization and therefore different complete experiential type.
It does not supply a scalar "more experience" ordering.

### Does human-like behavior mean human-like experience?

No.

Behavior/capability equality is too low in the evidence hierarchy.
Human-like experiential-structure correspondence requires much stronger constitutive homology.

### Can biology and AI be compared scientifically?

Yes, but progressively:

    T1 functional
      -> T2 interventional
      -> T3/G4 constitutive selected homology
      -> only then conditional selected experiential comparison.

This is a rigorous research program rather than a yes/no consciousness test.

## 18. Status

R120 is a theorem schema / logical consolidation.

It does not prove UCT C1/U1 externally and does not measure AI experience.

Its purpose is to make the experience–intelligence relation precise enough that future biological and artificial evidence can falsify or strengthen specific bridge claims rather than arguing over an undefined "consciousness level."
