## Primary empirical sources re-read

1. Johnson TR, Moralle S, Luo Z, Taylor DM. “Implanting microelectrode arrays in the bottom of the central sulcus targeting somatosensory area 3a for restoration of proprioception.” *Neurosurgical Focus* 60(2):E8 (2026). DOI [10.3171/2025.11.FOCUS25916](https://doi.org/10.3171/2025.11.FOCUS25916); PMID 41621104; PMCID PMC13503028. Role: one custom 32-channel area-3a FMA per macaque, chronic anatomical/recording viability and passive-arm-movement modulation. Limit: implantation-method record, not a source×neighbour×perturbation use experiment.
2. Yamada H et al. “Representation of Afferent Signals from Forearm Muscle and Cutaneous Nerves in the Primary Somatosensory Cortex of the Macaque Monkey.” *PLOS ONE* 11 (2016): e0163948. DOI [10.1371/journal.pone.0163948](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0163948). Role: same-experiment DR/SR cuff stimulation, peripheral-volley monitoring, stimulus-aligned recordings across area 3a and 3b/1, with response counts and latencies. Limit: two anaesthetized macaques, LFP response rather than downstream causal use, non-pure source classes and no experiential target.
3. London BM, Jordan LR, Jackson CR, Miller LE. “Electrical stimulation of the proprioceptive cortex (area 3a) used to instruct a behaving monkey.” *IEEE Transactions on Neural Systems and Rehabilitation Engineering* 16 (2008): 32–36. DOI [10.1109/TNSRE.2007.907544](https://doi.org/10.1109/TNSRE.2007.907544). Role: writable behavioural access. Limit: write direction is not a natural-source read-use test.
4. Krubitzer L, Huffman KJ, Disbrow E, Recanzone G. “Organization of area 3a in macaque monkeys: contributions to the cortical phenotype.” *Journal of Comparative Neurology* 471 (2004): 97–111. DOI [10.1002/cne.20025](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.20025). Role: mapping and inter-animal variation. Limit: no unique area boundary or owner.

## Retrieval and claim limits

- The 2026 paper was verified through its open manuscript/indexed full-text record current to 2026-10-10; its long-term goal is not counted as an executed stimulation result.
- The Yamada correction uses the authors' own methods, counts and limitations. The descriptive interaction is not a new animal-level statistical test.
- No cross-study fact is treated as if acquired in one animal or event.
- The search was targeted, not an exhaustive priority review. No claim of impossibility, exhaustive novelty or mathematical novelty is made.
