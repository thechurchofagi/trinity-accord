# Area-3a crossed-probe contract

## Frozen tuple

Freeze one animal, limb, source classes, area-3a ensemble, neighbouring 3b/1 ensemble, perturbation mechanism, event clock, movement/contact envelope, downstream endpoint, analysis inclusion rule and admissible collateral pathways before comparison.

## Capability conjunction

| Gate | Required evidence | Johnson 2026 | Yamada 2016 | Stop code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `A` local access | anatomically verified area-3a recording | supported | acute recording supported | `NO_LOCAL_ACCESS` |
| `S` source contrast | identified, monitored source intervention | not executed | DR/SR cuff contrast, imperfect purity | `NO_SOURCE_CONTRAST` |
| `C` consumer contrast | simultaneous 3a versus matched neighbour | absent from single-array installation | 3a versus 3b/1 response sites | `NO_CONSUMER_CONTRAST` |
| `T` event binding | common instrumented clock for source, response and endpoint | spike time exists; passive-movement clock not reported | stimulus-aligned SEP timing | `NO_EVENT_BINDING` |
| `G` consumer perturbation | validated reversible 3a perturbation plus neighbour/sham | future goal, not validated here | absent | `NO_USE_INTERVENTION` |
| `Y` downstream endpoint | predeclared consequence not analyst-defined from local channel | absent | functional relevance not tested | `NO_DOWNSTREAM_TARGET` |
| `H` experiential bridge | independently calibrated target interpretation | absent | absent | `NO_MINENESS_BRIDGE` |

The full application requires `A ∧ S ∧ C ∧ T ∧ G ∧ Y`; `H` is separately required before naming familiar mineness. `A` alone does not discharge another gate.

## Minimal session structure

1. Randomize DR/SR (or declared source alternatives) within blocks and record the peripheral volley.
2. Record 3a and 3b/1 simultaneously on the same hardware clock.
3. Interleave validated 3a perturbation, neighbour perturbation and sham.
4. Capture joint kinematics, surface contact and relevant motor output on that clock.
5. Predeclare the downstream endpoint and its causal contrast.
6. Reject trials with failed delivery, spillover, clock loss or movement mismatch; do not recode them as negative biological results.

## Inference ladder

- `A+S+C+T`: crossed response localization.
- `A+S+C+T+G+Y`: candidate consumer-specific causal use, conditional on perturbation fidelity and no uncontrolled spillover.
- plus independently admitted actual process/signature: UCT organizational application.
- plus `H`: only then a candidate familiar-mineness interpretation; ownership, agency and report still do not collapse into it.
