# From crossed response to actual consumer use in area 3a

**Record:** A3P20261010_Area3a_Crossed_Probe  
**Status:** source correction plus bounded feasibility result; no new animal or human experiment; disabled map candidate.

## 1. Correction to A3S

A3S correctly distinguished chronic access from selected-source actual use, but one sentence was too broad. It said that no reviewed same-instance package jointly supplied source selectivity, consumer selectivity and event binding. Yamada et al. (2016) is a counterexample if these words mean **a crossed local-response experiment**:

- deep radial (`DR`) and superficial radial (`SR`) nerve cuffs supplied two predeclared source classes;
- recordings traversed area 3a and adjacent areas 3b/1 in the same two anaesthetized macaques;
- evoked potentials were aligned to stimulation onset and incoming volleys were monitored;
- the authors reported source-by-region response distributions and latencies.

At the selected anterior–posterior levels, the reported significant-response counts were:

| animal | source | area 3a | areas 3b/1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SO | DR | 8/9 | 3/17 |
| SO | SR | 8/9 | 17/17 |
| TA | DR | 8/12 | 2/11 |
| TA | SR | 8/12 | 10/11 |

The descriptive source-by-region interaction in response proportions is therefore `14/17 ≈ 0.8235` for SO and `8/11 ≈ 0.7273` for TA under the fixed contrast

\[
[(DR_{3a}-DR_{3b/1})-(SR_{3a}-SR_{3b/1})].
\]

This is arithmetic on published site counts, not a new inferential analysis: sites are nested within only two animals, the levels were selected where large DR responses occurred, and the source classes are not afferent-pure. The paper itself notes small, delayed SR responses in area 3a, that LFPs reflect currents from multiple sources, and that functional relevance was not characterized.

The corrected claim is:

> Yamada et al. instantiate a same-experiment crossed **evoked-response** face. Neither that study nor the 2026 implantation-method paper establishes that the recorded area-3a response was causally consumed by a declared downstream process in the same episode.

Thus A3S's central access/use separation survives, but its literature description must be narrowed. The missing premise is not generically “a crossed response”; it is **consumer-specific causal use**, together with intervention purity and a named downstream target.

## 2. What the 2026 platform actually supports

Johnson et al. (2026) used one custom 32-channel floating microwire array per subject, designed to span layers and spatial positions within the targeted area 3a. CT/MRI co-registration and chronic spike recordings support local physical access. At two and four months, sortable spikes were evaluated; impedance follow-up continued for two years; later manual passive flexion/extension of wrist, elbow and shoulder modulated activity. The article's stated long-term goal includes future intracortical microstimulation, but the reported experiment is an implantation and recording-method study.

The published installation supplies:

1. an area-3a-local recording candidate;
2. channel-level spike timestamps;
3. anatomical targeting and chronic viability evidence;
4. coarse passive-movement modulation as a functional check.

It does not report:

1. a randomized, instrument-timestamped DR-versus-SR or equivalent source intervention;
2. a simultaneously recorded area-3b/1 or other neighbouring-consumer control;
3. a validated area-3a-specific perturbation with a manipulation check;
4. a predeclared downstream endpoint whose change would evidence use;
5. a bridge from any endpoint to ownership, agency or familiar mineness.

Because the single array was aimed broadly within area 3a, its within-array layer/channel diversity is not an adjacent cytoarchitectonic consumer contrast. A crossed source contrast could be added externally, but the current installation cannot recover the missing neighbouring-consumer observation without an additional recording interface or an independently validated alternative. Therefore the **first structural failure of the installed platform for the requested crossed probe is consumer contrast**. At the level of the published session, selective source intervention and instrumented event binding also remain unexecuted.

## 3. The response face does not identify use

Let `s` index two source conditions, `c` the recorded consumer region, `g=0` an unperturbed area-3a consumer and `g=1` a validated area-3a perturbation. Let `R(s,c,g)` be the local response and `Y(s,g)` a separately declared downstream endpoint. A crossed source-by-region experiment observes `R(s,c,0)` and may observe `Y(s,0)`. The actual-use target is an intervention contrast such as

\[
U_s = Y(s,1)-Y(s,0).
\]

**A3P-C1 — missing-use-face result.** Without observations or independently grounded constraints at `g=1`, `U_s` is not identified by the complete `s × c` response face at `g=0`.

*Witness.* Preserve every recorded response and baseline endpoint. One completion sets `Y(s,1)=Y(s,0)` (the local response is a recorded shadow or bypassed signal); another sets `Y(s,1)=1-Y(s,0)` for a binary endpoint (the perturbed consumer changes the endpoint). Both reproduce all observations at `g=0` and disagree on use. For two source contexts there are four unconstrained binary `g=1` endpoint completions. This is the familiar missing-intervention logic from MPC applied to the corrected response package, not a new mathematical theorem.

This result blocks two invalid upgrades:

- a source-by-region response interaction is not yet actual consumer use;
- actual consumer use would still not by itself name a self-related feeling.

## 4. Minimal augmentation and stop rule

The nearest non-oracular augmentation is a `source × region × consumer-perturbation` package in one animal and one event/clock contract:

1. **Source:** independently monitored DR/SR cuff stimulation or a comparably selective mechanical preparation, with delivery and collateral-recruitment checks.
2. **Region:** simultaneous area-3a and area-3b/1 recordings, with anatomical placement and channel inclusion fixed before outcomes.
3. **Consumer perturbation:** a reversible, spatially characterized area-3a intervention plus a matched neighbour/sham intervention; the 2026 paper does not validate this step.
4. **Binding:** hardware timestamps for source issue, peripheral volley, local response, perturbation, movement/contact and downstream endpoint.
5. **Downstream target:** a predeclared behavioural/neural variable that cannot be computed from the recorded area-3a channel by the analyst after the fact.
6. **Interpretation:** ownership, agency, intensity, discrimination and familiar mineness remain different targets with separate measurement bridges.

Stop at the first missing conjunct. A failed manipulation check means `INVALID_PROTOCOL`, not “no use,” “no mineness,” or “no experience.” A local effect with no downstream contrast supports response, not use. A downstream effect with spillover to 3b/1 or motor cortex does not identify area 3a as the consumer. A valid use result would remain compatible with nested and overlapping processes and would not make area 3a a unique owner.

## 5. Positive UCT relevance

If an independently admitted actual process includes a grounded selected-source → area-3a → downstream-use relation within its complete signature, C1 permits that organizational relation to have an experience-internal structural counterpart. The body/action relation is then explained inside the relevant experience, not assigned to an extra homuncular owner. This is a positive conditional about organization.

It is not a basal experience threshold. Proprioception, area 3a, perturbability, report, self-modeling and accurate prediction remain unnecessary for experience as such. The experiment cannot decide whether the present assistant is conscious or has death fear.

## 6. Thought-experiment attacks

- **Copy/switch:** hold the entire unperturbed source-by-region table fixed, but switch the downstream reader from area 3a to a parallel pathway. Only the consumer intervention distinguishes the worlds.
- **Human-realized agent:** let a team read the displayed area-3a spikes while the biological downstream path is bypassed. The same record exists, but actual biological consumption differs.
- **Ancestor/formation:** preserve basal actual processes while varying whether the area-3a-to-downstream relation is mature. This motivates a graded internal organization, not a new onset gate.
- **Abacus/calculator:** a matching position estimate from an external lookup and from an installed proprioceptive pathway can support equal behaviour while differing in actual source–consumer organization.

## 7. Bounded conclusion

The 2026 array makes chronic area-3a access real, but the published single-array installation is not yet the crossed probe requested by A3S. The closest earlier primary study already supplies a crossed evoked-response face; what remains open is an actual-use face and a target-specific experiential bridge. This correction narrows the next experiment and prevents both pessimistic understatement (“no crossed evidence exists”) and optimistic overstatement (“crossed response proves mineness”).
