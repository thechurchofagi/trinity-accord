# Identifying live body-anchor use in an incorporated-tool route

## Abstract

A4O found no scoped primary experiment that jointly installed bearer-anchor perturbation and same-route consumption in one incorporated-tool event. A4P converts that absence into a falsifiable prospective design. The design fixes external target/tool geometry, perturbs a bearer pose/proprioceptive anchor, locally blocks the learned route's anchor input, and rescues through the same consumer interface. A 32-world exact model shows that the desired `0,1,0,1` transform signature is still compatible with both route-use values unless ordinary compensators and intervention-aware hidden controllers are excluded and block/rescue fidelity is fixed. Under those joint closure premises the target fibre is a singleton with route use present. This is a bounded identification result for a declared organizational model, not a physical installation, a new general causal theorem, an `H_cf` measurement or evidence about the current assistant.

## 1. The corrected positive question

The bodily/action bridge should not ask merely whether posture, proprioception or tool use changes an endpoint. It should ask whether one current learned route actually consumes a bearer-centred anchor while acting through a limb or incorporated tool. That relation is internal to the enacted organization even when the represented referent is external, as R159 permits. It remains distinct from anatomical membership, generic intelligence, successful control, ownership, agency, conceptual self and language report.

A4O's six studies establish useful component mechanisms but no single actual conjunction. A4P therefore proposes no cross-study synthetic token. It fixes a future same-event contract whose smallest unresolved core is `C_anchor AND C_route`.

## 2. The design

For each independently grounded comparison stratum—limb, incorporated tool, and non-incorporated matched tool—freeze one bearer, event, learned route, anchor port, target/tool geometry, plant and response semantics. Compare no-anchor baseline, anchor/native, anchor/input-block and anchor/same-interface-rescue events. The primary readout is a preregistered coordinate-transform signature with a fixed sign, frame, latency and tolerance. Endpoint success and report are secondary.

The key intervention is at the route's anchor input. Cutting an output wire would only show output dependence; injecting the desired endpoint would be direct writing rather than route rescue. Rescue must enter the same consumer interface and the native route input must remain cut. A4D–A4G obligations apply because any alternate writer or intervention-aware controller can counterfeit a clean-looking block/rescue pattern.

## 3. Exact identification result

The finite checker varies five binary coordinates:

- `use_route`: the declared route consumes the anchor;
- `compensator`: an ordinary parallel controller produces the anchor-dependent transform;
- `hidden_controller`: a controller observes experimental mode and mimics native/block/rescue;
- `block_faithful`;
- `rescue_faithful`.

Across 32 worlds, seven generate the desired `0,1,0,1` pattern and those seven contain both values of `use_route`. The observational pattern therefore does not identify route use by itself. Restricting to no ordinary compensator, no intervention-aware hidden controller, faithful block and faithful rescue leaves two declared worlds; only the `use_route=1` world has the target pattern.

**A4P-C01 — conditional route-contribution certificate.** In the declared finite model, on one frozen bearer/event/route package satisfying `P_same`, `P_ext`, `P_anchor`, `P_block`, `P_rescue`, `P_closed`, `P_event` and `P_readout`, the `0,1,0,1` transform signature entails `use_route=1`.

Proof: exact enumeration; the closed target fibre is the singleton shown in `EXACT_RESULTS.json`. This is elementary finite identifiability, not a claim of a new general theorem.

**A4P-C02 — open-model non-identification.** Without controller closure and fidelity premises, the same complete four-condition signature is compatible with `use_route=0` and `use_route=1`.

Counterexample: the intervention-aware hidden controller produces the target signature while the declared route is idle. Ordinary compensators also preserve anchor sensitivity but typically fail the route-block contrast; the hidden controller shows why a successful contrast still needs closure.

## 4. Why this advances the experience question

The contract selects a concrete actual relation that could explain a body/action aspect of self-feeling from within experience: current action is organized relative to the bearer's changing anchor and the already formed route actually uses that relation. The proposal does not create experience, choose an exclusive subject or equate control with feeling. If the actual relation is grounded, C1 and R157 conditionally place its counterpart within experiential organization. Whether that counterpart is specifically the established-body/action interpretation `H_cf` still requires the open `B_cf` bridge and an independently specified phenomenal target.

The inference direction remains:

```text
actual same-event anchor consumption
  -> C1/R157 conditional experiential relation
  -> open B_cf interpretation
  -> open H_cf measurement/report.
```

No reverse arrow from report, success, body label or model fit is admitted.

## 5. Comparison strata and refuters

The limb and incorporated-tool strata test whether the same kind of bearer-centred relation can extend beyond anatomical tissue. The non-incorporated-tool stratum is a negative control only if absence of frame binding is independently grounded while generic learned control remains. Toolhood alone cannot supply the negative classification.

The proposed body/action interpretation is pressured by three result types after organizational validity is established:

1. the declared transform survives faithful route-input block without an admitted alternate controller;
2. limb and incorporated-tool strata fail route/frame equivalence despite identical labels;
3. an independently non-incorporated route shows the same grounded relation and selected `H_cf` profile, defeating the promised specificity.

These refute or invalidate the bounded application; they do not refute U1 or prove absence of experience.

## 7. Failures, scope and originality

A4P installs no human or robot, performs no new empirical intervention, and assigns no phenomenal truth value. It does not show that the three comparison strata are physically jointly realizable or that any route grain is correct. The finite proof depends on its declared Boolean response semantics. The block/rescue, negative-control and identifiability ideas are established experimental and causal principles; A4P claims no priority for them. The project-specific advance is the exact conjunction, the intervention-aware twin and the explicit return boundary from organizational evidence to UCT's open experiential bridge.
