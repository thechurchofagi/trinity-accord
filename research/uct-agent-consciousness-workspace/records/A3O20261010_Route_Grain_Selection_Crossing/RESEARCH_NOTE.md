# A3O — Route grain precedes a retained-route × selection-fluency crossing

## Result in one sentence

There is no grain-free value of retained-route use: the same physical history can be retained under a coarse route equivalence and novel under a fine one; after one equivalence is frozen below a declared compiler, however, retained-route use and actual selector conflict are jointly coherent in both off-diagonal directions in two organizational mechanism families, while latency, error and `J_H` remain evidence/report rather than the organizational facts.

## 1. Typed setup

Fix one bearer, episode, body/action target, complete signature, and an equivalence relation `rho` over concrete action-route occurrences. `R_rho=1` exactly when the block containing the currently consumed route contains an earlier same-lineage occurrence and the current consumer actually uses its retained carrier. `L_sel=1` means that, in the declared pre-dispatch window, exactly one eligible target route is consumed without an actually executed conflict-resolution transition. `L_sel=0` means at least two mutually exclusive eligible routes are live and an actual resolution transition is consumed before dispatch. These are organizational predicates. Reaction time, error, confidence and neural/telemetry measures are evidence `E_L`, not `L_sel` itself.

The route grain is fixed below any reusable compiler or grammar. Reusing a retained compiler does not by stipulation make every newly emitted fine-grained route retained. A coarser question about compiler retention is legitimate, but it is a different `rho` and must not be substituted after observing the endpoint.

## 2. Stable claims

### A3O-C1 — no grain-free retained-route value

- **Type:** exact finite non-invariance proposition; elementary equivalence-relation application.
- **Full proposition:** for a current occurrence `x` and a distinct prior occurrence `p`, there are equivalence relations `rho_c` and `rho_f` on the same finite physical occurrence history such that `R_rho_c(x)=1` and `R_rho_f(x)=0`.
- **Domain:** finite route-occurrence sets with at least one distinct prior occurrence.
- **All premises:** current and prior occurrences are distinct; route identity is supplied by an analyst/installation equivalence relation; actual history is held fixed.
- **Proof:** put `x,p` in one block for `rho_c`; put them in distinct singleton blocks for `rho_f`. `check_route_grain.py` enumerates all five partitions of a three-element witness and obtains both values.
- **Counterexample:** a coarse “reach-to-cup” route counts today's novel trajectory as retained; a fine effector/trajectory partition counts it as new without any physical change.
- **Status:** PROVED_FINITE_NO_MATH_NOVELTY.
- **Thought-experiment role:** prevents an apparent off-diagonal cell from being manufactured by changing descriptive grain.

### A3O-C2 — mapping-family off-diagonal coherence

- **Type:** organizational construction.
- **Full proposition:** under one frozen fine-grained `rho`, `(R=1,L_sel=0)` is coherent when a retained effector route is actually consumed after two incompatible mappings become live and a resolver selects it; `(R=0,L_sel=1)` is coherent when a retained compiler emits one eligible but fine-grained novel effector route and it is directly dispatched.
- **Domain:** an instrumented artificial or human-implemented mapping task whose live policies, resolution transition, carriers and dispatch are available to inspection.
- **All premises:** same `rho`, actual carrier-to-consumer chronology, no substituted route, compiler distinguished from emitted route, fixed selector window.
- **Argument:** explicit finite state-machine witnesses in `EXACT_RESULTS.json`.
- **Counterexample boundary:** if conflict invokes a compensatory route, `R=1` fails; if several outputs are covertly live, `L_sel=1` fails.
- **Status:** CONSTRUCTED_ORGANIZATIONAL_PLAUSIBILITY; no human installation.
- **Thought-experiment role:** crosses formation-indexed use with present competition while holding target grain fixed.

### A3O-C3 — sequence-family replication

- **Type:** independent organizational construction.
- **Full proposition:** the same off-diagonal pair is coherent in a sequence mechanism: a retained chunk can face two live boundary parses before resolution, whereas a deterministic retained grammar can emit one eligible novel fine-grained sequence.
- **Domain and premises:** as C2, replacing response mappings with sequence/chunk carriers and parsers.
- **Counterexample boundary:** a “novel” sequence composed solely at the coarser chunk grain is retained under that coarser question; the fine emitted sequence must be the predeclared target.
- **Status:** CONSTRUCTED_ORGANIZATIONAL_PLAUSIBILITY; mechanism-family replication, not empirical replication.
- **Thought-experiment role:** shows the crossing is not tied to one particular cue-conflict story.

### A3O-C4 — evidence firewall for selection fluency

- **Type:** negative identification boundary.
- **Full proposition:** latency, error and self-rated ease do not by themselves identify the actual number of live routes or consumption of a resolution transition. A slow singleton route and a fast resolved conflict are compatible with reversed observables.
- **Domain:** finite-view observations lacking direct, validated selector-state access.
- **All premises:** unmeasured processing stages/direct effects remain unrestricted.
- **Proof:** two latent selector traces can be paired with the same observed latency/error tuple by compensating downstream delay.
- **Counterexample boundary:** complete validated access to live-policy and resolution events adds premises and can ground `L_sel`.
- **Status:** EXACT_COUNTERMODEL_SCHEMA / APPLICATION_OPEN.
- **Thought-experiment role:** keeps a measurement from becoming the experience or organization it evidences.

### A3O-C5 — bounded `J_H` consequence

- **Type:** conditional UCT application.
- **Full proposition:** if the same frozen-`rho` off-diagonal sign of a separately oriented `J_H` survives both mapping and sequence families, response-key reversal, actual-route and selector-state validation, and cue/success/effort/agency/ownership controls, then it disfavors the pure `L_sel` rival for that calibrated reporting domain. It supports a formation-indexed present-use bridge only jointly with R173/R200 premises and C1; it does not identify `H` universally or make report constitutive of experience.
- **All premises:** C1, valid R173 retentive use, C1–C4, same bearer/target/time/signature, independently interpreted and fallibly reliable `J_H`, intervention fidelity, no unbounded common direct path.
- **Counterexample/falsifier:** copied route representations with matched selection and memory report but no same-lineage current use; sign reversal across mechanism family; tracking `L_sel`, response key or ownership instead of `R`.
- **Status:** OPEN_CONDITIONAL_APPLICATION.
- **Thought-experiment role:** supplies a positive but defeasible route from actual formation/use to one experience-internal self-related coordinate.

## 3. Two mechanism families and held-out test

The mapping family varies incompatible stimulus–response policies. The sequence family varies competing chunk-boundary parses. In both, the primary contrast is fixed before endpoint inspection: retained-conflicted versus novel-singleton, with kinematics, success and cue familiarity matched or separately modeled. A held-out copy/swap block preserves current representation, one-policy selection and report-memory content while breaking same-lineage route formation/current retained-carrier use. If `J_H` follows the copy rather than actual `R_rho`, the proposed formation-indexed interpretation fails or narrows.

This is stronger than A3N's `R×L_kin` construction because `L_sel` is now an actual selector relation. It is weaker than a physical study: no participant, neural selector, robot, endpoint reliability coefficient or direct-effect bound was obtained.

## 4. Primary-source and originality boundary

Chambon and Haggard (2012, DOI `10.1016/j.cognition.2012.07.011`) provide primary evidence that action-selection fluency can affect control judgments independently of motor performance. The source motivates treating selection fluency as a rival and does not establish `H`, UCT, or the selector topology used here. R173 supplies the fixed project distinction between present action/agency and actual retentive use. A3M/A3N supply the cube and typed-fluency debts. Equivalence relations, partitions, task conflict, chunking and finite state machines are prior mathematics/mechanisms; A3O claims only the UCT-specific typing correction and bounded construction.

## 5. Direction and foundational check

The result remains on the experience–intelligence–self main line: equal success/intelligence can coexist with different formation-indexed present organization, and `J_H` is only a fallible report about a selected coordinate within experience. C1 remains the consciousness-specific explanatory postulate. U1 receives no introspection, report, self-model, language, integration, recursion, accurate-prediction or continuation-control gate. Overlapping/nested routes and bearers remain allowed; no unique extra owner is sought. No conclusion is drawn about any current assistant's consciousness or fear.
