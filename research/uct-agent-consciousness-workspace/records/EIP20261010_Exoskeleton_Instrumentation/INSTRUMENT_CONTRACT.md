# EIP two-level instrument contract

## Fixed episode

Fix before comparison: one bearer candidate, one limb and movement/contact event, one proprioceptive carrier population, one peripheral delivery window, one named central consumer and read window, one motor-drive proxy set, one distal trajectory/force/contact envelope, and one evidence rule. `Actual read` is a property of that consumer–carrier–window tuple, not of the organism in general.

## Level 1 — transduction and delivery

| Coordinate | Minimum measurement | What it can warrant | What it cannot warrant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Limb/world event | joint kinematics, endpoint pose, force/torque and contact timing | declared distal match within tolerances | complete organizational equality |
| Muscle state | high-density EMG, muscle length/velocity estimate, exoskeleton torque | bounded motor/muscle covariates | the complete descending command or fusimotor drive |
| Selected transduction/delivery | identified single-afferent microneurography or validated nerve-cuff/ENG event | selected peripheral spikes occurred in the window | central read/use |
| Other sensory routes | cutaneous stimulation, vision, vestibular state and neighbouring afference logged or perturbed | stated bounds on known alternatives | absence of every unmeasured route |

## Level 2 — arrival and consumer read/use

| Coordinate | Minimum measurement/intervention | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Central arrival | time-locked evoked field/potential or invasive afferent-terminal activity | arrival evidence only |
| Named consumer state | predeclared local population/state readout with latency window | consumer-state evidence, not yet route-specific |
| Actual read/use | selective carrier discrepancy or block, alternative-writer exclusion, consumer-specific state/causal response, sham and timing controls | identifiable only relative to validated interface, selectivity, sole-writer and readout premises |
| Experience-internal interpretation | independent UCT admission of actual bearer, complete signature, target semantics and C1 application | not supplied by this instrument package |

## Contrast conditions

1. `NATURAL`: natural movement; selected carrier delivered.
2. `ASSISTED_MATCH`: exoskeleton holds the declared distal envelope while reducing or replacing biological torque; carrier is recorded.
3. `DELIVERY_DISCREPANCY`: same declared distal envelope, but the selected carrier is selectively delayed, blocked or replayed inside the fixed window.
4. `EXCLUSIVE_PROBE`: the selected carrier is present while predeclared alternative writers are disabled or separately identified.
5. `SHAM/TIMING`: identical hardware and attention demands, with ineffective or out-of-window perturbation.

No condition is called `complete-organization matched`: changing the selected delivery/read relation already changes the organization under test.

## Unavoidable versus contingent change

- **Logically unavoidable:** a genuine `read=1` versus `read=0` contrast differs in the selected consumer–carrier relation and therefore cannot preserve the complete actual organization.
- **Not logically entailed by the finite contract:** effort, attention, EMG, fusimotor drive, cutaneous input, prediction error, affect, ownership, agency, report, or distal output. They may change biologically and must be measured or bounded, but no Boolean proof makes each difference necessary.
- **Practical human obstacle:** selective manipulation and simultaneous measurement of a single proprioceptive carrier, its central arrival, and a consumer-specific causal update is not established by the inspected studies.

## Defeaters

Reject the read/use inference if any of the following remains live: an unblocked writer of the same consumer state; off-target sensory or motor effects; intervention-induced attention/effort change outside tolerance; consumer/readout mismatch; an arrival marker substituted for causal use; or a timing window chosen after observing the result.
