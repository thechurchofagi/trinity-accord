# Prospective cross-route practical-continuity contract

Version A5B-CRCE-v0.1.0. Disabled candidate; not a basal-experience condition or an installed phenomenal bridge.

## Typed objects

- `Pi subseteq R x T`: actual same-episode route participation in selected targets.
- `W`: separately actual perturbation/compensation organization, including timing, consumers and bypasses.
- `C_t in {0,1}`: under the frozen perturbation to one route for target `t`, another route changes its contribution and the same target is preserved.
- `J_t(Pi)`: both named routes participate in target `t`.
- `H_pc(t)`: selected felt practical continuity—target-directed action remains immediately carryable-through from within across the perturbation.
- `E_pc`: separately coded fallible evidence about `H_pc`, with the report channel and code fixed across conditions.

`Pi`, `W`, `C`, `H_pc` and `E_pc` are different types. `C` is not inferred from success alone. `H_pc` is not defined by `C`, source count, ease, agency attribution or report.

## Organizational contracts

- `F_NS`: no spurious compensation, `C_t -> J_t`.
- `F_NM`: no missed compensation, `J_t -> C_t`.
- `F_BI`: targetwise biconditional faithfulness, `C_t <-> J_t`.

Each contract is a claim about one actual `Pi,W` binding. Separate evidence for its directions cannot be spliced across tokens. `F_BI` identifies `J`, not the full `Pi` orbit.

## Candidate phenomenal bridge

`B_pc^comp` is the simultaneous package:

`TARGET AND CROSS_ORBIT AND ACTUAL_PI AND ACTUAL_WC AND MATCH AND SIGN AND DIRECTION AND COPY_SWITCH`.

Where:

- `TARGET` fixes `H_pc`, target identity and episode window independently of results;
- `CROSS_ORBIT` varies a predeclared `Pi` orbit rather than mere route names;
- `ACTUAL_PI` and `ACTUAL_WC` establish actual participation and compensation on the same token;
- `MATCH` fixes declared success, perturbation, kinematics, value/reward instruction, conceptual-self organization and report mechanism;
- `SIGN` independently warrants `P(E_pc=1|H_pc=1)>P(E_pc=1|H_pc=0)`;
- `DIRECTION` predicts a positive `H_pc` contrast for the compensation-capable condition;
- `COPY_SWITCH` changes the live compensation route while preserving output and report machinery.

The conjunction is sufficient only relative to its stated causal and measurement assumptions. It is not necessary for every bodily/action feeling.

## Prospective refuters and stop rules

1. Failure of `ACTUAL_PI`, `ACTUAL_WC`, `MATCH` or `SIGN` blocks the application; it is not evidence for `H_pc=0`.
2. Given those premises and adequate precision, a preregistered null or opposite `E_pc` contrast refutes `DIRECTION` on the declared domain.
3. If a copy/switch changes `E_pc` without changing the predeclared live compensation route, or changes the route without the predicted `E_pc` effect, the corresponding route-specific bridge fails, subject to fidelity.
4. Success does not prove `H_pc=C`, C1, complete `K`, subject count or a universal mineness law. Proxy twins remain admissible unless independently excluded.

## Exact finite boundary

For two routes and two targets, unconstrained `(Pi,C)` has 36 worlds. `F_NS` leaves 16, `F_NM` leaves 25 and `F_BI` leaves 9. Under `F_BI`, `C=00` still admits two route-swap orbits, one global-single-owner and one strict-distributed. No endpoint vector identifies the full orbit without further cross-target route-identity information.
