## 1. Question and result

R191 ended with a difficult request: find a non-circular transformation that distinguishes an adaptive reflex from experienced endorsement while preserving the basal-experience no-gate. That request is ambiguous. It can mean either:

1. the candidates agree only on a selected current descriptor such as OCCA, PC+BDCC or LGRU; or
2. the candidates are isomorphic in the declared **complete actual organization** `K*`, including every history relation claimed constitutively relevant.

These cases have opposite consequences. In case 1, a further actual organizational relation may distinguish the candidates, but its relevance to familiar mineness remains an open bridge problem. In case 2, UCT C1 already forbids a different complete experiential type, and every admissible internal `K*`-definable predicate is preserved by the isomorphism. Asking for an internal structural discriminator while holding the complete structure fixed is therefore inconsistent with the premises. An analyst's labels “adaptive reflex” and “experienced endorsement” cannot repair the inconsistency because they are not installed relations.

The substantive advance is a **signature-ladder repair** of the research question:

> Every twin argument must declare whether equality is at a selected projection, a history-expanded organization, or the admitted complete actual organization. A projected twin defeats only bridges that factor through that projection. A complete twin defeats every admissible internal isomorphism-invariant bridge but, under C1, also has the same complete experiential type. Neither result by itself identifies the external name “familiar mineness.”

This is not presented as new general mathematics. It combines the elementary isomorphism invariance already present in `A:C1`, the representation-fiber result `R149:COMPATIBILITY`, the transported-selector contract in `R157`, and the open target-naming boundary in `R173` to correct a recurring UCT research demand.

## 2. Typed setup

Fix two independently admitted actual process tokens or token-windows `P,Q`, one common declared complete many-sorted signature `K*`, complete actual structures `D_K*(P),D_K*(Q)`, and any bearer/time identifications required for the comparison.

Define three levels without silently moving between them:

- `pi_cur(D)`: a declared current selected projection, for example OCCA plus present bearer coupling;
- `pi_hist(D)`: a history-expanded projection adding actual prior occurrence, same-lineage continuation and present consumer use where relevant;
- `[D]_K*`: the complete actual organizational isomorphism type in the admitted signature.

An **admissible bridge candidate** `q` for this round is internal/extensional to `K*`, invariant under sort-preserving `K*`-isomorphism, and uses only physically grounded relations or transported parameters. It may not use the desired phenomenal name, a report, an analyst's “reflex/deliberate” source-class label, or the outcome by which its success will later be judged. This is a selector discipline, not a claim that every admissible `q` has the right phenomenal meaning.

An **external target interpretation** `B_fam` is a separately fixed relation that says which internal coordinate, if any, is correctly named familiar bodily/action mineness. `B_fam` cannot be defined merely as “whatever `q` returns” without circularity.

## 3. Stable claims

### R192-C1 — Complete-twin bridge invariance

**Type:** conditional consequence / UCT-specific application of standard isomorphism invariance.  
**Domain:** the same admitted `P,Q,K*`, complete structures and transported parameters.  
**All premises:** (i) `D_K*(P) ≅ D_K*(Q)` by a sort-preserving isomorphism `h`; (ii) `q` is admissible as above; (iii) any parameters are fixed or transported by `h`.  
**Statement:** `q(P)=q(Q)`. Under `A:C1`, the complete experiential structures are also isomorphic.  
**Proof:** a parameter-free first-order selector is preserved by isomorphism; with parameters, use their declared transport. More generally an isomorphism-invariant function is constant on isomorphism classes by definition. `A:C1` supplies the structural-experiential isomorphism only for admitted complete actual organization.  
**Counterexample limit:** a label-indexed function `q(D,label)=label` can differ, but it violates admissibility unless that label is independently installed inside `K*`. A partial decoder may also differ if its hidden inputs were omitted from the alleged equality; then the pair was not a `K*`-twin.  
**Status:** derived application; no novelty claim and no empirical premise discharge.

### R192-C2 — Projection-relative nonseparation

**Type:** exact factorization limit.  
**Domain:** any selected projection `pi` on the fixed comparison domain and any candidate `q=g∘pi`.  
**All premises:** `pi(P)=pi(Q)` and `q` factors through `pi`.  
**Statement:** `q(P)=q(Q)`. Equality of `pi` does **not** entail complete organizational isomorphism.  
**Proof:** substitution in `g`. The converse fails whenever two complete types lie in one `pi`-fiber.  
**Counterexample:** R191's frozen controller and formed controller share the declared current projection but differ in actual-retained-revision. The history bit separates them after expansion.  
**Status:** exact; an application of the existing fiber criterion, not a new theorem.

### R192-C3 — History expansion repairs one collision but not target naming

**Type:** conditional scope correction.  
**Domain:** the R191 formed/frozen pair under the declared four-coordinate finite witness.  
**All premises:** the prior revision is an actual occurrence; lineage, continuing carrier and present consumer use are real and same-instance; no copied value substitutes for the occurrence.  
**Statement:** adding actual-retained-revision separates the formed and frozen pair, so they are not twins in the expanded signature. This does not prove that the differing coordinate is familiar mineness, endorsement or deliberateness.  
**Proof/evidence:** direct coordinate inequality; `EXACT_RESULTS.json` verifies all 256 predicates over the current projection fail to separate the pair while the history coordinate separates it.  
**Counterexample limit:** an adaptive reflex can itself undergo conflict-driven revision and present use, restoring equality at the expanded projection. At that point one must either exhibit a further actual relation or accept the projected nonidentification.  
**Status:** exact finite scope result; actual application open.

### R192-C4 — Source-class and report labels are not organizational discriminators

**Type:** nonidentification result.  
**Domain:** a fixed admitted complete structure and any two external labels/reports attached without changing that structure.  
**All premises:** labels are not `K*` relations or transported parameters.  
**Statement:** swapping “reflex” and “endorsement,” or changing a report, changes no admissible `K*` predicate.  
**Proof/evidence:** immediate from R192-C1; the finite checker evaluates all 65,536 Boolean functions on the same complete four-bit state under a label swap.  
**Counterexample limit:** if producing the label/report changes actual consumer, memory, policy, timing or other organization, the pair is no longer fixed; the new relation must be named rather than smuggled in through the word.  
**Status:** exact conditional nonidentification; does not deny that reports can be caused by experience.

### R192-C5 — Target-identification obstruction

**Type:** underdetermination witness, not impossibility of future science.  
**Domain:** a finite complete-signature model plus sparse externally supplied familiar-mineness labels.  
**All premises:** target labels are not used to define the structural inputs; bridge selection is evaluated against the fixed labels.  
**Statement:** structural admissibility and sparse label agreement need not select a unique `B_fam`. In the displayed 16-state witness with two labeled endpoints, 16,384 Boolean bridges fit the labels.  
**Proof/evidence:** 14 unconstrained truth-table entries yield `2^14` compatible functions, enumerated exactly.  
**Counterexample limit:** richer independently warranted target constraints may reduce or eliminate the ambiguity. The count is not a population estimate and does not show that no unique bridge exists in reality.  
**Status:** exact finite witness; human/AI target identification open.

### R192-C6 — Use/test direction discipline

**Type:** epistemic/ontic separation requirement.  
**Domain:** one proposed installed route and one diagnostic protocol.  
**Statement:** actual route use is an ontic premise; a positive diagnostic is evidence about it. Without a separately defended reliability contract, neither direction is logical entailment. Neither variable is itself a named experiential target.  
**Evidence:** the finite epistemic model contains all four `(actual use, positive test)` pairs, including false positive and false negative.  
**Status:** methodological constraint; not a claim that every real test is unreliable.

## 4. The signature-ladder decision rule

For every future reflex/endorsement or ownership/mineness comparison:

1. **Name the bearer, interval and common signature.** “Same system” is not enough.
2. **List the fixed projection.** If only OCCA, PC, BDCC, LGRU or a report is fixed, call the pair a projection twin.
3. **Name every proposed expansion.** History counts only through actual occurrence, lineage, continuation and current use, not copied values.
4. **If the pair remains a projection twin, state only which factorized bridge fails.** Do not infer complete equality.
5. **If the pair is a complete twin, stop searching for an admissible internal discriminator.** Under C1, no different complete experiential type is available in that comparison.
6. **Separately fix the phenomenal target.** A structural difference may be experientially constitutive under C1 without being correctly named familiar mineness.
7. **Keep installation and diagnosis apart.** Passing a test does not insert the tested relation into the token.

This rule prevents an infinite cycle in which each successful reflex mimic triggers another unnamed mechanism requirement. It also prevents the opposite error: treating equality on a small descriptor as equality of complete organization.

## 6. Relation to external work

Chalmers's organizational-invariance argument explicitly concerns fine-grained functional isomorphs and gradual replacement. UCT C1 is retained here as a fixed project axiom rather than claimed as a consequence of R192. Doerig et al.'s unfolding argument and Kleiner–Hoel's falsification analysis highlight different underdetermination problems involving causal structures, predictions, inference and reports. They do not supply UCT's complete-signature ontology or its familiar-mineness bridge. Ellia and Tsuchiya's 2025 structural commentary emphasizes the difference between prediction and post-hoc accommodation; R192 uses that as a warning against selecting `q` after seeing the target labels, not as evidence for C1.

The closest internal predecessors are stronger than the external analogy for the present proof: `R149:COMPATIBILITY` already states the representation-fiber constraint; `R157:COORDINATE_TRANSPORT` and `R157:SEMANTIC_RESIDUAL` already separate transport from naming; `R173:FAMILIAR_MINENESS_BOUNDARY` keeps `B_fam` open. R192 therefore claims a problem correction and an application protocol, not a new theorem of mathematics or a first theory of organizational invariance.
