# UCT existing-experiments audit — human action, self-attribution, masking, and animal sensorimotor organization

**Research checkpoint:** EX20261008; 8 Oct 2026 (Asia/Shanghai). **Status:** literature-grounded audit + published-summary arithmetic only. **Baseline:** R180 v0.2 and R179/TA25 duplicate audit. Not a paper release; no actual raw dataset downloaded or newly conducted human, animal, EEG, or LLM experiment.

## 1. Target, not a new consciousness detector

This audit answers the author's question: can already published human and animal experiments constrain the unresolved UCT bridge from actual physical organization through goal uptake, predicted consequences and retrospective attribution to **selected named experiences**, without running new large experiments?

The historical formal basis is UCT I v1.2 C1 (strong, unverified structural–experiential identity), UCT II v1.1 failure-prone bridge methodology, UCT III v1.0 capability/experience type refinement, TA25 actual process vs reported agency, and R179/R180's source/uptake/attribution distinction. These are inherited; do not publish them again as novelty. In particular an *organization gate for a selected cognitive operation* is not the same as a forbidden *universal gate for basal experience*. No data below verify C1 as an independent physical law.

## 2. Physical-role and observation vocabulary

Specify bearer P, fixed interval I, declared physical signature K, action target c and evidence channel. Within this restricted, **incomplete** scientific view, differentiate:
- **M:** actual motor displacement/effector activity;
- **U:** whether an operative goal/proposal is used in forming the selected act (not mere proposal origin);
- **E:** predicted action consequences / comparator-related mechanism (which may also occur in imagery);
- **W:** actual sensory-consequence access regime;
- **J:** later explicit self/agency judgment;
- **B:** intentional binding (perceived time shifts, not an all-purpose phenomenal measure);
- **S:** sensory attenuation (neural/psychophysical, not identical to B or J);
- **L:** adaptive task capability (not identical to experience).

These are task-relative measured or hypothesized functions, NOT mutually exclusive levels of consciousness, a complete constituent signature, or seven synonymous readings of one hidden agency variable.

## 4. Three critical thought experiments and explicit inference steps

### 4.1 Imposed motion misattributed as self-motion

Hold external EMS as the objective source of the muscle act. The experiment H04 has trial-level changes in *reported* perceived authorship. Consequently **objective peripheral source alone is not sufficient to determine reported authorship** in this domain. This does not establish that the whole upstream human ontic structure is equal across different judged trials. A candidate UCT target bridge must represent how internally formed action plans, comparator signals, afferents and retrospective attribution interact. The early EEG contrast is evidence that an exclusively late linguistic reconstruction is an incomplete *functional* model; it does not establish felt authorship from a decoder.

### 4.2 Prediction without action; control without immediate movement

H02 yields tactile attenuation during *imagined* self-touch. H03 finds distinct ERP components for sound contingency conditions, including a condition with **no movement on the sound-producing trial**. A predicted consequence, control over event occurrence, issued command, actual movement, and a judgment of doing are different comparisons. A rule 'any prediction = felt movement' or 'any movement = felt trying' is not justified. This is stronger ecological constraint for R179/R180 than adding an extra SELF bit to the toy registers, but the claim that UCT alone invented the dissociation is false: these studies precede it.

### 4.3 Unconscious information, accessible judgment, and timing

H07: nonconscious grammar structure may support adaptive responses while *judgment knowledge* remains conscious. H06: outcome masking can disrupt the *intentional-binding effect*. The two do **not** conflict and do **not** jointly prove an experience threshold. They involve different targets, protocols, bearers and observations. They press one principled distinction: the same complete human organism can contain processes at differing levels of access. UCT basal experience (conditional on C1) need not be absent in any such sub-process, whereas the selected global-report, binding or adaptive-control function may have genuine task-dependent gates. The theory must not transfer a gate from one operational target to a universal E(P) predicate.

## 5. Arithmetic audit of an existing human study (no raw claims)

The publisher's published Table 2 provides action/outcome binding means by stimulation, site, valence, choice and binding subtype. In `PRYKE_PUBLISHED_TABLE2_RECHECK.json` we transcribed **outcome-binding rows only**, and recomputed their within-row reward-minus-punishment differences by subtraction. In the published overall report, reward mean 86.6 ms vs punishment 62.0 ms gives **+24.6 ms**; free-choice 77.1 ms vs instructed-choice 71.5 ms gives **+5.6 ms**. The original article reports the statistical tests above. We performed **no new significance testing**, and cannot infer repeated-measures uncertainty from grouped SDs. Repeated measures, grouping and factorial interactions prevent treating these arithmetic contrasts as independent effect estimates. A p=.120 is not proof of zero effect. These values also cannot identify named felt agency or its physical microstructure.

## 6. Negative model selection: no UCT-exclusive result established

Tests against overly simple *proxy* models:
1. **M-only (actual effector movement = authorship judgment):** descriptively counterexampled by H01/H04 at reported belief/judgment level.
2. **E-only (prediction = overt action experience):** underidentified by H02; predicting tactile consequence does not establish felt moving.
3. **J-only (late verbal reconstruction is all there is):** H04 early EEG predictive pattern is a challenge to late-only *measurement* accounts, not a causal knockout.
4. **W-independent binding (outcome awareness never influences B):** directly pressured by H06 in the studied masking regime.
5. **L-only (adaptive performance = conscious access to structural grammar knowledge):** H07 pressures conflation, while conscious predictive judgment remained important.
6. **UCT C1:** none of these assays measures complete token-relative ontic type and independently admitted phenomenal isomorphism, so the conditional foundational identity remains **neither independently confirmed nor refuted**.

The scientific increment is **empirical target partition and concrete adverse constraints**, not a new mathematical theorem, a unique UCT prediction, or an estimate of the probability that UCT is true. A regular comparator/inference model can also explain much of the experimental pattern.
