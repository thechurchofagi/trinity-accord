# Internal scientific review — GD v0.2.0

This is substantially AI-assisted internal review, not independent peer review.

**Positive result.** Retained states now have an actual effect on their generating physical deviation through explicit sensing and action. At equal nominal cross-coupling, the common transfer function is fixed while differential stability and noise rejection vary. Bench retention and live regulation need not rank routes identically. Exact limiting variance optima and noise dependence are available.

**Prior art.** Feedback, the usefulness of forgetting, information's viability value, cubic Routh tests, stationary Lyapunov equations and common/transverse modes are established. This checkpoint supplies a specific worked continuation, not a universal new theorem of consciousness. Global novelty unverified; do not promote this automatically to a standalone paper.

**Important restrictions.**

1. x,m,u are ideal linear coordinates, not established complete experiential carriers or simulated people. This is a linear analogue, not preservation of the predecessor's entire stochastic process.
2. The loop is internal, but the model's carrier, equilibrium and performance criterion were selected by the modeler. It does not derive stronger autonomous norms or life.
3. Bench retention clamps sensory input. Live J restores feedback. The tests must not be presented as simultaneous identical operating distributions.
4. J is differential physical variance or pulse energy. Common variance is unchanged; improvement percentages for J must not be attributed to all independent-noise components.
5. Stable/unstable classifications use the full cubic criterion. Reduction to p3>0 is proved only for the stated numerical base-parameter family.
6. Unstable models have no stationary covariance. Algebraic solutions outside the stable region are not accepted as physical noise estimates.
7. The exact C=sqrt(bs) optimum assumes the reduced block, body-only white noise, interior feasible C and K>a². Sensor noise, color, constraints or finite-interface dynamics alter it.
8. Rate sums are not equal metabolic cost or information bandwidth. Changes in successful overwriting and local controller dynamics are intentional, not hidden controls.
9. Limits take finite h and other rates fixed. Stationary convergence requires a stable limiting slow block and fixed noise intensities. Infinite coupling is not realized hardware.
10. Exchanging only actuator paths is not a mere relabeling. Paired sensing compensation restores a specified physical law, not complete ontic identity or phenomenal equality.
11. Replay equivalence is nominal. Unanticipated independent perturbations expose the lack of current feedback; this is an inherited feedback principle, not a new general identifiability theorem.
12. Tanh saturation explicitly breaks global recovery despite identical local linearization. Gaussian stationary variance is not a claim of infinite survival in a bounded physical domain.
13. Finite-time matrix exponentials vary continuously; stationary limits can fail. Functional thresholds do not determine basal experiential existence under UCT.
14. The selected parameter route is not biological evolution, development or actual learned adaptation. No learning or natural-selection run was executed.
15. C1/U1/P3/E2 remain conditional inherited theory; A5L's selected-phenomenal-target gap remains. No new experience gate or subject selector is introduced.

**Execution incident.** The first symbolic test used SymPy charpoly with a positivity-assumed symbol. The generated polynomial symbol lacked those assumptions, causing a false comparison failure. det(zI-A) confirmed the intended polynomial, and the verifier was corrected. Final runs pass. This failure must remain in the provenance.

**Decision.** Preserve as GD v0.2.0 in the same research stream. Its constructive gap closure is within-model causal use, not the completed organization-to-phenomenology bridge. The authoritative map has not received a fresh full semantic audit; keep candidate nodes disabled. No new DOI/OTS/Arweave action.
