## 1. What changed in the research question

The author asks for two coordinated routes: extreme thought experiments that expose the commitments and explanatory costs of competing consciousness theories, and carefully qualified use of existing or prospective experiments. He rejects treating present measurement resolution as if it captured complete physical organization. This round accepts that correction. Immediate empirical discrimination is not the sole form of progress, but intelligibility and compatibility alone are not proof that a theory is true.

The question selected here is narrow: **can a definite-experience thesis justify exclusion, and can coarse temporal observations ever constrain an exclusion thesis without measuring every microscopic detail?** The concrete target is not all of IIT, nor all consciousness theories. It is a stipulated non-overlap constraint on explicitly identified concurrent candidates, with separate evidence obligations for identifying the candidates.

The main positive result is a conditional route from coarse *occupancy* information to a limit on exclusive time-sharing. The main negative result is equally important: this route fails as a consciousness test if the signals are merely persistent reports, if time windows differ, if physical overlap is not established, or if an organizational process is silently relabeled as a separate subject. None of those semantic premises is discharged here.

## 3. Which exclusion thesis is being examined?

Three distinct propositions must be separated.

**D: definiteness.** Each specified experience has determinate content and/or a definite substrate within the theory's own description.

**X: substrate exclusion.** Two *separately instantiated candidates of the relevant type* cannot both exist at a common time/grain when their constitutive supports overlap in the sense used by the theory.

**R: report or task exclusivity.** Only one content, answer, interpretation, or task can be selected/reported at a time.

D alone does not entail X. Definite sets `{a,b}` and `{b,c}` can overlap without ambiguity about their members. This is a logical countermodel to a deduction from definiteness alone, not a physical realization of overlapping minds and not a refutation of IIT. IIT 4.0 explicitly frames the move from axioms to physical postulates as an inference to a good explanation, and adds a maximal-existence selection principle [1]. It can defend those additional commitments. Moon's earlier analysis already challenges the axiom/postulate relation and underdetermination [2].

X does not follow from R either. A single reporting bottleneck may summarize or suppress multiple processes; multiple output channels may be generated by a single larger system. Two experienced hands are two contents, not automatically two experiences or subjects in the relevant ontological sense. IIT itself permits distinctions and relations within one complex; its exclusion rule is not a ban on rich or overlapping contents [1].

For a comparison to count, the predicate under test must therefore say whether it names a physical route, a phenomenal distinction within one experience, a complete experience, a maximal complex, or a numerical subject. A switch between those meanings is not a theoretical victory.

## 4. Thought experiment A: fusion without erasing the parts by definition

Start with two actual organized processes, A and B. Add physically operative coupling and, conditionally, a larger actual process G containing them. Retain lineage, internal activity and boundary influences in the description. Do **not** assume that unchanged internal wiring or unchanged reports mean complete local organization is unchanged: connecting systems can change inputs, feedback, context and dispositions.

Under UCT's admitted-token ontology and C1, the continued existence of A and B as actual nonempty tokens is not annulled simply because G is also an actual process. Their experience-internal organization may change; the claim is not that their original complete qualia remain fixed. Existence of three process-indexed experiential structures does not by itself mean three fully individuated people.

Under IIT-style exclusion, **if** G qualifies as the selected maximal complex overlapping the candidates for A and B, A and B are not additional independent complexes at that grain. They can still contribute distinctions, causal operations, and reports within G. This is IIT's strongest relevant response, not a contradiction in its account.

The resulting comparison is conditional and genuine: a maximality rule may remove the independent status of an overlapping candidate; UCT does not impose that removal at the basal token level. But this round has not computed IIT 4.0 integrated information for a concrete biological or artificial fusion. It has not shown that this particular G wins. It has also not established that UCT's process predicate and IIT's complex predicate are the same observable target.

A useful minimal incompatibility statement is the following. Holding the *same target predicate* fixed, the conjunction of (i) local persistence of that target, (ii) an overlapping whole instantiating that target, and (iii) strict overlap exclusion is inconsistent. This is elementary conditional reasoning. Each premise, especially persistence under complete boundary-accountable change, needs independent defense. Merely accepting the conclusion one prefers is not evidence.

**Explanatory comparison:** UCT avoids a basal maximality-based deletion, but pays for that openness by needing a positive account of experiential relations, perspectives and individuation. IIT supplies a stronger selector, but must justify and implement that selector. Permitting more configurations is not automatically greater explanatory power.

## 5. Thought experiment B: slow mechanics and the hidden outside clock

Imagine replacing a neural process with a mechanically realized process that is extremely slow. The stipulated invariant is the actual causal order and organization relevant to the episode, not a resemblance of drawings. Physical delays, state storage, environmental inputs and their timing must be included. Slowing the internal apparatus while leaving external inputs at their old pace generally changes the full coupled organization.

Where a true order-preserving rescaling with corresponding environmental conditions is stipulated, an explanation based only on a human observer's wall-clock impatience would be inappropriate. However, this is not a specific defeat of IIT: IIT 4.0 explicitly considers temporal and spatial grain [1]. Nor does a biological implementation's empirical latency estimate automatically become a universal axiom forbidding slow substrates.

The scientific use of the example is to require a theory to distinguish intrinsic organization, the observer's sampling scale, and real timescale mismatch. It is not to claim that every slow machine has human-like experience, or that all forms of slowing preserve the same experience.

## 6. Thought experiment C: alternation and coexistence look identical in a coarse view

Consider two binary target indicators x_A(t), x_B(t) on a common normalized observation period. They may initially denote physical processes; use as phenomenal indicators requires additional premises.

In one history A occupies the first half and B the second half. Their overlap is zero. In another history both occupy the first half and neither the second half. Their overlap is one half. Both histories have mean occupancies (1/2, 1/2).

Thus coarse averages may erase the difference between alternation and coexistence. Repeating the same lossy measurement with greater numerical accuracy cannot recover that particular lost distinction. Refining the temporal observation operator can help, whereas merely increasing decimal precision need not.

But the converse is false: lack of full temporal resolution does not imply that *no* conclusion about coexistence is available. If the two actual occupancies are at least 4/5 and 7/10 on the same time measure, their overlap is at least 1/2. That is a counting constraint, not a microscopic reconstruction. Sections 7–9 express its exact scope and extend it to overlapping candidate families.

## 7. A finite-resolution exclusion contract

Fix a finite family of candidates i=1,...,m and one common probability/time measure mu on the same actual observation context. Let x_i(t) be binary existence indicators for **one fixed target type**. Physical overlap must be defined at an appropriate fixed grain; mere colocation in a voxel, shared input, equal state values, or an identical name is insufficient.

Let G be a graph whose edges join candidates that the specified exclusion thesis says cannot coexist. For IIT-style application the intended basis is independently established constitutive support overlap, not arbitrary statistical correlation. Edges may instead encode another explicit incompatibility claim. The graph is only a small relational part of organization; it is not a model of complete consciousness.

Under exclusion, the set of active candidates is an independent set of G at each time. Write p_i=integral x_i dmu. Let w_i >= 0 and let alpha_w(G) be the maximum sum of weights over an independent set. Then:

    sum_i w_i p_i <= alpha_w(G).                         (1)

**Proof.** The pointwise weighted sum is at most alpha_w(G). Integrate. No independence, stationarity, full microscopic description or Gaussian noise is assumed.

At the level of unrestricted abstract time-sharing, the vectors p attainable in this way form the convex hull of independent-set incidence vectors, the classical stable-set polytope [5]. Every schedule averages such vectors; conversely a finite convex combination can be realized by assigning the corresponding fractions of a unit interval. This converse is not a claim of biological realizability: switching costs, causal trajectories and duration constraints may narrow the physical schedules further.

Equation (1) is a necessary condition for the stipulated exclusion thesis, not a complete test of IIT. Satisfying it does not establish maximal integrated information, intrinsic unity or phenomenal consciousness. A UCT permission for overlap does not by itself predict violation of it in any particular empirical dataset.

## 8. Calibration, uncertainty and a quantitative overlap certificate

Suppose m_i(t) is a binary marker rather than the target itself. Let the measurement deliver an estimate hat_m_i with a valid one-sided occupancy error epsilon_i, and independently bound its temporal overcount:

    integral (m_i - x_i)_+ dmu <= eta_i.

Then a lower bound is:

    l_i = max(0, hat_m_i - epsilon_i - eta_i) <= p_i.     (2)

The allowance eta_i is not an invented reassurance. It requires evidence that the marker does not persist, generalize or respond to another source when the named target is absent. An external behavioral endpoint need not supply this evidence. A5L's direct-writer alternative remains live. Where the allowance is unknown, use no positive target lower bound and do not report an exclusion test.

If sum w_i l_i exceeds alpha_w(G), the observations are inconsistent with the **joint** contract consisting of exclusion, candidate identity/overlap, the common time measure and marker calibration. The mathematical inconsistency does not tell us which premise failed.

A useful general bound can quantify departures from the conflict rule. Let nu be the fraction of time at which at least one conflict edge has both ends active, W=sum w_i, and W>alpha_w(G). Then:

    nu >= max(0, [sum w_i l_i - alpha_w(G)] /
                    [W - alpha_w(G)]).                 (3)

**Proof.** On conflict-free times the score is at most alpha_w(G); on the remaining times it is at most W. Integrate and use the lower bounds. The bound is not asserted to be sharp for every possible marginal vector; explicit sharp fixtures are given below. If W=alpha_w(G), this weight vector supplies no constraint.

No mutual independence of marker errors is needed for this deterministic error-budget argument. If confidence intervals replace deterministic bounds, a simultaneous coverage guarantee must be supplied; marginal confidence levels alone are not a joint guarantee. Temporal autocorrelation affects how those intervals are estimated. No confidence intervals for real data are estimated here.

Uncertain support must also be retained. If several conflict graphs remain possible, a conservative test uses the largest alpha_w over them, or only confirmed conflict edges. A claimed violation may disappear when previously assumed overlap is unverified. Candidate birth/death or time-dependent supports need a revised time-indexed contract, not silent use of a fixed graph.

## 9. A non-pairwise thought fixture

Take five candidate supports S_i={q_i,q_(i+1)} with indices modulo five. Their intersection pattern is a five-cycle. The q_i are stipulated distinct physical support labels; this set construction does not prove they are conscious processes. Any non-overlapping selection contains at most two candidates.

If each candidate's occupancy is 1/2, every adjacent pair satisfies p_i+p_j<=1, so pairwise marginal checks pass. But the total is 5/2, exceeding the maximum exclusive concurrency 2. No abstract exclusive schedule has those five occupancies. This is the classical odd-cycle constraint, not a new graph theorem [5]. Its role here is to show why pairwise-looking evidence can fail to support one common exclusion-compatible history.

Equation (3) gives nu>=1/6. This fixture is sharp: use each of the five independent pairs for one of six equal slots, then activate all five for the sixth slot. Each candidate occupies three slots; only the all-on slot violates exclusion.

For the synthetic estimates hat_m_i=1/2 with epsilon_i=0.02 and eta_i=0.03 for every candidate, l_i=0.45. The bound becomes nu>=1/12. These are invented rational values used to check the inference rule, not measurements of human experience. If the physical support relation may instead be empty, alpha becomes 5 and there is no nontrivial bound.

The two-candidate case is the elementary intersection bound. It is credited to classical event/probability counting, which the author's earlier testament precursor also used. The five-cycle case and weighted extension do not establish global priority for the application.

## 10. Explicit countermodels and stopping conditions

**Different windows.** A is active only in the first half and B only in the second. Measuring A only in its half and B only in its half gives two means of one, even though they never coexist. Normalizing each observation separately creates a false violation. The same risk applies to combining incompatible tasks, different people, or separately elicited episodes as though they were one history.

**Persistent reports.** Let the targets alternate, but let two report/memory channels remain on after either target occurred. Both report means are one. Treating them as target occupancies yields a false overlap bound of one. The actual overcount is one half per channel; accounting for it restores zero as the certified minimum. This is a finite illustration of the inherited endpoint/source problem, not a new solution to it.

**Same voxel, different substrate.** Two processes can be aggregated into one measured region without sharing the theory's constitutive units. Apparent anatomical overlap alone is not enough to add a conflict edge.

**Two contents, one subject.** Simultaneous bodily ownership of two represented hands may be two contents in a single experiential organization. It does not automatically instantiate two competing complexes or people.

**Perturbation creates its target.** An introspective query or a coupling intervention can recruit a new joint process. Its outcome need not reveal that the same relation existed before the query. Record the intervention's causal contribution and the time of the claimed target.

**Outward evidence versus constitution.** Increasing endpoint precision does not determine whether an actually grounded coordinate belongs to the relevant complete constitution or is a downstream writer. That is exactly the unresolved A5L issue.

**Permissive theory.** Showing that UCT can accommodate a situation that X forbids is only a discrimination of commitments. UCT must separately identify which actual token relations it positively predicts. Accommodating everything at the chosen observational level does not count as explanatory success.

## 11. Existing empirical work: what it does and does not offer

The original third-arm illusion study combines ownership reports and threat-evoked skin conductance evidence [3]. A later supernumerary-hand study explicitly tries to distinguish ownership of each individual hand from simultaneously owning both [4]. These are valuable examples of plural body content and of why the meaning of "simultaneous" requires care. Neither item alone supplies temporally calibrated occupancy of two separately individuated subjects with known overlapping constitutive supports. It would be a category error to cite either as a refutation of IIT's complex-exclusion rule.

The COGITATE adversarial collaboration explicitly distinguishes mathematical/computational cores from biological implementation predictions [6]. Its multimodal methods and positive-control checks show how one can test declared implementation claims despite imperfect instrumentation. Its results do not establish the truth of UCT or license a blanket claim that measurement limits invalidate every negative result.

The existing project materials on body judgments and source use may help identify physical pathways and measurement error. Their raw datasets are not mounted in this turn and were not reanalyzed. The immediate eligibility questions are whether a dataset contains same-episode common-window measurements, marker-duration calibration, actual candidate support information and a target definition that does not switch from bodily content to subject number. Without these, do not plug questionnaire means or skin-conductance amplitudes into equations (2)–(3).

A feasible future first application could test a specified *single-content-at-a-time* body-representation model, rather than claim to test subject exclusion. That would require checking direct prior work [4] and showing a genuine new estimand or design; simple replication of dual ownership is not a new UCT prediction. No human experiment, clinical intervention or animal procedure is initiated here.

## 13. Contribution and next exact question

The result is a **scoped application and research redirection**, not a completed consciousness theory or a standalone-paper claim. The inherited ingredients include UCT token plurality, the report/target distinction, classic intersection bounds, stable-set/odd-cycle constraints and classical critiques of exclusion. The new project-level integration is to make temporal aliasing, error allowances and physically grounded incompatibility jointly explicit when evaluating an exclusion argument.

The next question is not "can another endpoint be added?" It is:

> For one concrete candidate episode, what evidence would make the proposed marker a lower bound on the *named experiential target*, rather than on a recorded body-content judgment or a physical information route?

Run this question in parallel with the fusion thought experiment. Specify what remains an actual local token under new coupling, what boundary influence changes, and whether the target is basal experience or independently individuated subjecthood. If these premises cannot be justified, preserve the conceptual comparison and the physical result but stop the attempted empirical subject claim. Do not compensate by collecting more of the same ambiguous measurements.
