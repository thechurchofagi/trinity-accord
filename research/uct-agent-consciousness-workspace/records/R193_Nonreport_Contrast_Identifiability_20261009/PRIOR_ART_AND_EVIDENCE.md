# Prior Art and Evidence Scope

## Fixed UCT sources

- **UCT I v1.2:** C1 remains the consciousness-specific explanatory axiom. Sections 10.1–10.3 distinguish structural sufficiency from reflection/measurement bridges; `Psi_v` may use independently interpreted no-report indicators but is not the full experience structure.
- **UCT II v1.1:** the BAC conditions require source anchoring, output blindness, cross-instance fixation, failure possibility and residual transparency. R193 follows those conditions by freezing the domain and constraint packages before enumeration.
- **TA25, D5:** declaring an experiential query does not construct it, validate measurement or identify it with attribution, accuracy or report. R193 preserves this boundary.
- **R149, R157, R173, R192:** respectively supply factorization/fiber discipline, coordinate-transport versus semantic-residual separation, actual retentive-history conditions, and the projection/complete-signature ladder.

Scope: primary design precedent for separating perception contrasts from task relevance and trial-by-trial report. ## Evidence limits

- The exact counts are mathematical consequences of a declared toy domain, not empirical frequency estimates.
- The no-report study shows that report-related activity can be experimentally separated from a perceptual contrast; it does not make a neural marker constitutive of experience.
- No external paper found or used here directly identifies familiar bodily/action mineness from report-independent data.
- No claim of first discovery is made for label symmetry, Boolean constraint propagation, no-report paradigms or organizational invariance.
