- UCT I v1.2: actual token/finite-view distinction, C1 and U1, reports not constitutive gates, and bridge failure discipline.
- TA25 §§3–4, D4–D5: functional self-model versus selected experiential target; a target query does not construct or validate its measurement.

## Primary empirical sources used within their scope

1. Miyazaki M, Hirashima M, Nozaki D. “The ‘cutaneous rabbit’ hopping out of the body.” *J Neurosci* 30(5), 2010. DOI `10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3887-09.2010` — held-stick rabbit paradigm and pointer reports.
2. Blankenburg F et al. “The cutaneous rabbit illusion affects human primary sensory cortex somatotopically.” *PLoS Biology* 4(3), 2006. DOI `10.1371/journal.pbio.0040069` — skin-rabbit/S1 association, not a held-tool target localizer.
3. Miller LE et al. “Sensing with tools extends somatosensory processing beyond the body.” *Nature* 561, 2018. DOI `10.1038/s41586-018-0460-0` — tool vibration motifs encode impact location.
4. Miller LE et al. “Somatosensory Cortex Efficiently Processes Touch Located Beyond the Body.” *Current Biology* 29(24), 2019. DOI `10.1016/j.cub.2019.10.043` — held-tool location processing and EEG decoding; the authors attribute location information to rod vibration patterns.
5. Wada M et al. stick-mediated cutaneous-rabbit study, 2020. DOI `10.1038/s41598-020-58536-z`.

PubMed and the publisher supplement for Miller et al. (2019) were checked on 12 October 2026. No source is treated as proving phenomenal identity, C1, ownership, or an infallible `H` channel. Cross-paper findings are not conjoined as one participant-level intervention.

## Novelty boundary

The enumeration is elementary finite case analysis. Source separation, negative controls, cut/rescue logic, and polarity calibration are established methodological ideas. The project-level contribution is the typed decomposition of A5I's proposed anchor inside UCT; no priority claim is made for the mathematics or empirical mechanisms.
