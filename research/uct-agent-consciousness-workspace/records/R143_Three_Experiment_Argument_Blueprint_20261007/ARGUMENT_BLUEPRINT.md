# What Organizational Change Explains About Experience

Argument blueprint for discussion, 7 October 2026, Asia/Shanghai. Not a manuscript or a new theorem. Parent: `5bdc0dd4ab18fa2c0fcf2d532a7721e3c52ee201`. Pinned foundations: A 1.2, B 1.1, C 1.0, D TA-TR-2026-24 1.0. R140 remains the formal companion; R141–R142 supply the formation/scope reviews.

## 1. The proposed thesis and its status

Under UCT, actual organization has an intrinsic experiential presentation and supports task-relative capabilities. Organizational formation can change constitutive relations and enabled capabilities without being a first ignition of experience. The explanatory task is to state what changes, whose process changes, and which conclusions follow from the physical specification and which depend on C1.

The three thought experiments below make that account readable. They are not three new empirical demonstrations or a logical proof of C1. The core results already appear in A and C. The present contribution is an explicitly scoped argument architecture and a source-overlap verdict. It does not establish a nonredundant fifth research-paper result.

## 2. Minimal definition panel

Reuse R138's definition contract rather than create a new ontology. An actual process token P has declared actual support, interval, accountable boundary and constitutive organization D(P). A scientific model/view is not automatically the complete actual organization. A structural type retains the declared state, operations, ports, temporal and constituent relations up to admissible isomorphism.

C1 identifies the complete token-relative physical organization with its experiential presentation Phi(P) in a common signature. The identity interpretation is an axiom, not a consequence of drawing two isomorphic objects. E(P) abbreviates nonempty experience, not wakefulness, selfhood, a particular visual content, or reportability. The task/resource contract M fixes a capability map J_M on complete types. Reports are designated behavior with a separately specified semantic and accuracy target.

Physical change need not change every token's complete type. A whole and a persisting sub-process require distinct, fixed comparisons. A new drawing, new label, or arbitrary coordinate change is not an actual constitutive change. More elements or stronger coupling does not define a universal experiential magnitude.

## 3. Three central thought experiments

### TE1 — All ancestors, including the formation of the proposed gate

**Question.** If intermediate organizations are retained for comparison, which difference is alleged to mark the first experience, and why does it have existence significance rather than changing an already experiential organization?

**Scenario.** Use A's stipulated actually instantiated valid comparison family. Include the formation history of a proposed gate rather than comparing only its absent/present endpoints. Distinguish physical/chemical prehistory from biological ancestry. Duration, a finite ancestry chain, arbitrary refinability and a connected mathematical family are different premises.

**Deductive result.** A:E1 gives E=1 throughout from U1 plus actual family members. A:E2 separately transfers a supplied fine structural chain under C1 and the fixed geometry. A:E3 is the separate connected-domain/continuous-binary-E conditional. Retain A §8.3's smooth-onset countermodel against inferring universal experience from physical graduality alone.

**Positive role.** The scenario motivates a common constitutive account across organizational differences. Within that account it makes room for the emergence of memory, perceptual organization and self-representation without assigning them the task of turning experience on. It does not establish that every history is smooth or that all experiential coordinates increase.

**Missing premise if used externally.** Independent justification for the relevant experience-existence regularity or for C1. R142's content/existence and architecture/episode distinctions remain mandatory. The scenario challenges a candidate onset account; it does not make every alternative inconsistent.

### TE2 — Three communicating processes: who gains and what is the whole?

**Question.** When formerly separate information can be jointly used, whose capability changes, and does the whole's organization reduce to independent juxtaposition?

**Scenario.** C §4.2: two sources receive independent fair bits X_A and X_B. On the fixed equality task, either isolated source achieves optimal accuracy 1/2; a recipient with both bits that computes equality achieves 1. Increased information access is an explicit resource difference. No return path is stipulated from the recipient to the sources.

**Necessary split.** A recipient C_r is not the encompassing whole W. The sources do not become constituents of C_r merely by transmitting to it. If an encompassing actual W with actual constituents is selected, declare its boundary and constituent slots separately. Do not switch the bearer mid-argument.

**Positive explanation.** A new usable relation between the two inputs explains the recipient's task success in the model. It does not improve the sources' capability by fiat. For an actual whole whose complete organization is nonproduct relative to a specified independent constituent comparison, A:NONSUM transports that nonproduct structure under C1. A:COEXISTENCE retains continuing actual local tokens.

**What is not established.** Receiving both bits alone does not prove one exclusive subject, a summed quantity of experience, or complete physical constitution. A model's observed statistical correlation is not a substitute for constitutive dependence; common input can correlate independent component mechanisms. The product comparison uses nonoverlapping constituent slots with compatible boundaries and ports. The abstract product need not itself be an actual token.

**Technical companion illustration.** A §9 / Appendix B already supplies a fixed-output three-register family: whole transition images have sizes 2,4,4,8 while the same implemented output process is preserved. Pair retention is 1,2,2,4 at one step and 1,1,1,4 thereafter. These are exact finite mechanism properties, not experiential counts. The one-link case already separates fixed-split nonproduct structure from sustained retention. Do not build a new toy model to repeat this existing example, or treat its four settings as a continuous ancestral path.

### TE3 — Equal storage and output, different usable memory

**Question.** How can organization differ before a selected behavior differs, and what changes when a new information path is actually used?

**Scenario.** Reuse C §7.2 exactly. Independent fair input bits X_t and mask bits N_t enter clocked circuits. Output precedes the memory update. Circuit A retains X_(t-1); circuit B retains X_(t-1) XOR N_(t-1), with no retained mask or alternative previous-input channel. Both initially output X_t. The fixed task is to output X_(t-1), after warm-up.

| Setting | Storage capacity | Installed readout | Fixed-task accuracy |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| A0 | one bit | current input | 1/2 |
| B0 | one bit | current input | 1/2 |
| A1 | one bit | stored bit | 1 |
| B1 | one bit | stored bit | 1/2 |

**Positive mechanism.** The same readout modification succeeds only where the retained task relation remains usable. Mere register count and marginal entropy do not explain the difference. A0 versus B0 and A0 versus A1 are different comparisons: the former hides a retention difference from the chosen readout, while the latter changes installed readout. An externally permitted modification is not already an installed ability.

**Conditional experiential conclusion.** Under actual realization/completeness and a fixed well-defined capability map, a genuine capability difference implies a different complete experiential type by C:P1. It does not establish a noticed difference, greater experiential magnitude or a human-like memory feeling. The unchanged score of B0/B1 likewise does not prove unchanged complete type. C:P2_FULL requires injectivity for full identification, and C:P3 blocks unconditional scalar ordering.

## 4. Narrative sequence versus proof dependencies

The reader encounters existence, whole/part relations, and capability in that order. The mathematical dependencies branch from a common foundation; they are not an implication chain from experience to integration to intelligence.

| Branch | Required premises together | Conclusion | Stable provenance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Experience existence | C1 and nonempty actual support; actual family members | U1 and E throughout that family | A:U1; A:E1 |
| Nonproduct whole | C1; common product/signature; actual whole/parts; physical nonproduct premise | Experiential nonproduct relative to that split | A:NONSUM, a14 |
| Persisting parts | P3; actual persisting parts; U1 | Their experience is not erased by embedding alone | A:COEXISTENCE, a33 |
| Capability difference | C1 type equivalence; fixed well-defined J on complete types | Different J entails different complete experiential type | C:P1, c01 |

There is no added rule U1 -> nonproduct, nonproduct -> task improvement, or task improvement -> more experience. A selected same-output example cannot establish equality of every possible behavioral law. Nor does one failed task prove absence of intelligence in every other task.

## 5. Selected existing proof spine

**Existence branch.** Apply C1's tokenwise isomorphism to the nonempty actual constitutive carrier; nonemptiness is preserved. Then apply U1 to each actual family member (a19). Nothing about small adjacent distances is required for this route. The common-geometry assumptions belong to E2/U2, not to U1.

**Whole/part branch (a14).** Suppose the actual complete whole D(W) is not isomorphic to the declared product of the actual constituent organizations D(P_i). C1 supplies h_W and h_i in a common category where the specified independent products preserve isomorphisms. If Phi(W) were isomorphic to the product of Phi(P_i), composing with h_W and the inverse product of h_i would make D(W) the prohibited product. Contradiction. This uses strong tokenwise C1 with the product structure; bare pairwise C1-OI alone is insufficient, as A §6.2's countermodel already shows. It does not settle all other decompositions or subject count. Apply P3/U1 separately to persisting parts (a33).

**Capability branch (c01).** Equal complete experiential types correspond to equal complete structural types under C1-OI. A well-defined J assigns equal values to equal types. Contraposition yields J(s) != J(t) -> Phi(s) != Phi(t). Genuine score-profile change is required; sampling fluctuation or changed tasks/resources without appropriate domain augmentation is not that premise. No order on experiential types follows. The converse requires an injective J, as stated in C:P2_FULL.

These proofs are restated for writing selection, not newly discovered. Full rules and every original node scope remain in R137's canonical ledger.

## 6. Positive account of the experience–intelligence relation

The common actual organization is doing the explanatory work. Its installed retention, routing and readout relations support specified abilities; C1 supplies its intrinsic experiential interpretation. The account does not add an independent experiential input to the physical update law.

Within C1/U1, retaining the same admitted complete actual organization while deleting all experience is not an allowed comparison. That is constitutive dependence under the axiom, not a causal intervention experiment showing that an extra experiential force produces intelligence. Conversely, E=1 alone does not guarantee success on a particular task, as the differently performing actual realizations would all retain U1.

This synthesis is stronger as an explanation than merely reciting that experience and intelligence differ, but its mathematical content is already in the four-paper foundation. It does not solve a missing mapping to every familiar sensory quality or valence label by terminology alone. D's mechanism/valence distinction remains a short boundary note, not a fourth central case about fear.
