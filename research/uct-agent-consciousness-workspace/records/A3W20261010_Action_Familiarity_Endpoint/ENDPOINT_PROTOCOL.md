# Candidate action-familiarity instrument v0.1

Status: unvalidated prospective draft for reporting adults. No data, recruitment or external contact performed. This refines A3N's existing question; wording alone is not a new phenomenal marker.

## Frozen primary referent and item

Before any trial, identify one enacted bodily action, the body/action frame and the two compared intervals. Ask immediately after the pair:

“In which interval did acting feel more like an already-established way of acting through this body?”

Refer to the felt manner of doing the named action during these intervals. Do not ask which movement was more recognizable, successful, easy, controlled or known to have been practised. Do not claim participants can always introspect this distinction.

Record: first / second / no discernible difference / cannot judge / not applicable. Separately record confidence. Keep uncertainty and applicability categories separate; do not recode them as unfamiliarity or discard them to improve fit. Preserve answer meaning and response-key mapping in the raw record. A rating difference is evidence about J_H, not direct observation of H.

## Pre-result semantic probes

Use neutral scenarios in randomized order; ask which question is being answered and why. No scenario has a known H truth value. No correct answer is “the trained action must feel familiar.”

| Probe | Intended referent check | What invalidates primary interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| First-use action with a clear sense of authorship | H_way can be asked even if agency is high; actual familiar score is unspecified | Equating authorship with establishedness |
| Practised action currently awkward | Difficulty and H_way questions may differ; no expected H score taught | Automatically translating difficulty into unfamiliarity |
| Familiar video of one's movement, with no current enactment | Identity recognition is a different task; primary item not applicable | Treating correct video identification as primary H evidence |
| Current enactment of a well-practised other-origin movement versus a little-practised own-origin movement | FAM's stress pair: origin and current familiar character can be asked separately; expected H ordering is unspecified | Treating other-origin as necessarily unfamiliar or own-origin as necessarily familiar |
| A knowingly new action that nevertheless seems familiar | The instrument permits a possibly mistaken familiar feeling/judgment | Forbidding the answer because training records say new |
| A remembered practiced action that feels unfamiliar now | Remembered practice and current H answer may conflict | Requiring a familiar answer from biographical knowledge |

Comprehension is about which referent is meant, not conformity to a bridge or a “right” familiar score. An optional free paraphrase is assessed without R/fit labels or trial outcomes. If answers collapse the questions, retain that failure and do not treat the instrument as calibrated. Any revised wording gets a new version and new held-out comparisons.

## Parallel, distinct measures

Collect separate items for movement identity recognition, cue familiarity, explicit practice knowledge, selection ease, effort, perceived control, body ownership and confidence, plus externally measured kinematics and success. Their correlation with J_H neither proves identity nor establishes independent variation. Their scores do not set actual RetBind, L_sel or bodily congruence. A learned response policy remains a rival even if these controls look favorable.

## Binding and sequence

1. Freeze P, present I0, earlier H0, actual frame consumer, route grain, action referent, proposed H_way and report-generation endpoint. Extended history and current-only organization are separate scopes.
2. Before target comparison, verify the same-instance route/current-use and congruence assignments; record uncertain assignment as a failed installation. Do not use J_H or output accuracy to assign the axes.
3. Freeze question version, applicability rule, semantic coding, paired order, key mapping, nuisance controls and the strengthened B_ret/B_fit ordering commitments before examining the critical contrast.
4. Counterbalance display order and semantic key coding. The code check concerns the represented answer under recoding, not an ontological claim that the actual report process is causally irrelevant to experience.
5. Use a held-out cue/content probe with the same action referent. Retain all absent, uncertain, excluded and comprehension-failed observations with reasons. Do not select the held-out examples by which bridge they favor.
6. Report judgment-level results unless a separately defended signed reliability relation/error bound connects J_H to H_way. Do not infer basal experience, a unique owner or another species/system's target from this reporting-adult instrument.

## Predeclared interpretation failures

- Recognition, practicedness, authorship or ease is substituted for the primary referent: content failure, not a refutation of H.
- The hypothetical first-use route actually reuses the selected retained route: cell assignment failure.
- Intervention changes the actual body-frame consumer or action meaning: binding/transport failure.
- J_H follows nuisance, demand or key artifacts: measurement/bridge-package challenge; do not isolate C1.
- Null or uncertain contrast with weak reliability: unresolved; never absence of experience.
- Reliable judgment contrast without independently justified H calibration: supports a J_H account; H interpretation stays open.

This is a bounded, reviewable pilot specification. It does not pretend a questionnaire creates a ground truth unavailable to the researcher.
