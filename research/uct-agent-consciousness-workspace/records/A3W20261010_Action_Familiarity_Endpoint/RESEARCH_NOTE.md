## 2. Positive target content and explanatory obligation

Keep three separately described targets:

| Target | Referent | What it does not settle |
|---|---|---|
| Basic self-acquaintance | The background first-person givenness or self-presence discussed in phenomenology | Whether a particular enacted action feels already established |
| H_way | The selected, graded familiar character of the present bodily manner of doing a specified action | Numerical identity, ownership, successful control, remembered practice or basal experience |
| Identity recognition | Judging that an observed movement is one's own | The present character of enacting that movement |

H_way is a proposed refinement of R173's partial familiar target in this reporting-adult action domain. It does not revise the older target globally or assert the targets are universally independent. It can coexist with novelty, awkwardness, agency or ownership; whether those combinations occur is an empirical question. Felt unfamiliarity of an action is not absence of self-presence or experience. This prevents a study of an acquired action quality from silently claiming an explanation of every form of mineness.

Under corrected R173/R157, an actually continued body-frame trace used by the current frame consumer has a transported experiential coordinate, conditional on all same-instance C1 premises. B_ret proposes that this experience-internal relation helps explain why this manner of bodily action has an established character. B_fit proposes that present bodily congruence can give that character even without this route-specific retention. These proposals answer a positive content question inside experience. Neither C1 nor formula transport supplies the familiar-quality naming, its ordering, or measurement calibration. No additional owner is introduced.

## 3. One primary philosophical source, with a restricted role

Fuchs (2016, DOI 10.1007/s11097-015-9449-4) discusses basic self-acquaintance in §3 and acquired bodily habits in §6. His body-memory account treats earlier learning as presently enacted capacities rather than requiring explicit recollection. This motivates distinguishing broad self-familiarity from action-specific familiar character. It is philosophical support for the target vocabulary, not a controlled validation of our endpoint. His organism/life-continuity claims are not imported as UCT consciousness-existence conditions, and his examples do not establish a universal retention-to-H mapping.

## 4. What a concrete endpoint can and cannot do

ENDPOINT_PROTOCOL.md preserves A3N's paired question, adds a fixed action/interval referent, an applicability response and pre-result comprehension screening, and separates a judgment J_H from its recorded response R_J. It also makes three checks explicit:

1. An observed familiar-self video is outside the primary enacted-action comparison; recognition alone cannot answer its H_way question.
2. A person may understand the question and believe an action is familiar despite knowing it is new, or report unfamiliarity despite knowing it was practised. The instrument must permit these answers. Otherwise it measures biographical inference by construction.
3. A reversal of the numeric/button code must not reverse the represented semantic answer. Requiring H to remain constant when the actual action or report-generation process changes would be a different, unjustified assumption.

These are controls on content and recording. They cannot prove a judgment truthful about H. A comprehension pass or stable response is compatible with demand characteristics, a learned answer policy, coarse introspection, or correlated cue/effort effects. Thus QC10's independent signed evidence remains missing. Where a truth-anchored endpoint is unavailable, a result tests competing accounts of J_H with bounded evidential relevance to H; it must not be reported as identifying H.
