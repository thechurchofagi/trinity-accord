## Requested corrections and their status

1. **OR quantifier in the abstract.** The initial phrase “either consumer” could suggest a class allowing just one OR consumer with the other constrained to a pure root. The n-query lower bound concerns both consumers independently ranging over arbitrary nonempty subsets. The revised abstract now states that exact class. **Resolved.**
2. **Boundary in the protocol table.** State once that all table rows assume n>=2. The revised manuscript has the sentence above the table. **Resolved.**
3. **Node-write chronology.** Specify that the node write precedes both reads and no intervening overwrite or compensation occurs. The revised section 7.2 retains these conditions. Its illustrated intervention is a nontrivial 0-to-1 write. **Resolved.**

A minor coverage clarification is retained: the 5,045 matrix/route checks in the current code explicitly test the **zero-mismatch/equal-column fiber criterion** for every declared small matrix and pair. They do not enumerate and compare every nonzero inverse-image set. The general full-readout and nonzero-mismatch fibers are justified by the displayed algebraic proof. Calling the table row “matrix/route fiber cases” is acceptable with this scope, but it must not be upgraded to exhaustive testing of every exact fiber.
