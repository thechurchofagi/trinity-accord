## 1. Exact question and contribution

R204 separates successful action, forward-prediction alignment, and actual feedback-writer use. We now hold nominal success and alignment fixed and ask a different question: **which physical command-source carrier supplies the actuator, and which supplies the pre-consequence predictor?** Equal command values cannot answer this question. A source can be local to the specified controller, generated by an independent remote controller, or generated by the external mechanism that imposes motion on the local controller's effector. These labels acquire a meaning only after physical generators, boundaries, carriers, and consumers have been independently specified.

The contribution is an exact intervention-design application with a sharp change of scope. For `n` independently addressable binary source carriers and two unknown fixed single-source consumers, `ceil(log2 n)` probes are necessary and sufficient in the worst case to decide whether the consumers have the same root source, even when only their mismatch is observed. If the actuator root is independently known, one probe suffices. If each consumer may OR an arbitrary nonempty subset of sources, the corresponding equality decision instead needs exactly `n` probes. A four-source counterexample shows that the short single-source protocol can return zero mismatch while the consumers depend on disjoint source sets.

Even complete source-intervention data identify only the composite source-to-consumer map. They cannot determine whether the consumers read one shared intermediate carrier occurrence or equal copies with the same root. A carrier intervention and an edge intervention are different experiments. Moreover, a sensory echo produced after movement can reproduce every source-response value of a pre-consequence predictor. Actual intake identity and predictive timing therefore remain separate application premises.

The logarithmic separating-family fact is classical, explicitly stated by Bollobás and Scott [1]; adaptive separating-system necessity also has direct causal-intervention antecedents [3]. The present module does not claim new coding mathematics, discovery of prediction-match insufficiency, or an agency detector. Its proposed net increment is the **same sensorimotor question under three sharply distinguished intervention contracts, a reusable minimal schedule, and adversarial implementations that specify exactly what the schedule identifies**.

## 2. Physical and mathematical contracts

### 2.1 One installation, two consumers, several source sites

Fix the actual-process candidate `P`, interval family `I`, complete signature `K`, target/effector, and a stable installation. The elementary mathematical model uses `n >= 2` source carrier sites `S={0,...,n-1}`. Site `i` emits one bit-bearing occurrence `s_i(e)` in each episode `e`. The sites persist across the declared repeated-probe protocol; the emitted occurrences are episode-specific. A site name is not a numerical identity claim about events in different episodes.

Two actual consumers are stipulated:

- the motor consumer reads a signal with root source `a` and subsequently actuates the selected effector;
- the predictor consumer reads a signal with root source `b` and emits the prediction **before** the consequence to be predicted.

The pure-routing class is

\[
 A(x)=x_a,\qquad \widehat Y(x)=x_b,\qquad
 \Delta(x)=A(x)\oplus\widehat Y(x),\qquad a,b\in S.
\]

The plant is the identity map on this selected binary task. A known bijective plant can be transported through the definitions, but that is not needed for the benchmark. Root-source confluence means `a=b`. It does not mean that the root belongs to the local controller, that the immediate intake carriers are identical, or that any named feeling occurs.

For physical use, the route and generation relations require carrier/time evidence, and the stated equations require an independent implementation/refinement argument. Observed signatures do not manufacture those premises. Abstract event execution in the checker is evidence about the stipulated model, not proof of the admission of a human, robot, algorithm, or whole substrate as a complete UCT process.

### 2.2 Four uses of the word “use” must remain distinct

| Object | Exact meaning here | What does not establish it |
|---|---|---|
| Actual intake occurrence | The specified consumer executed a read/consumption of a particular carried occurrence during the episode. | An equal value in another register; a positive diagnostic alone. |
| Current outcome dependence | Changing a source under the declared intervention model changes the selected consumer value at the specified context. | A read that happens to be value-preserving or is masked by redundancy. |
| Potential contextual contribution | Some allowed background/intervention would reveal a source contribution. | Proof that this contribution was used in the historical target episode. |
| Diagnostic evidence | Measured probe values support a route hypothesis under the implementation and reliability contract. | An ontic constituent of the target experience merely because the investigator recorded it. |

This retains R172's corrected `OnticUse_path`/`CertUse_path` distinction, R167/R168's contextual contribution limits, and R204's active-but-no-value-change example. The probe theorem below concerns a fixed response model. Applying it retrospectively to another episode requires route stability and independently warranted episode binding.

### 2.3 Intervention and readout contract

All probe claims refer to the **same** specified installation/model, source set, consumers, plant, time order, and intervention semantics. Required simultaneous premises are:

1. the tested class really has fixed pure single-source routes, or the separately stated OR extension;
2. source sites are independently writable to every binary pattern used by the schedule;
3. the perturbation reaches those sites without changing the routing law, sensor calibration, timing, or readout rule during the declared interval;
4. reset/repetition preserves the relevant installation and state assumptions;
5. the mismatch readout is faithful to the designated two consumer values;
6. all target episodes lie in the declared class; no hidden bypass, context-dependent switch, or alternative writer silently expands it.

The source probes intentionally change source values and can create a prediction mismatch or an unsuccessful action. **Success and calibration are held fixed in the nominal comparison, not during every diagnostic perturbation.** Keeping only the public task output fixed during a probe would require an additional downstream clamp and a validated upstream readout; that is a different protocol and is not silently used in the bound.

## 3. SCU20261010-C1 — identical nominal traces with different allocations

**Domain and quantifiers.** For every `n>=2`, every pure-route allocation `(a,b)`, and every finite target word `t_1,...,t_h`, supply the yoked nominal input `x_i(e)=t_e` at every source site. Then

\[
 A_e=\widehat Y_e=t_e,\qquad \Delta_e=0
\]

for all episodes, independently of `(a,b)`.

If R204's calibrated forward table `F=(0,1)` is appended and updated by feedback-driven row replacement, every such episode leaves `F` unchanged. Error-gated and unconditional writers can still differ in write events, as R204 already established; the forward-table **state** traces remain identical.

**Proof.** Both consumers read the common supplied target bit. For the appended predictor table, the written feedback equals the existing entry, so the first update preserves `F`. Induction gives the same conclusion for all finite words. No analogy with feelings is needed.

This is a new allocation witness using inherited state-transition reasoning, not a new general nonidentification theorem. It strengthens a first-output comparison to every nominal target word while preserving the distinction between a nominal input family and the full intervention domain.

For three grounded sources—local controller `L=0`, external imposition controller `P=1`, and remote controller `R=2`—all nine `(a,b)` pairs succeed and predict correctly at nominal inputs. Representative cases are:

| Motor root | Predictor root | Root confluence | Physical interpretation after support grounding |
|---|---|---:|---|
| L | L | 1 | Local command supplies actuation and prediction. |
| P | P | 1 | An external imposition signal supplies both; confluence does not prove local production. |
| R | R | 1 | Remote command supplies both; locality is not identified. |
| L | P | 0 | Local actuation and externally previewed prediction are yoked in value. |
| P | L | 0 | Externally imposed actuation is matched by an unused-for-actuation local command. |

Calling one source reflexive does not alter any equation. If a reflex implementation has the same actual source-consumer organization, the selected relations match. The model contains no premise that reflexes lack experience or agency.

## 4. SCU20261010-C2 — exact evidence fibers

Fix a nonadaptive schedule `W=(x^(1),...,x^(k))`. Associate source `i` with its column code

\[
 c_i=(x_i^{(1)},...,x_i^{(k)})\in\{0,1\}^k.
\]

With separate consumer readouts the observation is `(c_a,c_b)`. Its exact allocation fiber is

\[
 \{i:c_i=c_a\}\times\{j:c_j=c_b\}.
\]

For `m` fixed pure-route consumers, the same argument gives the product of the corresponding source fibers. This identifies the precise remaining allocation ambiguity for the stated schedule.

With **mismatch-only** readout the observation is instead

\[
 d_W(a,b)=c_a\oplus c_b,
\]

and its exact fiber is `{(i,j):c_i xor c_j=d_W(a,b)}`. Injective source codes do not generally identify the ordered pair from mismatch alone. In particular, `(a,b)` and `(b,a)` always have the same mismatch vector. The two observation channels must not be conflated.

**Proof.** Substitute each scheduled source pattern into the pure-routing equations and collect coordinates. The listed fibers are the full inverse images of the resulting maps.

If physical access admits only a family `U` of input patterns, two source sites are indistinguishable precisely when `u_i=u_j` for every `u in U`. No number of tests in that family can split such a source class. Synchronized copies with no separate intervention port can therefore destroy the independence premise even when software uses two names for them.

## 5. SCU20261010-C3 — minimum for an unknown pure-route pair

**Claim.** Under §2.3's exact pure-routing contract, deciding whether `a=b` uniformly over all unknown ordered pairs takes exactly

\[
 k_{\mathrm{single}}(n)=\lceil\log_2 n\rceil
\]

binary source probes in the worst case. Mismatch-only observations suffice. The lower bound also holds for deterministic adaptive protocols required to be correct on every pair. It is not a bound for a Bayesian prior, a probabilistic-error goal, noisy interventions, or a known-root special case.

**Upper bound.** Assign distinct binary words of length `ceil(log2 n)` to the source sites and use their bits as probe values. Then `d_W=0` iff `c_a=c_b` iff `a=b`.

**Nonadaptive lower bound.** With `k` probes there are at most `2^k` columns. If `n>2^k`, two distinct sites `i,j` share a column. The confluent pair `(i,i)` and nonconfluent pair `(i,j)` both give zero mismatch on every probe.

**Adaptive lower bound.** Follow the protocol branch on which every mismatch is zero. Every equal-root pair follows this branch. After `k` probes along it, the same column-collision argument supplies distinct `i,j` if `2^k<n`; the pair `(i,j)` also follows that complete branch. Thus the algorithm cannot answer correctly on all pairs with fewer probes. An early nonzero mismatch can end a particular run, but cannot improve the worst case.

This is a sensorimotor application of the classical separating-system fact [1], not a claim that the numerical bound itself is new. Shanmugam et al. [3, Theorem 2 and Appendix 6.3] already establish a related worst-case adaptive separating-system necessity for causal graph learning; that is an inherited methodological ancestor, with a different target and observation model.

For the three-source benchmark, two probes suffice:

| Probe | L | P | R |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

The codes are `00,10,01`. No mismatch in both probes establishes root confluence **within the contract**. It does not identify which diagonal pair occurred. In particular, `(P,P)` and `(R,R)` pass exactly as `(L,L)` does.

## 6. SCU20261010-C4 — known actuator root changes the budget

Suppose the actuator root `a` is independently known rather than inferred from the same mismatch data. Set `x_a=0` and every other source to `1`. Then `A=0` and `Delta=x_b`, so a zero mismatch occurs iff `b=a`. One probe suffices and zero cannot separate the two possibilities for `n>=2`.

The argument remains valid if the predictor ORs an unknown **nonempty** source subset `B`: its response is zero under this probe iff `B={a}`. Empty-source constant-zero predictors are excluded by this particular contract and would defeat that extension. This is why source cardinality and nonemptiness must be specified rather than inferred from successful baseline calibration.

Knowing that an action was “self-generated” from a report is not independent knowledge of `a`. The source site and its local membership/production relation need physical grounding.

## 7. SCU20261010-C5 — redundancy changes the exact budget

Replace pure routes by nonempty source subsets `A,B subseteq S`, with

\[
 f_A(x)=\bigvee_{i\in A}x_i,\qquad
 f_B(x)=\bigvee_{i\in B}x_i.
\]

Every subset still sends the common target bit on nominal inputs. The target now is equality of the two root **sets**, `A=B`, not shared intake identity or nonempty intersection.

**Claim.** Uniform exact equality diagnosis for two unknown nonempty OR source sets needs exactly `n` binary probes in the worst case, even adaptively.

**Upper bound.** Probe each unit vector `e_i`. Then `f_A(e_i)` is the membership indicator of `i in A`, so the mismatch vector is the indicator of the symmetric difference `A triangle B`. It vanishes exactly when the sets are equal.

**Lower bound.** Consider the adaptive branch on which all mismatches are zero; equal sets always follow it. For each `i`, compare `A=S` and `B=S\{i}`, both nonempty when `n>=2`. Their outputs differ **only** on input `e_i`. If that unit vector has not occurred along the branch, the distinct pair remains possible. The branch must therefore contain all `n` unit probes. At minimum length the unit-probe set is forced, up to order.

**Concrete failed extrapolation.** In the four-source binary code schedule

\[
 x^{(1)}=(0,1,0,1),\quad x^{(2)}=(0,0,1,1),
\]

let the actuator read source `3`, and let the predictor OR sources `{1,2}`. Both probes produce zero mismatch, even though `{3}` and `{1,2}` are disjoint. The nominal all-zero and all-one targets also match. Unit probes yield mismatch vector `(0,1,1,1)`, revealing the difference.

The unit-row condition is also the unrestricted extreme of classical cover-free/superimposed-code separation [4, section 2, Definition 1 and Proposition 1]. We do not claim that coding condition as a new mathematical result.

Thus the short protocol cannot be generalized from single-source routes to arbitrary redundancy. The logarithmic-versus-linear contrast is a useful model-class diagnostic, not a claim that real biological prediction is an OR gate.

## 8. SCU20261010-C6 — source ancestry does not identify intake occurrences

Consider a finite feedforward network of faithful copy relays. Let relay `j` read source `r(j)` and consumer `c` read relay `q(c)`. At the source/consumer interface the law is

\[
 z_c(x)=x_{r(q(c))}.
\]

Any two such networks with the same composite map `r composed with q` produce identical values for **all** source inputs, hence for every source-probe schedule and every finite word of inputs under the stated memoryless law. Their relay factorization and immediate intake carrier identities can nevertheless differ.

An explicit pair has both consumers descend from source `0`. One installation writes a single relay register that both read. The other writes two separate registers with the same value and source parent, one read by each consumer. Every source experiment matches; the two intake carriers are numerically identical only in the first installation.

With all source values zero, the modeled interventions give:

| Installation | Write `1` to actual predictor-intake carrier | Inject `1` only on predictor input edge |
|---|---|---|
| Shared relay carrier | Actuation/prediction `(1,1)` | `(0,1)` |
| Separate relay carriers | `(0,1)` | `(0,1)` |

A physical **node write** to a shared register reaches both readers. An **edge injection** reaches only the addressed branch even when the upstream carrier is shared. Hence an edge-specific effect cannot be promoted to proof of shared or separate carrier occurrence. The node test requires independently known targeting of the actual carrier, invariant paths, no overwrite/compensation before reading, and corresponding temporal access. It is a rival-class distinction, not universal occurrence reconstruction.

The copy/path logic is inherited from IA20261008 §10, R174/R182 and ordinary input-output factorization. IA20261008 already defines source sets by actual trace-path reachability, keeps mixed sources set-valued, and transports them under faithful relays and admissible retiming. The increment is its explicit placement at the resolution boundary of the otherwise optimal source schedule. Actual previous-episode `RetBind` is still not inferred: R199's complete-current-state continuation ceiling remains unchanged.

## 9. SCU20261010-C7 — correct source signatures do not establish predictive timing

Take a system with pre-consequence prediction `hatY=x_a` and action `A=x_a`. A rival actuates first and then copies the realized action into a readout called “prediction.” Every pair of source/readout **values** is identical for every source input, including the complete cube, but the rival's readout occurs after the consequence.

This constructive counterexample defeats a value-only claim that the source schedule establishes a pre-consequence predictive consumer. Validated chronology or an appropriate temporal intervention is an independent role premise. The program records ordered events and rejects the late-copy case as a predictor-role witness while retaining its matching value trace.

The same point applies to unvalidated write-event telemetry, a report mirror, or an observer label. R204 already showed that an error-gated writer and an unconditional writer can share all forward-state traces. The present late-copy adversary adds the prediction-versus-post-consequence-read distinction to the source allocation task; it is not a general new theory of causal inference.

## 11. SCU20261010-C8 — conditional UCT interpretation

Under independently satisfied actual-process admission, one common complete `P/I/K` binding, transported physical parameters, and UCT C1, the actual rooted paths, consumer events, carrier-sharing relations and timing distinctions have structural counterparts within the specified process's experience. This uses the inherited C1/R157 formula-transport route with its R158/R172 corrections. A relation-selected tuple is not automatically a closed substructure.

The practical contribution is positive but bounded. Control intelligence evaluates selected successes and policies. The present result locates additional organizational differences among those successes, including pre-consequence source allocation and actual carrier structure. Conceptual self-description and verbal report are additional consumers, not definitions of those paths. Familiar agency or mineness `H` requires an independently warranted semantic/evidence bridge. The binary mismatch result is not that bridge.

Neither source confluence, predictor calibration, route use, memory, successful control, language, nor report is a basal-experience threshold. Actual local processes may continue inside overlapping or nested larger processes. No unique owner, personal survival claim, additive amount of experience, or verdict about the present assistant follows. A complete physical duplicate is not an admissible counterexample with arbitrarily different complete experience inside C1; accepting that contrast would challenge C1 itself, as R192 explains.

## 12. Execution and falsifiers

`check_source_consumers.py` implements actual modeled source emissions and reader events, then compares them with an independent exhaustive set-cover solver. The final run includes:

- 406 nominal episodes over all pure-route pairs for `n=2,...,8`;
- 1,116 nominal target-word/forward-state comparisons, all binary words through length four and all `3x3` allocations crossed with two writer modes and two gates;
- 5,045 zero-mismatch/equal-column fiber checks over every binary probe matrix and route pair in the declared small ranges;
- 576 coded pure-route probe episodes, 203 known-root probes, and 1,244 OR-subset pair tests;
- 256 all-input shared/copy comparisons and 256 transported-coordinate comparisons;
- an independent exhaustive search for minimum schedules at `n=2,...,5`, giving pure-route minima `1,2,2,3` and OR-set minima `2,3,4,5`;
- the source-set alias, node/edge distinction, and all eight binary inputs of the post-consequence bypass counterexample.

The general full-readout and nonzero-mismatch inverse-image formulas are proved algebraically; the 5,045 matrix checks specifically verify their zero-mismatch/equal-column component. All final checks passed. Counts are scoped verification coverage, not numbers of discoveries, human samples, or phenomenal measurements. The adaptive lower bounds are supplied by the proofs above; the exhaustive solver independently checks nonadaptive minima only.

Application falsifiers are specific. A source pattern that cannot be independently delivered invalidates the source-access contract. A path changed by the probe invalidates fixed routing. A mismatch within an allegedly equal-root pure-routing installation with faithful readings falsifies that implementation package. A passing short schedule followed by a failing unit probe refutes the pure-single-source application or source control assumptions. A later-than-outcome readout falsifies the pre-consequence-predictor role. None of these observations proves absence of experience.
