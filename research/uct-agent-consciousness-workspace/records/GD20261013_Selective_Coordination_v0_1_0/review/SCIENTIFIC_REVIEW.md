# Scientific review and direction check

Checkpoint UCT-GD-20261013 v0.1.0. Internal AI-assisted review, not independent peer review.

## Findings retained

- The two-register baseline is a worked application of the classical noisy voter model. Increasing agreement can coexist with faster loss of source-coded information; neither quantity is experience.
- The four-register construction compares identical nominal total cross-copy attempt rates. All registers remain coupled for positive interface and internal rates. A fixed common-input first-moment response coexists with a route-dependent private source response.
- Selective routing has a positive finite-delay lower bound when internal departure and direct leakage rates are bounded. Fixed fractional leakage is not harmless at arbitrarily large public copying rates.
- Exact reflected-module moment equations and the standard killed-walk representation extend the mechanism. The limiting principal submatrix must retain boundary-exit losses. This is a constructive scope result, not a new foundation of stochastic processes.

## Adversarial checks

1. **Continuity versus human labels.** Analytic transition probabilities are not a proof that 'ownership' ratings, integer subject count, or every human qualitative label vary smoothly. UCT I already separates these claims.
2. **No recapitulation.** Mathematical parameter paths are not phylogenies, embryonic development, learning curves, or proof that all have the same order.
3. **Constitutive completeness.** Register states and rate matrices are not declared the complete ontic organization of a brain or biological body.
4. **No source/phenomenology substitution.** A readout recovering source code r is not evidence that the system experiences r as mine. A5L's semantic barrier remains.
5. **No preserved-local-law overclaim.** Unlike UCT-RC, local trajectory laws generally change here. Only the specified common response is held fixed across the main comparison.
6. **Fresh input.** Readout success is evaluated after controlled new preparation, not inferred from passive correlation with a hidden source. Full finite state spaces are explicit.
7. **Budget honesty.** Equal sum of cross-copy rates is not equal power consumption, successful copying rate, synapse count or biological resource cost. Per-node routing changes intentionally.
8. **No isolated magic register.** With lambda>0 and g>0 the selective four-register graph is connected. Local memories still leak through lambda; the lower bound is finite-time, not immortality.
9. **Leakage scaling.** Finite h versus h proportional to g yield different limits. Lambda and mu are fixed in the stated growth results. Uniformization and generator proofs retain actual boundary links.
10. **Observation scope.** The .75393/.50189 example concerns the best decoder of one specified register, not the global optimum over all readouts. The two-register optimality claim uses full total variation and was separately checked.
11. **Limit order.** All finite parameter models have analytic finite-time laws. Infinite coupling is a limiting comparison, not a realized process. The protected-subspace limit is not uniform at t=0 for initially exposed nodes.
12. **Prior art.** Copying models, spectral modules, integration/differentiation and the killing formula are inherited. Targeted search does not establish global priority for the package.
13. **Evolutionary selection.** No selection simulation was performed. Optimizing an objective that rewards preserving the desired source bit would not independently establish that natural selection builds phenomenal selves.
14. **Map boundary.** UCT I, A5L, guide and current state were read, plus supplied RC material. A complete all-node history semantic audit was not performed. Candidate nodes remain disabled.

## Publication judgment

Keep as an integrated research checkpoint. Do not create a DOI solely for this worked application. Before any standalone claim of consciousness-theoretic progress, the next study must connect selective routing to a particular endogenous distinction used by an actual self-maintaining system and test an independently motivated structural/experiential interpretation. It must not merely add another external readout and name it selfhood.
