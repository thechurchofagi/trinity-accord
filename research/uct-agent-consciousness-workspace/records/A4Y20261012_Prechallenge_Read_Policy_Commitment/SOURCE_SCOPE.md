# Source and prior-art scope

- UCT I v1.2: `P3`, `P6`, `C1`, `U1`, complete organization versus finite view, overlapping/nested processes and fixed-boundary cautions.
- R157: definable coordinate transport, semantic residual and report-domain limit.
- Effective R173: same-instance structural transport and open selected-mineness semantics.

## External primary/authoritative prior art

- Joseph Y. Halpern and Judea Pearl, “Causes and Explanations: A Structural-Model Approach. Part I: Causes,” *BJPS* 56(4), 2005, author PDF: <https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/halpern/papers/actcaus.pdf>. Limited use: declared contingencies and the insufficiency of naive but-for reasoning in redundancy cases.
- Cynthia Dwork, Vitaly Feldman, Moritz Hardt, Toniann Pitassi, Omer Reingold and Aaron Roth, “Preserving Statistical Validity in Adaptive Data Analysis,” arXiv:1411.2664 / STOC 2015: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.2664>. Limited analogy: adaptively selected responses can defeat ordinary fixed-test reasoning. This paper does not prove A4Y's mechanism-identification result.
- Moni Naor, “Bit Commitment Using Pseudorandomness,” *Journal of Cryptology* 4 (1991), 151–158, DOI 10.1007/BF00196774; author/IBM record: <https://research.ibm.com/publications/bit-commitment-using-pseudorandomness>. Limited analogy: commit before reveal. A4Y does not implement or prove a cryptographic commitment scheme.
- IETF RFC 9334, *Remote ATtestation procedureS (RATS) Architecture*: <https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9334.html>. Limited analogy: freshness handles/nonces and evidence appraisal. Fresh evidence still does not by itself bind a record to the operative consumer policy.

## Attribution and search limits

The Boolean enumeration, fixed-versus-adaptive policy distinction, commitments, nonces and attestation are not claimed new. No exhaustive mechanism-identification, software testing, cryptography or philosophy-of-causation review was performed. A4Y's citable unit is the scoped A4X application, its exact finite countermodel and its UCT boundary discipline; originality and standalone-paper suitability remain unestablished.
