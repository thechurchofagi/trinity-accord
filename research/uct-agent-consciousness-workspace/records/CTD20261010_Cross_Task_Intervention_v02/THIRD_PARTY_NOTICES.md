# Third-party sources and attribution

## Original human study and data

D'Angelo, M., Lanfranco, R. C., Chancel, M., and Ehrsson, H. H. (2026). *Parietal alpha frequency shapes own-body perception by modulating the temporal integration of bodily signals*. Nature Communications 17:53. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-67657-w

The article states a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The publisher's source-data workbook is retained unmodified and attributed to that study.

The two additional OSF workbooks are publicly provided at https://osf.io/ytga5/. The inspected data-node API metadata did not assign a node license. This record does not infer a license for that separate project merely from the article, claim ownership of the data, or apply a new blanket license to the original files. The factual aggregated counts and saved parameter tables are retained unmodified for reproducibility with exact source URLs and hashes.

No new human data were collected. The original authors have not reviewed or endorsed the present audit.

## Released BCI code

The separate BCI project https://osf.io/s5p4v/ explicitly links to CC BY 4.0 in its public license metadata. The receipt and license record are in empirical/author_inventory/LICENSE_METADATA_CHECK.json and s5p4v_license.json.

Five original MATLAB files and one ReadMe, with their original names and bytes, are preserved in literature/code_archive/ solely as the source snapshot for the computational audit. They remain the original authors' work. The Python implementation, new fits, conditional test, and response-counterpart calculations are separate new analyses and do not overwrite the released source.

## Other literature

The manuscript and literature audit cite Yarrow and colleagues (2011, 2023, and the 2025 correction), Cabrera, Lu, and Dosher (2015), Chancel, Ehrsson, and Ma (2022), Hahn, Wang, and Wei (2026), and Plackett (1954), with page or equation locations where relevant. Complete downloaded papers, whole-paper text extractions, and unrelated EEG archives are omitted from this record. Stable retrieval URLs and available hashes are retained.

Prior UCT and SCU works retain their own versions and publication status. CTD v0.1 remains a distinct unpublished archived version and is not relabeled as external validation.
