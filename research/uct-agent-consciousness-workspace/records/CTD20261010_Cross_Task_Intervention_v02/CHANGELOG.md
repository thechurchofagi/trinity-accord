# CTD v0.2 change record

## From v0.1 to v0.2

The unpublished width-level working paper is deepened into a response-model identification paper. The previous width constraints and their non-rejections remain frozen in the v0.1 record; they are not repackaged as evidence for a shared experiential or neural scale.

The new record reconstructs the source likelihood and information-criterion arithmetic, attempts all 180 Gaussian widths with explicit unresolved cases, checks the centered response family, fits four response families with fixed cell-wise partitions, instantiates a marginally equivalent criterion implementation, and supplies complete conditional paired-readout proofs and failure cases. Three exact scientific figures, a complete 18-page manuscript and independent internal technical reviews are added.

## Corrections retained

The first shifted-model CV execution is superseded because three training fits violated nesting. Every shifted training fit was rerun with its own training-fold unshifted optimum as an additional optimizer start. The final held-out Sigma-shift versus Prior-shift difference is −60.884594527 NLL, replacing the unreliable first near-tie. No held-out counts or full-data optimum were added to a training fit.

The final manuscript correctly states log-sigma bounds in milliseconds, treats the nested solution as an optimizer start, requires joint Gaussian criteria for the correlated-criterion sharp set, and distinguishes marginal equality from prospective joint separation. References and the prior published SCU version were corrected before final review.
