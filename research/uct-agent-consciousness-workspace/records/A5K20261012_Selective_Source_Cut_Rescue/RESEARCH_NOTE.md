# A5K Research Note — Selective Physical Source versus Phenomenal Source

## Result in one sentence

A same-instance cut–rescue signature can, under route closure, establish that a readout depends on a candidate physical route while a direct `P` reference stays usable; it still does not establish that the route is the source of selected experiential target `H`, because all six `H` laws remain compatible until a separate semantic bridge and orientation are supplied.

## 1. Why A5J's next intervention does not automatically solve A5J

A5J showed that two `P`-tracking channels and full `P×C` cue exclusion do not establish semantic source diversity. Its next question asked for a failure-capable intervention that cuts the target source while leaving direct physical position available. The phrase “target source” hides two claims:

1. an ontic claim that a particular physical route `R` is actually used by `Z`; and
2. a semantic/phenomenal claim that `R` realizes the selected `H_tip` coordinate.

Cut/rescue is suited to the first claim. Calling the cut route an `H` source already assumes the second. A5K separates them and asks what the signature alone licenses.

## 2. Exact finite construction

Let `P,C∈{0,1}` range over all four cells. A candidate physical route `R` carries `P`; a direct reference `A` also carries `P` and is preserved. The proposed channel `Z` reads either `R` or the direct `P` route, with fixed binary orientation. The selected target law `H(P,C)` is independently allowed to be `P`, `notP`, `C`, `notC`, `0`, or `1`.

Two distinct interventions can generate the same observed loss and recovery:

- route cut/rescue makes `R` unavailable and restores it;
- direct readout gate/rescue suppresses and restores `Z` regardless of its source.

Canonical mechanism states are absent, cut-not-rescued, and cut-and-rescued. Across 216 models, require `Z=P` at baseline, `Z=unavailable` after cut, `Z=P` after rescue, while `A=P` in every phase and factorial cell.

## 3. Stable result A5K-D1

**ID:** `A5K-D1-PHYSICAL-SOURCE-SEMANTIC-BARRIER`  
**Type:** domain-bounded identification and non-entailment proposition; exact finite proof.  
**Status:** proved for the declared family; disabled as a UCT graph premise; not a new general causal theorem or empirical result.

### Full proposition

Under the model of §2:

1. the signature admits 36 canonical models, both physical sources, and all six `H` laws;
2. requiring the candidate route to be cut and rescued (`RE`) leaves 18 models, both sources, and all six `H` laws;
3. additionally excluding direct readout gating (`NDR`) leaves six models, all with `Z` reading `R`, but still all six `H` laws;
4. an independent semantic bridge shape `SB`, restricting the selected target to `R` with either polarity, leaves two target laws: `H=P` and `H=notP`;
5. common orientation `CO` leaves the unique target law `H=P`.

### Proof

The checker exhausts the six `H` laws, two `Z` source choices, two orientations, and three states each for route and readout intervention mechanisms. A positive baseline over both `P` values fixes the physical readout orientation. A direct gate can suppress and restore a direct-`P` channel, so the signature and even an actual route intervention do not identify which mechanism caused the failure. Once direct readout intervention is excluded in this closed family, only an `R`-reading channel can fail and recover. But `H` never enters the physical output equation; therefore every `H` law remains compatible. `SB` adds the missing semantic association up to one complement, and `CO` removes that complement. Exact counts and witnesses are in `EXACT_RESULTS.json`.

### Countermodels

- **Direct-gate twin:** `H=0`, `Z` reads `P`, route `R` is not cut, and the experiment gates then ungates `Z`. The entire observed table, including intact `A=P`, matches the proposed route-source model.
- **Semantic-free route witness:** `Z` genuinely reads cut/rescued `R`, but `H=C`, `notC`, `0`, or `1`. Physical route dependence is unchanged.
- **Complement witness:** `Z` reads cut/rescued `R`, while `H=notP`. Physical source identification alone supplies no positive phenomenal pole.

## 4. Positive explanatory advance

The result is not merely another “correlation is not causation” warning. It identifies a usable positive layer:

```text
actual closed cut/rescue of R
  -> Z depends on R (organizational source)
  -> C1/R157 conditionally transports R if complete K is grounded
  -> independent SB interprets that coordinate as selected H_tip
  -> CO fixes the positive pole.
```

The first arrow is the bounded cut–rescue achievement. The second is conditional on UCT's C1 and the full R157 selector contract. The third remains the semantic residual; it cannot be obtained by relabeling the first. This keeps self-related location within experience without inventing an extra owner, while exposing exactly which claim remains criticisable.
