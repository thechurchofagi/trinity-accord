# Source, Target, and Felt Agency: A Bearer-Fixed Replay Test in UCT

Research note SB20261008, version 0.1, 8 October 2026. Dialogue extension begun after R177 and REVIEW-20261008-04, reconciled with concurrent R178 before saving; no scheduled round reserved. Not a submitted or published paper.

## 1. Question and contribution

Can actual action-to-feedback provenance explain a selected feeling of agency when feedback content and its downstream use are held fixed? This note answers the formal comparison question and identifies why its strongest matching condition prevents an empirical decision. It does not answer which candidate describes human experience.

The bounded deliverable relative to R177 is a frozen bearer/interval contract for the reviewer's live-versus-replay comparison, an executable witness, two independently stated competing interpretations, and a distinction between an exactly matched thought experiment and a test capable of producing evidence. A second, bodily application distinguishes the limb supplying tactile input from the limb represented as owned. That distinction narrows which provenance hypothesis is even plausible; it does not establish a new neural mechanism.

Ordinary causal modeling, deterministic composition, source attribution, multisensory body representation, and isomorphism preservation are established ideas. TA17 already permits nonexclusive self-membership; R158 already distinguishes ownership and agency; R159–R160 already distinguish internal body-frame use, anatomical reference, and evidence. None is claimed as a new discovery here. The construction operationalizes REVIEW-04's proposal, which receives priority credit.

## 2. Target meanings precede the mechanism

On a declared human action-feedback episode, let F_A(k) mean the selected phenomenal occurrence of feeling that one's action brings about the displayed event k. Let F_O(k) mean experiencing the represented limb k as part of one's body. Let F_T(k) mean experiencing touch as located at k. These are distinct descriptive targets, not quantities defined by the circuit below, questionnaire scores, correctness, or the presence of a report. A binary target is used only for a bounded presence/absence hypothesis, not as a complete theory of graded agency.

Q_A, Q_O and Q_T denote fallible reports; behavior and physiology are further evidence. They do not automatically equal F_A, F_O or F_T. For a human application the interpretation and measurement assumptions must be fixed independently. No actual episode satisfying the full contract is supplied here.

## 3. Frozen source-use instance

First construct an abstract finite electronic controller, not a person. Its actual episode contains current command A, live return S, preloaded replay R, a physical selector M, injection register J, feature register C, predicted feature V, comparator U, and optional report register Q. The interval contains command production, selected transmission, injection and use. Ports and event roles are fixed before the comparison.

All values are binary. At successive clock phases:

    S = A
    J = S if M = 1 else R
    C = J
    V = A
    U = int(C == V)
    Q = U

Q can instead be absent in both conditions. R is loaded independently before the current command; matching its value to A for a stipulated paired episode is not making it a causal descendant of A. Its initialization and the current selection are included in the episode history. There is no unmodeled adaptive replay controller in this construction.

The selected active feedback path is A→S→J→C when M=1, and R→J→C when M=0. A→V→U remains active in both. Thus **absence of current-command causation of feedback does not mean absence of every causal effect of the command on the comparator**. The selector relation records the terminal actually transmitting the value, not an analyst assigning a label after observing an outcome. A conditional-dependence check below separately verifies what changing A would do to J.

Two scopes are declared in advance, and never substituted for one another:

| Scope | Carrier and signature | Meaning of source question |
|---|---|---|
| P_down | J,C,V,U,Q and their actual downstream operations, with J,V as boundary inputs | The upstream active source path is outside this signature; its provenance predicate is unavailable, not false. |
| P_episode | A,S,R,M,J,C,V,U,Q and the realized transmission/initialization relations over the stated interval | The active feedback source path is admitted and can differ. |

The primary source-sensitive comparison uses P_episode. It is physically motivated by the specified controller assembly and trace, not by an ownership rating. This gives a coherent model boundary, not proof of a human experiential bearer or an exclusive subject boundary. For P_down, equal selected operations and histories prove selected equality only; complete ontic equality would require the independent completeness premise. No finite register model certifies its own ontic completeness.

## 4. Matched witness and two candidate interpretations

Take A=R=1. L has M=1 and Y has M=0. Both have J=C=V=U=Q=1, identical consumer operations, clock phases and post-injection register trajectories. The sole changed configuration parameter is M; the selected transmitting edge changes accordingly. M itself is part of the full episode organization, so this is not a claim that full organization is identical except for an invisible label.

Let G=1 mean the current command supplies J through the nominated feedback path inside P_episode. Then G_L=1 and G_Y=0. With R fixed, intervening on A changes J in L and not in Y. This is a direct verification of the explicit equations, not an inferred neuronal intervention.

For an independently admitted human domain D*, compare these deliberately strong hypotheses:

- H_live: F_A(k)=G on D*. Actual current-action feedback production determines the selected feeling.
- H_match: F_A(k)=U on D*. Executed prediction/feedback matching determines the selected feeling, irrespective of the selected external production route.

The thought-experiment predictions are (1,0) for H_live and (1,1) for H_match. Neither prediction is an observation. Neither hypothesis is asserted necessary or sufficient outside D*. In P_down, H_live cannot evaluate its nominated path: treating it as G=0 would silently turn scope exclusion into a negative fact. H_match can be evaluated functionally there, but its phenomenal identification is still unproved.

These hypotheses are intentionally simple rivals, not exhaustive alternatives. Bayesian inference, intentions, attention, learned priors and temporal context may change F_A without either equality holding. Feedback matching is an actual operation here, not merely equal scalar values in an analyst's table; that preserves R156's installed-use distinction.

## 5. What C1 licenses

For an actual valid P_episode, assume a grounded complete signature K, its complete physical organization D_K(P), the C1 isomorphism h_P, and an independently interpreted active-path relation containing the nominated events and interval. Then the finite active-path formula G transports to its corresponding experiential structural formula G^h. In L and Y, separately admitted grounded instances would therefore differ in that selected transported relation.

This is ordinary formula preservation under isomorphism. It does not identify G^h with F_A, F_O, F_T or familiar mineness. Neither abstract circuit actualization nor the human bridge follows from enumeration. Transport uses each token's own h; no one common h across both trials is assumed. Source paths outside K(P_down) cannot be imported by looking at a larger P_episode. The whole assembly's relation also cannot be transferred to the brain subtoken without an additional bearer relation.

C1 remains an interpretive axiom. No report, agency, correctness, integration threshold, or special owner is added as a condition for basal experience. This note does not decide whether the present assistant is conscious.

## 6. Exact matching blocks the proposed evidence

Let Z be the complete downstream trace fixed by the thought experiment. Let any permitted evidence E be generated from Z by one fixed measurement kernel p(E|Z). Since Z_L=Z_Y, their evidence distributions are identical. For deterministic readouts E=f(Z), equality is immediate. For stochastic readouts it follows from the identical conditional kernel. This covers fixed reports and all readouts restricted to that trace; it does not cover an observer directly measuring M or the upstream path.

Measuring M identifies which source condition was installed. It does not decide H_live against H_match: both already agree about M. If every possible phenomenal indicator is fixed or inaccessible, an imagined F_A contrast supplies no new evidence. Conversely, permitting a fresh freely varying report through a newly admitted channel relaxes the all-downstream-equal contract and must be declared as a different comparison. A later memory/report channel must itself be specified and validated; an interval change cannot be hidden.

This is not a no-report/no-experience argument. It establishes a limit of this particular evidence restriction. It also does not prove that distinct phenomenal structures cannot be empirically discriminated under a less restrictive protocol.

## 7. One prospective failure condition

The exact witness is a definition test. A prospective human study would need a different, explicitly less restrictive contract: verify live versus yoked production, match nominated feedback/feature/timing conditions to declared tolerances, ground source-path membership for the chosen bearer, and leave a separately validated agency measure free to vary. Do not select trials post hoc to equalize an outcome used as target evidence.

Under a predeclared measurement bridge that warrants F_A-positive episodes, an admitted replay episode with G=0 and F_A=1 rejects H_live's necessary direction. An admitted G=1 episode with F_A=0 rejects its sufficient direction. For H_match the corresponding failures are U=0/F_A=1 and U=1/F_A=0. A null difference in noisy reports is neither an equivalence proof nor proof that F_A is unchanged.

If changing the source route also changes attention, efference signals, represented features or timing, the simple matched comparison has failed; that may motivate another study but cannot be called an isolated provenance effect. If human source-path membership or the independent target bridge is unavailable, record the application as open. No study is conducted here.

## 8. Bodily cross-check: source limb is not ownership target

Petkova and Ehrsson (2009) stimulated the left real hand while a right rubber hand was seen receiving synchronous touch; participants reported transferred tactile experience and ownership. The hidden right hand was unstimulated. Their laterality control used proprioceptive drift, which must not be equated with ownership. This provides evidence against a blanket **same-stimulated-limb** requirement, conditional on the experiential report interpretation. Both real hands belonged to the same participant; it does not refute organism-level bodily-source requirements. [S1]

Guterstam, Petkova and Ehrsson (2011), Experiment 3, found a laterality contrast in the two-right-hands ownership item (S5), while the touch-duplication item (S6) did not show a significant contrast. This motivates separating touch-location and ownership targets. The nonsignificant result is not an equivalence test; nor does comparing significance levels establish an interaction or a matched-phenomenology causal contrast. [S2]

The resulting UCT application proposal is a two-relation description: tactile source-to-target routing T(s,k), and binding/use B(b,k) between k and an independently grounded somatic role b. The proposal **allows s and b to name different limb roles**. These variables are physical relations; their interpretation as F_T and F_O remains conditional. A source-role swap may preserve routed feature and target binding, while a binding change may preserve tactile provenance. That is a useful bridge restriction, not a discovery of bilateral transfer or a complete ownership mechanism. It extends R159's body-frame distinction by making the source/target equality assumption explicit.

Bashford and Mehring's imitation-BCI study reports ownership/control of an additional hand despite absent brain-signal control of its movements. It is direct counterpressure on a blanket actual-BCI-control necessity claim, already cited in R158. This run rechecked the authors' abstract, not raw data. It is not the precisely matched replay experiment above, and it does not eliminate every possible causal route from a participant or prove H_match. [S3]

## 9. Thought-experiment connections

Ancestor formation: the feedback route, predictor and self-attribution interpretation can acquire their roles at different stages. Their acquisition is not the ignition of basal experience.

Abacus/calculator: equal answers or identical display histories do not fix which current action produced them. This tests source claims without asserting a felt-agency contrast in either object.

Human-realized agent: a group may implement all controller registers while each person's experiential organization remains a separate question. The ensemble's active path is not automatically a constituent's felt agency.

Copy/rewire/memory: a copied replay can match present data while its current-command path differs. Provenance does not determine numerical personal identity; an earlier shared origin is not the current feedback path.

## 10. Publication judgment and next step

**Not ready as a new standalone breakthrough paper.** The executable scope repair and the cross-source application are useful research progress, but most machinery and all cited phenomena are established. There is no new dataset, independently admitted human phenomenal contrast, or demonstrated source-to-mineness bridge. Preparing a claim of solved consciousness or treating toy checks as experimental validation would exceed the result.

A defensible future paper could synthesize source identity, represented target and actual source use under UCT, but first needs either an independently supported discriminating application or a substantial explanatory result beyond this typed reconstruction. Following concurrent R178, stop the strict hidden-source test here. The next bounded task is an action-attempt versus passive/imagined-consequence comparison with represented content held fixed, physically grounded self-reference and one failure condition. Do not add decoder/certificate layers or repeat the nonexclusive-ownership result.
