## 1. Research target and inherited scope

The author's current instruction is to continue first-principles reasoning and adversarial thought experiments, using small exact programs to check mathematics rather than designing large human/animal studies. The target is the UCT overlap-composition gap: one actual physical event may belong to multiple local process descriptions. How can their structural descriptions agree on that event without duplicating it or deleting whole-level relations?

UCT I v1.2 §6 restricts its non-summative product comparison to nonoverlapping slots. Its §7 keeps subject individuation separate. TA25 defines complete actual organization, finite views and named experiential targets separately. These are inherited, not new findings. C1 is a strong interpretive identity axiom, not established by this note.

The previous local shared-update note proved the following. If closed models L=(A,M), R=(M,B) assign the same shared next state through u(a,m)=v(m,b) on a full Cartesian domain, fixing m,b and varying a forces u to ignore a; the reverse forces v to ignore b. Thus u(a,m)=w(m)=v(m,b). This does not forbid feedback: it diagnoses the false closed-subsystem assumption. Open descriptions use one installed h(a,m,b). On restricted domains the equality is instead constant on connected components of the allowed (a,b) support graph at each m. The prior note's realization and approximation qualifications remain in force.

## 2. Coherent physical identity transport

Fix a finite typed relational signature and an actual, physically anchored comparison structure O on carrier E. State, port, timing, mechanism and intervention relations must be included when constitutive. Let U_i cover E, with O_i=O|U_i. An induced restriction is **not automatically** a complete actual process: when that premise fails, it remains a view and must not be called an independently established experiential token.

Suppose h_i:O_i -> Y_i are structural isomorphisms. On a physically identified overlap S_ij=U_i intersection U_j define

    g_ij = h_j o id_(S_ij) o h_i^(-1).

Then g_ii=id, g_ji=g_ij^(-1), and g_jk o g_ij=g_ik on triple overlaps. Proof: both routes send h_i(e) to h_k(e) for the same physical e. Quotienting the disjoint Y_i carriers by these shared-e identifications yields exactly one comparison carrier class per e in E, preserves local injections, and transports every covered primitive relation.

This ordinary transport-of-structure result shows that grounded physical overlap maps admit a coherent family. It does not show that arbitrary independently proposed phenomenal restriction maps are correct. Defining compatible maps is not empirical validation of phenomenal compatibility. Nor does a comparison-carrier quotient prove numerical identity of subjective events or select a subject.

Countercase: three two-element local charts with overlap maps identity, identity, swap have a nonidentity round trip. Their quotient collapses two distinct elements inside a chart. Individual bijectivity is insufficient. Grounded shared identity excludes this arbitrary assignment. This is not a quantum-contextuality or consciousness experiment.

## 3. Carrier coverage is not interaction coverage

Fix an admissible class of relational structures on E. Suppose every **allowed** primitive relation tuple is entirely contained in some U_i, or is explicitly supplied as additional interface data. Suppose local restrictions agree and all data satisfy the model-class constraints. Then at most one labeled full structure has these data; if a compatible actual whole is admitted, it is reconstructed.

Proof: every allowed relation fact is decided locally or by the interface data, so any two completions agree on all primitive relations. Existence and determinism are separate obligations if no actual whole was independently supplied. One may not replace the allowed-support premise with an unsupported assertion that no currently unseen cross relation exists.

If r allowed Boolean relation facts are independently unconstrained and uncovered, the unrestricted completion class has 2^r labeled completions. Additional physical laws may reduce that number.

Example: E={a,m0,m1,b}, scopes {a,m0,m1}, {m0,m1,b}, {a,m0,b}. The binary links (a,m0),(m0,m1),(m1,b),(b,a) are covered. The ternary relation (a,m1,b) is not. Adding only this relation changes no induced local restriction. Shared identity can be perfectly coherent while the joint organization is still unspecified. A macro ternary relation can be implemented by lower-arity mechanisms at a finer grain; no fundamental three-body mechanism or consciousness score is claimed.

## 4. Conditional UCT overlap-composition and replacement

Let W be an independently admitted actual whole. Let a physically grounded open/relational diagram D include its local structures, overlap identities, time, ports, wiring and all constitutive joint mechanisms. Suppose these data reconstruct its complete declared organization: O(W) is isomorphic to Glue(D). Invoke local C1 only for independently admitted actual local processes with complete compatible signatures; otherwise keep the corresponding objects as views.

Transport all diagram maps and interaction relations along the strong tokenwise C1 isomorphisms. Then

    Phi(W) is isomorphic to Glue(transported D).

Proof: the coherent carrier quotient and the interaction-complete reconstruction supply Glue(D) ~= Glue(transported D). Compose with O(W) ~= Phi(W). A separately intended phenomenal gluing operation must be shown to agree with this structural comparison; it is not established merely by naming it. Actuality and C1 are premises, not consequences of constructing an isomorphic copy.

Replacement corollary: componentwise organization isomorphisms preserving corresponding states, physical ports/intervention semantics, overlap identities, wiring, joint mechanisms and timing induce a whole-organization isomorphism. With actual-whole/C1 premises, they preserve whole experiential structural type. In synchronous deterministic models the global step is the conjunction of local open constraints and wiring constraints. Preserving each constraint maps solutions bijectively; a unique old solution gives a unique new solution. An unspecified instantaneous loop need not have a unique solution.

This supplies a modular sufficient condition for the mechanical-brain thought experiment without enumerating every global state. It does not show that a real brain satisfies a proposed finite model. Equal open behavior alone can still hide different internal realizations and is weaker than the constitutive structural isomorphism required here.

A minimal failed replacement: y=u and y=u XOR v agree at boundary v=0 and disagree at v=1. Baseline testing does not establish the open-context premise.

## 5. Joint interaction invisible on every proper baseline scope

For three binary inputs x=(a,b,c) and one shared memory m, compare

    G0: x'=x; m'=m XOR a XOR b XOR c;
    G1: x'=x; m'=m XOR a XOR b XOR c XOR abc.

Both have sixteen states, are reversible involutions, and have the same variable dependency graph. Every input influences memory in both. For every proper subset of the inputs, set all omitted inputs to baseline 0 and vary the retained inputs and m. All those response tables agree because abc=0. This does not say the full embedded local organizations agree in every context.

At a=b=c=1, G0 flips m while G1 does not. G0 has eight fixed points/four two-cycles; G1 has ten fixed points/three two-cycles. Thus no conjugacy preserving the one-step operation identifies their complete modeled dynamics. Under justified actual macro realization and C1, their complete experiential structural types differ. No feeling, subject number, intensity or general intelligence ordering is calculated. Adding the joint term does not establish greater richness.

## 6. General completion formula and positive reconstruction

Use the established Boolean algebraic normal form, over GF(2):

    f(x) = XOR_(S subset [n]) c_S product_(i in S) x_i;
    c_S = XOR_(T subset S) f(1_T).

Substitution proves inversion: a term f(1_T) occurs 2^(|U minus T|) times in reconstruction of f(1_U), so modulo two only T=U remains.

For a scope family C let D_C={S: S is contained in some C_i}. Every zero-anchored face law f(x_Ci,0_outside) determines exactly the coefficients c_S with S in D_C. All other coefficients are free in the unrestricted Boolean law class. Therefore each compatible local record has

    2^(2^n - |D_C|)

full update-law completions. For four inputs, all pair faces leave 32 laws; all proper faces leave two; scopes {0,1} and {1,2,3} leave 64.

Proof: off-face monomials vanish; inversion determines the on-face coefficients; any choice of the omitted coefficients preserves the local records. If a separately justified model class excludes the omitted supports, the local laws uniquely reconstruct the update. Alternatively supply the missing coefficients. Setting unobserved coefficients to zero is a premise, not an inference. The result reconstructs an update law, not all physical implementation details.

The cover-generated function subspace is invariant under independent binary input relabelings: expanding each x_i XOR d_i produces only subsets of an original supported monomial, and D_C is downward closed. Mixing physically distinct ports is not an admissible independent relabeling. No universal consciousness metric is proposed.

For n>k, parity and parity XOR product_i x_i agree on every zero-anchored scope of size at most k. Their reversible memory embeddings have different fixed-point counts. Hence no fixed k suffices for the unrestricted law class. This does not assert that real brains require fundamental interactions of unbounded order.

## 9. Compact executable verifier

Save as check_core.py and run with Python 3.10+; standard library only.

```python
from collections import Counter
from itertools import product

def anf(values):
    a=list(values)
    for bit in range(len(a).bit_length()-1):
        for mask in range(len(a)):
            if mask & (1<<bit):
                a[mask] ^= a[mask ^ (1<<bit)]
    return tuple(a)

n=4
covers={
    'proper':tuple(c for c in range(16) if c.bit_count()==3),
    'pairs':tuple(c for c in range(16) if c.bit_count()==2),
    'overlap':(3,14),
}
down={k:tuple(s for s in range(16) if any(s&c==s for c in cs))
      for k,cs in covers.items()}
fibers={k:Counter() for k in covers}
for word in range(65536):
    f=tuple((word>>s)&1 for s in range(16))
    assert anf(anf(f))==f
    for k,D in down.items():
        fibers[k][tuple(f[s] for s in D)]+=1
for k,D in down.items():
    expected=2**(16-len(D))
    assert set(fibers[k].values())=={expected}
    assert len(fibers[k])==2**len(D)
    print(k,'undetermined coefficients',16-len(D),'completions',expected)

for n in range(2,11):
    N=1<<n
    f0=tuple(s.bit_count()%2 for s in range(N))
    f1=tuple(f0[s]^(s==N-1) for s in range(N))
    assert f0[:-1]==f1[:-1]
    fixed=[]
    for f in (f0,f1):
        F={(x,m):(x,m^f[x]) for x in range(N) for m in (0,1)}
        assert len(set(F.values()))==2*N
        assert all(F[F[s]]==s for s in F)
        fixed.append(sum(F[s]==s for s in F))
    assert fixed[0]!=fixed[1]
    if n==3: assert fixed==[8,10]
print('ALL CORE ASSERTIONS PASSED; no phenomenal variable computed')
```
