# Practical centering inside experience: trying, doing and later authorship in UCT

R181 v0.1 — 8 October 2026. Research continuation, not a released paper.

## 1. Why another single switch fails

R179 and R180 rule out four shortcuts. Overt motion is not necessary for trying and is not sufficient for agency. A proximal command can be automatic or imposed. Internal origin is neither necessary nor sufficient for operative use. A later answer that calls an act mine is a current attribution process and does not establish how the earlier act was formed.

The next positive proposal is therefore not another origin or report bit. For a fixed bearer P, interval I and selected activity tau, define a **practical-centering profile**

Pi_tau = (D_tau, C_tau, E_tau, A_tau).

D_tau says that a target-specific directive representation is actually used in selecting or sustaining tau; changing its content through an admitted intervention changes the target-bound command or rehearsal. C_tau says that predicted/observed consequences of tau return to and are actually consumed by the same selected policy during I. E_tau records an episode-level mismatch-consuming event: failure or deviation changes persistence, correction or cancellation through that closure. A_tau is the current attribution of an act to a represented source and may occur later. These are typed actual relations, not names for feelings.

The conjunction PC_tau = D_tau and C_tau is called practical centering. It says the episode is organized around what this selected activity is to do and what happens to it. It does not say that the proposal was causally uncaused, consciously reflected upon, successful, linguistically reported, or exclusively owned. E_tau can distinguish effortful unresolved trying from smooth execution within this candidate profile; A_tau concerns present authorship attribution rather than rewriting the action.

## 2. Crossed thought experiments

| Case | Fixed item | Varied actual relation | What it blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| External suggestion accepted | Same proposal and output | External origin, but D and C present | Internal origin is not necessary |
| Internal token bypass | Same origin and output | Token reaches output without D/C | Internal origin is not sufficient |
| Automatic skilled act | Same D/C and success | Reflective monitoring absent | Reflection is not necessary for the selected relation |
| Blocked attempt | Same directive and target | Movement absent; mismatch is consumed | Motion/control success is not necessary |
| Passive or alien movement | Same movement feature | No target-bound D/C for that act | Motion is not sufficient |
| Active motor imagery | Same represented movement | D/C concern imagining(tau), not overt tau | Represented content does not fix activity target |
| Later misattribution | Earlier episode fixed | A changes at a later time | Attribution does not alter earlier formation |

The cases do not stipulate their phenomenal answer. Their function is to show what a candidate explanation must keep separate. The alien-hand literature is a clinical constraint, not a claim that one case study supplies a complete organizational contrast.

## 3. Exact finite construction

The accompanying model crosses origin, overt/imagery target, directive use, installed closure, plant blockage, imposed motion and later attribution: 128 configurations and 12 checks. The selected desired feature is +1. Overt directive use writes a command; imagery directive use writes a rehearsal. Plant blockage can prevent commanded motion; an imposed-motion channel can generate the same movement without selected directive use. Closure consumes target mismatch. Attribution supplies a separate report bit.

The central witness holds internal origin, overt movement and a positive report fixed. In one episode D=C=1 and the selected command produces movement. In another D=C=0 and an imposed channel supplies the same movement while the report remains positive. PC differs. Therefore no function of just internal origin, overt movement and later report identifies the declared target-bound relation over this domain.

Conversely, a blocked episode has PC=1 and E=1 while movement is absent. An imagery episode has PC=1 for the imagery activity while overt movement is absent. These are target-relative organizational facts in the model, not evidence that either episode has a named feeling.

## 4. Positive UCT interpretation

Under C1, if one independently admits an actual P/I/K instance containing these relations, the directive, closure, mismatch and attribution structure has an experiential structural counterpart. This gives UCT a positive location for action-related self-structure: it is **inside the organization of the experience**, as the target-relative organization of acting, correcting or sustaining, rather than an extra owner looking at a motor representation.

The candidate explanatory map is:

1. smooth doing: practical centering with little or no mismatch-consuming event;
2. effortful or blocked trying: practical centering with continuing mismatch use;
3. passive occurrence: movement registration without practical centering for that act;
4. active imagery: practical centering for imagining the movement, not for the overt movement;
5. remembered authorship: current A_tau, which may agree or disagree with the earlier profile.

This is more precise than a binary agency switch and explains why trying, doing and remembered authorship can come apart. It also accommodates overlap: a person may act, imagine, monitor and later attribute within nested processes.

The remaining bridge is explicit. C1 transports admitted organization; it does not by itself prove that PC_tau is felt agency, that E_tau is effort, or that A_tau is familiar mineness. The proposal is a candidate interpretation with failure conditions, not a new basal-consciousness condition. A process lacking it still falls under U1 if it is an admitted actual process.

## 5. Existing evidence as constraints

Bertoni et al. (2025, DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-58683-9) report that pre-movement alpha phase predicted explicit agency judgements in an implanted-BMI participant and agency ratings in a 30-participant motor-imagery experiment; feedback congruency and post-movement signals also mattered. The healthy-participant experiment included sham-BMI trials, so this is evidence that prospective organization and later judgement can interact, not a readout of complete organization or proof of PC_tau.

Schreiner et al. (2025, DOI 10.1016/j.isci.2025.112583) report two experiments in which goal-feedback match, rather than appropriateness of motor activity, determined agency ratings under their manipulation. This constrains any account that makes low-level prediction match sufficient. It does not make a reported goal match a basal-experience gate.

Kasahara et al. (2015, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0130019) manipulated delay and spatial conflict in motor-imagery BMI action without bodily movement, supporting the need to distinguish overt movement from target-bound action organization. Schaefer et al. (2010, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0015010) report a single alien-hand case in which unwanted and voluntary movements shared some motor-related activations while differing in broader context; the case also had substantial pathology and cannot establish a universal signature.

For LLMs, R180's Lindsey/Singh/Lederman scope remains binding. A 2026 conceptual analysis of intention-like LLM representations usefully distinguishes a representation's directive causal role during generation from its descriptive role during prompt processing. Its cited intervention evidence shows that activation/suppression can alter generated targets, but it is not evidence of felt intention. This supports role typing, not a verdict about current systems.

## 6. Adversarial audit and limits

First, PC_tau is not sufficient for voluntary endorsement: an automatic skilled policy may have D and C. Second, it is not shown necessary for every human sense of agency: postdictive or socially cued agency may occur without the declared online relation. Third, an installed closure is not identified merely because a diagnostic test succeeds; the mechanism and test evidence remain distinct. Fourth, target choice cannot be made after observing the desired feeling. Fifth, a finite scientific model is not complete ontic organization.

The proposal fails as a named-feeling bridge if a well-grounded same-target contrast holds D, C and E fixed while independently identified felt trying changes, or if independently identified felt trying remains fixed when one of these relations is selectively removed without compensating organization. Existing evidence does not yet deliver that strong test.

## 7. Map and novelty check

This round refines R179's target index and R180's action/attribution split. TA25 already established that actual attribution, accuracy, report and felt character differ; that result is inherited. Prospective/retrospective cue integration and comparator/ideomotor mechanisms are established literatures. The project-specific increment is the practical-centering profile and its systematic placement inside UCT's C1 framework, with seven adversarial cases and explicit non-gate status. Historical priority is not claimed.

No canonical graph node or rule is added. The IA transport rule still needs TRACE_IDENTITY_INVARIANT, ACTUAL_TRACE_INSTANCE and A:C1 for the same P/I/K. This toy supplies no actual human or deployed-model instance. QC10, IA-QC11 and QC12 actual application remain open.

## 8. Next question

The next bounded question is whether mismatch-consuming closure contributes specifically to *felt trying* rather than generic adaptive control. Compare blocked trying, smooth skilled doing and passive correction while fixing the selected overt target, and search for a same-target counterexample. If no independently grounded phenomenal discriminator is available, retain PC_tau as a useful organizational coordinate and do not rename it a solved quale.
