## The conditional paired measurement and v1.0 extension

Under known common-center calibration, one same internal Gaussian draw, independent criteria and independent known lapse processes, one population joint-yes probability identifies shared variance. Externally bounded jointly Gaussian criterion correlation instead yields a sharp variance set. The centered readout loses covariance sign and is second-order near zero total correlation. The source's separate task blocks do not supply this joint measurement, and physical zero SOA is not independently established as the common center.

The new finite-sample module projects a classical Clopper–Pearson probability interval through the entire allowed covariance model. The confidence set covers the whole true-calibration population set with at least nominal probability. Empty intersections remain empty. Degenerate readouts return a full domain or an empty set, and unequal variances are projected analytically. A complete union over an honest calibration region gives 1-alpha-gamma coverage; finite regions and continuous kappa-only uncertainty are implemented, while general certified continuous projection remains open.

The fixed 480-scenario study retains all three profiles, four sample sizes and five generating variances. All 240 valid scenarios meet nominal coverage, often with broad intervals. Wrong criterion bounds and shared lapses can invalidate physical interpretation while probability coverage remains valid. The independent-resampling target is per-task variance with zero actual shared covariance. The study adds no human observations, Monte Carlo draws or source-model refits. Its full findings are finite_sample/OPERATING_CHARACTERISTICS_REPORT.md and Figure 4.
