# Unsuccessful results, repairs and limits — CTD v1.0.0

Internal PASS reviews report successful checks within a declared scope. They do not erase failures, establish an actual consumer route, validate UCT or constitute external peer review. Exact older root metadata remain under history/v0.2_release_metadata/.

## Unresolved source and empirical issues

1. **Two descriptive widths remain unresolved.** The fixed free-baseline, free-center Gaussian procedure recovers 178/180 widths within .1 ms, including 176 within .01 ms. P1 simultaneity at 8 Hz reconstructs as 196.651359 ms versus 169.55807 ms; P24 ownership at 13 Hz gives 120.484438 ms versus 127.66943 ms. Additional starts, conventional constraints and global column-order checks did not justify replacing source values. The original Gaussian implementation was not located in the inspected public inventories; this does not prove nonexistence. Reconstructed nuisance estimates are not author-released estimates.

2. **Descriptive widths are not probability laws or neural quantities.** Reconstructed curves leave [0,1] in 96 cases at observed SOAs and 129 somewhere on the real line; 100 have negative fitted baselines. Descriptive least squares can be useful without defining a Bernoulli law. Its width is not automatically the observer's effective sigma, a neural integration time or an experiential magnitude. The earlier proportional-width branch remains separate and does not validate a shared clock.

3. **IC arithmetic is corrected, but the source execution history is not reconstructed.** The correction does not reverse the aggregate preference. The public script names a workbook not found under that exact filename, starts the Prior loop at participant 21 and specifies bootstrapping differently from the Methods. Saved-objective reproduction fixes relevant data and parameter mapping but not the actual executed revision or seed. Published bootstrap intervals and full group VBA output were not reproduced. Correcting conventional AIC/BIC does not establish regularity at parameter boundaries.

4. **Chronology and independent replication are unavailable.** The public counts do not reveal trial order, serial dependence or drift. The 30-person selected sample was not recruited here. Artificial within-cell splits assume exchangeability; they are not recovered trial identities or chronological holdouts. No new participant, SOA or independently collected dataset enters the comparison.

5. **The centered family fails a necessary condition.** The conditional symmetry diagnostic rejects centered independent-binomial responding under its stated law. Nonzero centers, dependence and nonstationarity can each violate that law. The result does not identify a stimulation, sensory, decision, anatomical or experiential cause, and it does not reject BCI in general.

6. **Adding centers improves prediction without establishing adequacy.** The two centers are fixed across stimulation, and model specification was retrospective. Shifted count-table deviances remain substantial. Nominal chi-square references with small cell counts, boundaries and possible dependence are not definitive calibrated rejection tests. The descriptive participant CV interval conditions on the artificial partition and chosen modeling rule.

7. **Bounded optimization is not globally certified.** The new unshifted Prior total is .034919979 NLL above the source saved total; a source prior outside the declared box explains part of the difference. Full fits touch a bound in 12, 14, 13 and 14 participants for Sigma, Prior, shifted Sigma and shifted Prior. Starts, gradients and nesting checks do not prove unrestricted global optimality. Saved-parameter evaluation remains distinct from refitting.

8. **The first CV failure remains in history.** Three shifted-Sigma training fits were worse than their contained unshifted solution despite satisfactory numerical diagnostics. The first scores remain in empirical/results/pre_nested_cv_repair/. The final uniform repair adds each same-training-fold unshifted solution with zero centers as an optimizer start for both shifted families. All final nesting relations pass; no full-data or held-out parameters entered the repair.

## Limits of mechanism and finite-sample inference

9. **Marginal equivalence retains a threshold policy.** The constant-sensory counterpart preserves each task/condition halfwidth, including condition-dependent changes. It is not criterion jitter changing while every threshold remains fixed, nor the original normative observer merely relabeled. The construction excludes unique sensory attribution from this marginal law; it does not show that humans used the alternative.

10. **The paired protocol has not been implemented or validated here.** Source tasks occurred in separate blocks. Two reports about one external stimulus do not prove use of one internal draw. Resampling, answer copying, second-report contamination, mismatched centers and shared decision disturbances can defeat the interpretation. A known-routing apparatus witness would not itself validate a human experiential endpoint.

11. **Joint discrimination can be weak.** Some source-derived prospective probability differences are extremely small. Centered rectangles are second-order near zero total correlation. The local n-to-the-minus-one-quarter scale for shared variance assumes independent criteria and fixed known marginals; it is not universal for nonzero criterion covariance. Population-law dimension and known-simple-pair affinity bounds are not human sample-size or finite-sample optimality claims.

12. **Coverage requires the complete contract.** One shared Gaussian sensory draw is independent of jointly Gaussian criteria. Gaussian marginals or zero covariance alone do not prove independence outside that joint family. Common centers, effective variances, finite intervals, lapse rates and the criterion bound must be calibrated for the same mechanism. Pairs are iid with fixed sample size; ordinary binomial coverage does not cover optional stopping or arbitrary dependence. One paired-yes probability cannot independently estimate an unrestricted criterion-correlation bound.

13. **Valid coverage often gives poor precision.** The 240 valid cases achieve nominal coverage while retaining broad sets at large N. The 480-case grid is synthetic finite-law enumeration, not human evidence or proof that participants satisfy its assumptions. Every profile and fixed generating variance is retained. Nuisance quantities are known; estimated-calibration coverage is not demonstrated. The theorem supplies the general conditional guarantee, while the finite grid checks selected numerical operating characteristics.

14. **Negative-control targets differ.** A wrong criterion bound can under-cover a genuine shared variance while CP probability coverage stays valid. Shared lapse indicators and guesses can make the probability incompatible with the assumed family. Under independent sensory resampling, the evaluated target is per-task variance and actual shared covariance is zero. A small inclusion rate for a positive per-task target is a misattribution diagnostic, not failed coverage of an equally large true shared component.

15. **Emptiness is not precision or a unique diagnosis.** Incompatible probability intervals produce empty sets, not forced boundary estimates. Unconditional width assigns emptiness zero and is accompanied by empty probability and conditional width. An almost-always-empty shared-lapse case is not highly precise. Under the valid contract, emptiness rejects the combined package at level at most alpha; a nonempty set does not validate that package. Occasional narrow or empty sets also prevent using population width as a universal lower bound on unconditional expected confidence width.

16. **Continuous calibration remains partly open.** Honest calibration-region coverage and CP coverage combine to 1-alpha-gamma; two 95% guarantees do not automatically produce 95% joint coverage. Exact finite calibration unions and the continuous kappa-only case are implemented. General certified projection over arbitrary continuous multi-parameter regions remains open. Sampling a nuisance grid is not its complete union. No actual human calibration procedure is validated here.

17. **Floating-point checks are not machine-certified proofs.** An unequal-variance full-domain endpoint initially returned .4999999999999996 instead of .5 with an informative label. Version 1.0.1 closes it by an analytic guard. The original module and first receipt remain in history; the unchanged-grid rerun preserves all scientific numbers. The expanded check receipt separately identifies 366 near-saturation cases that cannot be adjudicated at its tolerance and does not count them as successful direct-membership checks. The theorem concerns exact probabilities.

## Record and interpretation boundaries

18. **Document and access repairs do not change scientific status.** The historical v0.2 heading-placement issue was fixed and verified at that stage. The current preflight repaired an abstract break and Figure 4 footer overlap. Some primary webpages were blocked or redirected; accessible primary copies and retrieval records define the reading scope. Contextual large PDFs and EEG archives are omitted with provenance. Portable input paths preserve execution-source snapshots and numerical-function identity; old outputs are not rerun solely to change paths.

19. **Anatomical and experiential bridges remain open.** Fitted variances, reports, covariances and scores do not establish a neural mediator, ownership experience, agency, mineness or a UCT-specific quantitative prediction. Same-instance source, consumer and time binding, independent experiential endpoints and prospective replication remain necessary. This paper does not close the separate bodily-familiarity H and physically grounded R-not-equal-L objective.

20. **Formal and external release status remain separate.** UCT-MAP-v1.1.2 is unchanged. All CTD2 and CTD3 candidate items remain disabled. The inherited open obligations and semantic-audit debt, plus actual-calibration and certified-continuous-projection obligations, remain as recorded in governance. Internal review and user publication authorization do not establish these premises. Older publication/persistence receipts are historical; these metadata claim no current DOI, timestamp or archive success.
