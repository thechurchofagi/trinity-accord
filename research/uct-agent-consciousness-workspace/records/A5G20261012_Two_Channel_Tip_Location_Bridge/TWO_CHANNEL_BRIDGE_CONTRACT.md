# A5G two-channel bridge contract

## Question and variables

The bounded question is whether two different behavioural tasks that both reverse with a crossed-tool manipulation identify `H_tip`, the experienced distal contact location, rather than merely tracking one shared organizational coordinate.

- `I ∈ {0,1}`: the declared uncrossed/crossed intervention.
- `O_I = I`: the actual anchored distal organizational coordinate established at protocol level by A5F.
- `H_I`: the latent target. The declared target families are `H_I = I xor b` for `b ∈ {0,1}` and the invariant alternatives `H_I = k` for `k ∈ {0,1}`.
- `E_TOJ` and `E_VTI`: decoded poles of tactile temporal-order judgment and visuotactile-interaction/extinction evidence.
- Each evidence channel reads either `O` or `H` and may have an unknown binary orientation: `E_j = S_j xor e_j`.

The observed convergence pattern is `E_TOJ(I)=I` and `E_VTI(I)=I`. This deliberately generous model gives both channels their best simple interpretation while keeping organizational and phenomenal sources distinct.

## Identification questions

1. Does two-channel convergence force `H` to track `O`?
2. If a channel is independently known to be `H`-sensitive but its pole is not fixed, does convergence identify which experienced location corresponds to the positive pole?
3. What extra contract is sufficient inside the declared model?

## Minimum positive contract

Inside this finite model, a bridge may be enabled only if at least one channel is established independently of the observed convergence to be:

1. **within-`O` target-sensitive**: changing `H` while holding `O` fixed can change the channel;
2. **orientation-fixed**: the channel's positive pole is tied in advance to the predeclared `H_tip` pole rather than fitted after observation;
3. **same-binding**: the channel, organization and target concern the same participant/token interval/grain;
4. **failure-capable**: a preregistered mismatch rejects the proposed bridge;
5. **non-definitional**: report, conceptual self, ownership and agency are not used as synonyms for `H_tip`.

The first two clauses are logical identification conditions in this declared model. The remaining clauses are UCT application safeguards, not consequences of the enumeration.

## Protocol-level failure matrix

Hold fixed anatomical anchor, grip route, external geometry and response mapping. Compare active tip-use, shaft-use and handle-use conditions at hand, midshaft and distal-tip locations.

| TOJ reversal | VTI localization | Licensed conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| yes | shifts to distal tip | cross-task support for an actual task-sensitive distal organizational coordinate; `H_tip` still open |
| yes | remains hand/midshaft-local | reject a shared distal-coordinate generalization |
| no | shifts to distal tip | tasks dissociate; do not pool them into one bridge |
| no | no distal shift | no support for the proposed distal comparison |

Even the first row does not identify `H_tip` unless the independently justified target-sensitivity and orientation clauses are met.
