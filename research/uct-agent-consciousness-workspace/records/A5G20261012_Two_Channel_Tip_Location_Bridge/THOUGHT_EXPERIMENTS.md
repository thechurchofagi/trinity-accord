| Family | Fixed terms | Varied term | Refuter / result | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ancestor / formation | present anchor–grip–tip organization and two readouts | whether formation supplies a separate `H`-sensitive route | one formed `O` route can feed both tasks; shared history does not create target independence | preserves formation relevance without making ancestry a gate |
| Abacus / calculator | one register and correct public outputs | one versus two display panels | two displays agree because both read one register; display plurality is not semantic independence | direct counterexample to “two tasks therefore two targets” |
| Human-implemented agent | larger task, reports and anchored coordinate | two human operators both consult the same coordinate versus one independently tracking felt location | independent operators can still share one source; organizational agreement does not identify the larger process's experienced location | blocks agent-label and observer-count inflation |
| Copy / switch / memory | copied reports, memories and task outputs | preserve or withhold the `H`-sensitive pathway while copying both evidence channels | both evidence traces can match with invariant or oppositely oriented `H` | separates evidential covariance from the target itself |

The fixed/varied terms expose a common-cause ambiguity. None decides consciousness existence, numerical identity or a unique owner.
