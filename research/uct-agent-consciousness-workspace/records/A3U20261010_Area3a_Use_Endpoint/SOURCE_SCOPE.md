## Primary and first-party sources used this round

1. Pearl J. “Direct and Indirect Effects.” *UAI* (2001), 411–420. DOI [10.1145/3501714.3501736](https://doi.org/10.1145/3501714.3501736). Role: path-specific direct/indirect effects require explicit causal-path and identification conditions. Limit: general causal framework, not area-3a evidence.
2. Robins JM, Greenland S. “Identifiability and Exchangeability for Direct and Indirect Effects.” *Epidemiology* 3(2), 143–155 (1992). PMID 1576220. Role: classic identification limits for mediation. Limit: not a neural implementation result.
3. Otchy TM et al. “Acute off-target effects of neural circuit manipulations.” *Nature* 528, 358–363 (2015). DOI [10.1038/nature16442](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature16442); PMID 26649821. Role: primary experimental demonstration that acute manipulation can disturb a downstream circuit and behaviour independently of the manipulated circuit's normal function. Limit: songbird/rodent systems, not area 3a.
4. Johnson TR et al. “Implanting microelectrode arrays in the bottom of the central sulcus targeting somatosensory area 3a for restoration of proprioception.” *Neurosurgical Focus* 60(2):E8 (2026). DOI [10.3171/2025.11.FOCUS25916](https://doi.org/10.3171/2025.11.FOCUS25916); PMID 41621104. Role: chronic area-3a access. Limit: no reported causal block/rescue/exclusion package.
5. London BM et al. “Electrical stimulation of the proprioceptive cortex (area 3a) used to instruct a behaving monkey.” *IEEE TNSRE* 16, 32–36 (2008). DOI [10.1109/TNSRE.2007.907544](https://doi.org/10.1109/TNSRE.2007.907544); PMID 18303803. Role: area-3a stimulation can be behaviourally discriminable. Limit: artificial write, not natural-source mediation or path-specific rescue.
6. Yamada H et al. “Representation of Afferent Signals from Forearm Muscle and Cutaneous Nerves in the Primary Somatosensory Cortex of the Macaque Monkey.” *PLOS ONE* 11:e0163948 (2016). DOI [10.1371/journal.pone.0163948](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0163948). Role: crossed source-by-region response face inherited from A3P. Limit: no downstream causal-use test.

## Claim limits

- The exact twin is self-contained and does not depend on a claim that biological perturbations always spill over.
- Otchy et al. establish existence and relevance of acute off-target mechanisms, not their presence in a future area-3a preparation.
- The source search was targeted, not exhaustive. No impossibility, priority or mathematical novelty claim is made.
- No cross-study capability is treated as a single installed experiment.
