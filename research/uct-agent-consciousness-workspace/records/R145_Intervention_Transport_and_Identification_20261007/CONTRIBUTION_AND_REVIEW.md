The manuscript supplies a family T^k_u(x,y)=(y+k,x+u+k), with output x+y over binary vectors. All states are reachable, every bit affects the readout, and register updates are mutually dependent. All common quotient-descending interventions preserve observational equivalence, including individual flips. Fixed-point output spectra separate labeled structures; a pointed self-loop additionally separates zero from nonzero parameters without relying on alphabet labels. A one-tick local-reset probe identifies k. A binary rank formula characterizes the specified affine probe design. Two-tick complete endpoint maps are identical.

## Adversarial checks and amendments

1. **Is this idle padding?** No extra state is added to one family member. All models have identical alphabets, and every state is reachable in two steps. Each individual register bit changes the immediate logical readout when flipped. This rules out this specific objection, not every possible notion of computational redundancy.
2. **Is everything globally integrated?** No such claim. For n>1 there are n independent interacting pairs. The n=1 witness already gives a mutually coupled elementary pair. This restriction was stated rather than claiming global strong connectivity.
3. **Are the models secretly isomorphic?** Labeled-output fixed-point sets distinguish every k. Alphabet relabeling can weaken that claim. The zero/nonzero pointed self-loop invariant survives input/output permutation; the manuscript explicitly separates the two strengths.
4. **Can unlimited adaptive testing help?** Not with the declared readout and descending actions: induction gives identical finite-history laws. A finite-product closure check for n=1 verifies the full class of 64 descending state maps without a trace cutoff.
5. **Does the reset contradict the indistinguishability theorem?** No: it does not descend on the full state space. Its output depends on the fiber representative. A stochastic description fitted to one initial distribution would not supply the all-state deterministic claim.
6. **Are flips causally irrelevant?** No, each flips a visible logical bit. Observable intervention effects need not identify the hidden implementation parameter.
7. **Does the reset identify experience?** It identifies k in the stipulated family only. Actual-token, constitutive-signature and C1 premises remain separate. Adding reset hardware may change the physical token and needs its own bridge.
8. **Is rank a new theorem?** No. The rank/count calculation is elementary binary linear algebra applied to the explicit probe family. Its fixed independent-trial protocol does not assert optimality over every adaptive experiment.
9. **Is timing irrelevant?** No. The two-tick cancellation is exact. One-tick intermediate access is part of the separating protocol. No observation of undeclared energy, timing or metadata is allowed in the blindness claim.
10. **Does this refute Chalmers or ICCR?** No. Those claims may require richer causal organization than the shared logical quotient. The finite witness tests an insufficient identification route, not every functionalist theory.
11. **Does a common quotient itself establish an actual macroprocess?** No. Macro realization must be justified independently, consistent with A §6.4. Whole and macro bearers must not be swapped mid-argument.
