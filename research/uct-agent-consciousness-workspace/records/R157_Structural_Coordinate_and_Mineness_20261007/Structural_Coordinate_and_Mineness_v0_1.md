# From body-centered structure to a selected experiential coordinate

R157 v0.1, 7 October 2026. Unpublished conditional UCT research note. Parent map R156-v1.0.

## 1. Exact advance and its limit

R155 constructed finite bodily-source and action-efficacy attribution. R156 showed that equal prediction, attribution values and current output can hide different installed use relations. Neither result selected a phenomenal coordinate. This note identifies a non-arbitrary step that **does** follow from C1: a physically grounded relation definable in the complete signature is transported to an experiential substructure by the C1 isomorphism. It also proves that the further semantic statement “this substructure is felt bodily mineness” is not obtained from that transport alone.

The mathematics is standard isomorphism preservation. The contribution is a precise placement of the remaining explanatory burden in UCT. It does not claim a newly discovered model-theory theorem, a human measurement of mineness, or a proof that every body-centered process has the same selected feeling.

## 2. Typed setup

Fix a complete many-sorted first-order signature K, an admitted actual process token P at a declared interval and boundary, its complete ontic K-structure D=D_ontic,K(P), its experiential K-structure E=Phi_K(P), and the sort-preserving K-isomorphism h:D->E stipulated by A:C1.

Fix a parameter-free K-formula theta(x_bar), or a formula with a declared parameter tuple p_bar whose physical roles and transport h(p_bar) are fixed. Define

    S_theta(D,p_bar) = {a_bar : D |= theta(a_bar,p_bar)}.

For a bodily candidate, theta may mention physically grounded typed relations such as candidate-body attachment, sensor routing, action-consequence routing, interoceptive routing, persistence and installed attribution use. Which relations belong in K must be declared before comparison. A feature computed by an analyst but absent from D is not thereby a K-relation. A physical realization must satisfy the UCT I §8.11 perturbation criterion for the claimed role.

This is an **internal structural selector**, not R126's arbitrary complete-type projection p:S_D->Z. It picks elements or tuples by relations inside each actual K-structure. Nevertheless, definability does not make theta the correct mineness selector: many report, memory or body-use relations can be definable.

The selected felt-mineness target F_O, if used, denotes a semantic classification of an experiential substructure or episode. It is not defined by writing F_O:=S_theta(E,h(p_bar)); that equation is exactly the bridge to be justified.

## 3. Structural-coordinate transport

### Proposition T1 — definable-coordinate transport

For every K-formula theta and every correctly sorted tuple a_bar,

    D |= theta(a_bar,p_bar)
    iff
    E |= theta(h(a_bar),h(p_bar)).

Consequently,

    h[S_theta(D,p_bar)] = S_theta(E,h(p_bar)).

Proof. Induct on theta. For atomic equality and relation formulas, the equivalence is the defining preservation/reflection property of a K-isomorphism. Boolean connectives preserve the induction equivalence. For an existential quantifier, a witness in D maps through h to a witness in E; conversely a witness in E has a preimage through the surjectivity of h. Universal quantification follows directly or by negation. Constants, functions and declared parameters are preserved by the K-isomorphism. The set equality follows tuplewise. QED.

This is tokenwise and signature-relative. It does not say that a finite descriptor is complete, that h is empirically identified, or that two different tokens share a selected coordinate. It does give a positive answer to one question: **if an actual body-centered role is part of complete organization and is K-definable, C1 places its counterpart inside experiential structure, not behind experience as an extra owner.**

R126:P1 showed that an arbitrary projection on complete types can always be transported and therefore selects no content. T1 avoids that particular vacuity only to the extent that theta is independently physically grounded and picks an internal role. It does not avoid semantic underdetermination.

## 4. The semantic residual

### Proposition T2 — structural transport does not fix the mineness name

Let L be a semantic vocabulary not contained in K, including a predicate-name `felt bodily mineness`. From A:C1 plus the K-definition of S_theta alone, no unique interpretation of that L-predicate follows.

Proof. Hold fixed D,E,h,theta and therefore the same transported set S_theta(E). Consider two metalinguistic interpretation assignments: nu_1 calls that set `felt bodily mineness`; nu_2 calls it `body-related experiential role` and leaves `felt bodily mineness` undefined or assigned under another separately specified criterion. Because the L-name and assignments are absent from K, both leave every K-fact, the C1 isomorphism and T1 unchanged. Thus the unique semantic name is not a consequence of those premises. This varies the interpretation of one fixed experiential structure, not the experience of a fixed complete organization, and therefore does not violate C1. QED.

T2 is not a claim that arbitrary interpretations are equally good. It identifies the missing premise. A proposed mineness bridge B_min(theta,F_O) must be fixed before outcome inspection, source-anchored, output-blind, covariance-respecting, capable of failure or undefinedness, and explicit about residual commitments, following B:BAC. Evidence can support or defeat B_min; C1 does not silently supply it.

### Three logically distinct layers

| Layer | Claim | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Structural | theta selects an actual body-centered role in D | Requires physical grounding and complete-signature placement. |
| Experiential transport | h carries the selected role to the theta-substructure of E | Conditional theorem T1 from C1 and the selector contract. |
| Semantic/empirical | the transported substructure realizes selected felt bodily mineness F_O, and a measurement tracks it | Open B_min plus a separate measurement bridge. |

This decomposition is the main positive result. It narrows the open problem without turning it into a report test or denying that UCT already treats complete actual organization as experiential organization.

## 5. Why report cannot silently be the target definition

Let Omega=Omega_rep union Omega_norep be a declared actual domain that includes tokens with and without a typed report channel. Let M_rep be an operational measure whose protocol requires that channel. Then M_rep is a partial map with domain Omega_rep, not a total measure on Omega.

### Proposition T3 — report-domain coverage limit

No claim quantified over all of Omega can be evaluated by M_rep alone unless an extension rule on Omega_norep is separately added. Assigning `no report` to `no mineness` is one such rule, not a consequence of C1, U1 or the fact that M_rep is undefined there.

Proof. By its protocol, M_rep has no value on Omega_norep. Function application outside its domain is undefined, so it cannot evaluate a total target there. Any total map agreeing with M_rep on Omega_rep must specify additional values on Omega_norep. The choice of those values is not encoded in the original partial map. C1 and U1 supply experiential organization/nonempty experience to admitted actual tokens but no report-to-mineness equivalence. QED.

T3 does not prove that every nonreporting token has bodily mineness. It blocks only the domain error. A report measure can remain valuable on Omega_rep and can be one consequence of mineness without defining the target universally. A narrower theory that explicitly restricts mineness to report-capable tokens is logically possible, but that restriction is an additional substantive semantic hypothesis and must not be presented as a UCT basal-experience result.

## 6. A candidate positive bridge contract

For a fixed body candidate b, token P, interval I and complete signature K, a candidate bridge should state all of the following together:

1. **Actual role:** theta_b picks a physically witnessed relation complex in D(P,I), not a name, score or observer-only grouping.
2. **Internal transport:** T1 identifies the corresponding theta_b-substructure in E(P,I).
3. **Target semantics:** B_min fixes what counts as felt bodily mineness and why theta_b is relevant, without defining the target from the observed answer.
4. **Contrast domain:** comparisons keep bearer convention, body candidate, time grain, signature and admissible perturbations fixed.
5. **Measurement:** any reports, behavior or physiological responses are linked by a separate, fallible measurement rule; missing report is not target absence.
6. **Failure:** B_min fails if an independently warranted F_O contrast occurs inside a theta_b-fiber, or if theta_b varies while independently warranted F_O remains invariant where the bridge claims injective sensitivity. A merely finite absence of counterexamples does not prove the unrestricted bridge.

Candidate theta_body for future comparison is the conjunction of grounded candidate attachment, body-directed sensory routing, body-directed action-consequence routing and installed attribution use over I. Interoception, affect and temporal persistence remain optional named extensions, not silently included premises. This schema is more specific than `confidence is high`, more internal than `a report was produced`, and less presumptive than `therefore mineness is present`.

It remains a **candidate**. This round establishes that it yields a well-posed structural coordinate and locates the semantic residual; it does not discharge B_min.

## 7. Four thought-experiment families

- **Formation/ancestors.** Installing a new grounded relation in theta_body changes the selected experiential substructure under T1, conditional on a valid actual token and common K. It does not prove a binary onset of mineness; B_min may be partial, graded or differently typed. U1 remains unchanged.
- **Abacus/calculator.** An externally written label or computed confidence need not satisfy theta_body. If the abacus is actually coupled into the declared relation complex, its substrate name does not disqualify it. The physical role, not `calculator` versus `brain`, decides structural selection; B_min still remains additional.
- **Humans implementing an agent.** A program-level variable named `self` is not enough. theta_body must be evaluated on a specified actual coordinated process with typed ports. Participant-level bodily structures and formation-level structures may coexist; T1 does not force one exclusive subject.
- **Copying/rewiring/memory.** Copying a register can preserve its value while failing to copy theta_body's routing relations. Rewiring can change theta_body while preserving a report string. Under T1 the selected experiential substructure changes only when the relevant complete structural role changes; numerical identity or experience transfer does not follow.

## 8. Direction and joint-premise audit

- T1 uses A:C1 **and** the R157 selector setup at the same P,I,K and parameter transport. Neither premise alone yields the conclusion.
- T1 is not inferred backward from a report or from empirical fit. It is conditional on C1, whose independent truth is not established here.
- T2 changes only a metalinguistic assignment outside K. It does not assign two experiences to one complete organization.
- T3 concerns measurement-domain coverage. It neither asserts nor denies mineness for Omega_norep and never modifies A:U1.
- The candidate bridge contract is an open methodological package, not a derivation of B_min. B:BAC is a requirement, not evidence that this particular bridge satisfies it.
- R126 arbitrary-projection transport, R155 fiber sufficiency and R156 installed-use distinction remain compatible: they concern, respectively, algebraic projection, selected-target recoverability and causal-role omission. T1 adds an internal-definability route; no old result is reclassified.
- The work serves the main question by connecting actual organization to an experiential coordinate while exposing exactly one remaining semantic/measurement bridge. It is not a generic control result. No proof-assistant, biological realization or empirical F_O observation is claimed.
