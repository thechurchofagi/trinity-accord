## Focused thought experiment — live loop versus yoked replay

Fix one action episode, downstream injection waveform, decoded feature, consumer, use time, report script (or no report), and all post-injection dynamics. In L, the current command actually causes the feedback reaching the port. In Y, that causal edge is disabled and an internally scheduled yoked replay injects the same waveform; the current command does not cause it.

Evaluate two predeclared scopes. Under `K_down`, beginning at the injection port, L and Y have no selected source difference, so neither C1 nor a source-sensitive bridge may infer a mineness contrast from omitted provenance. Under `K_episode`, containing command, causal path and use, L and Y differ in the source/use relation; C1 supports only a corresponding experiential structural difference. A separate `B_source` is required to predict a felt-mineness difference, while a source-blind feature bridge predicts none. A result imagined in advance is not empirical evidence and does not decide subject count, basal experience or report.

Acceptance for the next research round: freeze one of these scopes before comparison, explain why its membership is physically warranted, and state what observation or countermodel would reject `B_source`. Do not extend decoder/certificate machinery until this is answered or recorded as unavailable.
