## 1. Main result

A4J and A4K treated retained-relation use and present fit as two coordinates whose interventions could make different prospective commitments. Before calibrating effect signs or intervals, a prior question must be answered: do four actual organizational cells exist under one preserved comparison contract?

The answer is exact. Freeze bearer, enacted event, consumer, constitutive route, response meaning, time window and admission signature as one invariant slice `K`. The design is an actual same-bearer 2×2 only if the realized and independently witnessed image of `(R,F)` within one such slice is all of `{0,1}²`. Four assignment labels do not suffice.

This yields a stop result for the current archive. A4A and A4H–A4K provide formation, finite-model, question, prediction and interval contracts, but no actual human, robot or other bounded bearer supplies four invariant-preserving cells with independent pre-outcome witnesses of both relation use and fit. ## 2. Crossing-rank criterion

For actual event tokens `x` satisfying `K`, let `R(x),F(x)` be Boolean actual-state coordinates. Let `Q_K` be the image of tokens whose assignments agree with actual states and whose pre-outcome witnesses agree with those states without reading the assignment label or response endpoint.

`Q_K={0,1}²` iff a valid four-cell crossing exists. Since the codomain contains exactly four values, checking `|Q_K|=4` is equivalent. This is elementary finite set logic, applied here to distinguish treatment combinations from actual organizational combinations.

If within the slice `F=g(R)`, then `|Q_K|<=2`. The familiar case `F=R` gives only `(0,0)` and `(1,1)`: attempts to disable retained-relation use while preserving high relation-dependent fit, or enable it while imposing low fit, fail by definition or by physical realization. Calling the failed requests the missing cells would confuse intervention labels with actual organization.

The result is related in spirit, not identical in estimand, to established work on factorial potential outcomes and separable treatment components. A standard 2^K design defines outcomes for treatment combinations; separable-effect work emphasizes that component interventions and their pathway restrictions require substantive decomposition assumptions. A4L's narrower contribution is an UCT application-level admission test: the two organizational coordinates must be jointly reachable and evidenced inside one frozen actual-process slice before A4J/A4K predictions are meaningful.

## 3. Compensators do not receive a free pass

A compensator can sometimes break the structural coupling. For example, a remapping may keep a practiced relation in use while degrading present coordination, or supply coordination assistance while the retained relation is bypassed. That creates a genuine cell only if bearer, event, consumer, constitutive route and response meaning remain the same comparison objects.

The finite checker contrasts two cases. An invariant-preserving compensator produces all four cells and shows the contract is logically satisfiable. A comparison-breaking compensator splits trials across two route/event signatures; no fixed slice contains four cells. Pooling them would trade the intended question for another one.

Even the admissible case proves only installability. The compensator can have off-target effects, and complete organization will normally differ between cells. Those differences are allowed and must be recorded. What is forbidden is pretending that a changed referent or response code is the original factor.

## 4. Fidelity must concern the actual event

The label-echo countermodel assigns all four nominal cells while every actual token remains `(R,F)=(0,0)`. Its witnesses simply repeat the assignments. Nominal coverage is four; valid actual coverage is one. This preserves A4B–A4G's central lesson without restarting hardware provenance work: evidence of a requested state is not the state or its same-event use.

The bounded requirement is two pre-outcome, same-instance fidelity channels with declared error and ancestry. They may be imperfect. They may not be produced by the response endpoint, questionnaire code or assignment record alone. Failing this check invalidates the cell; it does not count as a null effect and does not reject either experiential candidate.

## 5. What this says positively about bodily mineness

The positive interpretation remains that a presently enacted bodily character may include the organization of using a retained context-to-continuation relation and its current fit. A4L removes the assumption that these must be exclusive or experimentally orthogonal coordinates. They can be nested, interacting or jointly constitutive within the same experience. A successful 2×2 would help compare restricted pure bridges, but its absence does not force a unique owner or make one factor a basal-experience gate.

Under C1 and the full R157/effective-R173 premises, an actually used, same-lineage retained relation has an experience-internal structural counterpart. C1 does not name it familiar, orient a report scale or make `R` and `F` independently manipulable. The selected `H_cont` remains inside experience; `J_cont`, effort, agency, ownership, practice memory and conceptual self-ascription remain distinct.

The next main-line question should therefore not add another fidelity certificate to this uninstalled factorial. It should test a mixed, non-exclusive bridge at the level of a strict thought experiment: when retained relation and present fit covary necessarily, what experiential distinction is still claimed, and what fixed/varied pair could refute that interpretation without requiring factorial independence?
