# Phenomenal-anchor contract

## Typed objects

- `H_way(p,e)`: the fixed phenomenal target—how a presently occurring movement way feels familiar-from-within for participant `p` in episode `e`. It is neither skill, recognition, ease, agency judgment, ownership report, nor autobiographical identity.
- `J(p,e)`: a binary comparative response explicitly instructed about `H_way`. Wording makes the report phenomenally targeted; it does not make it infallible or identical to `H_way`.
- `K(e)`: randomized response-key coding. The corrected response is `R=J xor K`.
- `S(e)`: a nuisance source/agency framing manipulation declared not to define `H_way`.
- `F(e)`: a verified formation/history contrast proposed to alter the retentive route relevant to familiar mineness while the declared performance/ease covariates are matched.
- `W(p,e)`: any nonphenomenal proxy that can drive all measured channels, including learned task familiarity or expectation.

## Candidate bridge package `B_A`

`B_A` is a conjunction, not a menu:

1. **Target stability:** wording and episode window fix `H_way`; no target is swapped after data inspection.
2. **Coding invariance:** after registered recoding, `R` is invariant under `K`.
3. **Nuisance invariance:** holding the declared comparison fixed, `R` is invariant under `S`.
4. **Formation sensitivity:** under a verified `F` contrast and declared matching assumptions, `R` differs across `F`.
5. **No identity shortcut:** clauses 1–4 do not assert `R=H_way` or `W=H_way`.

## Evidence rule

A preregistered violation of clause 2, 3, or 4 defeats `B_A` on its stated domain, subject to manipulation fidelity and positivity. Satisfaction of all clauses does **not** verify `B_A`: a proxy-only model `W=F` reproduces the same response pattern for mutually incompatible assignments of `H_way`.

Thus the anchor has asymmetric force: declared failures can reject this bridge; concordance cannot by itself identify the phenomenal measurand. This is a stop rule for overinterpretation, not a claim that reports are useless.
