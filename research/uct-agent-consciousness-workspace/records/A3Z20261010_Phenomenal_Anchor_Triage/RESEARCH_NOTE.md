# A3Z — Phenomenal-anchor triage after the measurand-identity barrier

## Result

A3Y showed that recovering a statistical latent class does not establish that the class is the fixed phenomenal target `H_way`. The natural next move is an independently specified anchor. This round tests what such an anchor can actually do.

The result is deliberately asymmetric. A phenomenally worded comparative judgment, a response-key intervention, a nuisance source/agency intervention, and a formation/history contrast can jointly impose preregistered observable implications. Failure of one implication can reject the candidate bridge package on its declared domain. Yet perfect satisfaction cannot establish phenomenal identity: one nonphenomenal proxy `W` can generate every measured channel while `H_way` is aligned, independent, or opposed.

This is a useful advance because it converts “find a phenomenal anchor” into a falsifiable but bounded contract. It does not solve the semantic residual and it does not create a new threshold for basal experience.

## Exact witness

Let `F,S,K` be Boolean and let `J(F,S,K)` range over all 256 Boolean functions. Correct response-key coding by `R=J xor K`. Require simultaneously:

1. `R` is invariant under `K`;
2. `R` is invariant under nuisance `S`;
3. `R` changes under formation contrast `F`.

Exhaustive enumeration leaves exactly two functions: corrected `R=F` and corrected `R=1-F`. The data therefore retain a polarity complement. More importantly, either survivor is reproduced by the proxy `W=F` while `H_way=F`, `H_way=0`, or `H_way=1-F`. The anchor battery constrains response organization, not phenomenal identity.

## Relation to prior mechanisms

Calvo-Merino et al. separated motor from visual familiarity in action observation; Chambon et al. and Sidarus et al. manipulated action-selection fluency and agency reports. These are valuable operational mechanisms. None is a gold-standard measurement of `H_way`, and this round does not rename them or claim them as UCT inventions. R195–R203 already established intervention direction, value contamination, signed report, and endpoint-orientation cautions. A3Z adds only the explicit one-way evidence contract for a phenomenally targeted anchor after A3Y's latent-identity result.

## UCT scope

`C1` remains the consciousness-specific interpretive axiom. `U1` remains its nonempty-experience consequence for admitted actual tokens. `B_A` is an application bridge for a selected content, not a premise of C1/U1 and not an admission criterion for actual processes. Nested or overlapping processes need no additional exclusive owner. No conclusion is drawn about whether the current assistant has consciousness or fear of death.

## Positive remainder

The retained positive explanation is organizational and conditional: if a bridge survives preregistered defeat tests, the installed organization can be described as carrying a retentive, action-specific discrimination that is stable to coding and specified nuisance changes. The remaining phenomenal interpretation must still be declared under C1 and cannot be obtained by relabeling this stable discrimination as `H_way`.
