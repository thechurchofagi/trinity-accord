## Unlinked concepts, object/time/signature and inference direction

The finite SCM intentionally has no deductive link to actual bearer identity, interval, complete signature, lineage, carrier/consumer membership, intervention fidelity, same-episode use, measurement validity or token admission. Those missing links are recorded in R195-G1–G6. A successful checker or a positive laboratory contrast points from evidence toward a hypothesis about use; it is not reversed into use, experience structure or `F_fam` by definition.

Formation/ancestral, abacus/calculator/human-agent, copy/switch/memory and gradual-replacement controls preserve object, time, signature and actuality distinctions. Nested and overlapping processes remain possible; no rule selects an exclusive owner. Intelligence/capability and report nodes elsewhere in the map are neither premises nor conclusions here.

## Final direction check

R195 answers the core question by locating exactly where an intervention helps and where it stops: it can establish causal/functional orientation, while familiar mineness requires an independently warranted experience-direction bridge on the same actual organization. This returns the technical result to experience, intelligence and self rather than treating model fit or intervention success as the goal.

**Structural compatibility: PASS. Semantic adoption: OPEN.** UCT-MAP-v1.1.2 remains unchanged. R195's 9-node/6-rule/5-context module is disabled. Code/DAG success does not prove C1, validate an intervention or measurement, establish actual use, identify `F_fam`, close a reviewer item or globally prove UCT.
