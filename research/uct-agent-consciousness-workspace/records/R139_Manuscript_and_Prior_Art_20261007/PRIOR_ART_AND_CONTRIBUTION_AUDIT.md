# R139 — focused overlap and contribution audit

7 October 2026. Targeted primary-source review for a technical synthesis, not exhaustive novelty certification. External full texts are not republished.

## Decision

The manuscript is supportable as a foundations/technical synthesis, not as the discovery of three new major consciousness theorems. Randomized interfaces, controller transport, observability constraints and intervention consistency have direct antecedents. The restricted LP remains a project extension beyond A–D, but its historical originality is not established.

This does not assert that the exact LP occurs verbatim in one earlier article. It rejects broad priority claims and identifies the remaining comparison obligation.
