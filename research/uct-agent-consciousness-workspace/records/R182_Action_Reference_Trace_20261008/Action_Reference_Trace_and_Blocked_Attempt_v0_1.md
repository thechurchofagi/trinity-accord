# Action-reference traces, blocked action, and the limit of a local trying criterion

R182 research note, 8 October 2026. This is a UCT research checkpoint, not a published paper or a claim of phenomenal validation.

## 1. Question and inherited boundary

R181 introduced a target-indexed practical-centering profile with actual directive use (D_	au), action–consequence closure (C_	au), mismatch consumption (E_	au), and later attribution (A_	au). The local coordinate

[
PC_	au := D_	auland C_	au
]

distinguished blocked attempt, passive displacement and active imagery at toy scope. REVIEW-20261008-05 then identified a real defect: R181 passed the target label and the role bits into the program rather than constructing a physical consumer-path instance. The later SB20261008 supplement repaired overt-versus-imagery target binding at finite toy scope and allowed one carrier to have several actual consumer targets. It did not construct a human body/action-reference relation or identify felt trying.

The bounded R182 question is therefore:

> Can an independently specified physical action-reference trace add a held-out organizational consequence beyond local ((D,C,E)), while avoiding biological, report, introspection and voluntariness gates?

The result is positive for an organizational distinction and negative for a phenomenal identification.

## 2. Fixed domain and definitions

Fix one actual bearer candidate (P), interval (I), physical signature (K), an overt target (	au), and two installed action contexts (a,b). These may be two tasks, postures or control contexts using one effector/reference calibration. They are not assumed to be two subjects. All identities below are physical token/path identities, not equality of values or names.

### 2.1 Target-binding instance

(TBI(P,I,K,	au)) records before interpretation:

1. the directive carrier (q);
2. the actual consumer path from (q) to the overt command port;
3. the consequence/mismatch return path;
4. target endpoints and boundaries;
5. admissible interventions that preserve the installed paths;
6. exclusions of report-to-target and analyst-label selection.

This inherits the SB20261008 toy repair. R182 does not claim an actual human TBI.

### 2.2 Action-reference trace

Let (R_a,R_b) be the calibration/reference inputs consumed in contexts (a,b).

[
ART_{	au}^{a,b}(P,I,K)
]

1. the TBI for (	au) is fixed;
2. one numerically identical physical carrier (R), with actual branching paths, supplies both contexts: (R_a=R_b=R) by token identity rather than value equality;
3. both consumer paths and the common carrier are actual members of the declared bearer and interval;

5. the trace is used by the action policy, not merely recorded or copied.

Two independent registers that happen to contain the same bit do not satisfy item 2. A prosthetic effector is not excluded; biological tissue is not a clause.

## 4. Local results

### R182-C1 — Local control equivalence does not identify the wider action-reference embedding

**Type:** finite countermodel / nonidentification result.

**Domain:** the 64 declared configurations.

**Premises:** fixed TBI, matched baseline (q,r_a,r_b), matched blocked-overt local (D,C,E), movement and report.

**Claim:** those local coordinates do not determine (ART_	au) or (EAC_	au).

**Proof:** shared and copied architectures have identical baseline local tuples, movement and report; only the former has one actual common carrier and hence (ART_	au). Substitution gives (EAC_	au=1) only in the shared blocked-overt cases.

**Counter-scope:** if the admitted signature already includes complete carrier identity and all branching paths, the architectures are not observationally matched at that wider scope.

### R182-C2 — A shared trace has a held-out cross-context consequence

**Type:** conditional finite theorem.

**Premises:** the stated command equations, fixed paths, and admissible A-side calibration perturbation.

**Claim:** the perturbation changes the B-context command in the shared architecture and not in the copied architecture.

**Proof:** direct substitution into (u_x=qoplus r_x).

**Limit:** this is a consequence of the declared architecture, not evidence that an actual person or prosthesis implements it. A hidden coupling could mimic the consequence; actual path evidence remains required.

### R182-C3 — Tissue, report and deliberate/reflex labels do not identify the coordinate

**Type:** crossed invariance result.

For each shared/copy architecture, changing the biological/prosthetic label, control label or report leaves (ART_	au) and (EAC_	au) unchanged. These labels are not causal inputs to the model. Consequently:

- biological tissue is not necessary in this domain;
- an artificial effector is not sufficient;
- a positive report is not necessary or sufficient;
- calling a process deliberate rather than reflexive adds no structural evidence.

### R182-C4 — The reflex twin blocks identification with felt trying

**Type:** missing-inference witness.

A deliberate-labelled and reflex-labelled blocked episode can share TBI, (D,C,E), (ART), (EAC), movement and report. Therefore any classifier restricted to these selected variables gives them the same result. The construction contains no independently admitted phenomenal target that would decide whether their experiences are the same or different.

This does **not** establish that reflexes lack experience or trying. It shows that the selected coordinate cannot, by itself, settle the named quality.

## 5. Focused thought experiments

### 5.1 Shared trace versus equal-valued copies

Fix all local values, outputs and reports. In one bearer a single calibration trace actually branches to two action contexts; in the twin, equal-valued copies feed independent controllers. Only the former predicts the declared cross-context perturbation. This prevents value equality from becoming physical identity.

### 5.2 Paralysed or mechanically blocked attempt

The command path, reference trace and mismatch loop remain active while overt movement is prevented. The construction permits (EAC_	au=1) with movement (0). It does not infer a report or exact human quality.

### 5.3 Reflex twin

Hold the whole selected action-reference obstruction structure fixed and change only an external label “deliberate/reflex”. Nothing changes. Any phenomenal distinction requires additional actual organization or an independently grounded semantic bridge.

### 5.4 Prosthetic incorporation

Replace a biological effector with a prosthetic one while preserving the actual shared reference paths and cross-context use. (ART_	au) may remain. Conversely, merely attaching a device without installing those paths does not create it. The relevant property is actual organization, not material category.

### 5.5 Active imagery

Reroute the same directive carrier to the imagery consumer. The target follows that path. (PC_{mathrm{imagery}}) may hold while overt (EAC_	au) does not. Simultaneous overt and imagery consumers remain possible as a set-valued target profile inherited from SB20261008.

## 6. Positive UCT interpretation and its exact limit

Under an independently admitted complete (P/I/K) and UCT C1, the actual common trace, its branching consumer relations, obstruction and mismatch use are not external metadata about experience. They have structural counterparts **within** the experience of that organization. No extra inner observer or exclusive owner is required to make the relations experiential.

This supplies a positive explanation of why smooth doing, blocked action, passive displacement, imagery and later report need not collapse:

- intelligence/control concerns what the policy and reference trace do;
- experience, under C1, includes the corresponding actual structural relations;
- “I am trying” as a concept/report is a later or additional process;
- the selected named quality *felt trying* is not independently identified by the finite model.

The candidate interpretation

[

	ext{an experience-internal blocked-action relation}
]

is therefore **conditional and partial**. The defensible conclusion is not “EAC is felt trying”, but:

> A local mismatch loop is insufficient. A wider actual action-reference embedding supplies a nonlocal organizational difference and a held-out consequence; even that enriched coordinate remains compatible with automatic/reflex organization and does not close the phenomenal name.

## 7. Empirical constraints, not proof

Evans, Gale, Schurger and Blanke (2015), DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0130019, studied agency judgements for motor-imagery BMI actions without overt bodily movement/proprioceptive feedback and found strong dependence on visual goal-congruent feedback. This corrects the earlier R181 author attribution and blocks “overt biological movement is necessary”.

Bertoni et al. (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-58683-9, relate pre-movement sensorimotor state and feedback congruency to agency judgements across implanted and healthy-participant paradigms. It supports separating pre-action organization, feedback and report; it does not instantiate the full R182 trace.

Imaizumi et al. (2017), DOI 10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2016.12.028, report that an embodied prosthetic arm and an unembodied one had different postural consequences in a small amputee sample. The limited result makes a useful counterexample to a fixed biological-device boundary, not evidence for C1 or (B_{mathrm{try}}).

No raw data were reanalysed. These sources constrain shortcuts but do not identify complete organization or validate a phenomenal bridge.

This is a scope refinement and positive explanatory map, not a new general mathematical theorem or publication-ready breakthrough.

## 9. Failure conditions

The R182 candidate fails or remains uninstantiated if:

1. carrier identity is inferred only from equal values;
2. the cross-context path is fitted after inspecting report or desired interpretation;
3. target binding follows a label rather than an installed consumer path;
4. hidden common inputs explain the perturbation consequence;
5. the intervention changes the architecture or bearer;
6. the admitted trace is only a finite model view of an unknown wider system;
7. the phenomenal name is treated as proved from (EAC_	au);
8. a prosthetic/artificial realization is rejected solely for its substrate.

## 10. Current verdict and next question

R182 establishes a recoverable organizational advance: generic local (D/C/E) is too small, while an actual shared action-reference trace makes a wider difference with a prospective consequence. It also establishes the stopping boundary: the enriched coordinate is still not an independently identified felt-trying marker.

Next ask whether one independently characterized temporal profile—continued target-specific counterfactual recruitment after obstruction versus rapid controller disengagement—adds information not already contained in (EAC_	au), and whether a reflex/shared-trace twin defeats it. If the twin survives again, preserve the negative result and turn to cross-scale overlap/subject organization rather than proliferating feeling labels.
