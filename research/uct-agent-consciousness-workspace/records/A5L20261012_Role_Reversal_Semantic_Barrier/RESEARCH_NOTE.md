# A5L Research Note — Role reversal and the semantic barrier

## Result in one sentence

A grounded role reversal constructs a positive organizational coordinate `X=P xor Q` while the A5K physical route and direct `P` paths stay intact, but the full endpoint signature `E=X` independently excludes none of eight candidate `H` laws whenever an `H`-free direct-`X` writer remains possible; target sensitivity is a separate premise, not a consequence of reversal.

## 1. Question inherited from A5K

A5K stopped after identifying a physical source route under strong closure because physical dependence did not determine the selected experience target `H_tip`. It proposed a second, prospectively oriented role/polarity reversal. A5L asks whether that reversal itself supplies the missing semantic bridge or merely creates another actual structural coordinate.

## 2. Exact construction

All eight `P x Q x C` cells are used. `Q` reverses a grounded role, so `X=P xor Q`; `R=P`, direct `A=P`, and direct `Z=P` remain unchanged. The selected target `H` ranges independently over `P, notP, C, notC, X, notX, 0, 1`. Endpoint `E` reads either `X` directly or `H`, with orientation 0 or 1. This yields 32 canonical models.

The observed table is `E=X` in every cell. It leaves ten models, both endpoint sources, both orientations, and all eight `H` laws. For every candidate `H` law there is an `H`-free direct-`X` model reproducing the table. Constraining the target shape to `H in {X,notX}` leaves four models. Independently requiring an `H`-sensitive endpoint and excluding direct `X` leaves two; common orientation leaves the unique `H=X` model.

**ID:** `A5L-D1-ROLE-REVERSAL-SEMANTIC-BARRIER`

### Full proposition

Under the family in §2:

1. a grounded `Q` reversal flips actual structural coordinate `X` at fixed `P,C` while `R,A,Z` retain `P`;
2. the full `E=X` signature leaves ten models and all eight target laws;
3. a direct-`X` endpoint reproduces that signature for every target law, so reversal independently refutes zero non-target `H` laws;
4. an independently justified target-sensitive endpoint with no direct-`X` path, together with target shape `H in {X,notX}`, leaves the complement pair;
5. common orientation selects `H=X`.

### Proof

`check_role_reversal.py` exhausts eight target laws, two endpoint sources, and two orientations. The direct-`X`, orientation-zero model satisfies `E=X` independently of `H`; consequently one witness exists for every target law. If `E` must instead read `H` and `H` is restricted to the role-coordinate shape, `E=X` is satisfied by `H=X` with orientation zero and `H=notX` with orientation one. Fixing the orientation removes the complement. Exact counts and witnesses are in `EXACT_RESULTS.json`.

### Countermodels

- **H-free role twin:** `H=P`, `E` reads `X` directly, orientation zero. All role-reversal observations match the aligned target model.
- **Constant-target twin:** `H=0`, `E` still reads `X`; the complete table is preserved.
- **Complement target:** `H=notX`, `E` reads `H` with inverted orientation. Even target sensitivity does not fix the positive pole.

The reversal is not empty. It creates an actual relation-sensitive coordinate distinct from physical position: `X` changes when role `Q` changes while `P,R,A,Z` do not. Under C1 and the full R157 selector contract, such an actually grounded coordinate is a candidate for conditional transport into experience. This gives a positive organizational account of body/tool-related mineness as sensitivity to an actual role relation rather than an extra exclusive owner.

## 5. Stop result

The endpoint-escalation program therefore stops at organizational `R` dependence plus grounded `X` structure.
