## Joint satisfiability and type checks

`S_H`, `C_H`, and `O_H` are jointly intelligible but not jointly established. No contradiction follows from an item being semantically admissible while unreliable, or from a reliable report existing without the candidate retained physical route. This is precisely why the premises must remain separate.

No object/time/signature/actuality/evidence substitution is licensed:

- `S_H` concerns a participant's interpretation policy in a declared reporting domain.
- `C_H` would concern the relation between a judgement and a particular experience on a target episode.
- `O_H` concerns actual physical route/use and fit on that same episode.
- observation-only recognition, abstract answer tables, stored practice labels, and finite models do not become actual same-instance organization.

## Direction and unlinked concepts

The only justified direction is: a finite semantic pass is compatible with both calibrated and uncalibrated response policies. Therefore it cannot identify calibration. The converse inference from stable answers to target truth is rejected. The neighbouring concepts `O_self`, `K_practice`, `A_control`, and `E_ease` are intentionally not connected to `H_way` by completed edges; their possible covariance remains open.

## Audit ceiling

The machine traversal checks identifiers, references, counts, conjunction structure, and the unchanged graph hash. It is not a proof of theoretical truth or a full semantic audit of all 424 rules. The inherited `SCU-AUDIT-DEPTH` item therefore remains open. Manual semantic review in A3X is limited to the listed target family and its downstream boundary.
