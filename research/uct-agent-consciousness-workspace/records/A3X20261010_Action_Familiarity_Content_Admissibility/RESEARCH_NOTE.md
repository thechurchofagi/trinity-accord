# A3X — Action-familiarity content admissibility

Version: `A3X-CONTENT-v0.1.0`  
Status: scoped conceptual and methodological advance; no participant data, no installed bridge, no completed-map extension

## Question

Can the A3W `H_way` endpoint be asked as a question about the familiar felt character of a **presently enacted bodily manner**, without silently becoming a question about self-source recognition, known practice history, control/agency, or ease? If the item survives that content check, what remains unproved?

## Typed vocabulary

For a reporting-capable adult performing a specified action `a` during interval `I`:

- `H_way(a,I)`: the selected graded familiar character of the present bodily manner of doing `a` in `I`.
- `J_H(a,I)`: the participant's coded judgement offered in response to the `H_way` item.
- `O_self(a,I)`: recognition or belief that the observed/performed movement is one's own or self-originated.
- `K_practice(a,I)`: knowledge or belief about one's practice history for the action or manner.
- `A_control(a,I)`: felt control or authorship in the current action.
- `E_ease(a,I)`: felt or observed ease, fluency, or low effort.

These are selectable targets, not an exhaustive partition of bodily self-experience. They may overlap and covary. The claim is only that none of the four proxies is definitionally identical to `H_way` in this application.

## Result 1: a semantic-admissibility gate

Define `S_H(p,D)=1` only when participant `p`, within declared domain `D`, meets all four content conditions:

1. **present-enactment applicability** — the item is treated as applicable only when the specified action is presently enacted through the relevant body; mere video recognition or narrative recall is not enough;
2. **referent paraphrase** — the participant's own paraphrase points to how the action presently feels as a bodily manner of doing, not merely to who produced it or how much it was practised;
3. **proxy non-substitution** — across held-out neutral vignettes, the participant does not state a rule that simply equates the answer with `O_self`, `K_practice`, `A_control`, or `E_ease`;
4. **coding invariance** — reversing response-key labels while keeping content fixed does not reverse the stated interpretation.

This is an **admissibility** gate. A failure means that the endpoint content is unresolved for that participant/domain. It does not mean that `H_way` is absent. A pass means only that the report policy is not visibly substituting one of the named proxies.

## Result 2: finite content checks do not calibrate truth

Let a finite content battery be `V={v_1,...,v_n}` and let its accepted answer vector be `a=(a_1,...,a_n)`. Define a response policy `pi_V` by lookup: `pi_V(v_i)=a_i`. The policy can pass every semantic check in `V` while being independent of the participant's particular `H_way(a,I)` value on later target trials. Two admissible worlds can therefore share `V`, `a`, and the overt response policy while differing in whether later `J_H` tracks `H_way`.

So, for unrestricted response policies:

`S_H = 1` does **not** entail an independently justified signed relation `C_H: J_H -> H_way`.

This is a finite non-identification witness, not a novel impossibility theorem and not a claim that calibration is impossible. Independent constraints, convergent methods, interventions, reliability studies, or a defensible causal measurement model could restrict the response-policy class. A3X supplies none of those.

## Result 3: three simultaneous warrant layers

An `H_way`-level organizational comparison requires three distinct layers:

`S_H AND C_H AND O_H`.

- `S_H` — content admissibility in the declared reporting domain.
- `C_H` — independently justified direction, sign, and reliability of `J_H` as evidence about `H_way`, with uncertainty and failure handling.
- `O_H` — same-instance actual organizational evidence: the candidate retained route/use and comparison fit are physically instantiated for the very episodes being compared.

Passing `S_H` does not discharge `C_H`. Passing both does not discharge `O_H`. A result below all three layers must stay at the judgement/content level. This decomposition prevents a slide from wording comprehension to experience, and from experience reports to an actual organizational bearer.

## Proxy-substitution stress cases

The cases are interpretation probes, not assigned phenomenal ground truth and not empirical evidence of dissociation.

| Case | Fixed | Varied | What it blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| recognized-self video, no enactment | visual source information | present enactment absent | `O_self` cannot make `H_way` item applicable |
| practised-other manner enacted now | current enactment | source is known other; practice high | source recognition cannot define `H_way` |
| little-practised own manner enacted now | current enactment; self-source | practice low | self-source cannot entail familiar manner |
| highly practised but perturbed/awkward enactment | practice history | ease/control reduced | practice cannot dictate the present judgement |
| novel guided but fluent enactment | low practice | ease/control high | fluency/control cannot define familiarity |
| knowingly new yet reported familiar | present enactment | declarative novelty held fixed | knowledge of novelty does not forbid the report |
| remembered practised yet reported unfamiliar | present enactment | declarative practice held fixed | remembered practice does not require the report |
| first-use action with clear control | control high | practice absent | agency/control cannot entail familiar manner |

Every vignette permits `no difference`, `cannot judge`, or `not applicable` where appropriate. No vignette tells the participant which `H_way` rating is correct.

## Four retained thought-experiment families

| Family | Fixed component | Varied component | Counterexample role |
|---|---|---|---|
| ancestor / formation | basal experience under UCT C1/U1 | learned action history and bodily manner | blocks making acquired `H_way` a gate for basic experience |
| abacus / calculator | accepted answers to a finite content battery | lookup implementation versus experience-sensitive response formation | witnesses `S_H` without `C_H` |
| human-realized agent | abstract input-output response table | which humans enact, judge, or relay the answer | blocks attribution from an abstract policy to an actual experiential bearer |
| copy / switch / memory | copied narrative or stored practice labels | actual current route, body, and retained use | blocks equating copied memory labels with same-instance familiar manner |

## Primary-source boundary

The checked neighbouring literature does not close `C_H`:

- Gardner, Goulden, and Cross (2015, DOI `10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2942-14.2015`) studied familiarity with **observed** movements and action-observation-network connectivity. Their operational material concerns recognizing/predicting observed action, not the familiar felt character of one's currently enacted manner.
- Bläsing and Sauzet (2018, DOI `10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01909`) tested recognition of learned, observed, and self-created point-light actions. It helps separate self-recognition, visual familiarity, and learning history, but it is not a calibration of present enacted `H_way`.
- Christensen and Grünbaum (2020, DOI `10.1093/nc/niaa019`) explicitly distinguish ability from phenomenal-character measures and bodily from external agency, and identify construct-validity and task-transparency problems. This supports the need for a content gate but does not validate the A3W item.

This search was deliberately narrow, not a systematic review. No checked paper supplies a signed, independently validated `J_H -> H_way` relation for this endpoint.

## Relation to UCT's founding aim

The result keeps the felt self **inside experience**: `H_way` is a possible selected phenomenal coordinate of an action episode, not an extra owner and not a condition for experience. It also keeps intelligence and report in their proper roles. Understanding a question and producing a stable answer are finite cognitive/report achievements; they are not automatically the experience itself. UCT C1 remains the consciousness-specific explanatory postulate for actual organization and is neither replaced nor strengthened by this protocol.

## Exact stopping point

A3X ends after specifying `S_H`, showing its non-calibration ceiling, and separating it from `C_H` and `O_H`. It does not produce participant observations, estimate reliability, assign `H_way` truth values, instantiate `B_ret` or `B_fit`, or add a completed graph node. The next main-line question is whether any independently justified and bounded `C_H` relation can be obtained before a real retained-route/congruence contrast is attempted.
