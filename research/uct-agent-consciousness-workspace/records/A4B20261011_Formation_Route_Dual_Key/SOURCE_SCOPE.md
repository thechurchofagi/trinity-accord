# A4B source scope

## Fixed project sources read

- **UCT I v1.2**, C1/U1, §§8.11–8.14 and §14.7. These preserve C1 as the single consciousness-specific interpretive axiom, derive nonempty experience through U1, require physical witnesses, place body-centered organization and the conceptual I inside experience, and forbid an extra owner prerequisite.
- **R157**, coordinate transport and semantic residual. Transport remains conditional on a complete actual same-token/signature binding and does not select a familiar-mineness label.
- **R173**, effective retentive binding. A retained value or archive is not enough; actual prior occurrence, continuation, current consumer and current use must be distinguished.
- **A3X/A3Z**, endpoint identity and one-way anchor limits. Organizational/behavioral discrimination does not construct H.
- **A4A**, randomized formation intervention. The target is a total formation effect without post-formation matching/adjustment; causal conditioning and intervention are distinct.

## Primary antecedents inherited and checked through A4A

- Robins and Greenland (1992), *Identifiability and Exchangeability for Direct and Indirect Effects*, DOI `10.1097/00001648-199203000-00013`.
- Pearl (2001), *Direct and Indirect Effects*, UAI 17; author-posted version `arXiv:1301.2300`.
- Hernán, Hernández-Díaz and Robins (2004), *A Structural Approach to Selection Bias*, **Epidemiology** 15:615–625.

These sources support the causal distinction among total, direct and path-specific effects and the danger of conditioning on descendants/common effects. A4B does not claim those ideas, XOR countermodels, randomized factorial designs, hashes, subprocesses or event logs as new mathematics or mechanisms.

## New scoped contribution

A4B's project contribution is the exact four-quadrant separation of formation effect from actual retained-route use, its two-key UCT application contract, and one auditable software-level joint installation with a foreign-copy challenge. No new empirical neuroscience claim is made and no new external source search was needed.
