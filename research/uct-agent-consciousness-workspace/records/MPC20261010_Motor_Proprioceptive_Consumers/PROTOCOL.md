# Consumer-gate protocol card

## Frozen item

One bearer/process candidate, interval, complete declared signature, plant movement/tactile consequence, selected downstream consumer, motor input carrier, proprioceptive/state input carrier, endpoint instrument, attention/body-state context, reset distribution and chronology.

## Factorial intervention

Cross branch-local availability at the selected consumer: `M in {0,1}` by `P in {0,1}`. The plant-driving path must remain separate so all four cells can preserve the declared movement and tactile consequence. Carrier existence outside the consumer is not consumer intake.

## Required endpoint vector

Record separately: perceived intensity/bias, discrimination precision/sensitivity, agency, ownership, and any language report. Do not average them into one self score. Every coordinate requires its own instrument and bridge.

## Actual-use evidence

Validate intake-event logging or a branch-local perturbation with declared sole-route/no-cancellation premises. Endpoint response alone does not identify a read event because eager and short-circuit consumers can share the full response table.

## Failure criteria

- `10` not physically realized;
- branch gate changes movement, contact, timing, muscle state, effort, attention or body-state context outside the frozen contract;
- endpoint signs or thresholds are fitted after observation;
- availability is called use without read evidence;
- intensity, precision, agency, ownership or report is substituted for another target;
- C1 or a basal experience gate is claimed from task performance.

## Stopping rule

Once the missing-cell functional ambiguity and the full-table use ambiguity are exposed, stop adding synthetic sources. The next admissible work is physical feasibility of one branch-isolation design or a proof that no current human manipulation meets the contract.
