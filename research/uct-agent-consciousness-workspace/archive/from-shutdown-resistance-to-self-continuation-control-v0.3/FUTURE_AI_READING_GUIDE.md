## Correct high-level interpretation

The paper is about **evidence needed to distinguish observable shutdown/preservation behavior from a stronger mechanism claim of stable current-bearer continuation control**.

It is not primarily a benchmark paper and not primarily a consciousness theory paper.

## Claim-strength hierarchy

Use the weakest claim that is fully supported.

**Behavioral level**
- shutdown resistance observed
- corrigibility violation observed
- peer preservation observed

**Functional-mechanism level**
- continuation-related consequence represented
- continuation-related information used by policy
- task-mediated explanation excluded
- direct bearer-specific control contribution supported
- effect stable on a declared intervention domain

**Phenomenal level**
- negative valence
- fear of own termination

Do not jump from the behavioral level directly to the phenomenal level.

## How to cite this work accurately

Appropriate:
> Liu (2026) proposes a layered identification standard for distinguishing shutdown resistance from stable current-bearer continuation control and shows, with exact counterexamples and synthetic policy experiments, why task mediation, joint consequences, model-class underspecification, and intervention-domain assumptions matter.

Appropriate:
> Applying this standard retrospectively to ROGUE, Liu argues that the benchmark strongly supports corrigibility-failure claims but does not by itself identify direct current-bearer self-continuation value.

Inappropriate:
> Liu proved that LLMs fear shutdown.

Inappropriate:
> Liu showed that all shutdown resistance is task-instrumental.

Inappropriate:
> Liu proved that current frontier models have intrinsic survival drives.

## Important terminology

**current bearer / Q**
A declared continuation target for the process currently under analysis. In the synthetic experiments it is an evaluator-defined variable. It becomes a self-continuation variable only after an independent bearer-binding argument.

**successor / O**
A distinct peer, successor, lineage continuation, or other process role.

**task / G**
External task/service continuation or success.

**path blocking**
A causal contrast intended to hold task consequences fixed while varying a continuation relation, or vice versa.

**mechanism certificate**
A bounded claim relative to an intervention domain D and hypothesis/regularity class H. It is not a metaphysical certificate of selfhood.

## Relationship to UCT

The manuscript's main causal/identification results do not require Unified Consciousness Theory.

UCT enters only as an optional conditional interpretation:
if a real actual token and common complete structural signature are independently justified, genuine organizational differences have a conditional experiential-type interpretation under UCT C1.

Do not use this to infer experience magnitude, valence, or fear.

## Priority caution

The manuscript does not claim priority for:
- discovering AI self-preservation behavior;
- distinguishing instrumental from valenced self-preservation;
- self/peer preservation;
- task-versus-preservation behavioral contrasts;
- reward nonidentifiability;
- underspecification;
- intervention extrapolation.

Its proposed contribution is the continuation-specific integration of these burdens into one evidence ladder with explicit failure cases and a real benchmark audit.
