## Human concern and AI development

Hongju Liu initiated and directed the research concern: as increasingly capable AI assumes more productive and cognitive work, what connects people to economic output when labor income becomes a weak distribution channel? He requested a durable public research starting point that can be improved as stronger AI and better evidence become available, without making admission to a particular journal the objective.

This statement records the human originator's contribution and current account. It does not establish a private discovery date, ownership of a broad idea, or global priority. Related economic questions were investigated long before this project; the paper cites close contemporary and historical precedents.

The first public DOI edition within this paper lineage is v1.1, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22871209, dated 2026-09-21. Its substantial AI-development statement identifies GPT-5.6 Sol. Version 1.2 adds the heterogeneous-demand model, corrective proofs and audit with substantial assistance from GPT-6 Astra Pro. Human problem initiation is not misrepresented as independent human derivation of every mathematical result. The AI contributions are not reduced to copyediting.
