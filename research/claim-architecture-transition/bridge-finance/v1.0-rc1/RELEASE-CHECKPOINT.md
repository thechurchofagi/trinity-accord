## Release scope

The manuscript will include the clearing-price result, a least-cost payment-schedule problem conditional on a current support target, and a separate extension where the same buyers can also undertake productive investment. Physical and financial accounts must close. Ordinary debt plus feasible insurance is a replication benchmark, not a deliberately weak comparator.

The investment extension is not to be mixed silently with the no-other-assets exchange benchmark. Macroeconomic demand effects require additional behavioral and supply assumptions and will be distinguished from the proved financing results. A verified exchange or production-accounting example is not evidence of a demand multiplier.
