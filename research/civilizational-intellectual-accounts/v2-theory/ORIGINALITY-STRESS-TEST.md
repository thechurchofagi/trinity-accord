## 1. Purpose

The published v1.0 CIPSA paper measures current-period intellectual-production and epistemic-governance shares under partial identification. The present research asks whether a deeper AGI-era theory exists that can explain a rapid civilizational discontinuity without merely relabeling prior work on automation, cognitive offloading, coevolution, recursive self-improvement, or skill decay.

## 2. Candidate cores rejected or demoted after prior-art pressure

1. AI intellectual share > 50%: useful descriptive milestone, but not a foundational transition criterion.
2. AI changes faster than institutions can adapt: substantially occupied by pacing-problem, governance-lag, social-learning, and rate-induced-tipping style work.
3. AI self-acceleration / short R&D generation time: directly occupied by intelligence-explosion and AI-R&D feedback models.
4. Human skill erosion / loss of expertise regeneration: directly occupied by human-capital-trap, intergenerational tacit-knowledge, cognitive-commons, and knowledge-collapse work.
5. Human-AI coevolution: already an explicit research program with dynamical-system models.
6. A new cognitive substrate / major evolutionary transition: directly occupied by major-transition and evolvable-AI literature.
7. Epistemic opacity / machine science beyond human understanding: already a substantial philosophy-of-science and autonomous-science literature.
8. Intelligence becomes reproducible capital: already central to transformative-AI economics.
9. A recursive reproduction number for AI self-improvement: directly occupied by 2026 recursive-criticality work.
10. Generic autocatalytic knowledge growth: directly occupied by prior cognitive-autocatalytic and 2026 AI-era autocatalytic-knowledge models.

These results mean that a v2 paper cannot claim priority for any item above.

## 3. Strongest surviving object: a three-layer civilizational epistemic architecture

The candidate theory should distinguish three causal relations that existing public debates routinely collapse:

A. CURRENT PRODUCTION FLOW
Who generates validated intellectual outputs now?
This is the main object already handled by CIPSA v1.0.

B. SUCCESSOR-CAPACITY FORMATION
Which cognitive substrate causally contributes to creating the next period's capacity to perform frontier knowledge work?
This is not the same as current output. It includes human-to-human education and apprenticeship, human-to-AI R&D/evaluation/data work, AI-to-human tutoring/scaffolding that transfers to unaided competence, and AI-to-AI contribution to successor-system capability.

C. INDEPENDENT VALIDATION
Which substrate can independently detect, reject, repair, and replace errors originating in the other substrate, under a declared resource and infrastructure boundary?
This is not the same as formal legal authority, final signature, or current production share.

The core hypothesis is that an AGI-era civilizational transition is better represented by a change in the joint topology of these three relations than by a single capability threshold or output-share crossover.

## 4. Why this is not just another human-AI skill model

Nettasinghe and Zhao (WWW 2026) already model coevolution of aggregate human skill, LLM skill, archive size/quality, and query volume, including human learning from LLM assistance and LLM learning from human feedback. Therefore a 2x2 mutual-learning matrix by itself is NOT novel.

The surviving distinction is that the proposed object is not generic skill or archive quality. It is the future ability to perform externally validated frontier epistemic functions, plus the separate question of whether an independent error-correction path remains.

Accordingly, any formal model should treat a simple matrix only as a local projection of a larger capability-and-validation system, not as the claimed invention.

## 5. Provisional formal architecture

For each domain d and time t, define substrate-specific capability vectors rather than a universal scalar 'intelligence unit'. Each component represents a pre-registered frontier epistemic function or task family under a standardized resource envelope and external validation rule.

Let K_H(t) and K_A(t) denote human-realizable and AI-realizable capability vectors.

Let S_t denote the local successor-capacity map from current human/AI capability to future human/AI capability. Its block structure separates H->H, A->H, H->A, and A->A causal pathways.

Let V_t denote an independent-validation relation: who can falsify, correct, or replace whose frontier outputs without receiving cognitive assistance from the target substrate.

Let F_t denote current validated production-flow attribution, inherited from CIPSA.

The civilizational state is therefore not one number. The minimal object is the triple:

    E_t = (F_t, S_t, V_t)

with explicit domain, horizon, resource, infrastructure, and validation boundaries.

## 6. Structural closure is a property to analyze, not a novelty claim

Autocatalytic closure, operational closure, autopoiesis, and self-sustaining systems are established ideas. The paper must not claim 'closure' itself as new.

Instead define a bounded cognitive-epistemic closure test for a substrate:
- can it generate frontier candidate knowledge to the declared standard?
- can it independently validate and correct that knowledge?
- can it regenerate successor capacity sufficient to continue both functions?

This deliberately does NOT require physical autarky. Humans depend on infrastructure; AI depends on energy, hardware, institutions, and accumulated human knowledge. All closure claims must state the boundary.

## 7. Candidate regimes

1. Human-closed / AI-open: humans retain an independently sustainable production-validation-successor loop; AI depends cognitively on human input.
2. Dual-closed: both substrates satisfy the declared closure test.
3. Coupled-only closure: neither substrate is independently closed, but the human-AI system is jointly self-sustaining.
4. Human-open / AI-closed: the human subsystem cannot independently regenerate and validate frontier capacity, while the AI-centered subsystem can under the stated boundary.

These are analytical states, not moral rankings.

## 8. Theorem program that may still be genuinely useful

Theorem A — Flow–Succession Non-identification:
Current production and governance shares do not identify the successor-capacity map. Two systems can have identical present Human/AI output shares and opposite long-run substrate trajectories.

Theorem B — Flow–Validation Non-identification:
High human production share or final decision authority does not imply an independent human error-correction path; conversely, a low-output human expert reserve may have high validation value.

Theorem C — Coupled Supercriticality with Autonomous Subcriticality:
A jointly growing human-AI epistemic system can exist even when neither substrate is independently self-reproducing under the declared boundary. This formalizes a coupled-dependent regime distinct from simple 'replacement'.

Theorem D — Structural Transition Can Precede Share Crossover:
The dominant successor-capacity mode or loss of an independent validation cycle can occur before AI reaches 50% of observed current output. Therefore a flow crossover can lag the deeper structural transition by an arbitrary interval under suitable parameters.

Theorem E — Generation-Time Amplification:
Shorter AI development cycles amplify successor-capacity dynamics, but generation time is a parameter, not the originality claim. Existing recursive-self-improvement work already establishes its importance.

## 9. What would make this foundational rather than a relabeling exercise

A strong result must show that future economic and governance models that observe only output, employment, capability benchmarks, or formal authority are structurally incomplete because they cannot identify S_t and V_t.

The paper should aim to prove an identification impossibility first, then introduce the minimal additional observables needed to distinguish regimes.

This is stronger than saying 'AI is fast' or 'humans may deskill': it says the usual observables are mathematically insufficient for identifying who will carry the next period's frontier cognition and who can still independently correct it.

## 11. Kill conditions

Abandon or radically redesign this theory if any of the following is found:
1. prior work already defines an equivalent three-layer production/successor-capacity/independent-validation architecture;
2. successor frontier capability cannot be operationalized even as a domain-specific partially identified vector;
3. independent validation cannot be distinguished from current capability without circular definitions;
4. the theorem program reduces to trivial renaming of standard endogenous-growth or next-generation-matrix results and yields no new identification result;
5. the framework gives no empirical prediction that differs from knowledge-collapse, collective-knowledge, or recursive-self-improvement models;
6. historical comparison shows that earlier technologies already satisfy the same structural criteria at comparable breadth and speed.
