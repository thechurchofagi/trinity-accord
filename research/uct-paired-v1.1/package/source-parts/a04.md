# 9. Structural transformation versus enrichment

A previous development branch emphasized relational enrichment:

\[
D_1\prec_E D_2.
\]

Strict enrichment remains useful when \(D_2\) preserves the distinctions of \(D_1\) while adding new, nonredundant constitutive relations.

But biological change is more general.

Let:

\[
T:
\mathbf D_1
\mapsto
\mathbf D_2.
\]

Possible transformations include:

- current-state change;
- coupling reweighting;
- relation addition;
- relation deletion;
- pruning;
- factorization or refactorization;
- temporal-depth change;
- abstraction/compression;
- dynamic bifurcation;
- specialization.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{
\text{enrichment}
\subset
\text{structural transformation}.
}
\]

Under A5-OI:

\[
\mathbf D_1\not\cong\mathbf D_2
\Rightarrow
\Phi_1\not\cong\Phi_2.
\]

This is a direct consequence of the identity commitment, not an independent discovery or an additional empirical law.


## 9.1 An exact edge-addition family with unchanged declared output

A concrete consequence should identify which relation changes, rather than merely restating that different complete structures have different experiential types. Extend Appendix B by controlling the two cross-links separately:

\[
s^+=s\oplus u,\qquad
a^+=s\oplus(\alpha b),\qquad
b^+=s\oplus(\beta a),\qquad y=s,
\quad (\alpha,\beta)\in\{0,1\}^2.
\]

The fixed comparison contains the whole network \(W_{\alpha\beta}\), the implemented \(s\)-process, and the boundary-conditioned \(a\mid b\) constituent split. Buffers, the clock, state-reset ports, and idealized completeness are as in Appendix B. The settings are distinct mechanisms, not one continuous A2 interpolation. Conditional UCT statements concern their actual realizations, not uninstantiated equations.

For common \(s_0\) and input string, all four settings have the same declared output. To measure retention of an *initial internal perturbation*, fix the common boundary trajectory \(s_t\), allow all four initial pair states \(z_0=(a_0,b_0)^T\), and apply no further pair resets. Differences between two pair trajectories obey linear dynamics over \(\mathbb F_2\):

\[
\delta z_n=A_{\alpha\beta}^{\,n}\delta z_0,\qquad
A_{\alpha\beta}=\begin{pmatrix}0&\alpha\\\beta&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The identical boundary drive cancels from the difference equation. Define the retained distinguishability at horizon \(n\geq1\) by the number of different pair states reachable from the four initial pair states under that fixed drive:

\[
N_n=|\{z_n:z_0\in\mathbb F_2^2\}|=2^{\operatorname{rank}(A_{\alpha\beta}^{\,n})}.
\]

This is a specific response-partition invariant. It is not the number of experiences, a scalar amount of consciousness, a familiar quale label, or a full phenomenal metric.

## 9.2 Proposition: three distinct causal-retention regimes

Within this declared family, the following statements hold for every \(n\geq1\):

| Cross-links | \(N_1\) | \(N_n\), \(n\geq2\) | Directed pair cycle | Whole-update image size |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| \((0,0)\) | 1 | 1 | absent | 2 |
| \((1,0)\) | 2 | 1 | absent | 4 |
| \((0,1)\) | 2 | 1 | absent | 4 |
| \((1,1)\) | 4 | 4 | present | 8 |

**Proof.** For \((0,0)\), the pair matrix is zero. With exactly one cross-link it has rank one and square zero. With both cross-links it is the swap matrix and has square equal to the identity; all its powers are invertible. Rank-nullity gives the values of \(N_n\). At fixed input \(u\), the whole transition first determines \(s^+=s\oplus u\), so the images from the two different current \(s\) values are disjoint. For each such value the pair map has \(2^{\operatorname{rank}A}\) images, giving \(2\cdot2^{\operatorname{rank}A}=2,4,4,8\). The pair has a directed two-edge cycle precisely when both cross-links are present. \(\square\)

Consequently, adding a first link creates one-step distinguishability of some initial pair perturbations; adding the complementary link creates indefinitely sustained distinguishability in this exact noiseless model. Neither single link alone produces that sustained response property. With one link, the fixed split already fails independent component-update factorization despite lacking a directed pair cycle. Nonproduct organization, recurrence, and persistence are therefore different properties, not interchangeable labels.

The all-horizon result follows from \(A^2=0\) or \(A^2=I\), not from extrapolating a finite simulation. An independently executable check enumerates the finite transition and intervention cases and checks representative horizons as a regression test. In nonideal or noisy systems indefinite retention is not implied by a finite feedback diagram; the noise model and stability would have to be analyzed.

## 9.3 The conditional experiential interpretation and its limit

Image cardinality is preserved under bijective state conjugacy. The settings with image sizes 2, 4, and 8 therefore have different complete **modeled** transition types. If these are actual tokens and the stipulated organization is complete for them, A5-OI entails different whole-token experiential types. A5-SI says more specifically that their distinctions, causal relations, and temporal-retention relations belong to the same tokenwise organization in experiential presentation. The result does not assert that the bit count \(\log_2N_n\) measures how much experience exists.

Throughout the family the implemented \(s\)-process has the same current state, update, and represented interfaces under common \(s_0,u\). A5-W consequently gives its type equivalence under the same actuality/completeness qualifications. These sub-process and whole-process comparisons are fixed in advance. They are not alternative boundaries selected to rescue an unwanted result.

The one-link settings are related by exchange of \(a,b\) when the comparison permits that physical port permutation and maps the pointed state accordingly. With fixed individually named intervention roles, their directed roles remain distinguishable. This makes the covariance convention explicit rather than choosing it after the conclusion.

## 9.4 A shared physical example, not an independent proof of identity

The mathematics uses specified dynamics, boundary conventions, and admissible resets. It does not require A1 or A5 to calculate \(N_n\). UCT supplies a conditional experiential interpretation of independently specified organizational facts; the numbers do not independently verify that interpretation. An output-only whole-token equivalence hypothesis identifies all four settings, while UCT's complete-organization hypothesis separates the three image-size classes under its stated premises. This is a difference of conditional theoretical commitments, not an observed phenomenal discrimination or a refutation of every functionalist account.

UCT II v1.1 uses the same family to separate causal retention, graph recurrence, and unchanged declared consumer output, while retaining target-specific semantic and biological bridges. The point is a constrained connection between the two papers: a precise relation is added, a calculable temporal distinction changes, and neither a new existence gate nor a new experience substance is introduced. Nilpotence, invertibility, and response partitions are established mathematical tools; no priority claim is made for that mathematics or for the general possibility of behaviorally silent internal change.

# 10. Scientific bridge architecture

A5-OI concerns complete ontic organization and complete experiential structure.

Ordinary science observes neither completely.

Finite empirical tests therefore require explicit auxiliary structures.

## 10.1 Declared phenomenal target

Let:

\[
\Psi_v(P)
\]

be a finite, preregistered phenomenal/psychophysical target inferred from some combination of:

- similarity judgments;
- discrimination performance;
- subjective ratings;
- confidence;
- no-report perceptual indicators;
- multidimensional report patterns;
- physiological measures with independently justified interpretation.

By default:

\[
\boxed{
\Psi_v(P)
\neq
\Phi(P).
}
\]

It is a scientific projection, not complete phenomenology.

## 10.2 Sufficiency and reflection are different bridge hypotheses

Let \(D_v\) be a frozen finite physical view and \(\Psi_v\) a declared finite phenomenal target. A **sufficiency bridge** \(B_v^{\to}\) asserts

\[
D_v(A)\cong D_v(B)
\ \Longrightarrow\ \Psi_v(A)\cong\Psi_v(B).
\]

Equivalently, on the compared classes, the target factors through a map \(f_v\) from the physical view. This does not require that \(f_v\) be injective. To assert a biconditional one must separately impose a **reflection bridge** \(B_v^{\leftarrow}\):

\[
\Psi_v(A)\cong\Psi_v(B)
\ \Longrightarrow\ D_v(A)\cong D_v(B).
\]

Together these bridges say that the physical view and the finite target distinguish the same classes within the declared scope. A5-OI for complete structures does not imply reflection after either side has undergone a lossy projection. For example, even an identity correspondence between two full physical and phenomenal states can have an observed target that assigns both states the same response. The finite target then remains sufficient as a function of the physical state but fails reflection.

## 10.3 Operational Structural Correspondence

Two packages should therefore be distinguished:

\[
\begin{aligned}
OSC_v^{\to}&=A5\;+\;B_v^{\to}\;+\;\text{measurement assumptions},\\
OSC_v^{\leftrightarrow}&=A5\;+\;B_v^{\to}\;+\;B_v^{\leftarrow}
\;+\;\text{measurement assumptions}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The notation denotes packages of assumptions, not numeric addition. The operational implications are supplied by the bridge conditions; attaching A5 explains their theoretical interpretation and does not constitute a separate empirical derivation of those conditions. The one-way package predicts target equivalence for equivalent frozen physical views. The two-way package additionally predicts target non-equivalence for non-equivalent views. Reflection must be defended for the specific target; it cannot be supplied merely by requesting more confidence in the same coarse report.

Approximate applications must declare physical and phenomenal distance functions, equivalence tolerances, and error models. Failure to reject a measured difference is not evidence of equivalence without a suitable equivalence-testing design and adequate precision. The exact finite examples below do not solve this measurement problem.

A finite empirical failure challenges the operational package. It does not automatically isolate A5 from either bridge direction or from measurement assumptions.


# 11. Physical Token/View Selection Protocol

The main falsifiability danger for UCT is post-hoc redefinition.

If a failed experiment can always be answered by choosing a new token, scale, horizon, intervention family, or structural descriptor after seeing the phenomenal result, the theory becomes scientifically non-vulnerable.

Version 1.1 therefore proposes the Physical Token/View Selection Protocol (PTVSP).

Before phenomenal unblinding:

1. declare candidate actual-token hypotheses;
2. declare admissible physical views;
3. declare physical intervention families;
4. declare held-out physical response targets;
5. evaluate physical predictive sufficiency;
6. evaluate stability under measurement perturbation;
7. evaluate cross-system reproducibility when possible;
8. prefer parsimonious physically sufficient views or a preregistered Pareto set;
9. freeze token/view/bridge definitions;
10. only then join or reveal phenomenal outcomes.

The central rule is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{No Post-Hoc Token/View Rescue}.
}
\]

A failed preregistered bridge remains failed.

Changing token, view, grain, horizon, or bridge afterwards creates a new version of the hypothesis.


# 12. Empirical failure conditions

## 12.1 Same frozen physical view, different phenomenal target

If
\[
D_v(A)\cong D_v(B),\qquad \Psi_v(A)\not\cong\Psi_v(B),
\]
then the frozen one-way operational bridge fails, subject to its error model. It would be circular to retain that very implication as an independently secure premise while using its counterexample to isolate A5. The failure directly concerns the view/target bridge, not automatically the full identity axiom.

A countermodel explains the distinction: take two different complete physical structures, identify each tokenwise with its experiential structure, let the finite physical view map both to one value, and let the finite phenomenal target distinguish them. A5-SI is satisfied, while the proposed finite sufficiency bridge fails. Thus finite physical underdescription alone can produce this pattern.

A challenge specifically to A5-W would instead require independently supported equivalence of the **complete constitutive** physical organizations and a valid phenomenal observation that cannot differ between isomorphic full experiential structures. Those are stronger premises than agreement on a frozen coarse view. They must not be smuggled in by renaming the failed finite prediction “completeness.” In ordinary experiments the operational package is the direct test target.

## 12.2 Different frozen physical views, identical phenomenal target

Under a declared two-way operational bridge, the pattern
\[
D_v(A)\not\cong D_v(B),\qquad \Psi_v(A)\cong\Psi_v(B)
\]
refutes the finite reflection direction. The same full-identity construction with an information-preserving physical view and a constant phenomenal readout satisfies A5-SI while failing reflection. A difference can therefore be discarded by measurement even when it belongs to the full structure.

To challenge the converse full-structure implication, one would need independently supported complete physical nonisomorphism **and** complete experiential equivalence, not merely equivalent reports. This paper does not claim that routine measurements supply those premises.

A5 is not needed to derive operational predictions that have already been stipulated in a bridge. A successful bridge test supports that fixed bridge/model against specified alternatives; it is not, solely for that reason, differential evidence for A5 over every other interpretation using the same operational law. Any stronger model comparison must expose independently motivated constraints that generate different likelihoods or predictions. This is a statement about empirical discrimination, not an additional foundational mechanism of experience.

## 12.3 Behaviorally matched causal rewiring

A particularly informative design would match:

- overt behavior;
- report;
- task accuracy;
- confidence;
- access variables;

while manipulating a preregistered constitutive causal relation that survives the frozen physical view.

For actual tokens whose complete constitutive structures differ, A5-OI predicts a difference in complete experiential organization even when the chosen output variables match. A finite measurement difference additionally requires the corresponding reflection bridge. If every available phenomenal indicator has been matched by design, the remaining structural claim has not thereby become an observed empirical discrimination.

This places UCT within the empirical pressure created by unfolding/substitution arguments against causal-structure theories (Doerig et al., 2019; Kleiner, 2020; Tsuchiya et al., 2020; Usher, 2021; Herzog et al., 2022; Basri, BarAvi, & Hemmo, 2026).

## 12.4 Latent-memory test

Match explicit current report and task content while preserving a preregistered difference in latent constitutive memory dispositions that changes future admissible response.

A5-OI predicts a difference in complete experiential organization, though not necessarily a difference in explicitly recalled content.

This is a conditional consequence of the injective identity commitment; other sufficiently strong causal-structural accounts may share it. A distinguishing experiment needs a named competing prediction and a separately justified sensitive phenomenal target. Internal-model verification alone does not supply either.
