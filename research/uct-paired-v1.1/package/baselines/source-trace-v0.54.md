## Audit interpretation

For each theory, Σ_T is the minimal discriminating signature and R_T^res is the set of target-theory commitments not reconstructed by the UCT translator.

A faithful relation therefore records:

T → (reconstructed primitives, Σ_T, B_T, R_T^res, REC/EMB/REI/CON/EMP).

This ledger is a source-fidelity device, not proof that the target sources are correct.
