\hypertarget{s1.-what-this-revision-changes}{%
\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
\section{S1. What this revision
changes}\label{s1.-what-this-revision-changes}}

Paper I supplies the basal process ontology and the intended tokenwise
physical-experiential identity. Paper II reconstructs or embeds
specified operational mechanisms under explicit bridges, while
separately recording reinterpretations, conflicts, empirical status, and
unrecovered commitments. The paired revision keeps these tasks separate.

The published v1.0 editions remain identifiable at DOI
10.5281/zenodo.23005588 (I) and DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23008262 (II). New
versions do not change their historical claims or bytes. Candidate
identifiers rc3 and rc4 describe internal development stages, not new
published editions.

The present review identified five principal improvements. First, a
scientific view or estimate is no longer silently equated with a token's
complete ontic organization. Second, the common comparison signature
explicitly retains the current pointed state, intervention ports, update
and reset operations, temporal relations, and constituent structure.
Third, finite bridge failures are no longer used circularly to isolate
the full identity axiom. Fourth, a four-setting model proves which
internal distinctions are erased or retained under particular connection
changes. Fifth, Paper II corrects IIT terminology, source-domain
fidelity, numerical-threshold language, and missing reproducibility
materials.

No additional macro-experience existence threshold, compulsory universal
subject selector, or separate human-quality generating mechanism is
introduced. Biological specification and finite measurement remain
application tasks within the common explanatory framework.

This is a revision-specific ledger. The restored v0.54 ledger is
retained separately as historical source provenance; its older R/EMP
labels are not the current taxonomy. The v1.0-rc4 relation ledger is

\hypertarget{s3.1-iit-4.0}{%
\Needspace{4\baselineskip}
\subsection{S3.1 IIT 4.0}\label{s3.1-iit-4.0}}

\textbf{Source:} Albantakis et al.~(2023), DOI
10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465, particularly System integrated
information, Composition, and Relations. The system quantity is
\(\phi_s\), distinction information is \(\phi_d\), and relation
information is \(\phi_r\). IIT's reference prior, intrinsic-difference
measure, repertoires, admissible partitions, normalization,
maximum/minimum rules, and tie conventions remain imported bridge
specifications. A system-level zero criterion is not a generic
graph-factorization theorem. Computational reconstruction is conditional
F3/V0; an exclusion conflict requires a specified continuing lower
actual UCT process and the exact conflicting source claim. Neither full
IIT phenomenology nor neural predictions are certified by this
reconstruction.

\textbf{Source:} Lamme (2006), DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2006.09.001. A
cycle/SCC calculation is a local formal primitive, not complete
conscious vision. Sensory content, recurrent timing, stabilization, and
the phenomenal role must remain explicit. The graph primitive can be
local F4/V0; source-faithful perceptual recovery remains conditional on
the sensory and empirical bridges. The four-setting circuit is not a
biological RPT validation.

\textbf{Sources:} Dehaene and Changeux (2011), DOI
10.1016/j.neuron.2011.03.018; Mashour et al.~(2020), DOI
10.1016/j.neuron.2020.01.026. Preserve amplification, sustained
representation, broad availability, and the workspace architecture.
Consumer identities, content coordinates, readout conventions, and
anatomical/timing claims are not obtained from bare reachability. The
numerical access thresholds in the toy examples are sampled operational
criteria, not demonstrated neural ignition or an independently
established dynamical bifurcation.

\textbf{Source:} Brown, Lau, and LeDoux (2019), DOI

detailed source framing; this revision does not claim a new exhaustive
full-text audit of every HOT variant. Causal targeting is a primitive.
Semantic aboutness, mental-state identification, and phenomenal
necessity/sufficiency remain bridge or residual commitments.

\hypertarget{s5.-numerical-reimplementation-and-corrections}{%
\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
\section{S5. Numerical reimplementation and
corrections}\label{s5.-numerical-reimplementation-and-corrections}}

\texttt{verification/reproduce\_shared\_models.py} is new v1.1 code, not
the unavailable original generation code or the earlier release-side
reconstruction. Archived CSVs supply sweep inputs and are used only
afterward for output comparison. Equations, fixed parameters,
initialization, synchronous update, convergence tolerance, and controls
are stated in the script.

Seven archived CSV comparisons pass the declared absolute tolerance of
\(10^{-9}\): 1,354 rows and 11,336 cells, with a largest discrepancy of
approximately \(6.674\times10^{-11}\). The first model restarts at zero
for each grid point and stops below maximum state change \(10^{-13}\),
with 20,000 iterations allowed. The second uses 39 transitions after its
specified initial state. No historical claim of zero parameter tuning is
certified.

The first model's \(\rho=\sqrt{6q}\) is the spectral radius of its
connection matrix. Its crossing of one is not onset of a directed cycle:
both edges are already nonzero throughout the stated gate interval. Nor
is that quantity the nonlinear fixed-point Jacobian's spectral radius.
The negative control removes the gated return edge, not the other edge,
and retains the original local interaction calculation.

The high-access values are first qualifying sampled grid points, not
exact bifurcations. In particular, the drive \(s=-0.2\) crossing is
0.4725 on the stated gate grid. The second model's first tested
higher-order and access crossings are approximately 0.4032960894 and
0.7215083799. CSV column names inherited from v1.0 are preserved for
reproducibility; historical words such as ignition or RPT in a column
name do not override these corrected interpretations.

The numerical laws calculate the same outputs without invoking A1 or A5.
Model reproducibility supports the stated mathematical consequences, not
independent empirical truth of the ontology or complete biological
recovery.

\hypertarget{s6.-reproducing-the-package}{%
\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
\section{S6. Reproducing the
package}\label{s6.-reproducing-the-package}}

Extract the deposited audit ZIP into a new directory. Python 3.10 or
later and its standard library suffice for the checks:

\begin{Shaded}
\begin{Highlighting}[]
\ExtensionTok{python}\NormalTok{ verification/check\_rc4.py}
\ExtensionTok{python}\NormalTok{ verification/check\_transformations.py}
\ExtensionTok{python}\NormalTok{ verification/reproduce\_shared\_models.py}
\end{Highlighting}
\end{Shaded}

The scripts regenerate CSVs and JSON result files beside the code.
Archived baselines are retained separately and must not be overwritten
with regenerated values. \texttt{audit/source-provenance.json}
identifies baseline and assembled-source digests. SHA256 manifests
permit file-integrity checking. Checksums and timestamps establish byte
identity or preservation evidence, not correctness of a theory.

PDF rebuilding additionally requires Pandoc, XeLaTeX, the listed TeX
packages, and the DejaVu fonts available in the build environment. Font
files are not distributed. \texttt{build.py} records the rendering
route. A regenerated PDF may differ at the byte level because of
environment or PDF metadata; the deposited PDF is the publication
artifact to use for preservation checks.

The source-trace file referenced in historical Paper II was absent from
the audited v1.0 ZIP. This release restores that separately recovered
historical document and labels its old taxonomy as historical. The prior
v1.0 publication is not altered. New code and current results are
labeled as new, rather than pretending to recover unavailable
development history.
