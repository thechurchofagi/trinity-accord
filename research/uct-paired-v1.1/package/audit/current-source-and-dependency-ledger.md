# S3. Current nine-theory source-fidelity ledger

This is a revision-specific ledger. The restored v0.54 ledger is retained separately as historical source provenance; its older R/EMP labels are not the current taxonomy. The v1.0-rc4 relation ledger is also retained unchanged. The current manuscript's F axis denotes formal relation/recovery class, V denotes empirical validation, and REC/EMB/REI/CON denote relation types. These are classifications of specified subclaims, not a total ranking of entire theories.

## S3.1 IIT 4.0

**Source:** Albantakis et al. (2023), DOI 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465, particularly System integrated information, Composition, and Relations. The system quantity is \(\phi_s\), distinction information is \(\phi_d\), and relation information is \(\phi_r\). IIT's reference prior, intrinsic-difference measure, repertoires, admissible partitions, normalization, maximum/minimum rules, and tie conventions remain imported bridge specifications. A system-level zero criterion is not a generic graph-factorization theorem. Computational reconstruction is conditional F3/V0; an exclusion conflict requires a specified continuing lower actual UCT process and the exact conflicting source claim. Neither full IIT phenomenology nor neural predictions are certified by this reconstruction.

## S3.2 Recurrent Processing Theory

**Source:** Lamme (2006), DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2006.09.001. A cycle/SCC calculation is a local formal primitive, not complete conscious vision. Sensory content, recurrent timing, stabilization, and the phenomenal role must remain explicit. The graph primitive can be local F4/V0; source-faithful perceptual recovery remains conditional on the sensory and empirical bridges. The four-setting circuit is not a biological RPT validation.

## S3.3 Global Neuronal Workspace

**Sources:** Dehaene and Changeux (2011), DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2011.03.018; Mashour et al. (2020), DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.01.026. Preserve amplification, sustained representation, broad availability, and the workspace architecture. Consumer identities, content coordinates, readout conventions, and anatomical/timing claims are not obtained from bare reachability. The numerical access thresholds in the toy examples are sampled operational criteria, not demonstrated neural ignition or an independently established dynamical bifurcation.

## S3.4 Higher-Order Theory

**Source:** Brown, Lau, and LeDoux (2019), DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2019.06.009. Retain appropriate higher-order representation of a first-order mental state, not merely an observer's causal-tracking description. The historical source ledger supplies the detailed source framing; this revision does not claim a new exhaustive full-text audit of every HOT variant. Causal targeting is a primitive. Semantic aboutness, mental-state identification, and phenomenal necessity/sufficiency remain bridge or residual commitments.

## S3.5 Predictive processing and active inference

**Sources:** Hodson et al. (2024), DOI 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2023.105473; Pezzulo et al. (2024), DOI 10.1016/j.biopsycho.2023.108741. Model states, generative and inference distributions, policy structure, and preferences must be identified without a target-output oracle. Pezzulo et al. explicitly formulate basic sentient behavior without phenomenological commitments. This must not be relabeled as a universal phenomenal-existence assertion to create a conflict with A1. Conditional formal computation does not establish a system's model semantics or superiority over alternatives.

## S3.6 Dendritic Integration Theory

**Source:** Aru, Suzuki, and Larkum (2020), DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2020.07.006. Preserve pyramidal-cell compartments, bottom-up/top-down interaction, and thalamocortical context. A nonlinear interaction functional alone is not DIT. The local sigmoid example is deliberately labeled DIT-like; biological necessity/sufficiency and implementation remain residual. Anatomical bridges do not become universal experience-existence gates under UCT.

## S3.7 Memory Theory of Consciousness

**Sources:** Budson et al. (2022), DOI 10.1097/WNN.0000000000000319; Budson and Paller (2025), DOI 10.2147/NSS.S522975. Preserve explicit-memory architecture and the strong all-experience wording rather than reducing the theory silently to any causal trace. Temporal retention is an exact local primitive; calling a state an explicit-memory system requires further bridges. The A1 conflict is with an explicitly universalized necessity reading on a domain containing an actual nonmemory witness. A witness outside a biological source's domain does not refute its restricted claim.

## S3.8 Attention Schema Theory

**Sources:** Graziano and Webb (2015), DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00500; Wilterson et al. (2020), DOI 10.1016/j.pneurobio.2020.101844. Preserve the simplified internal model of attention, its control role, and awareness attribution. Generic feedback or compression is not the full signature. Representation grounding and source-specific behavioral predictions remain necessary bridges or residuals. A conditional causal representation is not an empirical confirmation of AST.

## S3.9 Temporo-spatial Theory of Consciousness

**Sources:** Northoff and Huang (2017), DOI 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2017.07.013; Northoff and Zilio (2022), DOI 10.1016/j.bbr.2022.113788. Preserve the differentiated roles: nestedness as state/level predisposition, alignment as content/form prerequisite, expansion as phenomenal correlates, and globalization as cognitive consequence. Time and scale appear in scientific views, but changing the analyst's view does not change an unchanged actual token. Scale/frequency predictions and neurophenomenal mappings are not recovered merely by defining four generic structural functions.
