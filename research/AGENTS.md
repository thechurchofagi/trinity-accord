# Research publication language policy

User instruction, effective 2026-09-24: all papers are to be produced and published in English only by default. Apply this to new papers, manuscript revisions, abstracts, and accompanying publication materials. Do not generate a Chinese paper, translation, or Chinese abstract unless the user explicitly requests one.

This policy is prospective. Preserve already published Chinese editions and their citation/version records. Everyday discussion and review explanations may remain in Chinese.

## Publication preservation and AR budget

Every paper publication must have OTS timestamp evidence and Arweave preservation, bound to the exact published bytes. Submit OTS promptly; claim Bitcoin verification only after verification succeeds. Upload the PDF and its verified proof to Arweave, and claim completion only after anonymous SHA-256 readback passes. Preserve previous editions and receipts.

User instruction, effective 2026-09-24: paper Arweave spending is separate from daily upload-count limits. Each published paper edition (report number plus version-specific DOI) must have total cumulative archive fees strictly below 0.1 AR. This includes multiple paid attempts and bundles for the same edition; 0.1 AR exactly is not authorized. Existing unrelated daily limits, wallet reserve, rolling-spend ceiling, identity checks, and duplicate-post protection remain in force. A shared bundle's entire cost is conservatively counted against every covered paper. Reuse a recorded transaction for readback; never repay just because readback is delayed.
