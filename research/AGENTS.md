# Research publication language policy

User instruction, effective 2026-09-24: all papers are to be produced and published in English only by default. Apply this to new papers, manuscript revisions, abstracts, and accompanying publication materials. Do not generate a Chinese paper, translation, or Chinese abstract unless the user explicitly requests one.

This policy is prospective. Preserve already published Chinese editions and their citation/version records. Everyday discussion and review explanations may remain in Chinese.
