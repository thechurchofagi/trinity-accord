**R02 — Universal versus probabilistic route scope.** Theorem R1 concerns all admitted source-route pairs. The group generated by relative transformations need not be the actual route distribution. Section 6 gives a specific 1/2 versus 1/3 counterexample to replacing a two-route law with uniform sampling of the generated group. The quantitative claims retain their own source/route assumptions.

**R03 — Calibration resource type.** Theorem R3 is a zero-error alphabet bound for tags depending on the route only under the Cartesian full domain. It is not an entropy identity, arbitrary interactive lower bound, or total physical memory count. The restricted-domain witness is retained.

**R04 — Continuity terminology.** The introduction defines continuity as target retention/recoverability across declared stages, not topological continuity, all-times access, personal identity or identical qualia. Section 8 explicitly separates endpoint recovery and uninterrupted availability.

Gao et al.'s accessible full text was checked at the introductory and holonomy/synchronization sections; this is not an exhaustive theorem-level novelty clearance. Neural-drift studies motivate the question without being presented as tests of this model or UCT.
