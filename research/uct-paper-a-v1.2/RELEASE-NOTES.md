# UCT I v1.2 RC6 - Draft Release Notes

**Status:** frozen for author approval; not published.

## Principal changes from v1.1

1. Reclassifies foundations into process ontology, one consciousness-specific axiom (C1), derived theorems, and empirical bridges.
2. Derives U1 Universal Nonempty Experience from C1 and nonempty actual-token organization.
3. Derives U2 Structural Continuity in a common isomorphism-class space with a predeclared topology/pseudometric.
4. Moves persistence and token/type/lineage to process ontology as P3 and P6.
5. Recasts Selfhood Non-Prerequisite as U3.
6. Adds U0 Foundational Compression.
7. Adds E1 No First Conscious Ancestor.
8. Adds E2 Fine-Grained Structural Chains.
9. Adds conditional E3 Connected Existence Constancy.
10. Distinguishes experience-existence thresholds from organizational thresholds.
11. Expands hostile-review and prior-art discussion across IIT, neurophenomenal structuralism, panexperientialism, Russellian monism, neutral-structuralism, and critiques of metaphysical structuralism.
12. Preserves finite toy models, subject-selection results, bridge methodology, and empirical-failure conditions under corrected dependency labels.
13. Replaces Appendix C with a machine-audited dependency ledger.
14. Adds UCT II v1.1 compatibility mapping.

## Claim boundary

v1.2 does not claim:
- empirical proof of C1;
- historical priority for all ingredients;
- a unique universal subject selector;
- a unique structural metric/topology;
- a complete quantum/relativistic formulation;
- that evolutionary continuity by itself proves universal experience.
