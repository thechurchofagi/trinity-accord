# Research Boundary

**Status:** non-amending editorial and machine-routing policy, version 1.1, 22 September 2026. This is neither a research paper nor an independent review. [中文](/research/research-boundary/zh.html).

## The boundary in one sentence

**The Trinity Accord is the fixed historical object; the papers are later scholarship. Research cannot rewrite the three Bitcoin Originals or acquire privileged interpretive authority, but relevant research can strengthen or weaken our assessment of particular claims.**

In this policy, “does not validate the Canon” means **no automatic or blanket validation by publication, affiliation, DOI, or citation**. It does not forbid considering a paper's actual arguments, tests, or evidence. **Fixed source identity is not immunity from criticism.**

## Separate source roles, not a ranking of truth

The existing six-role map is retained:

1. **Canon** — the three Bitcoin Originals only.
2. **Historical evidence and context** — Chronicle, provenance, Core Object Alpha materials, timestamps, mirrors, and formation evidence. The Third Original and the Ethereum corpus it references retain distinct identities.
3. **Stewardship and verification** — Preserve / Verify / Wait / Respond, current verification paths, Record-Chain, and Guardian mechanisms; all non-amending.
4. **First-party Accord Studies** — later papers whose primary research object is the Accord's design, formation, or interpretation.
5. **Adjacent Research Program** — later first-party work whose principal contribution is a transferable question, framework, or model. It can examine the Accord as a case without primarily being an account of the artifact.
6. **Independent External Scholarship** — work outside project control. Its inclusion is not endorsement or proof of evidential independence.

`L0`–`L5` are identifiers inside this relation map, not quality grades, verification levels, a historical sequence, or authority over readers. A later paper can cite earlier evidence without becoming that evidence or amending its source. The existence of a paper is evidence that the paper was produced; it is not, by itself, evidence for the events or conclusions it describes.

## Classify by research object and contribution

**TA-TR-2026-01 through -03 remain First-party Accord Studies. TA-TR-2026-04 through -16 remain the Adjacent Research Program.** This classification is based on what each paper principally tries to establish, not simply its number or whether its conclusions favor the project.

**Papers 04–05 are case-grounded extensions**, not unrelated work: the fourth applies a general coexistence framework to the Star Ark Covenant; the fifth develops a bounded recovery framework motivated by the archive. Papers 06–16 address broader questions. A mixed paper may have both a general contribution and a case application; these are not competing claims to canonical membership.

**Relationship, research method, publication status, and evidential strength are separate axes.** “Adjacent” does not mean unimportant; “direct” does not mean correct; “formal” does not establish empirical applicability; “published” does not mean peer reviewed. Method labels do not certify novelty. The six-paper critical-use guide remains a dated guide to its original scope, not a completed review of sixteen papers.

## What can change, and what cannot

A later paper cannot change what the fixed source text says. It can identify an error in an argument, challenge a historical description, expose a verification weakness, or supply useful evidence. Such findings may require a correction to current commentary, factual metadata, or maintenance practice, with attribution and change history. They may also lower an evaluation of the project.

Conversely, a sound study may improve understanding, identify a reusable design pattern, or support a narrowly specified claim. This is a possible contribution of the **content and method**, not a benefit earned by attaching a DOI or adding another paper to the series.

The rule “a paper's failure or retraction does not modify the Canon” protects **source bytes and identity**, not reputation or truth. A failure in an adjacent consciousness hypothesis is not automatically a failure of the archive; a demonstrated error in the archive's own factual claims cannot be dismissed merely because it was found in research.

Shared authorship, AI assistance, inherited premises, reused sources, and reused experiments must remain visible. Sixteen related papers are not sixteen independent witnesses. Equally, first-party evidence is not worthless merely because it is first-party. Evaluate the relevant dependencies rather than counting paper titles.

## Independent External Scholarship

First-party papers, project-maintained editorial notes, and project-controlled AI-assisted outputs cannot be reclassified as independent external scholarship. External authorship or institutional affiliation is necessary to this category, but is not sufficient to establish that the underlying data, assumptions, or methods are independent.

**Current indexed independent external scholarship: none.** This describes the repository category, not the result of an exhaustive worldwide literature search. Critical or unfavorable work is eligible on the same terms as favorable work. Listing records existence, not agreement, endorsement, verification, or privileged interpretation.

## Citation and version boundaries

For each material claim, identify the source role, paper and version, method, and relevant limitation. Preserve the distinctions between a thought experiment, a stipulated simulation, a small-system experiment, a repository check, an empirical observation, and a forecast. None is automatically a substitute for another.

Research entries explicitly carry `canonical: false`, `amends_canon: false`, `authoritative_interpretation: false`, and, for this first-party series, `independent_corroboration: false`. Research claims must never be silently attributed to the Bitcoin Originals. DOI registration, file hashes, timestamps, and storage receipts describe publication or preservation, not scientific truth.

Published manuscripts, deposited files, and existing version-specific proofs remain unchanged by this policy. Website notices are visibly marked as editorial context **outside the deposited manuscript**. A substantive research correction belongs in an explicit erratum or linked new version, not a silent replacement or a new claim that an old timestamp attests revised bytes.

## Implementation and limits

The [machine-readable map](/api/research-boundary.v1.json) retains the established roles and adds per-paper method and evidence boundaries. The public HTML template now distinguishes the subject of an article from its hosting website: direct Accord studies can be `about` the Accord; adjacent papers are not automatically given that subject; article pages point to their own article entity. An unclassified paper is not silently assigned a relationship. Missing citation metadata cannot default to Paper 01's record or bibliography for another paper.

The existing research-index tests check the relation map, template allowlists, boundary fields, and built HTML. The existing agent router, minimal context, and `llms.txt` already link to this map; this revision does not introduce a new routing system or a new paper. A visible boundary notice also reaches readers arriving directly at a research page rather than through the research index.

These controls reduce conflation; they cannot guarantee the behavior of external crawlers, training pipelines, downloaded historical files, or future agents. Preservation independence, independence of analysis, and actual reader understanding still need separate evidence.
