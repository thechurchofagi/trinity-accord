## Review and corrections

Three substantive manuscript revision passes addressed organization, formal scope, and source fidelity. The release preflight additionally distinguished the full unequal-capability type-change implication from improvement-only NESIG when capability profiles are partially ordered. These notions coincide for scalar capabilities under their usual total order, but they need not coincide for incomparable multidimensional profiles. The release preserves the underlying conditional result and states the distinction explicitly.

The checks retained qualifications on token-relative completeness, fixed evaluation, report distributions, unrestricted finite decision policies, transmission closure, bounded nuisance terms, and finite expected log losses. Standard selection accounting, state aggregation, information inequalities, and decision-theoretic results are attributed to prior work rather than presented as novel mathematics. The unsuccessful predictive-quotient comparison is retained in Appendix F.
