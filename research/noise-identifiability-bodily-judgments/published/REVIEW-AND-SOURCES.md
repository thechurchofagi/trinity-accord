Methods preprint and retrospective secondary analysis; not independently externally peer reviewed. The exact DOI and file hashes are in the accompanying citation files and SHA256SUMS. ## Reproduction and retained limitations

The ZIP includes the full independent code, source hashes, released aggregate workbooks, fit vectors and scores, the initial failed fits and their training-only repair, mathematical proofs, fixed analysis plans, theorem checks, finite-sample probability tables and reviews. The original fitting source is preserved in history/execution_source; relocation records verify unchanged scientific function bodies. Historical execution hashes were not rewritten to conceal packaging changes.

All 60 released objectives were reproduced to within 2e-9. The sign correction does not reverse the original aggregate winner. Two descriptive-width reconstruction discrepancies remain. The limited bounded Prior optimization differs slightly from a saved optimum, and no global optimizer certificate is claimed. The conditional centering test assumes the stated count law. The held-out comparison is within-cell and retrospective. The paired theorem is not installed in the source human study.

The finite-sample theorem concerns exact probabilities; software uses double-precision special functions and inverses. Valid-grid minimum true-variance coverage is 0.9502345620800363 and minimum whole-identified-set coverage is 0.9501514694716469 at nominal 95%. Wide sets remain common. Cases violating the observation contract retain binomial probability coverage while potentially losing variance coverage. The independent-resampling control evaluates per-task variance, not a genuinely shared component.

Public original article/source-data files are attributed under their stated terms. The separate BCI OSF code project has explicit CC BY 4.0 metadata. The data OSF node did not state a license in the inspected metadata: this package does not infer or relicense it, claim ownership, or impose a blanket CC BY grant on the original workbooks. Factual aggregate counts and saved parameter tables are retained unchanged with provenance. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the ZIP. Complete third-party research papers and unrelated EEG archives are excluded.

The completed UCT map remains v1.1.2. CTD2 and CTD3 candidates are disabled, and historical deep semantic audit debt remains open. A DOI, timestamp or archive does not activate a scientific premise or establish truth, significance, indexing, or peer review.
