## Nature of the study

This is a theoretical preprint. The complete manuscript is in Chinese and contains an English title and abstract. It has thirteen numbered sections, twenty-one thought experiments and thirty bibliographic entries. The method combines conceptual analysis, explicit finite constructions, mathematical arguments within declared assumptions, and comparison with prior theoretical and empirical literature. No empirical or computational research experiments were conducted or are reported. Software used for document preparation or publication integrity checks is not presented as a scientific experiment.

The endogenous reference field hypothesis proposes that content-dependent processing and operative reference constraints reciprocally constitute a running organization with a phenomenal aspect. This bridge is a substantive hypothesis. It does not follow merely from assigning mathematical names to causal relations. The organizational profile is multidimensional; task intelligence, evidential uncertainty, negative reward and phenomenal intensity are not interchangeable quantities.

## Specific arguments and their limits

The first separation construction gives a family of finite mechanisms with identical maximal modulation strengths but different numbers of distinguishable responses. The second gives mechanisms that agree on every ordinary input-output sequence while differing under shared internal intervention interfaces. These establish limitations of particular organizational summaries and ordinary behavioral equivalence under the stated constructions. They do not independently establish the presence or absence of experience, and no new general mathematical tool is claimed.

Response differentiation is expressed through a resolution profile. Redundant response labels do not by themselves increase differentiation, and a stability bound relates profiles when corresponding response distributions change by a bounded amount. The resolution parameter represents an analytic discrimination scale, not a threshold at which consciousness comes into existence.

The manuscript also defines a family of inclusion-minimal mechanism supports relative to a specified response task, intervention family and omission protocol. It retains multiple sufficient supports and distinguishes joint tasks, implementation redundancy and newly constituted organization. A support count is not a subject count. The construction does not choose a unique natural physical grain or provide a universally applicable rule for locating all conscious subjects.

The thought experiments examine coordinate changes, copies, overlapping descriptions, common resources, simple controllers, stable activity and replay, graded coupling, initialization and learning, loss rescaling, intelligence, meditation, temporal scaling, synchronous and asynchronous implementation, gradual neural replacement, brain-computer offloading, and branching or reunion. They test consistency and expose the hypothesis's costs. They do not substitute stipulated outcomes for empirical findings.

## Prior work and source boundaries

The manuscript's thirty-item bibliography is the authoritative list of cited sources. It includes journal articles, author manuscripts, explicitly identified preprints, an animal-consciousness declaration, and official technical documentation. These source types have different evidential roles: a declaration is not an experiment, and a preprint is not represented as a peer-reviewed finding.

The core comparison acknowledges perspective and reference constraints in [Safron's IWMT](https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2020.00030) and the [Projective Consciousness Model](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02571); learning and internal representation in [Cleeremans](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00086); exclusion in [IIT 4.0](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465); overlapping subjects in [Roelofs and Sebo](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-024-02167-x); and the prior [multidimensional analysis of animal consciousness](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2020.07.007). The manuscript also discusses extended consciousness, minimal experience, biological evidence, AI memory and training, computational implementation, and challenges to inference across computational descriptions.

Its response signatures are distinguished from the stationary-process causal states of [computational mechanics](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9907176). It does not inherit that literature's optimality conclusions merely by using an equivalence relation on responses. Separation sets, total variation distance and minimal supports are established mathematical tools. The originality claim concerns the particular reference hypothesis, finite constructions and connected consistency arguments; it is not a claim to have originated consciousness multidimensionality, overlapping minds, nonbiological realization or those mathematical tools. The source comparison is not an exhaustive global priority search.

## Remaining theoretical commitments

The hypothesis explicitly accepts that simple artificial mechanisms may satisfy parts of its organizational description. An intuitive objection to such mechanisms is not resolved by silently adding an unmotivated complexity threshold. The proposed bridge and the adequacy of the chosen reference relations remain open to criticism.

Unresolved issues include natural grain selection, exact subject individuation, the mapping to particular phenomenal qualities, and the organization of positive or negative valence. Graded organization does not prove that every phenomenal transition must be continuous. Conversely, weak evidence does not mean weak experience. The paper makes no established finding that present AI systems have consciousness and does not infer guaranteed human-machine coexistence from a consciousness hypothesis.
