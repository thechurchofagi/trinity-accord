# Method, scope, and version note — TA-TR-2026-10 v1.2

## Research method

This paper is a theoretical and conceptual study. Its method combines:

1. explicit causal-model definitions and intervention semantics;
2. finite constructive counterexamples and separation arguments;
3. subject-scope analysis through minimal mechanism supports and decomposition profiles;
4. systematic thought experiments covering copying, shared memory, communication, replay, training, asynchronous execution, gradual replacement, branching, and behaviorally equivalent implementations;
5. comparison with neighboring consciousness theories and contextual-modulation research.

No human, animal, or machine-consciousness experiment is reported in v1.2. The small numerical training proof-of-concept published in v1.1 is intentionally not used as evidence in this revision.

## Originality boundary

The paper does not claim to originate multidimensional consciousness, overlapping subjects, nonbiological realization, contextual modulation, gain modulation, minimal support concepts, total variation, packing numbers, or causal-intervention analysis as general ideas. The claimed contribution is their specific synthesis into ERF: a cross-substrate reciprocal-reference process theory joined to an experiential-attribution framework that distinguishes organizational type, actual realization instance, causal continuation, support families, decomposition profiles, and graded phenomenal organization.
