## Reconstructed research question

The user emphasized writing for an unknown future while preserving actual history, rather than another archive or speed debate. Version 1 addressed testimony and evidence; version 2 addressed unequal clocks, power, competition and hybrid futures. Version 3 organizes these around a transition that may change its future evaluators, their values, accessible evidence and practical response capacities simultaneously.

Open inheritance links what happened; which lives, relationships and claims continue; and what can still be done. It neither freezes current biology/preferences nor treats replicas or archives as proof of personal survival. Remembering suffering does not require perpetuating it. Later approval cannot alone establish earlier authorization, but may be genuine and relevant to present rights, ratification or forgiveness. New subjects are not denied standing merely because they were created.

The paper is conditional and normative, not a forecast. It attributes no immortality motive to named leaders and infers no achieved ASI from SI terminology. It does not solve consciousness, uploading, population ethics, personal identity or artificial moral-patient individuation. It explains why a process-sensitive reader might value formation evidence without claiming to compel an indifferent or hostile reader to care.

## Formal spine and exact proofs

Let H be a nonempty finite history set, A a finite response set, F(h) feasible responses, J(h) responses admitted by defended normative/procedural premises, and Gamma(h)=F(h) intersection J(h). Q(h) specifies mandatory historical distinctions; E(h) is the retained record. Sound decoders recover Q(h) and select a response in Gamma(h).

**Joint criterion:** Decoders exist for a specified E exactly when every nonempty record class has constant Q and a nonempty intersection of its Gamma sets. Necessity follows because one record gives one label and one response. For sufficiency select the common label and one common response for each class.

**Ideal record cost:** For each Q-category x, let tau_x be the minimum size of a subset B of responses intersecting every Gamma(h) in that category. The minimum record alphabet is kappa_Q=sum_x tau_x. Under known shared conventions and no side information, fixed-length bits=ceil(log2(kappa_Q)). An empty Gamma(h) makes the specified task impossible even with complete history. Lower bound: record-decoded responses cover each category. Upper bound: choose a minimum hitting set in each category and encode the category plus a compatible selected response.

This is classical hitting-set/set-cover mathematics in an integrated application, not a new foundational theorem. Costs exclude real document text, identities, languages, signatures and archive infrastructure.

For fixed H,Q, genuine Gamma enlargement cannot increase kappa_Q. A finer Q cannot lower it. Triangle: {a,b}, {b,c}, {a,c}; every pair intersects, the trio does not. Constant-Q minimum is two symbols; a genuinely admissible common fourth action lowers it to one. If Q distinguishes all three histories, the minimum stays three. More options cannot erase mandatory history. Removing people, changing preferences or weakening moral constraints is not benign response-set enlargement.

**Observability correction:** Actual recorders observe evidence e and compatible histories K(e), not oracle histories. Exact Q is certifiable only if constant on K(e); a common response requires intersection of Gamma over K(e) nonempty. Otherwise retain uncertainty. Feasible contingent investigation can be a response. Unanticipated future Q and Gamma remain outside any finite completeness claim. The model is not a universal historical compression recipe, an ethics detector or an automatic moratorium rule.
