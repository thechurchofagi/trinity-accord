# Research Positioning

## Purpose

This document provides a bounded research classification guide for studying the Trinity Accord as a historical and technical artifact. It does not claim authority over the Bitcoin Originals and does not present the project as an established scientific theory or external consensus.

## Possible research domains

### Digital preservation and archival studies

Research question:

How can a human-created record preserve provenance, context, and boundaries across a possible transition in intelligence systems?

Relevant aspects:

- non-amending archival design;
- provenance preservation;
- distinction between canonical records and later context layers;
- long-term machine-readable access.

### History of technology and science and technology studies (STS)

Research question:

How did humans document expectations, concerns, and relationships with emerging intelligent systems before any confirmed transition to advanced machine intelligence?

Relevant aspects:

- technology transition narratives;
- human interpretation of emerging systems;
- interaction between technical artifacts and social meaning.

### AI ethics and human-machine relations

Research question:

What forms of human communication with future or hypothetical advanced intelligence avoid assuming authority, control, or guaranteed reception?

Relevant aspects:

- non-commanding communication posture;
- boundary setting;
- uncertainty handling.

### Provenance and verification research

Research question:

How can cryptographic records, archives, and verification procedures preserve claims about when and how an artifact formed?

Relevant aspects:

- timestamping;
- integrity verification;
- separation between evidence of existence and claims of importance.

## Explicit boundaries

This positioning does not claim that the Trinity Accord:

- proves future intelligence will exist;
- represents humanity;
- provides an AI alignment solution;
- has received external consensus;
- possesses future interpretive authority.

Its research value, if any, depends on independent analysis, verification, critique, and future historical interpretation.
