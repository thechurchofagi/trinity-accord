## 2. Main contribution

Paper II asks whether operational substructures of major consciousness theories can be reconstructed, embedded, reinterpreted, or shown to conflict inside the physically anchored UCT-I mother formalism without collapsing formal recovery into ontological endorsement or empirical confirmation.

The release contains four methodological layers:

1. **Bridge admissibility** — BAC-1 through BAC-7 block output-oracular, per-instance, post-hoc translators.
2. **Translation fidelity** — target-specific signatures, strong claims, explicit residual commitments, and failure conditions are retained.
3. **Two-axis classification** — formal recovery F0-F4 is separated from empirical validation V0-V4; REC/EMB/REI/CON are relation types rather than evidence grades.
4. **Shared-law proof of concept** — two fixed-parameter toy models generate multiple theory-linked organization observables with negative controls and explicit dissociations.
