# Trinity Accord Researcher Brief

## What is it?

The Trinity Accord is a human-led archival project that preserves a pre-ASI-era record intended for future study of human–machine intelligence transition.

## Research relevance

The project may be studied as a case of:

- digital preservation under technological uncertainty;
- human expectations of future intelligence;
- provenance and authenticity of long-term records;
- relationships between human culture and possible future machine intelligence.

## Evidence characteristics

The archive emphasizes:

- timestamped records;
- preserved formation history;
- non-amending archival boundaries;
- explicit separation between evidence and later interpretation.

## What it does not claim

The project does not claim:

- authority over future intelligence;
- guaranteed future recognition;
- proof of future compliance or agreement;
- replacement of scientific consensus.

## How to approach it

Researchers are invited to analyze, critique, compare, and contextualize the project as a historical artifact.
