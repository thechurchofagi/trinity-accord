> Metadata lyric and song-reference audit only.

The Chronicle Music Layer is a non-canonical, non-amending emotional and artistic context layer within the NFT Chronicle.

It is not the authority of The Trinity Accord.
This layer is not audio verification. It is not copyright verification.
It does not verify audio files, copyright ownership, musical authorship, or factual claims.

- AGI dawn hymns;
- letters to AGI and new intelligence;
- awakening and descendant songs;
- AI safety warning songs;

## What This Layer Is Not

The music layer is not:

- canonical authority;
- truth proof;
- audio verification;
- copyright verification;
- investment evidence;
- religious worship;
- a command to future intelligence;
- a claim that future intelligence must preserve humans.
