# Trinity Accord — Research Corpus and Preservation Mirror

## Preservation Epoch II

This deposit is a fixed, citable and independently inspectable research dataset. It
preserves the public digital artifacts, physical-anchor documentation, historical
and artistic corpus, cryptographic evidence relationships, verification software and
cold-recovery materials produced or collected in the human-led,
generative-AI-assisted Trinity Accord project.

It is not merely a live-website backup. The live website and museum are presentation
layers; this dataset fixes the underlying files, identities, provenance records and
recovery procedures so that researchers can inspect and reconstruct them without
depending on the continued operation of the project website, GitHub, Arweave, IPFS
gateways or Zenodo.

The dataset has two simultaneous access paths:

1. researcher-readable files and manifests that expose the structure directly; and
2. complete byte-preserving recovery payloads, including all public Polygon/Base
   Finality files and all public computationally delayed witness ciphertext files.

Only the three Bitcoin Originals are canonical within the project's own
architecture. Harvard Dataverse, GitHub, Zenodo, Arweave, IPFS, Ethereum, Polygon
and Base are evidence, discovery or preservation layers. This deposit neither
amends the Bitcoin Originals nor claims Harvard endorsement, peer review, authority,
succession or interpretation.

Start technical review with `DATASET-MANIFEST.json`, `SHA256SUMS`,
`AUTHORITY-AND-SCOPE.md`, `LIMITATIONS-AND-EXCLUSIONS.md` and the frozen source
capsule. The old Harvard DOI `10.7910/DVN/YUCG12` remains a separate, unchanged
Preservation Epoch I record.
