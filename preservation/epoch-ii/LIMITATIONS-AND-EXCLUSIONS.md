# Limitations and exclusions

- Hashes establish byte identity, not philosophical truth, artistic importance,
  physical-object identity or institutional endorsement.
- Remote photographs, microscopy and video document Core Object Alpha but do not
  equal direct physical forensic examination of its observable flaws.
- The 139 publication-authorized historical rows remain commitment-only until exact
  SHA-256 and byte-count matches are recovered. Historical exact-byte inventory is
  therefore incomplete even though this does not create a current selected
  project-content gap.
- The three computationally delayed witness archives preserve complete public
  ciphertext packages. Their absent plaintext and unlock material are intentional.
- Media CAR verification preserves the recorded parent-versus-leaf CID boundary. It
  does not convert a verified leaf archive into an unsupported full-DAG claim.
- Seven externally delivered ERC-1155 roots remain part of the truthful 250/257
  wallet observation. They are not Trinity Accord project content; therefore the
  project-content gap is zero while the external wallet-level byte inventory remains
  incomplete.
- Cumulative Bitcoin node checkpoints (approximately 88.65 GB) are operational
  reproducibility caches rather than Epoch II research-corpus payload. Their
  identities and latest recovery guidance are retained without uploading every
  superseded checkpoint.
- Superseded museum packages are presentation history. The selected current package
  supports offline reconstruction; the museum interface is not Canon.
- Still-valid credentials, access tokens and third-party secrets are security
  material, not research payload.
- Component-specific copyright, license and third-party-rights notices remain in
  force. The Dataset-level public-access terms do not relicense them.
