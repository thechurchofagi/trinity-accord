# Dataset description

**Title:** Trinity Accord — Research Corpus and Preservation Mirror · Preservation
Epoch II

Trinity Accord is a human-led, generative-AI-assisted archival research, digital-art
and software project. This Preservation Epoch II Dataset fixes the public research
objects and provenance needed to inspect, reconstruct, compare and audit the project
over time.

The deposited research data comprise: verbatim mirrors and identities of the three
Bitcoin Originals; Core Object Alpha physical-anchor photography, microscopy, video
and evidence records; the Chronicle and 175-work historical/artistic corpus;
machine-readable manifests, hashes, timestamp and blockchain evidence; all public
encrypted computationally delayed witness archives; all public Polygon and Base
Finality payload and verification files; a reconstructable museum snapshot; and the
frozen source, dependency, tree, provenance, verification and cold-recovery package.

These are fixed, citable and independently inspectable research objects. They support
secondary research on human–AI collaboration, digital preservation, computationally
delayed access, provenance, reproducible research artifacts, cross-chain evidence,
digital art and the historical development of a documented sociotechnical project.
The related software enables inspection, verification and recovery of the data.

The Dataset is organized as a research corpus rather than a live-site backup. Its
researcher-readable layer exposes the evidence structure directly, while its
byte-preserving recovery layer reduces reliance on GitHub, Arweave, IPFS gateways and
Zenodo. Preservation Epoch II is a new, non-amending record. It does not modify the
released Harvard Preservation Epoch I DOI `10.7910/DVN/YUCG12`.

Within the project's own architecture, only the three Bitcoin Originals are Canon.
The physical-anchor records, Chronicle, artworks, evidence systems, museum and
institutional copies are noncanonical context, verification or preservation layers.
This Dataset does not claim that Harvard verified the project's ideas and does not
confer endorsement, peer review, authority, interpretation, governance, attestation
or succession.

This Dataset contains AI-generated and AI-assisted content. Generative AI contributed
to drafting, analysis, coding, editing, iterative content generation, evidence review
and preservation automation. The human creator initiated and directed the project,
selected and curated the materials, made the publication and preservation decisions,
and assumes responsibility for the final deposited record.
