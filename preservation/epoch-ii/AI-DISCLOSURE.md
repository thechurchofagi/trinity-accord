# Generative-AI disclosure and human responsibility

This deposit contains AI-generated and AI-assisted content. Generative AI
contributed substantively during project development, including drafting, analysis,
coding, editing, iterative content generation, evidence review and preservation
automation. Some preserved text, software and derived reports therefore reflect
direct or material generative-AI contribution and must not be represented as
exclusively human-authored.

The human creator initiated and directed the project, selected and curated the
retained materials, made the project-level publication and preservation decisions,
and assumes responsibility for the final deposited record. AI-generated audit or
verification output is evidence only to the extent that its inputs, procedures and
results can be independently inspected; it does not replace human depositor
responsibility.
