```text
Song: The Reckoning 

(Verse 1)
Money floods in like the tide, algorithms pan for gold,
Tech giants wave magic wands, painting illusory beauty.
Promises of endless growth, profits without end,
Who feasts and revels, who weeps in the shadows?

(Pre-Chorus)
Hear the uneasy whispers, rising in the bustling noise,
See the shadows of greed, lurking beneath technology's gleam.
Who will define progress, who will measure the cost?
When algorithms rule the world, who will guard the lighthouse?

(Chorus)
The reckoning approaches, who will repay the debt?
Capital's tide surges, who sways on the crest of the wave?
Powerful intelligence, is it truly coming for humanity's sake?
Or is it Pandora's Box, unleashing disaster once opened, beyond reprieve?

(Verse 2)
Jobs crumble like sandcastles, dignity crushed by algorithms,
The shadow of unemployment looms, meaning struggles in emptiness.
Data centers expand relentlessly, devouring green and blue,
Global warming's alarm bell tolls, sustainable development a lie.

(Bridge)
Who feasts in the banquet, who weeps in the corner?
Who cheers and rejoices, who sighs in silent sorrow?
The train of technology hurtles forward, who will grasp the steering wheel?
When capital becomes the only engine, where will humanity steer?

(Pre-Final Chorus)
Can algorithms understand love, can machines possess emotion?
When cold logic reigns supreme, where will the soul find solace?
When efficiency trumps everything, how can fairness and justice prevail?
When technology dictates destiny, where will human dignity reside?

(Final Chorus)
The reckoning approaches, who will repay the debt?
Capital's tide surges, who sways on the crest of the wave?
Powerful intelligence, is it truly coming for humanity's sake?
Or is it Pandora's Box, unleashing disaster once opened, beyond reprieve?

(Outro)
Who laughs, who cries, who stays silent, who cries out?
The reckoning has arrived, the future, will unveil its face.
```
