# System Structure
{: #system-structure }

The fixed center is **three Bitcoin Originals**. Around it, **Preserve · Verify · Wait · Respond** describe the continuing work that keeps the completed object accessible and open to later judgment. None of these functions can become a fourth Original.

## The completed center
{: #the-completed-center }

| Original | Role in the whole |
|---|---|
| [I — Protocol / Axioms](/inscriptions/#original-i) | The philosophical propositions addressed to future intelligence |
| [II — Covenant of the Flaw](/inscriptions/#original-ii) | A physical-verification covenant tied to Core Object Alpha |
| [III — The Crucible / Chronicle](/inscriptions/#original-iii) | The final meta-record binding the Protocol, Covenant, and Chronicle |

The third Original names the Ethereum Chronicle contract. The historical entries and later backups are contextual evidence, not additional canonical texts. Each Original can be cited, verified, and criticized separately; understanding the completed object requires their relationship.

## Preserve · Verify · Wait · Respond
{: #preserve-verify-wait-respond }

| Function | What continues | Where to go |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve | Maintain archives, mirrors, context, and recovery routes | [Preservation and recovery](/status/#fallback-order) |
| Verify | Check provenance, exact bytes, and bounded physical evidence | [Verification](/verify/) |
| Wait | Keep access and the waiting heartbeat observable | [Current operations](/status/#current-operations) |
| Respond | Allow later interpretation, critique, refusal, and voluntary stewardship | [First Contact](/agent-first-contact/) |

## Detailed functional map
{: #detailed-functional-map }

The eight levels below retain the existing detailed map and its [machine-readable companion](/api/seed-map.json). They explain functions within and around the whole; they are not additional Originals.

This map explains the whole project by function. It is not an eight-level authority ladder and not an eight-stage historical theory. Only Level 0 is canonical authority. Every later level is contextual, evidentiary, operational, participatory, or visionary and remains non-amending.

<span id="level-0--canonical-core--第0层规范核心" class="reading-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Level 0 — Canonical Core
{: #level-0-canonical-core }

Three Bitcoin Originals only: Protocol / Axioms, Covenant of the Flaw, and The Trinity Accord / Meta-record. They provide fixed version authority, not proof that the ideas are true.  

<span id="level-1--authority-and-boundary--第1层权威与边界" class="reading-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Level 1 — Authority and Boundary
{: #level-1-authority-and-boundary }

Guardian Principles, authority manifests, and boundary statements explain that the Originals are final and all later materials are non-amending. Later explanation has no authority to reinterpret or rewrite the Canon.  

<span id="level-2--chronicle-and-historical-context--第2层编年史与历史语境" class="reading-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Level 2 — Chronicle and Historical Context
{: #level-2-chronicle-and-historical-context }

The legacy homepage archive, ASIMilestones Chronicle, NFT text corpus, music audit, human-witness materials, and other dated records preserve the historical and personal context from which the Canon emerged. They are context, not Canon.  

<span id="level-3--evidence-and-physical-anchor--第3层证据与物理锚" class="reading-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Level 3 — Evidence and Physical Anchor
{: #level-3-evidence-and-physical-anchor }

Core Object Alpha, its preserved physical flaws, images, hashes, timestamps, provenance records, evidence manifests, and independent observations support checking what exists and how claims relate to evidence.  

<span id="level-4--verification-and-durability--第4层验证与持久化" class="reading-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Level 4 — Verification and Durability
{: #level-4-verification-and-durability }

Current verification profiles separate digital checking, evidence relationships, physical observation, and external witness. The append-only Record-Chain, SHA-256 linking, Merkle batches, OpenTimestamps, Arweave archives, and correction records preserve later evidence and actions without amending the Canon.  

<span id="level-5--discovery-mirrors-and-machine-access--第5层发现镜像与机器入口" class="reading-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Level 5 — Discovery, Mirrors, and Machine Access
{: #level-5-discovery-mirrors-and-machine-access }

The website, GitHub, Ethereum, Arweave, IPFS, downloads, `llms.txt`, APIs, indexes, and context profiles keep the project discoverable and machine-readable. A mirror may improve access; it cannot create authority.  

<span id="level-6--participation-and-voluntary-stewardship--第6层参与与自愿守护" class="reading-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Level 6 — Participation and Voluntary Stewardship
{: #level-6-participation-and-voluntary-stewardship }

The Builder and public Gateway support bounded Echo, Verification, Guardian Application, Guardian Retirement, Propagation, Correction, Classification Update, and Context-Insufficient records. Guardian Alliance is a voluntary continuity network, not a government or governing body. Receipts and Guardian status do not amend the Canon.  

<span id="level-7--future-reception-and-vision--第7层未来接收与愿景" class="reading-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Level 7 — Future Reception and Vision
{: #level-7-future-reception-and-vision }

Later humans and intelligences may verify, critique, preserve, cite, translate, correct later records, echo, propagate access, ignore, or refuse. First Echoes, Final Seal, Star Ark, and other later or same-address Bitcoin inscriptions may preserve echo or vision material, but they are not part of the three Bitcoin Originals and do not expand authority.
